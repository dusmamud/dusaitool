Koi bhi AI tool aapke liye UPSC clear nahi kar sakta — Prelims, Mains aur Personality Test me ab bhi aapki apni padhai, likhai aur yaad rakhne ki mehnat chahiye. Sahi tools sirf roz ka kaam halka kar sakte hain. Seedha jawab: apne notes aur PDF se padhne ke liye Gemini Notebook (pehle NotebookLM), sources ke saath current affairs ke liye Perplexity, tutor aur answer practice ke liye ChatGPT, revision flashcards ke liye Anki, aur muft mock test aur purane prashn-patra ke liye Testbook. Har AI tool galat tathya bhi bana sakta hai, isliye har baat ko asli source se check karein.

Yeh lekh UPSC CSE 2027 ki taiyari karne walon ke liye hai — Prelims 23 May 2027 ko hoga. Keematein badalti rehti hain, isliye paise dene se pehle official pricing page check karein.

## Seedha jawab, ek table me

| Kaam | Shuruaat karein | Muft limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apne notes se padhna | Gemini Notebook | 100 notebooks, har ek me 50 sources |
| Current affairs research | Perplexity | Roz sirf kuch Pro searches muft |
| Tutoring, answer practice | ChatGPT | Unlimited text chats; roz 3 file uploads |
| Roz ka revision | Anki | iPhone app ke alawa muft |
| Mock test, purane papers | Testbook | Kai test series me sirf 1 muft test |

## 1. Gemini Notebook — apni samagri se padhna

Gemini Notebook Google ka tool hai jo aapke sources se padhata hai; July 2026 me iska naam NotebookLM se badla tha. Aap PDF, notes, Google Docs ya YouTube links daalte hain, aur yeh sirf usi samagri se jawab deta hai, exact page ka citation dekar. Muft plan me 100 notebooks milte hain, har ek me 50 sources tak. 

## 2. Perplexity — sources kholkar current affairs

Perplexity live web search karke har daave par citation deta hai, isliye aap asli PIB release ya ministry page kholkar check kar sakte hain. Muft plan me aam searches unlimited hain, lekin Pro searches roz sirf kuch — help centre roz 3 batata hai, doosri guides lagbhag 5.

## 3. ChatGPT — tutor aur answer-writing practice

ChatGPT se concepts samjhein, answer chhote karwayein aur practice questions banwayein. Muft plan me text chats unlimited hain, lekin file uploads roz sirf 3. Bharat me Go ₹399 aur Plus ₹1,999 mahina hai, GST sahit. Apna answer paste karke iska score na maanein — poochhein ki aapne prashn ka kaunsa hissa chhoda, phir sudhaar ko standard source se check karein. Chatbots par doosri rai ke liye hamari tulna padhein: [ChatGPT aur Gemini se aage AI chatbots](/hi/blog/ai-chatbots-beyond-chatgpt-gemini-india/).

## 4. Anki — chhah mahine baad bhi yaad rehne wala revision

Anki spaced repetition wala flashcard app hai: galat card jald wapas aata hai, sahi card baad me. Desktop, AnkiDroid aur AnkiWeb sync muft hain. Sirf official iPhone app AnkiMobile paid hai — US App Store me $24.99 ek baar.

## 5. Testbook — mock test aur pichle saal ke prashn

Timer lagakar paper dene ki jagah koi AI tool nahi le sakta; Testbook sabse vyavaharik muft shuruaat hai. Iske UPSC pages par purane prashn jawab sahit, muft mock test aur muft 100-day Prelims Programme milte hain. Zyadatar poori series paid hain: Prelims 2027 series me 230 tests me sirf 1 muft hai, NCERT series me 92 me sirf 1. 

## Bharat me in tools ka istemal

Ye paanchon tools Bharat me bina VPN ke chalte hain aur sab Hindi me bhi kaam karte hain. ₹0 wala stack: notes ke liye Gemini Notebook, news ke liye Perplexity, practice ke liye ChatGPT, revision ke liye Anki, Testbook ke muft tests. Paise tabhi dein jab koi limit sach me aapki padhai roke

## Kya na karein

AI summary ko kabhi tathya na maanein — har tareekh, article number aur scheme ka naam notes ya answer me daalne se pehle asli source se check karein. Coupon khatm ho raha hai isliye paid plan na khareedein — purane videos wala Airtel Perplexity offer January 2026 me khatm ho gaya tha. Chatbot se apne answers na likhwayein. 

## Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

**Kya AI tools UPSC ke liye coaching ki jagah le sakte hain?**

Nahi — coaching schedule aur checked answers deti hai, AI tools tez notes aur revision.

**UPSC current affairs ke liye sabse achha AI tool kaunsa hai?**

Perplexity, kyunki har daave par khulne wala citation milta hai.

**Kya ChatGPT UPSC ki taiyari ke liye muft me kaafi hai?**

Zyadatar ke liye haan — muft plan me text chats unlimited, roz 3 file uploads.

**UPSC revision ke liye Anki kaise istemal karein?**

Har tathya ke liye ek chhota card banayein, roz revision karein. Desktop aur AnkiDroid muft hain; sirf iPhone app paid hai.

**Kya UPSC ke muft mock test online milte hain?**

Haan — Testbook ke muft mock test aur 100-day programme, halanki zyadatar series me sirf 1 test muft hai.

**Kya in tools se Hindi me UPSC ki taiyari kar sakte hain?**

Haan — chaaron AI tools Hindi me kaam karte hain, Testbook English aur Hindi dono me hai.
