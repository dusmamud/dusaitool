A mandi trader's day runs on four things: today's price, a ready buyer, a payment that arrives, and a record that survives. The price tools are government-run and free, and the rest have free tiers that work in India without a VPN.

## 1. Agmarknet — daily mandi prices, free

Agmarknet is the Government of India's agricultural market information network, under the Ministry of Agriculture. Mandis report arrivals and prices; the portal publishes the minimum, maximum and modal price per commodity. Watch the modal price, not the maximum. Sources disagree on coverage — around 3,000 mandis in some reports, 4,367 in a government release. Either way it is the widest free mandi price source in India, though data lags a day or two. Confirm the rate with your mandi before promising a farmer a price — a published rate is not a guaranteed offer.

## 2. eNAM — live bids from integrated mandis

eNAM, the National Agriculture Market, is the government's electronic trading platform, launched in 2016. It connects participating mandis so traders and buyers can bid online. Official figures disagree — 1,656 mandis in one 2026 release, 1,522 in another — so check the portal for your mandi. Free price information covers 247 commodities through the eNAM app. If your mandi is on eNAM, you can watch live bids and sell beyond your district.

## 3. WhatsApp Business — your buyer network, free

Most buyers already live on WhatsApp, so this is where deals happen. The free app gives you a business profile, a product catalogue, chat labels, quick replies and greeting messages. Broadcast lists send today's rates to up to 256 contacts at a time. But a broadcast only reaches contacts who saved your number, and repeated unsolicited messages can get your number flagged, so send rates only to buyers who asked for them.

## 4. Khatabook — the digital udhaar khata

Mandi trade runs on credit, and a paper bahi gets lost or disputed. Khatabook is a free digital ledger app for Indian small businesses: each customer gets a separate khata, you record udhaar given and payments received, and the app sends payment reminders by SMS or WhatsApp. Enter every deal the same day, and send the reminder before the due date. It also supports UPI collection through a QR code.

## 5. Vyapar — bills, GST and stock in one place

When billing grows beyond a khata, try Vyapar. Its Android app is free for life and covers GST invoicing, basic inventory, customer and supplier ledgers, and payment reminders, and it works offline. Paid desktop editions start from around ₹3,399 a year in independent guides, so re-check the official pricing page before paying. Start on the free mobile app; pay only when you need desktop billing.

## Using these tools together in India

Check Agmarknet and eNAM in the morning, note the modal price, and send the day's rates to your buyer broadcast list. When a deal is agreed, confirm rate, quantity and payment date in the same chat. Record the deal in Khatabook the same day, and raise the bill in Vyapar if it is a GST sale. Gemini's free tier (₹0 in India) can draft a rate message in Hindi, and Google Sheets can hold a table of deals. Never paste a buyer's phone number, bank details or Aadhaar into any AI chatbot.

## What to avoid

Do not trust a single price screenshot forwarded in a group; check Agmarknet or eNAM first. Do not pay for a "premium mandi rate" feed that only republishes free government data, and do not promise a farmer another mandi's maximum rate. Do not run the whole business on one phone with no backup: export your khata and bills weekly. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs or bank passwords with anyone claiming to be from a price app or a buyer.

## FAQ

**Which is the best free source for daily mandi prices in India?** Agmarknet, which publishes daily min, max and modal prices from mandis across India. eNAM adds live bids for integrated mandis only.

**Is eNAM free for traders?** Price information for 247 commodities is free through the eNAM app and portal. Trading happens through your registered mandi.

**Can I run my mandi business on WhatsApp Business alone?** For buyers and rates, yes. But WhatsApp is not a ledger: pair it with Khatabook and Vyapar, or unpaid deals will pile up.

**How do I collect udhaar payments faster?** Record every credit sale the same day in Khatabook, send a reminder a day before the due date, and share your UPI QR code with it.

**Is Vyapar really free?** The Android app is free for life. Desktop editions are paid, from around ₹3,399 a year — re-check the official pricing page first.

**Should I trust AI price predictions for my crop?** No. AI tools can summarise published prices and draft messages, but they cannot predict tomorrow's mandi rate.
