AI tools can help Indian illustrators draft concepts, explore styles and speed up client revisions, but the finished work should still carry your own hand. Firefly is the safest free start for client work, Leonardo AI gives the most generous free daily allowance, and Midjourney delivers the most polished style references. Keep AI as a draft stage, not the final art.

## Adobe Firefly

Firefly is Adobe's image generator, trained on licensed Adobe Stock and public-domain material, so its output is the most defensible choice when a client asks where an image came from. The free plan offers daily generations, and Adobe says output from its own Firefly models is safe for commercial use. Standard costs $9.99 a month.

## Leonardo AI

Leonardo gives every free account 150 fast tokens a day, enough for dozens of test images and a serious daily practice habit. Free creations are public and limited to the Auto model, while paid plans from $12 a month unlock private generations and more models. Never generate unreleased client work on the free plan, since those images are public.

## Midjourney

Midjourney has no free plan on its website or in Discord; the only limited trial is in the niji journey mobile app. Basic costs $10 a month for about 200 minutes of fast generation time, while Standard at $30 a month adds unlimited relaxed use. Its strength is atmosphere and painterly style, so illustrators use it for mood boards and style references rather than final deliverables.

## Using these tools in India

All three work in India without a VPN and charge in US dollars, so budget ₹800–₹2,500 a month for a paid plan. For commissions, charge for your time, revisions and usage rights, not the tool's output, and tell clients honestly where AI was used. Keep AI drafts out of your main portfolio unless the client agreed.

## What to avoid

Never sell raw AI output as original illustration, never upload a client's confidential brief to a free plan where generations are public, and never promise a fixed date on a free tier that can run out mid-project. Prices and limits change often, so re-check official pricing pages before paying or quoting.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I sell AI-generated illustrations to clients in India?**

Yes, if the terms allow commercial use and the client knows AI was involved. Firefly's own models and Leonardo's paid plans are the safest routes.

**Which free AI tool is best for an illustrator starting out?**

Leonardo AI, for its daily 150 tokens, with Firefly alongside for anything a client will publish. Together they cover practice and safe drafts at zero cost.

**Should AI work appear in my illustration portfolio?**

Only as process or clearly labelled experiments. Clients hire you for a recognisable style, and generic AI images hide exactly that.

**How should I price an AI-assisted commission?**

Price your hours, revisions and usage licence, as for any job. AI may shorten drafting, but your judgement is still the work being bought.

## Final verdict

Start free with Firefly for client-safe drafts and Leonardo for practice; pay for Midjourney only when its style work earns its keep.
