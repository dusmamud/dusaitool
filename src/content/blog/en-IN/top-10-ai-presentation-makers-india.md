Gamma gives the best free start: 400 one-time credits turn a prompt into a polished deck.

## Top 10

1. Gamma: full deck from one prompt; free 400 one-time credits.
2. Canva: Magic Design makes template slides; free AI uses limited.
3. Beautiful.ai: auto-formats polished business decks; 14-day trial only, no free plan.
4. SlidesAI: turns text into Google Slides; free 12 presentations a year.
5. Slidesgo: template-led AI decks, editable PPTX; free 3 monthly presentations.
6. Presentations.AI: prompt-to-PowerPoint decks; free Starter, 100 credits, 20 slides.
7. Gemini in Slides: AI help inside Slides; free basics, full features paid.
8. PowerPoint Copilot: drafts outlines, restyles slides; free web chat, in-app paid.
9. Pitch: team pitch decks with analytics; free 100 one-time AI credits.
10. Plus AI: AI inside PowerPoint and Slides; 7-day trial, card required.

**Which AI presentation maker is best free in India?** Gamma, for its 400 free credits; Canva backs it up.

## Final verdict

Start with Gamma's free credits, keep Canva for visuals, pay only when a tool earns it.

Prices and free limits change, check the official pricing page before paying.
