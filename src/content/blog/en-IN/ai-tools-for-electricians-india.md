An electrician in India does not need AI that wires a house. You need tools that win the job, book the visit, send a quote, and get you paid. Jobber and Housecall Pro cover the whole job. Zoho Invoice handles GST quotes and bills for free. Start free, and pay only when jobs grow.

## Short answer

| Tool | Best for | Free option | Starting cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jobber | Growing teams | 14-day trial, no free plan | About $29–$49/month |
| Housecall Pro | Small crews, online booking | 14-day trial, no free plan | $59/month billed annually |
| Zoho Invoice | Solo electricians, GST | Free: 500 invoices/year | ₹0 |

## 1. Jobber — best for growing teams

Jobber is field-service software for trades like electrical work. Build quotes that customers approve from their phone; an approved quote becomes a scheduled job. It adds drag-and-drop scheduling, invoicing, payment reminders, and job costing that tracks labour and material costs. There is no free plan, only a 14-day trial. Check the official pricing page first.

## 2. Housecall Pro — best for solo and small crews

Housecall Pro covers online booking, scheduling, estimates, invoices, and card payments. Customers book online; technicians send estimates and take payment from the mobile app on site. Basic is $59 a month billed annually, $79 month to month, for one user. It has a 14-day trial, no free plan after that. Prices are in US dollars; add payment fees.

## 3. Zoho Invoice — best free option for solo electricians

Zoho Invoice is free, with up to 500 invoices a year and two users. It creates GST invoices, converts an approved quote into an invoice, sends payment reminders, and accepts UPI, card and net-banking payments. It supports GSTR-1 filing and GSTR-3B export. Setup takes longer, and it does not schedule jobs, so pair it with a calendar.

## Using these tools in India

All three work in India without a VPN. Zoho Invoice is the most India-ready, with GST billing and UPI payments built in. Jobber and Housecall Pro charge in US dollars, so weigh the monthly fee against the jobs it wins. For finding work, a Google Business Profile matters more than any of these tools. ## What to avoid

Do not pay for a yearly plan before finishing the free trial. Do not promise a deadline or price the tool cannot guarantee, and never share logins or bank details with anyone offering setup help. Prices and limits change, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## FAQ

**Which tool is best for a solo electrician in India?** Zoho Invoice: it is free, handles GST billing, and covers quotes, invoices and reminders. Use a simple calendar for bookings until job volume justifies a paid tool.

**Do Jobber and Housecall Pro have free plans?** No. Both offer a 14-day free trial with no credit card, then charge a monthly subscription. Neither has a permanently free plan, so budget for the subscription before you start.

**Do these tools work without a VPN in India?** Yes, all three work in India without a VPN. They need stable internet, especially on site when sending an estimate or taking payment.

## Final verdict

Zoho Invoice is free and best for solo electricians; Jobber or Housecall Pro suit growing teams.
