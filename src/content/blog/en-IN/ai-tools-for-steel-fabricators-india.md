Steel fabrication shops in India run on drawings, quotes, cutting lists and delivery dates. The right tools cut the paperwork so welders can weld. For most small workshops, three free tools cover the whole job: FreeCAD for designs, ChatGPT for quotes and client messages, and Zoho Invoice for billing and GST paperwork.

## Short answer

| Job | Best pick | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Designs and drawings | FreeCAD | Free |
| Quotes and client messages | ChatGPT | Free |
| Billing and GST invoices | Zoho Invoice | Free |

## 1. FreeCAD — designs and drawings

FreeCAD is a free, open-source CAD program for drawing gates, grills, staircases and sheds in 2D and 3D. Change one measurement and the whole drawing updates. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, needs no account, and its LGPL licence allows paid commercial work. Expect a learning curve: spend a few evenings on tutorials before your first client drawing.

## 2. ChatGPT — quotes and client messages

ChatGPT drafts quotations, WhatsApp replies and material lists from a short prompt in English or Hindi. Paste your steel rate, sizes and labour cost, and ask for a quote with GST shown separately. The free plan covers unlimited text chats, but uploads, images and voice are limited. Always re-check every rate and measurement — the chatbot can invent numbers.

## 3. Zoho Invoice — billing and GST

Zoho Invoice turns an accepted quote into a GST invoice, sends payment reminders and tracks payments. It is free for Indian businesses, with up to 500 invoices a year and two users, and supports UPI, cards and net banking. It can file GSTR-1 and export GSTR-3B data. Past 500 invoices a year, you need a paid route.

## Using these tools in India

Most Indian shops work on WhatsApp and phone calls, not email. Draft the quote in ChatGPT, send it on WhatsApp, then raise the invoice in Zoho Invoice once the client accepts. Update steel rates in a simple sheet weekly, because an old quote can wipe out your margin. ## What to avoid

Never send a drawing or quote you have not measured twice. Never use a free CAD plan that bans commercial work for paid jobs. Never promise a delivery date the chatbot suggested — check your queue first. Never pay before re-checking the official pricing page — prices and limits change.

## FAQ

**Can AI design a steel gate for me?**

It can draft the drawing and cutting list, but check every measurement, joint and load before cutting — AI does not know your site.

**Which tool is fully free for paid work?**

FreeCAD and Zoho Invoice. FreeCAD has no paid tier, and Zoho Invoice is free for Indian businesses within its limits.

**Can I use ChatGPT in Hindi?**

Yes. Prompt in Hindi or Hinglish, ask for the reply in the same language, and check the numbers before sending.

**Do I need paid plans to start?**

No. Start free, and pay only when you hit a real limit, like Zoho Invoice's 500-invoice yearly cap.
