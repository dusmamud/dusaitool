## The direct answer

Parents of a nursery child in India ask the same questions every day: did my child eat, did they sleep, did they play, and are they happy. A nursery school that answers those questions warmly and regularly earns trust that no advertisement can buy, while one that stays silent makes parents worry and look for another school. The school also has its own daily work: attendance, photos, festival activities, fee reminders and teacher planning, all of which take time away from the children. The direct answer is simple: use WhatsApp Business for daily parent updates, ClassDojo for classroom moments and behaviour points, Seesaw for children's work portfolios, Canva for activity sheets and notices, and ChatGPT for parent messages and activity ideas.

These tools work because they match what Indian nursery parents actually want: a short message, a photo of their child playing, and a teacher who replies. A photo of a child painting or singing is worth more than a long report, and a quick message about lunch or a nap stops a worried parent from calling the school ten times. But there is a hard rule that matters more here than anywhere else: a child's photo, name or personal detail must never be shared publicly or with strangers, and photos should go only to that child's own parents. All five tools have free plans, so check the official page before paying for any premium feature.

## 1. WhatsApp Business: the daily update parents actually read

WhatsApp Business is the most important tool for an Indian nursery school, because it is the one every parent already checks. A short daily update with one photo, such as what the children ate, what they painted, and what song they sang, tells a parent their child had a good day. The Business app adds quick replies for common questions, labels for each class, and a catalogue for fee details and school timings, so the office does not answer the same question fifty times. A broadcast list per class keeps each child's updates private to their own parents. The app is free, and only mobile data or Wi-Fi costs apply, so check the official page.

How to start: agree one update time each day, one photo per child per week, and one teacher responsible for sending it. Watch out for: never share a child's photo in a mixed group, never post children's photos publicly, and never share a child's address or personal details on WhatsApp.

## 2. ClassDojo: classroom moments and positive behaviour

ClassDojo is a classroom app where teachers share photos, videos and moments from the day with parents, and give positive points for good behaviour such as sharing, listening and helping. For a nursery school, its value is that parents see their child's day as it happens, instead of waiting for a term-end report. A parent who sees their child sharing toys feels proud, and a teacher who records positive moments has something kind to say at the parent meeting. ClassDojo is free for teachers, and premium adds more features, so check the official page before paying.

How to start: share two or three moments per class each day, and give points only for positive behaviour, never as punishment. Watch out for: photos in ClassDojo should go only to that child's own parents, and the school must have written permission from parents before sharing any photo.

## 3. Seesaw: a portfolio of each child's work

Seesaw is a digital portfolio app where teachers collect each child's drawings, songs, stories and activities in one place, and parents can see their child's progress over the term. For a nursery school, it replaces the paper folder that gets lost or torn, and it lets a parent see how their child's drawing changed from September to March. Teachers can record a child's voice telling a story, which parents treasure far more than a worksheet. The basic plan is free, and premium is paid, so check the official page before upgrading.

How to start: add one piece of work per child each week, and show parents the portfolio at the next parent meeting. Watch out for: keep the portfolio private to the child's own parents, and never use a child's work or photo in advertising without written permission.

## 4. Canva: activity sheets, notices and festival posters

Canva helps a nursery school make colourful activity sheets, alphabet charts, festival notices and birthday posters without hiring a designer. Its free plan has templates for worksheets, flashcards and notices, and teachers can add the school name, the activity, and a picture, then print them or share them on WhatsApp. A clean notice about a fancy-dress day or a Diwali celebration, sent a week early, means fewer confused parents and happier children. The free plan covers most templates and basic editing, and Pro is paid, so check current pricing on the official page before upgrading.

How to start: make one activity sheet and one notice for next week's festival, and print them for the classroom. Watch out for: keep worksheets simple and age-appropriate, and never use another school's photos or a child's photo in a public poster.

## 5. ChatGPT: parent messages, notices and activity ideas

ChatGPT handles the writing a nursery school does every day: a polite fee reminder, a notice about a holiday, a message to parents about what their child learned this week, or ideas for a rainy-day activity. Give it the facts, such as the date, the activity and the age group, and it drafts a short message in Hindi or English that sounds warm, not formal. It can also suggest simple activities with paper, colours and songs that need no expensive material. The free plan is enough for daily writing, and paid plans add more features, so check current pricing on the official page.

How to start: ask it to draft a short Hindi notice for next week's activity day, then read it and fix the date and time before sending. Watch out for: AI can invent a date or a detail, so check every fact before sending a message to parents.

## Using AI in India: nursery schools

Nursery schools in India have their own shape, and the tools should fit it. First, parents: Indian parents worry most about safety, food and happiness, so a short daily update with a photo matters more than a long term-end report. Second, festivals: Diwali, Holi, Janmashtami and Independence Day are big moments in a nursery, so plan activities and notices around them a week early. Third, language: children and parents may speak Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi or another language at home, so send messages in the language parents actually read. Finally, safety: a child's photo, name and address are sensitive, so share them only with that child's own parents, with written permission, and never publicly.

## What to avoid

Avoid posting any child's photo, name or video publicly, on social media or in advertising, without the parents' written permission. Avoid sharing a child's personal details in a mixed group, and avoid using a child's photo in a poster for the whole school. Avoid sending fee reminders in a rude tone, and avoid trusting AI for dates, fees or activity details without checking them. Avoid comparing children publicly or giving behaviour points as punishment, because nursery children learn from praise, not shame.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best free tool for nursery parent updates in India?**
WhatsApp Business, because every Indian parent already has WhatsApp and reads it daily. A short daily update with one photo, sent at a fixed time, builds more trust than any long report.

**Is it safe to share children's photos on ClassDojo or Seesaw?**
Yes, if photos go only to that child's own parents and the school has written permission from parents. Never post children's photos publicly or in a mixed group, and never use them in advertising without permission.

**How do I make activity sheets for nursery children for free?**
Canva's free plan has worksheet and flashcard templates, and you can add your school name and simple activities, then print them. Keep sheets simple, colourful and age-appropriate.

**Can ChatGPT write notices for a nursery school?**
Yes, it can draft fee reminders, holiday notices and activity messages in Hindi or English. Give it the real date and details, and check every fact before sending, because AI can invent a date.

**Should a nursery school use Seesaw or ClassDojo?**
They do different jobs: ClassDojo is best for daily moments and positive behaviour points, while Seesaw is best for a term-long portfolio of each child's work. Many schools use ClassDojo daily and Seesaw for portfolios.

**How often should a nursery school message parents?**
Once a day with a short update is enough, plus a notice when there is real news such as a holiday or an event. Daily spam annoys parents, while silence worries them, so one warm message a day is the right balance.
