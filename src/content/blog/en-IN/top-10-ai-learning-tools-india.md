ChatGPT, Gemini and Khan Academy cover needs — doubts, concepts and practice — while others handle notes, maths, flashcards, languages, writing.

1. ChatGPT explains concepts and quizzes you; free: unlimited chats, 3 uploads daily.
2. Gemini answers in Hindi inside Google apps; free with Google account.
3. NotebookLM turns PDFs into audio summaries; free: 100 notebooks, 50 sources.
4. Khan Academy teaches maths to computing free; fully free.
5. Photomath scans maths problems and shows steps; free for most problems.
6. Socratic by Google explains homework from a photo; completely free.
7. Quizlet turns notes into flashcards and tests; free: flashcards, Match, ads.
8. Duolingo teaches 40+ languages in short lessons; free, ads, hearts limit.
9. Perplexity answers with citations; free: unlimited basic searches, 3 Pro daily (guides say 5).
10. Grammarly fixes spelling and grammar; free: unlimited checks, 100 AI prompts monthly.

**Which one should a student try first?** ChatGPT for doubts, Khan Academy for concepts, NotebookLM for revision.

## Final verdict

Start free with ChatGPT, Khan Academy and NotebookLM; pay only when a free limit blocks you.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
