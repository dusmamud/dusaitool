In India, Cal.com gives the most free booking power; Calendly is the simplest start. All ten work here in a browser, without a VPN.

1. Calendly — booking links; free: one event type, one calendar.
2. Cal.com — booking pages; free: unlimited event types, one user.
3. Reclaim.ai — defends focus time; free Lite plans one week ahead.
4. Motion — auto-schedules tasks; no free plan, seven-day trial only.
5. TidyCal — booking pages; free: unlimited booking types, one calendar.
6. Google Calendar — appointment schedules; free: one booking page.
7. Microsoft Bookings — team booking pages; included with Microsoft 365 only.
8. Doodle — group polls and booking; free: one poll, one page, ads.
9. YouCanBookMe — custom booking pages; free: one page, one calendar.
10. Morgen — AI daily planner; sources disagree, but recent ones say trial only.

## FAQ

**Which free tool suits a solo freelancer?** Cal.com, for unlimited event types; Calendly for one meeting type.

## Final verdict

Start free with Cal.com; keep Calendly for one meeting type, and pay only when limits bite.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
