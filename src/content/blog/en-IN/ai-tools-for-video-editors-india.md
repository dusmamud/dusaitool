Video editors in India juggle three jobs: cutting raw footage, turning long videos into short clips, and delivering clean files to clients on deadline. AI tools help with each job, but free plans are narrow and prices change often. The shortlist: Descript for spoken video, OpusClip for short clips, Runway for generated shots.

| Tool | Best for | Free plan | Paid from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Descript | Spoken video and podcasts | 60 minutes a month, 720p, watermarked | $16 a month, billed yearly |
| OpusClip | Short clips from long videos | 60 credits a month, 1080p, watermarked | $15 a month |
| Runway | AI-generated shots | 125 credits once, watermarked | $15 a month |

## Descript: edit video by editing text

Descript transcribes footage and lets you cut video by deleting words in the transcript, making interview and podcast edits faster than scrubbing a timeline. Free gives 60 media minutes a month, 100 AI credits once (not monthly), and watermarked 720p exports — a trial, not client delivery. Hobbyist is $16 a month billed yearly ($24 monthly), with 10 media hours, 400 AI credits a month, and watermark-free 1080p export.

## OpusClip: long video to short clips

OpusClip turns a long recording into scored vertical clips with animated captions for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok. One credit covers one minute of source video, so 60 free credits a month handle about an hour of footage. Free clips are 1080p but watermarked, have no editor, and stop exporting after three days. Starter is $15 a month, removes the watermark, and adds the editor.

## Runway: generate shots you never filmed

Runway creates video from text or still images, and its Aleph tools can restyle or extend a shot — useful for B-roll and quick fixes when reshooting is impossible. Free gives 125 credits once, never refreshed, and free videos carry a watermark. Standard is $15 a month ($12 billed yearly) with 625 credits a month; at 12 credits a second, that buys about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5 video.

## Using these tools from India

Descript, OpusClip, and Runway all work in India on a normal connection, with no VPN needed, and all three charge in US dollars, so currency charges apply. CapCut is different: banned in India since 2020, so do not build a client workflow around it. Prices and free limits change without notice — re-check the official pricing page before paying or promising a client a deadline.

## What to avoid

Do not promise a client 4K delivery on a free plan — every free plan here caps resolution or watermarks exports. Do not spend one-time credits on test renders, and never upload unreleased client footage to a tool whose storage and retention terms you have not read.

## FAQ

**Which tool is best for YouTube talking-head videos?** Descript: transcript editing cuts interview and tutorial footage fastest, and captions plus Studio Sound clean up speech. See our [AI tools for YouTube creators](/blog/ai-tools-for-youtube-creators-india/) guide.

**Can I use the free plans for paid client work?** Not safely. Descript and OpusClip watermark free exports, OpusClip clips expire after three days, and Runway's 125 credits never refresh. Bill the client for at least the cheapest paid plan.

**Do I need all three tools?** No. Most editors need Descript plus OpusClip, or OpusClip plus Runway. Add a second tool only when one job is earning money every week.

## Final verdict

Descript for spoken video, OpusClip for short clips, Runway for generated shots — check prices before paying.
