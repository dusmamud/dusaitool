Auto parts makers in India juggle buyer orders, raw-material stock, and quality checks — and spreadsheets break once orders cross a few dozen a month. The fix is a small MRP or inventory tool linking orders, stock, and production in one place — here are three that work in India, starting free.

| Tool | Best for | Free start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zoho Inventory | Orders and stock | Free, 50 orders/month |
| Katana | Small-batch production planning | Free, up to 30 SKUs |
| Odoo | Full MRP, quality, and traceability | Community free, self-hosted |

## 1. Zoho Inventory — orders and stock, made in India

Zoho Inventory tracks sales orders, purchase orders, and stock, with batch and serial tracking. It plugs into Zoho Books for GST invoicing and Indian couriers. The free plan covers 50 orders a month for one user; paid plans start at about ₹999 a month. Limit: light assembly only, not full MRP.

## 2. Katana — visual production planning for small batches

Katana is a cloud MRP tool built for small makers. Its visual board shows what to make, what is in stock, and what is short, with bills of materials for each part. The free plan covers up to 30 SKUs with unlimited users. Paid pricing is custom-quoted, so check Katana's own pricing page first. Traceability and routings cost extra.

## 3. Odoo — the full system, free if you self-host

Odoo combines MRP, inventory, quality checks, sales, and purchase in one open-source system. Set a bill of materials, raise a manufacturing order, and Odoo reserves stock automatically. The Community edition is free and self-hosted, with Indian GST and e-invoicing support; Enterprise adds quality management and shop-floor tools. Trade-off: it needs hosting and setup, so budget time or a local partner.

## Using these tools in India

Start with one product line, not the whole factory. Test with real orders and stock for two weeks. Use batch or serial numbers from day one — auto buyers ask for traceability during audits. Keep GST invoicing in the same system. If you also sell to retail buyers, see our guide to [AI tools for auto dealers in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-auto-dealers-india/). Check the official pricing page before paying — prices and limits change.

## What to avoid

Do not buy a full ERP before your orders are digital. Do not skip quality checkpoints to save time — one rejected batch costs more than the software. Never promise a delivery date from a spreadsheet; check live stock first.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which tool is best for a small auto parts unit in India?** Zoho Inventory — Indian, GST-ready, free for 50 orders a month. Move to Katana or Odoo when production planning matters more.

**Can I really start for free?** Yes. Zoho's free plan, Katana's free plan, and Odoo's Community edition all cost nothing to start. Read the limits — orders, SKUs, or self-hosting effort — first.

**Do these tools handle GST?** Zoho and Odoo support Indian GST invoicing, including e-invoicing in Odoo. Katana is global, so pair it with your accounting software for GST.

**What if my buyer needs traceability?** Use batch or serial tracking from day one. Odoo's traceability is strongest; Katana charges extra, Zoho's is basic.

## Final verdict

Start free: Zoho for orders, Katana for production planning, Odoo for a full MRP system — pick by your biggest pain point.
