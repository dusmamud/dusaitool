If you run a BPO call centre in India — twenty agents answering support calls for a telecom client, a night shift handling US customers, or a small team doing outbound sales — your costs are people, and your quality is what keeps the client. The best AI tools for BPO call centres help agents answer faster and more accurately, turn long calls into short summaries, and train new hires without pulling your best agents off the floor. The direct answer is simple: start with ChatGPT for reply drafts and knowledge answers, Otter.ai for call transcripts and summaries, Krisp for clearer call audio, and Google Sheets to track quality, with Zoho Desk when tickets outgrow a shared inbox.

Indian BPOs work in a particular reality: agents switch between English, Hindi and Hinglish in a single call, night shifts fight background noise and tired voices, and clients judge you on first-call resolution and customer satisfaction scores. AI helps most in the gaps between calls — drafting the right reply, summarising what was promised, checking whether the agent followed the script, and giving a new hire a practice partner. None of this replaces a trained, patient agent: a customer who is upset about a bill wants a human who listens, not a faster robot.

## 1. ChatGPT: reply drafts and knowledge answers

ChatGPT is the fastest way to help agents write better replies. Give it the customer’s problem and the policy, and it will draft a clear, polite answer in simple English or Hindi, suggest what to say when a customer is angry, and rewrite a long policy paragraph into three sentences an agent can actually say. Team leaders can use it to write call scripts, update the knowledge base, and draft coaching notes for agents. It works best as a draft machine: the agent reads, edits, and then speaks, so the answer stays accurate and human. The free plan is enough for drafting help; check current pricing on the official page before paying for a team plan.

How to start: pick your five most common customer problems, draft approved replies with ChatGPT, and put them in a shared document every agent can use. Watch out for: never paste a customer’s name, account number, address or payment details into ChatGPT, and never let an agent read an AI draft without checking it against the real policy.

## 2. Otter.ai: call transcripts and summaries

Otter.ai transcribes calls and meetings in real time and turns them into a searchable summary with action items. For a BPO, that means the agent does not have to type notes while talking, the team leader can read what was promised without listening to the whole call, and disputes about what was said become easy to settle. The free plan includes a limited number of transcription minutes each month, which is enough to trial it on one team, and paid plans add more minutes and integrations. Use it first on training calls and escalations, where an accurate record matters most. The free plan includes limited monthly transcription minutes; paid plans add more, so check current pricing on the official page.

How to start: trial it on one team’s escalation calls for two weeks, and check whether leaders actually read the summaries before paying. Watch out for: recording calls needs consent and must follow your client’s rules and Indian law, so get written approval before switching it on.

## 3. Krisp: clearer calls from noisy rooms

Krisp removes background noise from calls in real time — the fan, the traffic, the colleague on the next desk, the dog at the agent’s home during work-from-home shifts. It works on the agent’s side and can also clean up the customer’s side, so both people hear each other clearly. For an Indian BPO with open floors, night shifts and work-from-home agents, clearer audio means fewer repeated questions, shorter calls, and better customer satisfaction scores. The free plan covers a limited number of minutes per day, which is enough for one agent to trial it properly. The free plan includes limited daily noise-cancellation minutes; paid plans add more, so check current pricing on the official page.

How to start: trial it with five agents on the noisiest shift for a week, and compare their call handling time and repeat questions. Watch out for: noise removal cannot fix a bad headset or a weak internet connection, so check the basics before blaming the tool.

## 4. Zoho Desk: tickets that never get lost

Zoho Desk turns every customer contact — email, chat, call or social message — into a ticket in one queue, so nothing gets lost in a shared inbox. A team leader can see which tickets are waiting, which agent owns each one, and which customers have waited longest, and can set rules that route a billing ticket to the billing team automatically. Its AI assistant can suggest replies and summarise long ticket threads. For a small BPO the free plan is a real starting point, and the paid plans add automation and reporting as the team grows. The free plan covers basic ticketing for small teams; paid plans add automation, so check current pricing on the official page.

How to start: move one client’s email queue into Zoho Desk for two weeks, and check whether any ticket goes unanswered before paying. Watch out for: a ticket system only works if agents update tickets honestly — a queue full of stale tickets hides problems instead of solving them.

## 5. Google Gemini: training help in Indian languages

Google Gemini is useful for the training side of a BPO. A trainer can ask it to write a call script for a new process, turn a long policy document into a short quiz, or draft practice conversations where a new hire plays the agent and Gemini plays a difficult customer. Because Gemini supports Hindi and other Indian languages, trainers can prepare material in the language new hires actually think in, not just formal English. It also helps team leaders summarise the week’s quality issues into a short coaching note for each agent. The free version through the Gemini app is enough to try; paid Google AI plans add more features, so check the official page.

How to start: ask it to turn one long policy page into a ten-question quiz, and use it in your next training session. Watch out for: Gemini can get a policy detail wrong, so a trainer must check every training answer against the real policy before teaching it.

## Using AI in India: BPO call centres

The pattern that works for Indian BPOs is human-first and language-aware. Agents must handle Hindi, Hinglish and English in the same shift, often with customers who are frustrated before the call begins, so tools that draft replies or summarise calls are only useful if a human checks them. Night shifts need extra care: clear audio, short breaks, and a leader who is actually awake and available. Costs matter too, because most BPO contracts are won on price, so start with free tools, prove the value on one team, and only then roll out a paid tool to the whole floor.

## What to avoid

Avoid any tool that promises to replace agents with a chatbot, because upset customers want a human, and a bad bot drives them to a competitor. Avoid pasting customer personal data into a public chatbot, recording calls without consent, or buying a tool your client has not approved. Avoid judging agents only by AI scores without listening to real calls, and avoid letting AI write replies about refunds, closures or legal matters without a human check.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best AI tool for BPO call centres in India?**
There is no single best tool, because a centre needs reply help, call summaries, clear audio and ticketing. Start with ChatGPT for drafts, Otter.ai for summaries, and Krisp for audio. Add Zoho Desk only when tickets outgrow a shared inbox.

**Can AI replace call centre agents?**
No. AI can draft replies, summarise calls and check quality, but it cannot listen with patience, calm an upset customer, or solve a problem that needs judgment. Centres that replace agents with bots usually lose customers and then lose the client.

**How much do AI tools for BPOs cost?**
The starting set is free: ChatGPT, Otter.ai, Krisp and Google Sheets all have free plans with limits. Zoho Desk has a free plan too, and paid plans add minutes and automation, so check current pricing on each official page.

**Is it legal to record and transcribe calls in India?**
Recording usually needs consent, and your client’s contract and Indian rules set the details. Get written approval from the client and inform agents and customers as required before switching on any recording or transcription tool.

**Can AI help train new agents faster?**
Yes. Gemini can turn policy pages into quizzes and practice conversations, ChatGPT can draft call scripts, and Otter.ai summaries let a new hire learn from real calls. But a new agent still needs a human coach listening to their first live calls.

**How do I check call quality without listening to every call?**
Score a sample of calls each week in a Google Sheet — greeting, listening, accuracy, resolution and politeness — and use Otter.ai summaries to spot problems faster. Coach from the pattern, not from one bad call.
