An MBA in India is a sprint: weekly cases, group presentations, internships, then placements. Start with ChatGPT for drafting, Perplexity for cited research, Gamma for decks, Grammarly for placement writing, and Notion for notes. All five work in India without a VPN, and all have a free tier.

MBA work is judged live: a case discussion rewards the student who has read the numbers. AI helps most in preparation — turning a long case into a brief, checking your logic, drafting a deck, rehearsing answers.

## 1. ChatGPT: your case-study sparring partner

Paste a case summary and ask ChatGPT to list the core problem, options and trade-offs — then argue with it. It handles frameworks like Porter's Five Forces, but verify every claim: the model can invent facts. The free plan offers unlimited everyday text chats, with limits on uploads, voice and deep research. India-only Go costs ₹399 a month (UPI payment); Plus is ₹1,999 a month. Never submit AI output as your own work — most B-schools treat that as misconduct.

## 2. Perplexity: research you can cite in class

A case discussion fails on a number nobody can verify. Perplexity answers with clickable citations — ideal for market size, competitor revenue or a SEBI rule. Quote the source, not the chatbot, in your presentation. Free gives practically unlimited basic searches, plus 3 Pro Searches a day and 1 Research query a month. Older guides quote 5 Pro Searches a day; treat 3 as safe.

## 3. Gamma: decks without the formatting fight

Gamma turns a written outline into a clean deck in minutes, exporting to PDF, PPTX and Google Slides. Use it for the first draft, then rewrite the argument and fix the numbers yourself. Free is one-time: 400 credits at sign-up, no refresh, 10 slides per prompt, Gamma badge on exports. A 10-slide deck costs about 40 credits, so free covers eight to ten decks. Plus is about $9 a month billed annually, adding 1,000 credits a month.

## 4. Grammarly: placement writing that reads professional

Placement season is won in writing: your CV, cover mails and LinkedIn summary. Grammarly catches spelling and grammar mistakes, flags the wrong tone, and offers clearer rewrites. Run your CV through it, then read it aloud yourself. Free includes spelling, grammar and tone checks plus 100 AI prompts a month. In India, Plus is ₹392 a month billed annually (₹1,000 monthly), via local payments, with 2,000 AI prompts a month.

## 5. Notion: one place for notes, cases and deadlines

An MBA means notes, case briefs, club work, applications and deadlines across five apps. Notion puts them in one workspace: a page per subject, a case database, an application tracker. Notion AI is not free: free workspaces get only a limited AI trial; full AI needs Business at $20 a month. The workspace is free for one person: unlimited pages, 5 MB uploads, 7-day history. ## AI tools in India: what actually matters

All five work in India with no VPN and accept Indian payment methods; Go and Plus are priced for India. Pick one paid tool at most, only after the free tier has run out. Every figure here was checked against public sources in November 2026; re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## What to avoid

- **Submitting AI output as your own work.** B-schools check; getting caught costs more.
- **Trusting numbers without a source.** Verify figures against the company report first.
- **Pasting confidential material.** Never put case packs into a free chatbot.
- **Paying for five subscriptions at once.** Start free; pay for one bottleneck only.
- **Letting a deck replace preparation.** A polished deck with a weak argument still fails.
- **Sharing logins or shared paid accounts.** They get locked, and you lose your notes.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool is best for MBA case studies?**

ChatGPT for analysis, Perplexity for cited facts. Verify every number through Perplexity's citations or the original source.

**Can MBA students use AI tools for free in India?**

Yes. All five have free tiers that work in India without a VPN. But Gamma's credits never refresh, and Notion's AI is only a limited trial.

**Is it okay to use AI for placement preparation?**

Yes, for preparation, not impersonation. Practise answers, but write your own CV and know every claim on it.

**Which AI tool makes the best MBA presentations?**

Gamma is fastest for a first draft and exports to PowerPoint. For the final deck, PowerPoint or Google Slides give the most control.

**Do I need a paid AI subscription during my MBA?**

Most do not. Free tiers cover a typical term. Pay only if a limit keeps blocking you.

**Will recruiters know if I used AI on my CV?**

They may not know, but they will notice a generic CV or claims you cannot explain.
