A website in India in 2026 does not need code, but it does need a decision: what the site is for, who will visit it, and whether **a subdomain and builder branding** are acceptable. AI website builders generate a site from a prompt, but free plans differ wildly: some publish free on a subdomain, some only let you preview free, and some have no free plan at all, only a trial. The tool that builds fastest does not always publish free, and the tool that publishes free does not always look best.

The short answer for India is this: build free in Wix for a business or personal site, because its free plan needs no credit card and includes AI creation tools, hosting, and drag-and-drop editing. Build free in Framer for a designer portfolio or startup landing page, because its free plan includes 500 AI credits, a free Framer domain, and 1 GB of bandwidth. Build free in WordPress.com for a blog or long-term platform, because its free plan includes an AI guided website builder, with ads shown to visitors. **A custom domain always needs a paid plan**, on every builder, and every free plan uses the builder's own subdomain and branding.

This article is written for India on purpose: small businesses, freelancers, [creators](/blog/ai-tools-for-youtube-creators-india/), students, and shop owners, Hindi, Hinglish, and English sites, a normal phone and laptop, and no assumption that you have a paid plan, a domain, or coding skill. It also carries a warning that matters for website builders more than for any other tool in this series: a free AI site is fine for a brochure or a landing page, but the moment you need logins, a database, payments, or real app logic, you have hit the **60 to 70 percent wall** these tools cannot finish, and you will need a developer or a different platform.

## Wix: the most complete free path for a non-developer

Wix's free plan is the most complete: no credit card required, with hosting, the drag-and-drop editor, and thousands of templates, and its pricing page says all plans include AI creation tools, with an AI website builder to create your site in no time with AI. Wix prioritizes ease of use with pre-made templates, drag-and-drop elements, and a wide range of business tools, which makes it best for small businesses and personal sites that want to start free and upgrade later. The AI generator asks a few questions, builds a multi-section site, and lets you publish on a Wix subdomain at no cost, which is genuinely usable for free for a straightforward brochure site.

The trade-offs are standard: free sites use a Wix subdomain and show Wix branding, and while Wix gives basic SEO tools, it excludes selling online or accepting payments on free. Paid plans add a custom domain, free for a year, more storage and marketing tools, with a 14-day money-back guarantee, and Wix shows local pricing by region, so check the price in your country, because Wix's paid price varies by region. For an Indian small business, Wix free is the best starting point if you need a brochure site with your services, your contact, and your location, and you can live with a Wix subdomain and branding until you can afford a custom domain.

Use Wix free in a specific way:

1. Answer the AI generator's questions honestly, with your real business, your real services, and your real location.
2. Edit the generated site yourself, replacing AI text with your own words, [your own photos](/blog/free-ai-photo-editors-india/), and your own prices, because an AI-generated site with generic text will not convince a customer to call you.

Framer's free tier hosts simple sites on a framer.website subdomain with a Framer badge, with page and bandwidth caps, which makes it aimed at small, beautiful sites, not sprawling catalogs.

Framer gives you [more design control](/blog/ai-tools-for-graphic-design-beginners-india/) than most AI builders, which also means a steeper learning curve. It is best for designers, portfolios, and startup landing pages, where typography, layout, and polish matter more than business tools. For an Indian designer or [freelancer](/blog/ai-tools-for-freelancers-india/), Framer free is the best portfolio option, because a portfolio *lives or dies* on how it looks, and Framer's polish will make your work look better than a generic Wix template will. For a shop owner who needs business tools, bookings, or payments, Wix free is better, because Framer has fewer built-in business features.

Use Framer free in a specific way:

1. Spend your 500 AI credits on generating the site structure once, then edit by hand, because AI credits are for generation, not for unlimited regeneration, and regenerating ten times will burn your credits before you have a site.
2. Keep your site small, a handful of pages, because 1 GB of bandwidth and page caps will punish a large site with many images.
3. Compress your images before you upload them, because high-quality visuals on a free bandwidth cap will load slowly or hit the cap.

## Using no-code AI in India: domain, language, and the 60 percent wall

Domain is the first paid decision every Indian site owner faces. Every free plan uses a subdomain, like yoursite.wixsite.com or yoursite.framer.website, and a custom domain, like yourbusiness.com, needs a paid plan on every builder. A subdomain is fine for a test, a portfolio, or a brochure, but a customer will trust yourbusiness.com more than yoursite.wixsite.com, and a business that wants customers to call, visit, or pay should plan for a custom domain as its first paid upgrade, before any other feature. Wix paid plans add a custom domain free for a year, WordPress.com Personal adds a free domain for a year, and Framer Basic adds a custom domain, so compare the first-year domain cost, not just the monthly price, when you choose a builder.

Language for Indian websites is [Hindi, Hinglish, English, and regional languages](/blog/ai-translation-tools-hindi-english-india/), and AI builders generate best in English. An AI-generated Hindi site can sound generic, and an AI-generated Hinglish site can mix formal and informal language, which confuses visitors. Write your site's key pages yourself, in the language your customers actually use, and let AI generate structure, not final text: let AI build the sections, the layout, and the draft, then replace every AI sentence with your own words, your own prices, and your own contact details, because a site with generic AI text will not convince a customer to trust you.

The 60 to 70 percent wall is the most important limit in this article, and it is simple: a free AI site is fine for a brochure or a landing page, but the moment you need logins, a database, payments, or real app logic, you have hit the wall these tools cannot finish. A shop that needs online payments, a school that needs student logins, or a business that needs a customer database cannot be built free with Wix, Framer, or Durable alone, and will need a developer, a different platform, or a paid plan with e-commerce and database features. Plan for that wall before you build, not after: if your site will need payments in six months, choose a builder whose paid plan includes payments, even if you start free, because migrating a site to a new builder later costs more time than choosing right at the start.

## What to avoid

- **Do not plan a free live site on Durable**, because Durable free is for building and previewing, while publishing a live site generally needs a paid plan.
- **Do not plan a free site on Squarespace**, because it has no free plan, only a 14-day trial.
- **Do not buy Hostinger hosting just for its AI builder**, unless you were going to buy hosting anyway, because the builder is a plan feature, not a standalone free product.
- **Do not publish an AI-generated site without replacing its text, photos, and prices with your own**, because generic AI text will not convince a customer.
- **Do not build a large, image-heavy site on Framer free**, because 1 GB of bandwidth and page caps will punish it.
- **Do not ignore the 60 percent wall**: if your site will need logins, a database, or payments, plan for a developer or a paid e-commerce plan from the start, not after you have built a brochure site that cannot grow.

A final trap is building a site and never updating it. Beginners often generate a site with AI, publish it, and then forget it, with old prices, old photos, and old contact details, which damages trust more than having no site at all. A website is not a one-time build, it is a **living page**: update your prices, your photos, and your contact every month, and check that your phone number, your address, and your hours are still correct, because a customer who calls a wrong number will not call again.
