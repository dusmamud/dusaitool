A footwear shop lives or dies on sizes: a customer asks for a UK 8 in tan, you have UK 7 and UK 9, and the sale walks out. Good software does three jobs: it bills fast, shows which sizes are left, and remembers the customer. No AI counts your stock. These tools automate the boring parts: barcode scanning updates stock, low-stock alerts warn before a size runs out, and GST reports are ready for your accountant. Prices change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## Vyapar

Vyapar is an Indian billing and inventory app for small shops. Create each shoe with size and colour as a variant, scan its barcode at sale time, and that exact size's stock drops by one. It supports GST invoices, low-stock alerts, and customer ledgers for udhaar, and works offline. The Android app is free for life with basic billing and inventory, per several reviews. Paid desktop editions start near ₹3,399 a year plus GST. It runs on Android and Windows only.

## myBillBook

myBillBook, from Bengaluru-based FloBiz, is a mobile-first GST billing app used by over one crore small businesses in India. It covers GST bills, barcode billing, inventory, e-invoicing, and automatic GSTR reports. The free plan is disputed: the company's site describes a free mobile plan with 15 invoices a month, while other sources say there is no free plan, only a trial. Paid plans start near ₹399 a year.

## Zoho Inventory

Zoho Inventory is the strongest pick when stock, not just billing, is the problem. A footwear shop can hold hundreds of size-colour combinations, and Zoho handles item groups, variants, barcodes, purchase orders, and stock counts across locations. The free plan is genuinely free: 50 orders a month, one user, one location. Paid plans start at ₹999 a month for 500 orders, excluding GST.

## TallyPrime

TallyPrime is the heavyweight option, and most single-counter footwear shops do not need it yet. It combines accounting, invoicing, inventory and GST compliance in one desktop program accountants already know. It is not free: Silver costs ₹22,500 plus GST one-time, or ₹750 a month on rent; Gold costs ₹67,500 plus GST. Yearly TSS costs ₹4,500 (Silver) or ₹13,500 (Gold); without it, GST updates stop. A 7-day free trial is available.

## WhatsApp Business

WhatsApp Business is the cheapest sales tool a footwear shop can own, because it is free and customers already use it. The free app gives you a business profile, a catalogue of up to 500 items with photos and prices, quick replies, labels, and broadcast lists. It cannot track stock or take payment — orders arrive as messages and you follow up with a UPI link. For a small shop, the free app is enough.

## Using these tools in an Indian footwear shop

Enter your real stock first, one design at a time, with every size and colour as a separate line. Without size-level stock, low-stock alerts are meaningless. Use barcode stickers so billing takes seconds. Footwear shops share the same problem as [boutiques](/blog/ai-tools-for-boutiques-india/) and [bakers](/blog/ai-tools-for-bakers-india/) — fast counter billing matters more than fancy features. Keep one system of record: do not also keep stock in a notebook, or the two will disagree within a week. Message customers when their size arrives, but only those who asked for it. The same discipline applies to [chemists and pharmacies](/blog/ai-tools-for-chemists-pharmacies-india/).

## What to avoid

- Do not buy software before entering your stock.
- Do not trust "free forever" claims. Check the vendor's pricing page first.
- Do not keep stock in two places — one record only.
- Do not broadcast every arrival. Unwanted messages get your number blocked.
- Do not let AI invent product details. Check every size and price first.
- Do not skip TallyPrime's yearly TSS renewal. Without GST updates, invoices go out of date.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which is the best free billing app for a small footwear shop in India?**
Vyapar's free Android app is the safest start. myBillBook's free plan is disputed, so confirm it on the official site first.

**How do I track shoe sizes in inventory software?**
Create each design once, then add every size and colour as a separate variant with its own barcode and stock count.

**Can I run a footwear shop on WhatsApp Business alone?**
You can show products and take orders, but you cannot track stock, take payment, or produce GST bills. Pair it with Vyapar or myBillBook.

**Do I need GST billing software for a small footwear shop?**
If GST-registered, you need compliant invoices with tax breakup and HSN codes. Below the threshold, a simple bill is enough.

**Is TallyPrime worth it for a single footwear shop?**
Usually not at first. It is worth it when your accountant works in Tally, your GST workload is heavy, or you run multiple counters.

**How much does it cost to start with software for a footwear shop?**
You can start at zero: Vyapar's free Android app plus the free WhatsApp Business app.

## Final verdict

Start free with Vyapar for billing and WhatsApp Business for customers; move to Zoho or Tally only when stock and GST outgrow them.
