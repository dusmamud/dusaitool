Yes. A carpenter in India can use free AI tools today to find work, draw measurements, send neat quotes, and reply to customers faster. The best starting set is ChatGPT for quotes and messages, SketchUp Free for 3D drawings, and Canva for a simple rate card. None of them replaces skill, measuring, or a site visit.

| Job | Tool | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quotes | ChatGPT | Neat estimates and replies in Hindi or English |
| Drawings | SketchUp Free | 3D model of a wardrobe or kitchen in the browser |
| Rate card | Canva | Rate card and before-after photos for WhatsApp |

## 1. ChatGPT — quotes and customer messages

ChatGPT's free plan costs ₹0 and, on OpenAI's current pages, gives unlimited everyday text chats, though file uploads are limited. Paste your material list and labour rate, and ask for a neat itemised quote in Hindi or English. Always recheck every number yourself — it can miscalculate, and a wrong quote costs real money. Plus costs about ₹1,999 a month in India.

## 2. SketchUp Free — 3D drawings and measurements

SketchUp Free is a free 3D modeller that runs in a browser — no download, and it works in India without a VPN. Draw a wardrobe or kitchen to exact millimetres, show the customer how it will look, and catch size mistakes before cutting wood. The catch: the free plan is for personal, non-commercial use only, so paid client work needs the Go plan (about $129 a year). It needs internet; exports are limited to PNG and STL.

## 3. Canva — rate card and customer photos

Canva's free plan costs ₹0 and gives about 5 GB of storage, free templates, and a small monthly AI allowance (around 20 uses). Use it to make a one-page rate card or before-and-after photos of finished work for WhatsApp and Instagram. It supports Hindi text, which helps when customers prefer Hindi. Background remover and resizing sit on paid plans — Pro is about ₹500 a month in India.

## Using these tools in India

Customers usually decide on WhatsApp, so keep everything phone-friendly: send the quote as a short message or PDF, the drawing as a phone screenshot, and the rate card as one image. Confirm measurements at the site before ordering material, and take a small advance before buying costly plywood or hardware. Bigger jobs? ## What to avoid

Never send an AI-written quote without checking the maths. Remember SketchUp Free is non-commercial, so paid work needs a paid plan. Never promise a delivery date from a tool's estimate, and never share a customer's home photos without permission.

## FAQ

**Can a carpenter use these tools without English?**

Yes. ChatGPT and Canva both handle Hindi, and voice dictation works on your phone. SketchUp's menus are in English, but its drawing tools are mostly visual.

**Are these tools really free?**

The basic plans cost ₹0, but each has limits: ChatGPT limits uploads, SketchUp Free bans commercial use, and Canva limits AI uses and storage. Prices change, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

**Will a 3D drawing win more customers?**

Often, yes — customers understand a picture faster than a list of numbers. But the drawing must match real measurements, or it creates disputes later.

## Final verdict

Start free with ChatGPT, SketchUp Free and Canva — but measure twice and check every number.
