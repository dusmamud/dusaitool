None needs a VPN in India. Prices change often, so re-check the official page before paying.

## 1. PaddleIQ — AI match stats from one phone

PaddleIQ is built specifically for table tennis. Set one iPhone at the corner of the table and record a match. It combines computer vision with the sound of ball contact to follow rallies, then produces stroke statistics, match analysis, coaching priorities and automatic highlight clips. The catch is platform: the beta runs on iPhone only, needs 1080p video at 60 frames per second, and Android is still in development. Beta access is free; a paid Pro plan is in development, price not yet finalised.

## 2. SportArc — AI coach with 3D technique review

SportArc is a video editor and AI coach whose coaching and match-review features focus on table tennis. Import a recording, choose the stroke and the impact frame, and the app recreates the movement in 3D, showing joint angles, centre of mass and the power chain, with feedback on strengths and areas to work on. Match Review adds shot placement and reports; Auto Cut trims recordings into rally clips. Confirm pricing on the official site.

## 3. GotThePoint — free scoreboard and tactical breakdown

GotThePoint turns raw match footage into a tactical breakdown with a live scoreboard, statistics and key-moment replays. Upload a match video, control the score timing yourself, and the app renders the overlay in your browser — no upload wait, no editing skill needed. It reports serve win rate, streaks and placement heatmaps. It is free, and still in beta: the scoreboard, statistics and shot tagging work today, while AI analysis is still in development.

## 4. Kinovea — free slow-motion study for coaches

Kinovea is not an AI app, but it is the most useful free tool for studying technique frame by frame. It is free and open source: it slows video to a few percent of normal speed, draws lines and arrows, measures angles, and compares two videos side by side. A coach can show exactly where a forehand loop breaks down. It runs on Windows only (7 to 11), not on Apple-silicon Macs.

## 5. Playo — find a table, a club and a partner

Tools are useless without somewhere to play. Playo is India's large sports community app, with over five million users and more than 5,000 verified venues across 100-plus cities. Search for table tennis, compare venues by location, ratings, pricing and photos, then book a table and pay online. Venue charges apply and availability varies by city.

## Scores and rankings

For live scores, use the official **World Table Tennis (WTT) app**, which offers real-time scores and draws from WTT events and the latest weekly ITTF world rankings. The free **TT Tracker** extension gathers WTT, ITTF and ETTU tournaments into one live feed, updating scores roughly every 30 seconds. It is unofficial and not affiliated with WTT or ITTF — a convenience layer, not an official source.

## Using these tools in India

Most Indian players will use a mix: record matches on a phone, analyse them with PaddleIQ or SportArc, study slow motion with Kinovea, and book the next session on Playo. Android users should note PaddleIQ is iPhone-only for now, so SportArc, GotThePoint and Kinovea are the realistic first choices. Match videos are large, so upload over Wi-Fi.

## What to avoid

- **Do not assume a tennis AI app covers table tennis.** SwingVision is for tennis, pickleball and padel only.
- **Do not pay for a beta feature.** If AI analysis is "in development", use the free parts and wait.
- **Do not trust one price screenshot.** Re-check the official page before paying.
- **Do not film other players without consent.**
- **Do not replace your coach with an app.** AI spots patterns; a coach watches you live.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool is best for table tennis beginners in India?**
Start with free GotThePoint and Kinovea for slow-motion review — both teach you to watch your own game.

**Is there a free AI table tennis coach app?**
Partly: PaddleIQ is free in its iPhone beta and GotThePoint is free. Fully free, unlimited AI coaching does not exist yet.

**Can I use these tools on Android?**
Mostly. SportArc, GotThePoint and Playo work on Android; Kinovea runs on Windows. PaddleIQ is iPhone-only for now.

**How do I book a table tennis table near me in India?**
On Playo, select table tennis, search your city, compare venues, then book and pay for a slot.

**Where can I check live table tennis scores and ITTF rankings?**
Use the official World Table Tennis app for live scores and weekly ITTF rankings; the free TT Tracker extension gathers WTT, ITTF and ETTU matches into one feed.

**Do I need a VPN to use these tools in India?**
No. All five work in India without a VPN. If any tool asks for one, check its official support page.
