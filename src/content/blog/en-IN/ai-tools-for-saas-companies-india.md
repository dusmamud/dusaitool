For most Indian SaaS teams, use Intercom for support and onboarding, Chargebee for billing, and Mixpanel for analytics. Together they cover trial signup to renewal without building billing or analytics in-house. All three work in India without a VPN, but none is free forever — check current limits before you commit.

## Short answer

| Tool | Best for | Free start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intercom | Support and onboarding | 14-day trial, no free plan |
| Chargebee | Subscription billing | Free tier up to a billing cap |
| Mixpanel | Product analytics | 1M events a month free |

## Intercom — support and onboarding

Intercom is an AI-first support platform built around Fin, its AI agent. Fin reads your help articles and past chats, answers common questions, and hands tricky cases to a human. There is no permanent free plan; a 14-day trial needs no credit card. Essential starts at about $29 per seat a month on annual billing, plus about $0.99 per Fin resolution.

## Chargebee — subscription billing

Chargebee, founded in Chennai, automates recurring billing, invoicing, trials, upgrades and failed-payment recovery. It connects to many gateways but does not process payments itself, so you still need a gateway and a tax solution for global sales. A free tier exists up to a cumulative billing cap, but sources disagree, reporting both $100,000 and $250,000. Paid plans start around $599 a month — confirm the cap on the official pricing page first.

## Mixpanel — product analytics

Mixpanel shows what users do inside your product through funnels, retention curves and flows, revealing where trials stall. Its free plan covers up to 1 million events a month, with 10,000 session replays and five saved reports per seat. Growth pricing is usage-based beyond that, and group analytics is a paid add-on. The free plan blocks access at the cap, so it never sends a surprise bill.

## Using these tools in India

All three accept Indian teams and cards, and none needs a VPN. Chargebee's Indian roots suit local billing habits, while Intercom and Mixpanel bill in dollars, so watch exchange rates and GST. Test UPI checkout before promising a launch date. This is general information only, not professional advice; check official pages before paying.

## What to avoid

Do not buy all three on day one, do not let an AI agent settle refund disputes without human review, and do not track personal data you have no use for. Avoid annual contracts until a trial proves real usage.

## FAQ

**Which tool should a new SaaS startup try first?**

Start with Mixpanel's free plan, because knowing where users drop off guides every other decision. Add Chargebee when paid subscriptions begin, and Intercom once tickets take real time.

**Is Intercom free for small teams?**

No — only a 14-day trial without a credit card. If budget is tight, start with a cheaper helpdesk and move over when ticket volume justifies it.

**Does Chargebee work with Indian payment gateways?**

Yes, but it does not process payments itself. Confirm your gateway is supported and test a real INR payment before launch.

**Can Mixpanel replace Google Analytics for SaaS?**

For product behaviour, yes — it is stronger on funnels and retention. For marketing traffic, keep a web analytics tool alongside it. See also [AI tools for customer support](/blog/ai-tools-for-customer-support-india/).

## Final verdict

Intercom, Chargebee and Mixpanel cover support, billing and analytics — start free or on trial, then pay only as usage grows.
