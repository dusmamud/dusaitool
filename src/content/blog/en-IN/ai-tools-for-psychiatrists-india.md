For Indian psychiatrists, the best mix is Practo Ray for appointments and prescriptions, Heidi for free AI therapy-note drafts, and Freed for fast SOAP notes. No single tool does all three well, so pick one clinic system plus one scribe. The psychiatrist — not the software — stays responsible for every diagnosis and prescription.

## Practo Ray

Practo Ray is a clinic system built for Indian doctors. It handles appointment scheduling, patient records, digital prescriptions, billing, and SMS reminders for confirmations and follow-ups. Its official page lists plans from ₹999 to ₹1,999 a month. Some reviews report extra per-appointment fees, so get the full quote in writing before paying.

## Heidi

Heidi is an AI medical scribe that listens during a consultation and drafts a structured note. Its free plan offers unlimited basic transcription, with about 10 advanced Pro Actions a month. Paid plans are reported at roughly $40 to $150 a month, but sources disagree, so re-check Heidi's official pricing page. Check every draft; Heidi warns its notes may contain errors.

## Freed

Freed is an ambient AI scribe for solo clinicians and small practices. It records the session and produces a structured SOAP note, usually within minutes. It supports more than 90 languages and offers psychiatry templates, though the final note is in English. Pricing starts at $39 a month for 40 notes, with unlimited notes from $79 a month. A 7-day trial is included; there is no free plan.

## Using these tools in India

Start with Practo Ray if missed appointments or lost follow-ups are your main pain. Add Heidi first for notes, since its free plan lets you test draft quality before spending. Freed is the better paid scribe for busy clinics. For wider clinic options, see our guide for [doctors and clinics](/blog/ai-tools-for-doctors-clinics-india/). Get patient consent before recording, store notes securely, and check Indian privacy and medical-record rules.

## What to avoid

Do not let any AI tool diagnose a patient, choose a drug, or set a dose. Do not record without patient consent, and do not paste identifiable patient details into a free general chatbot. Avoid tools that cannot explain where data is stored or deleted. If a note looks wrong, correct it before signing.

## FAQ

**Can AI write therapy notes for psychiatrists in India?**

Yes. Heidi and Freed can draft therapy notes from a recorded session. Read, correct, and sign every draft, because AI notes can miss context or invent details.

**Which tool is best for psychiatry appointments and follow-ups?**

Practo Ray: it combines scheduling, prescriptions, billing, and SMS reminders in one Indian clinic system. A scribe alone does not manage appointments.

**Are these AI tools free for psychiatrists?**

Heidi has a free plan with unlimited basic transcription, though advanced actions are capped. Freed has only a 7-day trial, then costs from $39 a month. Practo Ray is paid; re-check its official page, as prices change.

**Is it safe to record therapy sessions with AI?**

Only with patient consent, secure storage, and privacy terms you have read. Avoid personal phones and free chatbots, and keep the signed note as the official record.

Practo Ray runs the clinic; Heidi drafts notes free. Freed suits solo psychiatrists who want fast SOAP notes — always review before signing.
