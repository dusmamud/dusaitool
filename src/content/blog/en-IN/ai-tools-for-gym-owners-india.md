Most Indian gyms lose money not when members quit, but when renewals slip and UPI payments go unmatched. The right tool reminds members before expiry, collects UPI fees, issues GST invoices, and flags absent members. For most single-branch gyms, Centrl Studio is the safest start; personal-coaching trainers should try ABC Trainerize.

## Centrl Studio

Centrl Studio is organised around one job: getting paid on time. Renewal reminders reach members on WhatsApp before plans expire, members pay through a UPI link, and a GST receipt with the CGST and SGST split arrives in the same chat. The cheapest plan is ₹499 a month for 30 members, with a 14-day trial. Watch the member cap.

## ABC Trainerize

ABC Trainerize is coaching software for personal trainers, not a full gym billing system. It covers workouts, nutrition and habit tracking, messaging, and a client app. The free plan covers one client forever; paid plans start at $10 a month for two clients, and the 30-day trial needs no credit card. Payments and nutrition are paid add-ons, so the real bill can exceed the entry price.

## Gymdesk

Gymdesk is the simplicity pick: memberships, billing, bookings, attendance tracking with at-risk alerts, a member app, and a website. Pricing is $75 a month for up to 50 members, rising to $200 for up to 250. The catch for India is payments: its cheaper Gymdesk Payments option serves the US and Canada, so Indian gyms need their own processor at $25 a month extra.

## Using these tools in India

Check UPI support, GST invoicing, and WhatsApp reminders first — those three decide whether fees arrive. Test with your front-desk staff for a week, not just in a demo, and confirm member data can be exported. Prices change often, so re-check the pricing page first.

## What to avoid

Do not buy features your gym will never use, and never sign a long contract untested. Avoid tools without GST invoicing, and never promise members a feature until you have tested it yourself.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool is best for a small gym in India?**

Centrl Studio, with UPI links, GST invoices, and WhatsApp reminders from ₹499 a month. For personal training, choose Trainerize.

**Can I manage my gym for free?**

Partly. Trainerize is free for one client, and most tools offer 14- to 30-day trials. No verified free plan covers a whole gym's billing.

**Do these tools handle GST invoices?**

Centrl Studio issues GST invoices with the CGST and SGST split. Gymdesk and Trainerize are built mainly for other markets, so confirm GST support with your CA first.

**Will Gymdesk work with UPI payments in India?**

Not through Gymdesk Payments, which serves the US and Canada. Use your own processor for $25 a month extra, after confirming it works in India.

## Final verdict

Centrl Studio is the safest pick for most Indian gyms; Trainerize suits trainers, Gymdesk suits small gyms. Start with a trial, test fee collection with real members, and only then pay.
