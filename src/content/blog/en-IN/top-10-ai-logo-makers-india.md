For Indian startups, Canva and Wixel are the best free picks; Looka gives the most polished paid results. All work in India without a VPN; Looka's download price varies by source.

## Top 10

1. Canva — templates plus AI help; free PNG, SVG needs Pro.
2. Wixel (Wix) — AI logos; free SVG downloads, 120 credits monthly.
3. Adobe Express — text-prompt logos; free PNG, daily generations limited.
4. Hatchful (Shopify) — template logos; completely free, no watermark.
5. Looka — polished AI logos; design free, download from $20.
6. LogoAI — AI concepts from business details; preview free, download paid.
7. Brandmark — unlimited free generation; pay once from $25.
8. Design.com — AI logos; free download, non-exclusive licence.
9. Logo.com — AI logo maker; free plan gives one PNG download.
10. Ideogram — text-to-image logos; 30 free weekly credits, generations public.

## FAQ

**Which AI logo maker is fully free in India?** Hatchful and Wixel let you download a finished logo without paying.

## Final verdict

Start free with Canva or Wixel; pay Looka when your brand needs a polished kit.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
