For CAT aspirants, AI tools work best as a doubt-solver and mock-test analyst, not as a replacement for real mocks. ChatGPT handles quant practice, Claude sharpens VARC and DILR reasoning, and NotebookLM turns mock mistakes into a revision notebook. All three work in India on free plans, and none needs a VPN.

| Need | Best pick | Free limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quant practice | ChatGPT | Unlimited text chats; uploads limited |
| VARC and DILR | Claude | Usage resets every five hours |
| Mock revision | NotebookLM | 100 notebooks, 50 sources each |

## ChatGPT — quant practice

ChatGPT is the strongest all-rounder for Quantitative Ability. Paste a question you got wrong, ask it to explain the concept, then ask for three similar practice questions on the same topic. It can also generate CAT-style sectional sets and check your answers one at a time. Always re-check its solutions for hard quant and DILR questions, because it can produce wrong answers with full confidence.

## Claude — VARC and DILR

Claude is the best pick for Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension. Paste a tough RC passage and ask it to state the main point in one sentence, then explain why each wrong option fails. That builds the inference skill VARC actually tests. It also walks through DILR sets step by step. Note that a Claude account requires you to be at least 18 years old.

## NotebookLM — mock revision

NotebookLM, now called Gemini Notebook, is where your mocks become revision material. Upload mock scorecards, error notes and concept PDFs into one notebook, then ask it to list your repeated mistakes by topic and build a focused study guide. It can even turn your error log into an audio overview for your commute. Its answers stay grounded in your uploaded material.

## Using these tools in India

All three run in India without a VPN and accept UPI payments if you ever upgrade. ChatGPT Go costs about ₹399 a month in India, roughly ten times the free limits, so upgrade only if you keep hitting the free caps. Prices and limits change often, so re-check the official pricing or help page before paying.

## What to avoid

Do not let AI write your whole preparation. Generating questions without attempting full-length mocks is the most common trap. Never copy an AI answer into a mock analysis without verifying it against the official solution, and never trust a tool that claims to predict exact CAT questions.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can AI tools replace CAT coaching?**

No. They explain doubts and analyse mocks well, but they cannot set your strategy or replicate the pressure of a timed, full-length mock. Use them alongside mocks and previous-year papers.

**Which free AI tool is best for CAT quant?**

ChatGPT, because its free plan covers unlimited everyday text chats and it can generate endless practice variations on your weak topics, from arithmetic to geometry.

**Is NotebookLM really free for CAT revision?**

Yes, with a Google account. The free plan gives you 100 notebooks and 50 sources per notebook, enough for a full season of mock error logs.

**Can I use these tools on my phone?**

Yes. All three have mobile apps or mobile-friendly websites and work fine on ordinary 4G data, so revision on the metro or bus is practical.

## Final verdict

ChatGPT for quant, Claude for VARC and DILR, NotebookLM for mock revision — a free, India-ready CAT toolkit.
