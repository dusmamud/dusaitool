A juice bar in India does not need expensive software. The best setup for most small outlets is free: WhatsApp Business for orders, Vyapar for billing and stock, Canva for the menu, and ChatGPT for writing. Petpooja, a proper restaurant POS, is only worth paying for once daily orders outgrow one phone.

## The short answer

| Tool | Best for | Free option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp Business | Orders | Free app |
| Vyapar | Billing, stock | Free mobile tier |
| Canva | Menu design | Free plan, 5 GB |
| ChatGPT | Writing, costs | Free plan |

## 1. WhatsApp Business — orders without commission

WhatsApp Business is the most useful tool for a small juice bar: customers already live on WhatsApp. The free app gives you a business profile, a catalogue of up to 500 items with photos and prices, quick replies and labels. A customer browses your juices, adds items to a cart in the chat, and sends the order as one message. You confirm and take UPI payment. It suits roughly 10–30 orders a day; the paid WhatsApp API adds automation but costs money.

## 2. Vyapar — billing and stock on one phone

Vyapar is an Indian billing and inventory app, and its Android mobile tier is free for life — not a trial. It handles GST-compliant invoices, basic inventory with low-stock alerts, batch and expiry tracking, and customer and supplier ledgers. For a juice bar, that means tracking kilos of fruit and billing with CGST and SGST calculated automatically. It also works offline. The free tier is single-device and basic. Paid desktop editions start from around ₹3,399 a year — check Vyapar's own pricing page before paying. General information only, not tax advice.

## 3. Canva — a menu that looks professional

Canva's free plan is the fastest way to make a menu, a rate card and offer posters without hiring a designer. It includes a drag-and-drop editor, over 1.6 million templates, 4.7 million stock assets, and 5 GB of storage. Search for "menu", pick a free template, and replace the text with your real items and prices. Use at most two fonts and your own photos of your juices. The free plan's AI allowance is limited, around 20 AI uses a month in recent checks, and background removal and Magic Resize are paid. Keep one master menu file so print and WhatsApp prices never disagree.

## 4. ChatGPT — writing, combos and cost math

ChatGPT's free plan helps with daily writing: menu descriptions, combo names, Instagram captions, replies to bad reviews, and a simple sheet of daily costs. OpenAI's Free Tier FAQ says everyday text chat is unlimited, subject to safeguards, while uploads, images and voice have separate caps. Exact limits changed during 2026 — trust the in-app notice. Use it for arithmetic you can check, like cost per glass. Never let it invent a health claim: a juice is not a medicine.

## 5. Petpooja — the full POS for a busy outlet

Petpooja is one of India's most widely used restaurant POS systems, covering billing, kitchen order tickets (KOT), recipe-level inventory, and Swiggy and Zomato integration, with GST billing built in. For 50-plus orders a day, one queue for counter, phone and aggregator orders is genuinely valuable. It is also the most expensive tool here, and its pricing is confusing. Its own pricing page lists POS Core at ₹10,000 for the first year per outlet, plus ₹7,000 a year on renewal, excluding GST — while independent comparisons quote ₹12,000. Get a written quote first; online ordering, QR ordering and kitchen display have been reported as paid extras. No free plan — demo only.

## Using these tools in India

Most customers pay by UPI, so record UPI payments cleanly. Fruit prices swing daily, so low-stock alerts and quick price edits beat a beautiful menu you cannot update. Delivery through Swiggy and Zomato costs roughly 18–30% headline commission per order, with the all-in cost after ads, discounts and GST on commission often reaching 30–40% per 2026 benchmarks — Check each tool's official pricing page before paying — prices and limits change.

## What to avoid

Do not pay for a yearly POS plan before a month on the free tools and counting your real daily orders. Do not let an AI tool write health claims on your menu — no juice cures diabetes, improves eyesight or detoxes a liver. Do not buy followers or fake reviews. Do not keep three different prices in three places. Never hand your WhatsApp number, UPI details or customer list to an "AI automation" agent in a chat — real tools have an official app or website.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which free AI tool should a juice bar start with?**
WhatsApp Business for orders and Vyapar for billing and stock. Both are free and work on one Android phone.

**Can a juice bar take orders on WhatsApp without a website?**
Yes. The WhatsApp Business catalogue is your menu: customers browse items, add them to a cart in the chat, and send the order as a message. Payment happens by UPI.

**Is Petpooja worth it for a small juice bar?**
Usually not at first. Petpooja costs around ₹10,000–₹12,000 for the first year per outlet by published figures that disagree. It becomes worth it when daily orders pass roughly 50.

**How do I price juices for Swiggy and Zomato delivery?**
Work backwards from the commission: if the platform takes roughly a quarter of the order value, most restaurants list aggregator prices 17–22% above counter prices.

**Can ChatGPT write my juice menu for me?**
It can draft descriptions, combo names and social posts. But check every fact and never let it invent health benefits.

## Final verdict

Start free with WhatsApp Business, Vyapar and Canva; pay for Petpooja only when daily orders outgrow a phone-based setup.
