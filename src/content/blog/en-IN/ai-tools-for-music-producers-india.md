Indian music producers can now record, master and release songs without a studio. BandLab gives you a free DAW for recording and mixing, LANDR polishes finished mixes with AI mastering, and DistroKid puts songs on Spotify and JioSaavn for a flat yearly fee. Start free, pay only when a track is ready to release.

Prices and limits change, so re-check each tool's official pricing or help page before paying or promising a client a deadline.

## BandLab

BandLab is a free digital audio workstation that runs in the browser and on phones, so a producer in any Indian city can record vocals, program beats and mix without buying software. It includes instruments, loops and cloud collaboration, and projects are unlimited. The trade-off is limited plug-in support compared with a desktop DAW, and serious mixing still benefits from proper monitors and a treated room.

## LANDR

LANDR's AI mastering analyses your finished stereo mix and applies EQ, compression and loudness processing tuned for streaming. Previews are free and unlimited, so you can hear the result before paying anything. Sources disagree on exact prices — recent reports put a single WAV master at about $10, with subscriptions from around $13 a month — so re-check LANDR's official pricing page before paying.

## DistroKid

DistroKid distributes unlimited songs to Spotify, Apple Music, JioSaavn and more for a flat $24.99 a year on the Musician plan, and you keep 100% of store earnings. The catches matter: billing is in US dollars, so Indian cards need international transactions enabled, and your music can be removed if you stop paying, unless you buy Leave a Legacy at $29 per single.

## Using these tools in India

In India, the practical stack is BandLab for daily work, LANDR previews to judge a master, and DistroKid only once you release several songs a year. Keep a rupee budget for forex charges on dollar billing, and check JioSaavn delivery before promising a client a release date. For related live-performance work, see our guide on [AI tools for band musicians](/blog/ai-tools-for-band-musicians-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not pay for mastering before the mix is finished, do not release AI-generated songs without checking the tool's commercial-use terms, and never promise a client a release date until the distributor confirms delivery.

## FAQ

**Can I produce a full song for free in India?**

Yes — BandLab covers recording, mixing and basic mastering at no cost, and LANDR previews cost nothing. You pay only for a downloadable master or distribution.

**Is DistroKid worth it for one song a year?**

Probably not. At $24.99 a year plus possible add-ons, a single release is cheaper through a pay-per-release distributor. DistroKid wins when you release often.

**Will AI mastering replace a human engineer?**

No. It is fast and consistent for demos and streaming releases, but a human engineer still hears problems AI misses on complex mixes.

## Final verdict

Start free with BandLab, master with LANDR previews, and release through DistroKid only when you ship often.
