## The short answer

Start with three free tools: Gemini for study, ChatGPT for concepts and interview practice, and GitHub Copilot Student for coding help in your editor. Add Gemini Notebook for revising from your own lecture PDFs, and Perplexity for answers with sources. All five work in India without a VPN, and none needs a credit card on its free plan. Re-check official pricing before paying.

## Quick comparison

| Tool | Best for | Free plan |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Concepts, interviews | ₹0; unlimited text chats |
| Gemini | Study, summaries, voice | ₹0; free AI Plus year for eligible students |
| GitHub Copilot | Code in VS Code | ₹0 for verified students; 2,000 completions a month otherwise |
| Gemini Notebook | Revising own notes | ₹0; 100 notebooks, 50 sources each |
| Perplexity | Research with sources | ₹0; unlimited basic searches, 3 Pro Searches a day |

## 1. ChatGPT — the all-rounder

ChatGPT is the most useful general study partner for most engineering students. Ask it to explain a tough topic from thermodynamics, signals, or data structures in simple steps, then ask follow-ups until the idea clicks. The free plan gives unlimited everyday text chats, but uploads, images, voice, and research are limited. Go was listed at ₹399 a month in India in October 2026 and Plus at ₹1,999 — check the live price before buying.

## 2. Gemini — the best free deal for Indian students

Gemini is Google's assistant, and for Indian students it has the strongest free offer. Eligible college students aged 18 and over can claim one year of Google AI Plus free, redeemable by 31 December 2026, which roughly doubles free usage limits and adds 400 GB of storage. After the year it renews at ₹399 a month unless you cancel, so set a reminder before renewal. It handles summaries, explanations, and voice chats.

## 3. GitHub Copilot — coding help inside your editor

For coding, nothing beats a tool inside your editor. GitHub Copilot suggests lines and functions as you type in VS Code or JetBrains. Verified students get Copilot Student free through GitHub Education, with unlimited code completions and a monthly allowance of AI credits for chat and agent features. The catch: Copilot can confidently write wrong code. Always run, read, and test its code yourself.

## 4. Gemini Notebook — revision from your own material

Gemini Notebook, earlier called NotebookLM, is the best free tool for revising from material you already trust. Upload lecture slides, lab manuals, and previous-year papers, and it answers only from those sources, then builds summaries, quizzes, flashcards, and mind maps. The free plan allows up to 100 notebooks with 50 sources each.

## 5. Perplexity — research with sources you can check

Perplexity is an answer engine rather than a chatbot. Ask about a new technology, a research paper, or a company you are interviewing with, and it returns an answer with clickable sources. The free plan offers practically unlimited basic searches, 3 Pro Searches a day, and 1 Research query a month. Its real value is verification: when another chatbot gives you a claim about a standard or a library version, check the cited source here before putting it in a report.

## Using these tools well in India

Claim the student offers before spending anything. Apply for the Google AI Plus student year and GitHub Education in your first semester, because verification can take days, and keep your college email active, since most student offers depend on it. Verify anything affecting marks against your textbook. For exam season, see [AI tools for exam prep](/blog/ai-tools-for-exam-prep-india/), and for placements, see [AI tools for interview prep](/blog/ai-tools-for-interview-prep-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not upload personal data, passwords, API keys, or college login details to any AI tool. Do not buy a paid plan because a YouTuber told you to; buy it only after the free limit has actually blocked your work twice. Do not submit AI-written assignments or lab records without checking and rewriting them in your own words — vivas and plagiarism checks catch this quickly. Never trust one AI answer for safety-critical work; check the standard.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool is best for engineering students in India?**

No single tool wins. Use Gemini for study, ChatGPT for explanations and interviews, Copilot Student for coding, Notebook for your own PDFs, and Perplexity for cited sources.

**Is GitHub Copilot really free for students?**

Yes, for verified students. Copilot Student is free through GitHub Education, with unlimited completions and a monthly AI credit allowance.

**Can I use these AI tools without a VPN in India?**

Yes. ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Gemini Notebook, and Perplexity all work in India on a normal connection.

**Will using AI tools get me in trouble with my college?**

It depends on use. Understanding a concept, debugging code, or practising questions is normally fine. Submitting AI work as your own can count as misconduct.

**Should I pay for ChatGPT Plus or Gemini Pro as a student?**

Usually not in your first year. Free plans cover most study and coding work. Pay only when you repeatedly hit a limit.

## Final verdict

Start free with Gemini, ChatGPT and Copilot Student; pay only when a limit actually blocks your work. That covers notes, coding, projects, and placements without spending a rupee yet.
