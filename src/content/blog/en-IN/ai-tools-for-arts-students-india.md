Arts students in India do not need a dozen paid apps. Understanding concepts, turning notes into clear essays, finding citable sources and revising for exams are all covered by a small free stack: ChatGPT for study help, Gemini for guided learning, Perplexity for sourced answers, Grammarly for clean English, and Elicit with Zotero for papers. All work in India without a VPN. Prices and limits were checked in November 2026; re-check official pricing before paying.

## Short answer

| Tool | Best for | Free limit |

## 1. ChatGPT — the everyday study partner

ChatGPT is the most versatile tool here. Ask it to explain a theory, compare two thinkers, outline lecture notes, or quiz you on a chapter. Study Mode asks questions back instead of handing over answers, which suits exams. Free text chats are unlimited, says OpenAI; uploads, voice and research are limited. Go costs ₹399 a month, Plus ₹1,999 (October 2026). Start free; upgrade only if limits block you.

## 2. Gemini — best student deal in India right now

Gemini, Google's assistant, has two advantages for Indian students. Guided Learning tutors step by step instead of dumping answers. Eligible Indian college students aged 18+ can get Google AI Plus free for 12 months, with double the limits and 400 GB storage. Redeem by 31 December 2026 via SheerID verification; a payment method is required. It auto-renews at ₹399 a month after the free year, so set a month-eleven reminder.

## 3. Perplexity — answers you can actually cite

Chatbots sometimes invent facts, which hurts essays. Perplexity searches the live web and shows sources under every answer. The free plan gives practically unlimited basic searches, 3 Pro searches a day and 1 Research query a month. Older guides quote 5 a day; 3 is what Perplexity's help centre showed in late 2026. Never cite Perplexity — cite its source, after reading it.

## 4. Grammarly — clean English without the guesswork

Grammarly catches errors that cost marks: subject-verb agreement, article mistakes, awkward phrasing. Free covers spelling and grammar corrections, tone detection, and 100 AI prompts a month. Rewrites, plagiarism and AI-text checks need Pro, listed at $12 a month on annual billing (2026). It works in browsers and Google Docs, but will not fix a weak argument.

## 5. Elicit and Zotero — research papers and citations

Dissertations need real academic sources, not chatbot summaries. Elicit's free Basic plan searches 125 million+ papers, with AI summaries and chat; PDF extraction is capped. Plus was listed at $11 a month (academic annual billing); most undergraduates will not need it. Pair it with Zotero, a free open-source reference manager that saves sources in one click and inserts APA or MLA citations into Word and Google Docs. Free cloud storage is 300 MB, so keep big PDF collections local.

## Using these tools in India

All five run on ordinary Indian broadband and mobile data, no VPN, with usable free tiers. Hindi and Hinglish prompts work too — but write the final essay in your university's required language. Check if your college library already pays for a tool first. Keep notes in one place all semester.

## What to avoid

Do not submit AI-written text as your own work. Most Indian universities treat undisclosed AI work as misconduct, so use AI to learn and polish, and write the argument yourself. Do not paste personal data into a free tool without reading its privacy terms. Do not buy a yearly plan in your first week; use the free tier for a month first. Never trust one AI answer on dates or quotations without checking its source.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool is best for arts students in India?**

No single best tool: ChatGPT is the best all-rounder, Gemini the best value with its free one-year student offer, Perplexity the best for research, with sources on every answer.

**Are these AI tools really free for Indian students?**

Yes, each has a permanent free plan in India. Limits are real: ChatGPT caps uploads, Perplexity caps Pro searches at 3 a day, Grammarly caps AI prompts at 100 a month.

**Can I use AI tools for my exams?**

For preparation, yes — summaries, quizzes and practice questions are legitimate. Never cheat in an exam or submit AI-written work as your own; check your university's AI policy.

**Can AI write my essay for me?**

It can draft, but do not submit it. AI essays are often generic, sometimes invent quotations, and can count as misconduct. Write your own argument; use AI for structure and grammar.

**Which tool is best for finding research papers?**

Elicit, for searching and summarising papers, with Zotero for citations. Perplexity suits quick factual questions with web sources.

**Do these tools work in Hindi?**

Yes — all three accept Hindi prompts and explain concepts in Hindi or Hinglish. Write the essay in your university's required language, then polish with Grammarly.

Start free with ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, add Grammarly and Elicit for writing and research — pay only when a limit actually blocks you.
