Ten apps bring guided meditation to India, mostly free.

1. Insight Timer: huge free library.
2. Medito: completely free, no ads.
3. Balance: AI sessions, first year free.
4. Headspace: beginner courses, limited free basics.
5. Calm: sleep stories, limited free content.
6. Wysa: free AI chatbot and meditation.
7. Amaha: free Indian self-care tools.
8. Aura: AI short sessions, limited free.
9. Heartfulness: free Hindi meditation.
10. Let's Meditate: free guided library, no ads.

General information only, not professional advice; prices and limits change, so re-check official pages before paying.
