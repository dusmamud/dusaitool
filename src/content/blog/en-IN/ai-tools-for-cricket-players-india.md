## 1. CricHeroes — live scoring, stats, AI highlights

CricHeroes is the best starting point. Its app scores matches ball by ball for free, with wagon wheels, run-rate graphs and MVP lists. It live-streams from a phone and creates automatic AI highlights. Players get profiles with batting and bowling stats and city, state and national leaderboards. 23+ BCCI state associations use it, per its Play Store listing. Advanced CricInsights analytics are paid — check in the app first.

## 2. StanceBeam Striker — bat sensor for training

StanceBeam is a Bengaluru company whose Striker sensor fits on any bat handle and turns it into a smart bat. It measures 11 swing metrics live: bat speed, speed at impact, angles, power and shot efficiency. The app auto-records a four-second video of each swing, overlays the metrics and builds summaries for your coach. IISc Bangalore validated it, the company says. The sensor was listed near ₹3,999 (was ₹8,999); prices change, and the app gives no data without it.

## 3. PitchVision — video and ball tracking for academies

PitchVision is built for academies, not solo players. Its PV/ONE system turns nets into smart nets, combining video analysis with ball data — line, length, pace and deviation — each ball saved as a cloud clip. Indian academies use it: a Hyderabad academy applies it to AI bowling biomechanics. It lists an India contact on its site. Expect academy pricing; ask what is included. Its free app can draw on phone video for simple feedback.

## Using these tools in India

All three work in India without a VPN. CricHeroes and StanceBeam are Indian products; the sensor sells on amazon.in and the company's site — check delivery, warranty and GST first. PitchVision depends on your academy having installed it, so ask the coach. Keep children's match videos private and share data only with a trusted coach or parent. Also see [AI tools for athletes in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-athletes-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not buy a sensor before two or three months of regular nets — you will not know what the numbers mean. Do not trust one metric, never pay for coaching that guarantees selection, and avoid posting children's match videos publicly. Re-check prices before paying.

## FAQ

**Which tool should a beginner start with?**

CricHeroes. Scoring, profiles and highlights are free and suit school, club and gully matches. Add paid tools only after a coach names a weakness to measure.

**Can StanceBeam replace a coach?**

No. It shows what your bat did — speed, angles, efficiency — but only a coach turns numbers into a corrected grip, stance or drill. Use it as evidence for coaching.

**Is CricHeroes really free?**

Basic scoring, live scores and streaming are free, per its Play Store listing. Advanced analytics like CricInsights are paid — confirm in the app.

## Final verdict

Start with free CricHeroes; add StanceBeam or academy PitchVision analysis only for a specific weakness.
