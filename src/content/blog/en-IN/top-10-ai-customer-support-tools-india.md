Indian small teams should start free, then pay only for AI resolutions they use. These ten tools work in India without a VPN; some free limits differ across sources.

1. Zendesk: AI ticketing for large teams; 14-day trial only.
2. Intercom: Fin AI agent answers chats; 14-day trial only.
3. Freshdesk: Freddy AI ticketing; free for two agents, six months.
4. Zoho Desk: Zia AI helpdesk; free for three users.
5. HubSpot: CRM-linked tickets and chat; free for two users.
6. Tidio: Lyro AI chat for stores; free fifty conversations monthly.
7. Crisp: Shared inbox and live chat; free for two seats.
8. Help Scout: Shared inbox, AI answers; free five users.
9. LiveChat: AI chat and Copilot replies; 14-day trial only.
10. Chatwoot: Open-source live chat; free two agents, 500 conversations monthly.

**Which free tool is best for a small Indian team?** Zoho Desk, free for three users, or Help Scout, free for five users.

## Final verdict

Start with Zoho Desk or Help Scout free, add Tidio for chat, and pay for AI only when volume grows.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
