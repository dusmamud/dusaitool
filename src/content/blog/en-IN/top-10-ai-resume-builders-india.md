Rezi and Teal give the best free value: PDF downloads and ATS checks.

## Top 10 AI resume builders

1. Rezi — ATS-scores your resume; free: one resume, three PDF downloads.
2. Kickresume — polished templates; free: four templates, unlimited downloads.
3. Novoresume — clean one-page ATS-safe resume; free: one page, branding, two credits.
4. Teal — builder plus job tracker; free: unlimited resumes and PDFs, limited AI.
5. FlowCV — simple ATS-friendly builder; free: one resume, unlimited PDFs, no watermark.
6. Canva — hundreds of free templates; free PDF download, AI help limited.
7. Resume.io — fast guided builder; free plan exports TXT only, PDF needs payment.
8. Zety — step-by-step wizard; free plan exports TXT only, trial auto-renews.
9. Enhancv — standout creative layouts; free access lasts seven days, with branding.
10. ChatGPT — drafts and rewrites bullets; free plan covers everyday text chats.

## FAQ

**Which free builder actually gives a PDF?** Rezi, Teal, FlowCV, Kickresume and Canva.

## Final verdict

Start with Rezi or Teal for a free PDF, use Canva for design, and check prices before paying.

Prices and free limits vary by source — check the official pricing page before paying.
