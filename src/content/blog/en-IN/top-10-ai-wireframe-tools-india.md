These ten AI tools turn prompts and sketches into wireframes in India.

1. Google Stitch: prompt-to-UI, 350 free generations.
2. Visily: sketch-to-wireframe, 300 free credits.
3. Uizard: prompt-to-wireframe, 3 free generations monthly.
4. UX Pilot: prompt-to-wireframe, free daily credits.
5. Banani: prompt-to-UI screens, 20 free generations monthly; sources vary.
6. Relume: sitemap-to-wireframe, 1 free project.
7. Figma AI: first-draft wireframes, 500 free credits.
8. Framer AI: prompt-to-website, 500 free trial credits.
9. MockFlow: AI wireframes, 100 free credits monthly.
10. Miro AI: prompt-to-wireframe, 3 free boards.
