For a small plastic factory in India, three tools cover most daily work: Vyapar for GST billing and buyer ledgers, Zoho Inventory for resin and finished-goods stock, and Odoo Community for mould-wise production planning. Start with the free plans, keep one tool as the single source of truth for stock, and re-check official pricing before paying.

| Tool | Best for | Free limit |

| Vyapar | GST billing, buyer ledgers | Free mobile app |

| Odoo Community | Moulds, production, BOM | ₹0 licence; self-hosted |

## 1. Vyapar — billing and buyer ledgers

Vyapar is built for Indian small businesses and runs on Android and Windows, online or offline. It creates GST-compliant invoices, tracks stock with low-stock alerts, and keeps buyer and supplier ledgers with payment reminders. The basic Android mobile app is free for life; desktop plans start around ₹3,399 a year, per third-party trackers. Confirm exact prices on Vyapar's official page before paying.

## 2. Zoho Inventory — stock and orders

Zoho Inventory is a cloud tool for orders, warehouses, and purchase orders, with barcode support and Zoho Books and marketplace integrations. Its free plan covers 50 orders a month for one user — enough for a small unit with a few dozen dispatches. Paid plans start around ₹999 a month billed annually in India. It needs internet.

## 3. Odoo Community — moulds and production

Odoo Community is free, open-source ERP software you host yourself. Its Manufacturing module handles bills of materials, work orders, and work centres, so you can track resin per mould, machine time, and finished output. There is no licence fee, but hosting, setup, and maintenance cost money or staff time; advanced quality features sit in the paid Enterprise edition. Use it only when the two tools above stop coping.

## Using these tools in India

Most Indian plastic units are MSMEs in clusters like Daman, Silvassa, and Baddi. Start with Vyapar on the shop-floor phone, add Zoho Inventory when dispatch tracking breaks, and move to Odoo only when orders justify an ERP. Keep GST invoices, HSN codes, and e-way bills in one system. Selling finished goods online? See our [AI tools for Amazon sellers](/blog/ai-tools-for-amazon-sellers-india/) guide. All three work in India without a VPN.

## What to avoid

Do not buy a full ERP before order volume needs it, and never keep stock in two systems at once — that is how resin "disappears". Never promise a delivery date from a spreadsheet updated last week. Avoid tools needing a VPN in India or charging an export fee for your data.

## FAQ

**Can a small plastic unit run on free plans alone?** Yes, for a while. Vyapar's free mobile app plus Zoho Inventory's 50 free orders a month cover billing and stock for many small units. Upgrade when orders, users, or warehouses outgrow the free limits.

**Which tool tracks moulds and resin best?** Odoo Community: its Manufacturing module links each mould to a bill of materials and tracks resin use per production order. The other two track stock, not mould-wise production.

**Do these tools handle GST and e-way bills?** Vyapar and Zoho Inventory both support GST invoicing, and Vyapar can generate e-way bills. Odoo needs Indian localisation set up. Confirm current GST features on the official help page before relying on them.

## Final verdict

Start free with Vyapar for billing and Zoho Inventory for stock; add Odoo only when production planning gets complex.
