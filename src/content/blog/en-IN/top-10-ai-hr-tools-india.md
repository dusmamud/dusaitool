Indian teams need one ATS, one screening tool and one sourcing tool — these ten cover that stack, several free.

1. Zoho Recruit: Chennai-built ATS, Zia AI matches candidates; free, one job.
2. Freshteam: Freshworks ATS, GPT resume parsing; free up to 50 employees.
3. Breezy HR: visual ATS, 50+ job boards; free Bootstrap, one role.
4. Manatal: AI scores and recommends candidates; 14-day trial from $15/user/month.
5. Workable: ATS with AI screening summaries; 15-day trial from $299/month.
6. TestGorilla: skills tests, AI-scored video answers; free tier, 10 credits/month.
7. Juicebox: plain-English search across millions of profiles; free sourcing plan.
8. Keka: Indian HRMS, AI hiring, video interviews; free trial (length varies), quoted pricing.
9. HireVue: AI video interviews for high-volume hiring; demo only, custom pricing.
10. Greenhouse: structured hiring ATS for large teams; quote only, no free plan.

**Which free tool should a small Indian startup try first?** Zoho Recruit or Breezy HR — both free for one role; test real hiring before paying.

## Final verdict

Start free with Zoho Recruit or Breezy HR, add TestGorilla for skills, pay only as hiring grows.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
