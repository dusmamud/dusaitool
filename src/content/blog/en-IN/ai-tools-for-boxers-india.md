Prices change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying. Nothing here is professional coaching or medical advice: consult a qualified coach or clinician for injuries, head impacts, or returning to training after pain.

## PunchLab: classes with punch tracking

PunchLab is the most practical boxing app for Android users in India. It offers follow-along workouts led by real coaches — shadowboxing, bag work, HIIT and conditioning — and tracks punches with your phone's motion sensors. Strap the phone to the bag and it measures strike speed, volume and power. It has over 500,000 Google Play installs, with a 4.7 rating from few reviews. October 2026 listings show about $17.99 a month or $79.99 a year; check the Indian listing before paying.

## Heavy Bag Pro: the best value combo caller

Heavy Bag Pro, listed as Boxing Training: Bag Workouts on the App Store, is built for solo bag or shadowboxing training. It calls out combinations round by round, runs a timer for up to 16 rounds, and lists over 1,000 combinations across boxing, kickboxing and Muay Thai. Its Android version has over 1,000,000 installs and a 4.9 rating. The free version gives three workouts plus an ad-free interval timer. Premium is about $9.99 a month, $59.99 a year, or $129.99 once for lifetime access.

## FightCamp: the most complete, and the most expensive

FightCamp is the most proven punch-tracking system here and by far the most expensive. Wrist sensors count and score every punch, and the app runs trainer-led boxing, kickboxing, strength and core workouts. It holds 4.9 from over 23,000 US App Store reviews. The catch for India is cost: hardware is listed at $299 to $899, plus about $39.99 a month, in US dollars before shipping, customs and tax. Most gym boxers should spend that money on coaching and gloves instead.

## TrainHeroic: when a coach runs your programme

TrainHeroic is not a boxing app; it is a coaching platform that strength and conditioning coaches use to deliver programmes. A boxing or S&C coach builds your weekly plan in it, and you follow it in the free athlete app, logging sessions and tracking personal records. Coaches pay from about $9.99 a month for one athlete, with a 14-day trial and no free plan. TrueCoach is the closest alternative, from about $29.98 a month for five clients, also with a 14-day trial and no free tier.

## Hevy: the free strength log

Boxers get stronger in the gym, and Hevy is the best free place to record that work. It logs sets, reps, weight and rest timers, tracks personal records automatically, and charts volume over time on phone and watch. The free tier allows unlimited logging, capped at four routines, seven custom exercises and about three months of graph history. Hevy Pro removes those caps and adds a guided Trainer feature for about $23.99 a year, per October 2026 listings. ## Using these tools in India

All five tools work in India without a VPN, and all charge in US dollars, so your bank may add a foreign-transaction fee. PunchLab and Heavy Bag Pro are the natural pair for a boxer in a local gym: one counts your punches, the other calls your combinations, and neither needs more than a phone, a bag and a strap. Keep your sparring log simple: after each session, write the date, partner, rounds, one thing that worked, one thing that broke down, and any head impact or pain.

## What to avoid

Do not trust an app that claims to score technique from your phone camera without showing evidence. Camera-based AI boxing coaches are new, launched mostly in 2026, and several are iPhone-only. Do not buy sensor hardware from abroad without confirming shipping, warranty and import duty for India. Do not copy a random online plan into a fight camp; a plan built for someone else's weight and injury history can overtrain you. Do not train through concussion symptoms, dizziness or a headache after sparring: stop, rest, and see a doctor. Do not pay for a yearly subscription on day one; try the free version for two full weeks first.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which boxing app is best for beginners in India?**
Heavy Bag Pro: free to try, on Android and iPhone, and its lifetime purchase is the cheapest long-term option. Add Hevy for strength logging.

**Can AI apps replace a boxing coach?**
No. An app can count punches and log lifts, but it cannot see your guard drop or correct footwork live. Use apps between coached sessions.

**How should I log sparring sessions?**
Date, partner, rounds, one thing that worked, one that broke down, and any pain or head impact. A notes app works; the pattern over weeks matters.

**Is FightCamp worth the price in India?**
For most Indian boxers, no. Hardware is $299 to $899 plus about $39.99 a month before shipping and tax.

## Final verdict

Start free with Heavy Bag Pro and Hevy; add PunchLab for punch tracking and TrainHeroic only if a coach runs your programme.
