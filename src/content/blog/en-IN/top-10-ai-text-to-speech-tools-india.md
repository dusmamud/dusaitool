## Top 10

1. Play.ht — podcast voices and cloning; free: 12,500 characters monthly.
2. NaturalReader — reads PDFs aloud; free: basic voices unlimited.
3. Voicemaker — 1,500+ voices; free: 25,000 credits monthly.
4. Notevibes — expressive voices, emotion tags; free: 90+ voices, no sign-up.
5. IBM Watson — developer API, neural voices; free: 10,000 characters monthly.
6. Descript — edit audio by editing text; free: 1 media hour monthly.
7. Clipchamp — Microsoft editor, built-in narration; free: 1080p exports, no watermark.
8. CapCut — video editor with voiceovers; free: text-to-speech in browser.
9. Edge Read Aloud — reads web pages aloud; free: unlimited, no download.
10. VEED — browser editor, AI voices; free: 10-minute exports, watermarked.
