A good listing in India today is not just a photo and a phone number. Buyers scroll through dozens of flats on their phones, and the listings that get calls are the ones with a clear description, honest photos, a floor plan and a price that looks believable. AI tools can now do much of that preparation for free, but only within strict limits, and none of them replaces checking the property papers, visiting the flat, or talking to a local broker.

The short answer is this: for an agent or an owner selling in India, the strongest free combination is [BoxBrownie AI Copywriting](https://www.boxbrownie.com/ai-copywriting) for the description, [CubiCasa](https://www.cubi.casa/free-services/) for a free 2D floor plan, [Matterport](https://matterport.com/plans/free)'s free plan for a 3D tour, and [Magicbricks](https://www.magicbricks.com/) PropWorth plus [Housing.com](https://www.housing.com/) price trends for a sanity check on the asking price. Virtual staging from [REimagineHome](https://www.reimaginehome.ai/) or [Spacely AI](https://www.spacely.ai/pricing) can make an empty flat look lived-in, but staged photos must be labelled as staged, or buyers feel misled when they visit.

## BoxBrownie AI Copywriting: 10 free listing descriptions a month

BoxBrownie is a real-estate marketing service, and its AI Copywriting tool writes listing descriptions from the details you feed it. On the free account you get 10 AI copywriting jobs a month, and you can choose a 150, 200 or 250-word description. That is enough for two or three new listings a week if you use one job per listing and edit the result, rather than regenerating until it sounds perfect.

The tool works best when you give it real facts: carpet area, facing, floor, age of the building, distance to the station or school, and the one genuine selling point, such as a large balcony or a quiet lane. It does not know your flat, so if you feed it vague input, it will invent attractive-sounding claims. Never publish a description without reading every line and deleting anything you cannot prove, especially claims about approvals, water supply, or "5 minutes from" distances. In Indian listings, an exaggerated claim is not just embarrassing, it can kill a deal when the buyer visits.

BoxBrownie also offers paid human copywriting and image services, but the free AI jobs are the useful starting point for a small agent. Use it for the first draft, then rewrite the opening two lines in your own words, because buyers in India respond to specific, local details, not generic luxury language.

## CubiCasa: a free 2D floor plan from a phone scan

A floor plan answers the question every buyer asks after the photos: how big are the rooms, really, and how do they connect? CubiCasa's free floor plan app scans a home with a phone in about 5 to 10 minutes and produces a LITE 2D floor plan with room dimensions, delivered as a PNG or JPG. That free tier is genuinely useful, because a drawn floor plan used to cost money or require measuring every wall by hand.

For Indian flats, a floor plan is even more valuable than in many other markets, because carpet area, built-up area and super built-up area are routinely confused in listings. A CubiCasa plan shows the internal layout, but it does not prove the legal carpet area. State the area figure from your sale deed or RERA listing, not from the scan, and mention whether the figure is carpet or built-up. A scan also struggles with very dark rooms, cluttered storerooms and odd-shaped balconies, so walk the flat once with the lights on and clear the main rooms before scanning.

Paid add-ons exist for 3D plans, total area calculations and branded styling, but the free 2D plan is enough to attach to a listing on a portal or to send on WhatsApp before a site visit.

## Matterport: a free 3D tour with 1 active model

Matterport lets buyers walk through a flat on their phone before visiting, which saves useless site visits for both sides. Its free plan allows 1 active model, captured with a phone or a 360 camera, and shared as an interactive 3D tour. That single model is the catch: if you have five listings, you cannot keep five free tours live at once. Use the free model for your most important listing, or rotate it as listings close.

A phone-captured tour is good enough for a standard 2BHK or 3BHK, but it will not match a professional shoot for a luxury villa. Shoot in daylight, open all doors, and hide personal items, because a 3D tour shows everything, including the mess in the second bedroom. Also remember that a tour is heavy on mobile data, so always pair it with normal photos for buyers on slow connections. Matterport's paid Starter plan, listed at $9.99 a month for 5 models in an October 2026 directory, is the next step if you list regularly, but check the current price on the official plans page before paying.

## REimagineHome: first 5 virtual staging designs free

Empty flats photograph badly. REimagineHome turns a photo of an empty room into a furnished-looking room using AI, and the first 5 designs are free with no credit card required. That is enough to stage the hall and the master bedroom of one flat, which are usually the two photos buyers look at first.

The honest rule for staging in India is disclosure. A staged photo must be marked as virtually staged in the listing, because a buyer who travels across the city expecting furniture will be angry, and an agent who hides staging loses trust fast. Staging also cannot fix a real problem: a dark room, a broken window or a damp patch will still be visible on the visit, and in photos it often looks worse once furniture draws attention to the walls. Use staging to show possibility, not to hide condition, and keep at least one unstaged photo of every room.

REimagineHome also lets users design with real products, but for an Indian listing the staging style matters: a warm, simple Indian living room reads better than a stark foreign-looking interior. Pick furniture and colours that match what a buyer in that city would actually put in the flat.

## Spacely AI: credit-based virtual staging

Spacely AI is another virtual staging tool, aimed more at designers and agencies. It works on credits, and its pricing page lists top-ups from $9 for 200 credits, with larger studio and agency plans above that. An October 2026 directory of free real-estate tools lists a free plan with starting credits, but the current pricing page does not prominently state the free credit balance, so treat the free amount as something to confirm on the pricing page before you plan a batch of stagings around it. This is exactly the kind of disagreement between sources that this article flags rather than hides: the directory says a free plan exists, the vendor page confirms paid credit pricing, and the exact free balance is not verified here.

Use Spacely the same way as REimagineHome: stage one or two key rooms, label them as virtually staged, and keep real photos alongside. If you stage every room of every listing, buyers start to distrust all your photos, which is the opposite of what staging is for.

## Indian portals: Magicbricks, Housing.com, 99acres and NoBroker

For buyers, the most useful AI in India is already inside the big portals, and using it costs nothing beyond the portal's normal listing terms.

Magicbricks offers PropWorth, an AI-based property valuation calculator, and PropIndex, an AI price index built from listing data, user activity and trends. PropWorth is useful as a starting range for a neighbourhood, but it is an estimate from listing data, not a registered valuation. Two flats in the same society can differ sharply by floor, facing, furnishing and urgency, so use PropWorth to test whether your asking price is in the right zone, then refine it with recent comparable sales in the same building or lane.

Housing.com uses AI image recognition to categorise and tag property photos and to filter out irrelevant or low-quality images, which makes its search results cleaner. It also publishes price trends for localities. For an agent, that means your cover photo matters more than ever: a clear, well-lit hall photo will be tagged and shown properly, while a dark or irrelevant image may be pushed down.

99acres runs an AI chatbot that answers buyer questions in natural language and recommends properties. It helps buyers shortlist, but it does not verify ownership or approvals, so a buyer should still check the title deed, RERA registration where applicable, and society dues before advancing money.

NoBroker's model is different: it connects owners and seekers directly without brokerage, and adds paid services such as rental agreements, home loans, legal help and packers and movers. For tenants especially, NoBroker is often the first search, but its brokerage-free promise applies to the connection, not to every add-on service, so read the charges for each service separately.

Listing on these portals is not always free for agents: owner listings are often free or cheap, while agent and builder packages are paid. Check the current listing terms on each portal before budgeting, because portal pricing changes without notice.

## Lead follow-up tools: useful, but mostly US-focused

A second group of tools handles lead follow-up: an AI that answers listing questions on your website, books appointments, or calls back missed leads. An October 2026 directory lists free tiers such as Roof AI at 5 leads a month, Homebot for up to 25 past clients with monthly home-value digests, and Smith.ai at 25 calls a month. These figures come from a US-focused directory, not from the vendors' India pages, and phone-answering tools built for US numbers may not work well with Indian numbers, WhatsApp, or Hindi and regional-language callers.

Treat this category as experimental in India. If you try one, test it with your own number first, check whether it supports WhatsApp or only phone calls, and never let it quote prices or promise site visits without your approval. For most Indian agents, a fast personal WhatsApp reply still beats an automated caller that misunderstands the buyer.

## Using real-estate AI in India: language, payment and verification

Language comes first. Most specialist real-estate AI tools are built in English, while many buyers search in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali or Hinglish. Write the listing headline and the first two lines in the language your local buyers actually use, even if the rest of the description is English, and check any AI Hindi translation yourself, because a mistranslated area name or possession date creates confusion.

Payment is the second trap. Several tools price in dollars, and a $9 or $19 monthly plan becomes roughly 750 to 1,600 rupees before taxes and card charges, and may renew automatically. Use a card you monitor, note the renewal date, and cancel the week a listing closes rather than paying for months you do not use. Free plans are safer here: BoxBrownie's 10 jobs, CubiCasa's free plan and REimagineHome's 5 designs cost nothing to try.

Verification is the third point, and it matters more than any tool. No AI tool in this article checks ownership, RERA status, loan eligibility, or society approval. Before a buyer pays a token amount, the documents to check are the sale deed or allotment letter, the RERA registration for new projects, the society share certificate and dues, and the approved plan for the flat's layout. An AI price estimate is a starting point for negotiation, not a valuation certificate.

All of these tools work in India without a VPN, because they are ordinary websites and apps. If a tool asks you to install a VPN or sideload an app to use it, stop and treat it as suspicious, since legitimate real-estate tools do not need that.

## What to avoid

- **Publishing AI descriptions unchecked.** Invented area, facing, or possession claims destroy trust and can create legal trouble.
- **Hiding virtual staging.** Always label staged photos as virtually staged and keep real photos alongside.
- **Quoting an AI price as a valuation.** PropWorth and similar estimates are ranges from listing data. Cross-check with comparable sales and a local broker.
- **Paying for many tools at once.** Start free, find the one bottleneck in your work, such as descriptions or floor plans, and pay only for that.
- **Sharing client data carelessly.** Do not paste a buyer's phone number, budget, or loan details into an AI tool you have not checked for privacy terms.
- **Trusting a chatbot for legal answers.** Rent agreements, token amounts and RERA questions need a lawyer or the official portal, not a chatbot.
- **Using US phone tools blindly in India.** Test number support, WhatsApp support and language support before relying on them.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I sell a flat in India using only free AI tools?** Yes, for the marketing part. BoxBrownie covers descriptions, CubiCasa covers a floor plan, Matterport's free plan covers one 3D tour, and REimagineHome covers staging for one flat. Selling itself still needs site visits, document checks and negotiation, which no free tool does for you.

**Is a Magicbricks PropWorth estimate the same as a bank valuation?** No. PropWorth is an AI estimate from listing data and trends, useful for checking whether an asking price is reasonable. A bank valuation follows its own inspection and rules, and it can differ, especially for unusual floors, older buildings, or distress sales.

**Do I have to tell buyers that a photo is virtually staged?** Yes, and you should. Mark staged photos clearly in the listing and keep at least one real photo of each room. Buyers who discover staging only on the visit feel cheated, and that feeling kills deals.

**Which free tool should a new agent try first?** BoxBrownie AI Copywriting, because descriptions are the bottleneck for most new agents, and 10 free jobs a month cover a realistic starting volume. Add CubiCasa next, because a floor plan makes a listing stand out on every portal.

**Can these tools work for rental listings too?** Yes. Descriptions, floor plans, tours and staging help rentals just as much as sales, and rental listings usually need faster turnaround, which is where free AI drafts help most. NoBroker is also worth checking first for rental demand in your city.

**Is it safe to upload property photos to an AI staging tool?** Generally yes for empty-room photos, but avoid uploading photos that show personal documents, nameplates, or valuables. Read the tool's privacy terms before uploading photos of an occupied home, and get the owner's permission first.

## The final verdict, and a checklist before you pay

Start with BoxBrownie for descriptions, CubiCasa for a free floor plan and PropWorth for price checks; never quote an AI price without checking it. That combination costs nothing, covers the three things buyers check first, and leaves you free to spend money only on the bottleneck you actually hit, whether that is more staging credits, more 3D tours, or a paid portal package.

Before paying for any tool, run this checklist: confirm the free limit on the official pricing page today, test it on one real listing, check whether it supports your buyers' language and WhatsApp, note the renewal date and price in rupees, and keep the real photos and documents ready, because no AI tool replaces a visit, a deed check, or an honest price.
