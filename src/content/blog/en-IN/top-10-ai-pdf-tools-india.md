In India, free chatbots cover most PDF summaries, while dedicated tools add citations, editing, and offline use. Never upload bank or passport papers.

1. ChatGPT: answers and summarises PDFs; free plan, daily upload limits.
2. Gemini: summarises PDFs in Google Drive; free, 10 files per message.
3. Gemini Notebook: cited answers across PDFs; free, 50 sources.
4. ChatPDF: quick PDF chat, no sign-up; free, 2 PDFs a day.
5. PDF.ai: PDF chat, data extraction; free, 1 upload a month.
6. AskYourPDF: page-cited summaries; free, 1 document a day.
7. Smallpdf: AI chat, edit, convert; free, 2 tasks a day.
8. Adobe AI Assistant: summaries and answers in Acrobat; paid AI, free trial.
9. UPDF AI: summarises, translates, and chats; free, 5 PDFs total.
10. PDFgear: offline Copilot chat and editing; free, unlimited, no sign-up.

**Which free AI PDF tool is best for students in India?** Gemini Notebook, for cited answers across papers at no cost.

## Final verdict

Start with Gemini Notebook for study and ChatPDF for quick questions; pay only when limits block work.
