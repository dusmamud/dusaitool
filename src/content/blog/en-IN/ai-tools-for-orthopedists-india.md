For an orthopedics clinic in India, the best setup is Practo Ray for appointments, Heidi Health for consultation notes, and Physitrack for physio follow-ups. Together they cover booking, the consult, and home exercises after surgery or injury. None reads X-rays or MRI scans — that needs separate, hospital-grade imaging software.

| Need | Best pick | Starting cost |
|---|---|---|
| Appointments and clinic | Practo Ray | From ₹999/month |
| Consultation notes | Heidi Health | Free plan available |
| Physio follow-ups | Physitrack | Quote needed for India |

## 1. Practo Ray — appointments and clinic management

Practo Ray is India-built clinic software used by over 50,000 doctors. It covers appointments, online bookings, SMS reminders, billing, prescriptions, and patient records, and complies with India's ABDM digital health mission. Its plans page lists CM Atom at ₹999/month on a one-year term, with a free trial. Third-party reports mention extra per-appointment fees, so check official pricing first.

## 2. Heidi Health — AI scribe for consultation notes

Heidi Health is an ambient AI scribe: it listens to a consultation and drafts structured notes, referral letters, and patient instructions, so the doctor types less. The free plan offers unlimited basic transcription and 10 Pro Actions a month. Paid Clinician pricing is reported from $110 to $150 per user per month — sources disagree, so re-check before paying. It supports 110+ languages; audio is not stored after transcription.

## 3. Physitrack — physio exercise follow-ups

Physitrack is built for physiotherapy follow-up, which matters after most orthopedic treatment. Clinicians pick from 18,000+ exercise videos, build a home programme, and send it via PhysiApp, which records adherence, progress, and discomfort. It also adds scheduling, telehealth, and an optional AI scribe. No India list price was published — ask for a written rupee quote first.

## Using these tools in India

Practo Ray is the India-native piece: rupee pricing, SMS reminders, and ABDM compliance. Heidi and Physitrack work in India without a VPN, but charge in foreign currency with no India price, so confirm currency, taxes, and patient-data handling first. Also see our guide to [AI tools for physiotherapists](/blog/ai-tools-for-physiotherapists-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not let an AI note enter a patient record without a doctor reviewing it. Do not upload X-rays or MRI scans to general chatbots and expect a diagnosis. Do not promise an AI-based diagnosis or recovery date, and do not pay for an annual plan before trying the free plan or trial.

## FAQ

**Can AI tools read my patients' X-rays?**

No. None of these three tools interprets imaging. X-ray and MRI analysis needs dedicated, regulated imaging software used in hospitals and diagnostic centres.

**Is there a free option for a small orthopedics clinic?**

Yes. Heidi Health has a free plan for basic notes, and Practo Ray offers a free trial. Start with those, measure time saved, and only then pay.

**Do these tools work in India without a VPN?**

Yes. All three are web-based and work in India without a VPN. The catch is pricing: Practo Ray charges in rupees, while Heidi and Physitrack bill in foreign currency.

**Will patients actually follow app-based physio exercises?**

Many do, especially with video demonstrations and reminders. PhysiApp tracks adherence, so you can see who stopped and call them back. Explain exercises in person first.
