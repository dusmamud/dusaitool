2. Fireflies: transcribes meetings; unlimited transcription, 400 minutes storage per team.
3. Otter: live-transcribes meetings; free 300 minutes monthly, 30 per call.

7. Notta: transcribes 58 languages; free 120 minutes monthly, 3-minute calls only.
8. tl;dv: records meetings with transcripts; unlimited recordings, AI notes, 10 meetings.
9. Read AI: transcribes meetings with coaching; free five meetings monthly, one hour each.
10. Evernote: notes with AI search; free 50 notes, one notebook, one device.
