A cosmetics shop rarely loses money on the wrong lipstick shade. It loses money quietly: a cream that expired on the shelf, a serum that ran out during a festival weekend. The best tools keep four things straight — products, stock, customers, and billing — and staff will actually open them on a normal phone on a busy Saturday.

So start simple: Vyapar for billing and stock, WhatsApp Business for the catalogue, and Canva for posters. Add Zoho Inventory when you also sell online, and TallyPrime only when your accountant needs full books. Prices change often, so re-check the official pricing page before you pay. ## 1. Vyapar — billing and stock from your phone

Vyapar is made in India for small Indian shops. It creates GST-compliant invoices with CGST and SGST worked out automatically, pulls HSN codes for every product, and shares the bill on WhatsApp. It tracks stock with low-stock alerts plus batch and expiry tracking — vital for cosmetics. The Android app has a genuinely free tier; paid desktop plans start from around ₹3,399 a year. The free tier is for one device and one user; Android and Windows only, not iOS.

## 2. Zoho Inventory — when the shop also sells online

Zoho Inventory is the right second step when your shop also takes orders on Instagram, Amazon, or its own website. It tracks stock across locations, manages sales and purchase orders, and syncs stock so a serum sold in the shop is no longer shown as available online. The free plan covers up to 50 orders a month for one user and one warehouse; Standard is listed at about ₹999 a month on annual billing for 500 orders a month.

## 3. TallyPrime — full accounts your CA already knows

TallyPrime is India's most widely used accounting software, and almost every CA already knows its reports. It handles full GST accounting, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B preparation, e-invoicing, and batch-wise inventory. For a shop that has grown into a distributor or a small chain, it is the safe choice. TallyPrime Silver costs ₹22,500 as a one-time licence plus 18% GST, with renewal at ₹4,500 a year from year two; rental starts at ₹750 a month. There is no free plan, so buy it when your CA asks for it.

## 4. WhatsApp Business — your catalogue and your regulars

WhatsApp Business is free, and for a cosmetics shop it is the most valuable customer tool, because your customers already live there. The catalogue turns your profile into a mini storefront: products with photos and prices that customers can browse. Quick replies answer the same questions in one tap, and labels sort chats by status. Broadcast lists reach up to 256 contacts who saved your number — message only people who agreed to hear from you. For more on keeping customers happy, see our guide to [AI tools for customer support in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-customer-support-india/).

## 5. Canva — posters, price cards, and festive offers

Canva makes shop-window posters, counter price cards, and Instagram posts for a new launch. The free plan is genuinely usable, with more than 1.6 million free templates, 5 GB of storage, and a small monthly allowance of AI features such as Magic Write. Premium templates and photos, marked with a crown, need the paid Pro plan. Filter for free templates only, use your own product photos, and keep one consistent colour and font.

## Using these tools in an Indian cosmetics shop

Enter every product into Vyapar with its batch and expiry date on the day it arrives, and check the low-stock list every Monday before ordering. Put your 20 to 30 best sellers into the WhatsApp catalogue, and update it the same day a price changes. Make one Canva poster a week for your broadcast list. Each month, list products expiring in the next 90 days, discount them at the front of the shelf, and never sell an expired product.

## What to avoid

Avoid buying a full ERP before your shop needs it; a ₹50,000 system nobody opens is worse than a free app used daily. Avoid any tool that promises to auto-generate product claims — "removes pimples in seven days" — because false claims about cosmetics can bring legal trouble. Avoid entering stock in two places, such as a diary and an app, because the two will disagree within a week.

## FAQ

**Can I run a cosmetics shop with only free tools?**

Yes, for a single counter: Vyapar's free app, WhatsApp Business, and Canva's free plan cover billing, catalogue, and posters.

**Which tool tracks expiry dates for creams and serums?**

Vyapar tracks batch and expiry dates; Zoho Inventory tracks batches. Enter dates on arrival and check monthly.

**Do I need TallyPrime for a small cosmetics shop?**

Not at the start — Vyapar handles billing and GST reports.

**Is WhatsApp Business really free?**

Yes — catalogue, quick replies, and 256-contact broadcasts are free. The paid API is for large volumes.

**Can these tools help me sell on Amazon or Instagram too?**

Yes — Zoho Inventory syncs stock across shop and online channels. See our guide to [AI tools for Amazon sellers in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-amazon-sellers-india/).
