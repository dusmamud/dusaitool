For most Indian coffee planters, start with Plantix for leaf-disease diagnosis, KisanGPT for voice advice in Indian languages, and Fasal for sensor-based monitoring. All three work on ordinary Android phones in India, no VPN needed. Start free, test on one block, and confirm big decisions with a local agronomist first.

| Need | Best pick | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Spot leaf disease fast | Plantix | Free app |
| Ask questions by voice | KisanGPT | Free app |
| Monitor estate and irrigation | Fasal | Free app; sensors and plans paid |

## 1. Plantix — photo diagnosis for sick plants

Photograph a diseased coffee leaf and Plantix's AI suggests the likely disease, pest or nutrient problem within seconds, with treatment options. The free Android app covers about 30 crops, detects 780+ plant damages, works in 19 languages, and has over 10 million downloads, mostly in India. Coffee coverage may be thinner than for rice — confirm serious diagnoses with a local expert before spraying.

## 2. KisanGPT — voice-first farming advice

KisanGPT (listed on Google Play as KissanAI) is a voice-first AI assistant for farmers. Ask about crop care, pests, weather or mandi prices in Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi or other Indian languages — useful in Coorg, Chikmagalur and Wayanad. The basic app is free. Treat answers as a starting point: prices and scheme details change often, so verify them on the day you act.

## 3. Fasal — sensors and AI for coffee estates

Fasal is a Bengaluru agritech platform built for horticulture crops including coffee. Its Fasal Sense sensors track micro-climate, soil and crop conditions, and its AI turns that data into irrigation, disease and pest alerts. The app is free to download, but sensor hardware and advisory plans are paid, so get a written quote first. Best for larger estates, where water and disease timing affect bean quality.

## Using these tools on an Indian coffee estate

Most coffee grows in Karnataka, Kerala and Tamil Nadu, often on shaded slopes with patchy mobile signal. Download the apps over Wi-Fi before heading to the field, and photograph leaves in daylight. For selling, compare local buyer quotes with mandi and Coffee Board prices, and never promise a delivery date based only on an app's yield estimate. If you run a café too, see [AI tools for coffee shops](/blog/ai-tools-for-coffee-shops-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not spray a chemical based on one photo diagnosis alone. Do not pay for sensors or premium plans before trialling one block. Do not share bank details or OTPs with anyone claiming to be a buyer found through an app. Prices and free limits change — re-check official pages before paying.

## FAQ

**Can AI tools really diagnose coffee leaf rust?**
They can flag likely problems from a photo, but no app replaces lab testing or an agronomist — act fast, then confirm.

**Do these tools work without a VPN in India?**
Yes — Plantix, KisanGPT and Fasal are all available in India on ordinary phones and networks, with no VPN needed.

**Which tool should a small planter try first?**
Plantix — free and immediately useful for disease checks. Add KisanGPT for voice questions.

**Can AI help me sell my coffee for a better price?**
Only indirectly, by improving quality and timing. Check live mandi prices and buyer quotes yourself; no app guarantees a price.

## Final verdict

Plantix for disease checks, KisanGPT for voice advice, Fasal for monitoring — start free, verify advice locally.
