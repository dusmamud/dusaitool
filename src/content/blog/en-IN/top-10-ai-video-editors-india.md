These ten tools work in India without a VPN. CapCut is left out because it is still blocked here.

1. Instagram Edits — free Reels editor; AI captions, 4K export, no watermark.
2. Canva — design editor; five lifetime AI clips, four seconds, silent.
3. Clipchamp — Microsoft browser editor; unlimited watermark-free 1080p exports.
4. Descript — text-based editor; one free hour monthly, watermarked 720p exports.
5. Runway — image-to-video AI; 125 one-time credits, about four short clips.
6. Pictory — script-to-video AI; 14-day trial, three projects, watermarked 720p.
7. InVideo — prompt-to-video AI; weekly free credits, watermarked exports (sources disagree).
8. VEED — browser editor; free 720p exports, watermark, ten-minute cap.
9. Filmora — desktop editor; free version, watermark on every export.
10. Adobe Express — quick social editor; two lifetime five-second AI videos free.

**Which free editor exports without a watermark?** Clipchamp and Instagram Edits; most others watermark free exports.

## Verdict

Start with Clipchamp or Instagram Edits for clean free exports; pay only for AI generation you actually use.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
