For most Indians, Canva is the best free poster maker; PosterMyWall is fastest for ready festival templates. All ten work in India without a VPN.

## Top 10

1. Canva — templates plus AI artwork; free plan, about 20 AI uses a month.
2. Adobe Express — poster templates, print-ready PDF; free daily AI generations.
3. PosterMyWall — ready festival and sale templates; 5 free monthly downloads.
4. Microsoft Designer — quick prompt-based posters; free, but personal use only.
5. Ideogram — artwork with readable text; 30 free credits weekly, generations public.
6. Sivi — one brief, many banner sizes; 12 free credits monthly.
7. Piktochart — information posters and notices; 2 free PNG downloads ever.
8. Dreamina — CapCut's AI image maker; 120 free credits a day.
9. VistaCreate — template posters like Canva; free, unlimited standard downloads.
10. Fotor — AI poster generator; limited free credits, watermarked exports.
