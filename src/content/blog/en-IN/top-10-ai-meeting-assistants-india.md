For most Indian teams, Fathom is the best free start: unlimited recordings and transcripts.

1. Fathom: unlimited free recordings and transcripts; advanced summaries capped at five monthly.
2. Fireflies.ai: unlimited free transcription in 100+ languages; storage capped, reports vary.
3. Otter.ai: live transcription with speaker labels; free plan gives 300 minutes monthly.
4. tl;dv: unlimited recordings and transcripts; AI notes cover about ten meetings monthly.
5. Read AI: meeting reports and engagement scores; free plan covers five meetings monthly.
6. Granola: bot-free notes from device audio; free notes visible for 30 days.
7. Notta: multilingual transcription in 58 languages; free plan gives 120 minutes monthly.
8. Zoom AI Companion: summaries and action items included with paid Zoom plans.
9. Gemini in Meet: Google's note-taking bundled with Workspace Business Standard plans.
10. Krisp: noise cancellation plus meeting notes; seven-day trial only, no free plan.

**Which free AI meeting assistant is best in India?** Fathom, for unlimited free recordings.

Start with Fathom free, add Otter or Fireflies when minutes grow, and check built-in Zoom or Gemini first.

Prices and free limits change: check the official pricing page before paying.
