No AI tool can clear UPSC for you — Prelims, Mains and the Personality Test still need your own study, writing and memory. The direct answer: use Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) to study from your own notes and PDFs, Perplexity to research current affairs with sources you can open, ChatGPT as a tutor and answer-writing partner, Anki for spaced-revision flashcards, and Testbook for free mock tests and previous-year questions. Every AI tool can invent facts with total confidence, so never memorise or write anything in an answer until you have checked it against the original source.

This article is for aspirants preparing for UPSC CSE 2027 — Prelims is scheduled for 23 May 2027. Prices change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## 1. Gemini Notebook — study from your own material

Gemini Notebook is Google's tool for studying from sources you upload; it was called NotebookLM until Google renamed it in July 2026. You add PDFs, notes, Google Docs or YouTube lecture links, and it answers only from that material, citing the exact page — so it will not invent a constitutional article number the way a general chatbot might. The free plan gives up to 100 notebooks with up to 50 sources each.

## 2. Perplexity — current affairs with sources you can open

Perplexity searches the live web and puts a numbered citation on nearly every claim, so you can open the original PIB release or ministry page before trusting anything. Use it for daily news follow-ups and for the "why it matters" line of a Mains answer. The free plan gives unlimited ordinary searches but only a few Pro searches a day — Perplexity's help centre says 3, other guides quote around 5. 

## 3. ChatGPT — tutor and answer-writing practice

Use ChatGPT to explain concepts, shorten answers and generate practice questions. Free plan: everyday text chats are unlimited, but file uploads are limited to 3 a day. In India, Go is ₹399 a month and Plus ₹1,999, both including GST. Never accept the score it gives your answer — ask which part of the question you ignored, then check its corrections against a standard source. For a second opinion on chatbots, read our comparison of [AI chatbots beyond ChatGPT and Gemini](/blog/ai-chatbots-beyond-chatgpt-gemini-india/).

## 4. Anki — revision that survives six months

Anki is a flashcard app built on spaced repetition: cards you get wrong come back soon, cards you get right come back later. Make one short card per fact and review daily. Desktop, AnkiDroid and AnkiWeb sync are all free. The only paid part is AnkiMobile, the official iPhone and iPad app, a one-time purchase of $24.99 in the US App Store.

## 5. Testbook — mock tests and previous-year questions

No AI tool replaces sitting with a timer and a question paper; Testbook is the most practical free starting point for that. Its UPSC pages carry previous-year questions with answers, free mock tests and a free 100-day Targeted Prelims Programme. Most full test series are paid: the UPSC Prelims 2027 mock test series lists 230 tests with only 1 free, and the NCERT-based series lists 92 tests with 1 free. 

## Using these tools in India

All five work in India without a VPN, and all work in Hindi as well as English. The ₹0 stack: Gemini Notebook for notes, Perplexity for news, ChatGPT for practice, Anki for revision, Testbook's free tests. Pay only when a limit actually blocks your study, and never pay for two tools that do the same job.

## What to avoid

Never treat an AI summary as a fact — verify every date, article number and scheme name against the original source first. Do not collect tools instead of studying. Do not buy a paid plan because a coupon expires today — prices and offers change; the Airtel Perplexity offer in old videos ended in January 2026. Do not let a chatbot write your answers for you. 

## Frequently asked questions

**Can AI tools replace coaching for UPSC?**

No — coaching gives you a schedule and evaluated answers; AI tools give faster notes and revision.

**Which AI tool is best for UPSC current affairs?**

Perplexity, because every claim carries a citation you can open.

**Is ChatGPT free enough for UPSC preparation?**

For most, yes. Everyday text chats are unlimited on the free plan, with 3 file uploads a day.

**How do I use Anki for UPSC revision?**

Make one short card per fact and review daily. Desktop and AnkiDroid are free; only the iPhone app is paid.

**Are there free UPSC mock tests online?**

Yes — Testbook's free mock tests and 100-day prelims programme, though most full series include only 1 free test.

**Can I prepare for UPSC in Hindi with these tools?**

Yes — all four AI tools work in Hindi, and Testbook offers English and Hindi tests.

## Final verdict

Start free with Gemini Notebook for notes, Perplexity for current affairs and Anki for revision — pay only when a limit actually blocks you. Add ChatGPT for answer practice and Testbook's free tests, verify every AI fact against its source, and let the last ten years of question papers decide what you study next.
