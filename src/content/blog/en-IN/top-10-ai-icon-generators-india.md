Ten tools that make app icons, all working in India.

1. Recraft makes SVG icons; free daily credits.
2. IconScout makes text-to-SVG icons; free limited.
3. CleanVector makes icon sets; free batch.
4. Appicons.ai makes app icons; first free.
5. appicon.co resizes icons free; no account.
6. Iconikai makes app icons; 5 free daily.
7. Imagcon makes iOS, Android icons; free exports.
8. IconsMint extends icon sets; features free.
9. Canva icons, logos; free downloads.
10. Firefly makes icon art; free with Adobe account.

## FAQ

**Which free tool suits beginners?** Canva is easiest; Recraft gives editable vectors.

## Final verdict

Start with Recraft for editable icons, Canva for quick icons.
