These ten tools turn text into natural speech in India without a VPN. Test Hindi and English voices first.

## Top 10

1. ElevenLabs — voices and cloning; free: 10,000 characters monthly.
2. Murf — studio voiceovers; free: 10 minutes total, no downloads.
3. Google Cloud Text-to-Speech — API; free: 4 million Standard characters monthly.
4. Azure AI Speech — neural voices; free: 500,000 characters monthly.
5. Amazon Polly — AWS text-to-speech; free: 5 million characters monthly, first year.
6. Speechify — reads PDFs aloud; free: 10 basic voices.
7. TTSMaker — browser voiceovers; free: 20,000 characters weekly.
8. Narakeet — scripts to voice; free: 20 files, 1,000 characters each.
9. WellSaid — corporate narration; free trial: 3 download minutes monthly.
10. Luvvoice — MP3 voiceovers; free: 10,000 characters monthly (homepage says 20,000).

## FAQ

**Which free tool is best for Hindi voiceovers?** Test Google Cloud and Azure first; both offer Indian voices and large free allowances.

## Final verdict

ElevenLabs for quality, Google Cloud for volume, TTSMaker for quick free voiceovers — test voices before paying.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
