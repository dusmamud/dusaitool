## Buffer: the free scheduler that most people actually need

[Buffer](https://buffer.com/)'s free plan connects up to three social channels and stores up to ten scheduled posts per channel at any given time, for a total of thirty queued posts across the account. The limit is **queue size, not monthly volume**: once a post publishes, the slot frees up. There is also a **lifetime limit of eight unique channel connections**, which Buffer counts permanently across your account history, so do not connect and disconnect channels carelessly, because every connection counts toward that lifetime total, even if you disconnect it later.

The AI Assistant runs on *GPT-4* and is included on every tier including the free one, with no daily cap. It generates post ideas, repurposes long-form content into platform-native shorter posts, and adjusts tone for LinkedIn versus TikTok. That repurposing is Buffer's most useful AI feature for Indian creators: write one idea once, and Buffer can turn it into a LinkedIn post, a TikTok caption, and an Instagram caption, each in the right length and tone, instead of you copying the same text to every platform. Buffer also exposes thirty days of analytics on the free tier, which is enough to spot weekly patterns but not seasonal ones, and it includes a basic landing-page builder and the browser extension.

The paid jump is Buffer Essentials at 5 dollars per channel per month on annual billing, 6 dollars monthly, for unlimited scheduling, a hashtag manager, and best-time-to-post recommendations. For a solo operator running three channels that is 15 dollars a month for unlimited scheduling. The Team plan moves to 10 dollars per channel per month and is the only way to add a second user, since Essentials is locked to one seat. Buffer's free analytics do not include Reels-specific engagement metrics on Instagram, only unified post metrics, so if Reels are your primary format, the free tier will not tell you which Reels are pulling and which ones are dead weight, and you should check Reels metrics in Meta Business Suite instead.

Use Buffer in a specific way:

1. Connect only the three channels you actually post to, because of the lifetime eight-connection limit.
2. Fill the queue once a week, ten posts per channel, and let published posts free up slots for the next week.
3. Use the *AI Assistant* to repurpose, not to invent: give it your own idea, your own product, or your own story, and let it adjust length and tone, instead of asking it to invent a post from nothing, because invented posts sound generic and your audience will ignore them.

## Canva: visual production, not scheduling

[Canva](https://www.canva.com/)'s free plan in 2026 includes more than 2.1 million templates, 5 GB of storage, fifty AI credits total, lifetime and not monthly, and access to roughly 250,000 free [photos](/blog/free-ai-photo-editors-india/) and graphics. The free tier covers ninety percent of routine social media graphic work: Instagram posts, Facebook covers, LinkedIn carousels, TikTok thumbnails, and Pinterest pins, all sized correctly out of the box. That sizing matters, because a post designed for Instagram will look cropped on LinkedIn and wrong on TikTok, and Canva free gives you the right size for each platform without you looking it up.

What the free tier does not include in 2026 is important to know before you start: Background Remover, Magic Resize, Brand Kit locked to three colors and two fonts with no custom font upload, transparent PNG export, video downloads with transparent backgrounds, and Canva's new *Design Intelligence* suite. Premium stock elements are charged per use, not per plan. Free users are also capped at three AI-generated designs per day and ten [video renders](/blog/free-ai-video-editing-captions-india/) per week regardless of length. Canva Pro is 15 dollars a month or 120 dollars a year and adds the brand kit, background remover, premium templates, 1 TB storage, and Magic Studio AI tools. Verified K-12 teachers and registered nonprofits get the equivalent of Pro for free through Canva for Education and Canva Nonprofits respectively.

Canva free integrates with Buffer, so the workflow design in Canva, queue in Buffer runs end-to-end without leaving either tool. That workflow is the free stack for most Indian small businesses:

1. Design the post in Canva.
2. Download it.
3. Upload it to Buffer.
4. Let Buffer's AI Assistant adjust the caption for each platform.
5. Schedule it. For Hindi posts, check spelling at full size, because Canva's AI text can make Hindi spelling errors that are invisible at thumbnail size and obvious at full size. For Hinglish posts, write the caption yourself and let Canva handle only the visual, because AI Hinglish captions often mix formal and informal language in the same post.

## Meta Business Suite, Publer, Metricool, and Zoho Social

Meta Business Suite alone, plus Canva free for visuals, is the zero-cost stack for a solo creator on one platform, Instagram, or on Instagram and Facebook together. Meta Business Suite is free, it is the native tool for Facebook and Instagram, and it gives you native features like Reels metrics that third-party free tools do not. Its limit is scope: Facebook and Instagram only, with no cross-platform analytics. If you post only to Instagram and Facebook, you do not need Buffer at all, and you should not pay for a scheduler to do what Meta's own free tool already does.

[Publer](https://publer.com/) free connects three social accounts excluding X, with ten scheduled posts per account at a time, which makes it a useful alternative to Buffer if you do not use X, or if Buffer's lifetime connection limit is a problem for you. [Metricool](https://metricool.com/) free covers one brand with twenty posts a month, with no LinkedIn or X, which makes it better for analytics than for scheduling, because twenty posts a month covers a small business posting every other day, but not a creator posting daily. [Zoho Social](https://www.zoho.com/social/) free covers one brand across six specific channels, one each of X, Instagram, Facebook Page, LinkedIn Page, LinkedIn profile, and Google Business Profile, which makes it the broadest free channel coverage, if you need all six channels and can live with one brand.

[Hootsuite](https://www.hootsuite.com/), [Later](https://later.com/social-media-scheduler/), and [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/) offer trials rather than free plans, and Hootsuite's *Perch*, its content creation, planning, and publishing platform, connects to *Wisdom*, a social-first AI agent that takes a post from AI draft to scheduled, published content in one workflow, but it has no free plan, with paid plans from 99 dollars a month per user. For a solo Indian creator or small business, Hootsuite's price is not justified until you have a team, multiple brands, and a posting volume that free tools cannot handle. Start free, measure your actual posting volume for a month, and pay only when a free limit repeatedly blocks you.

## Using social AI in India: language, timing, and what not to automate

Language for Indian social media is not one language. Hindi posts work for Hindi audiences, English posts work for English audiences, and Hinglish posts work for the large audience that thinks in Hinglish and reads Hinglish fastest. AI captions often fail at Hinglish, because they mix formal Hindi, informal Hindi, and English in the same caption, which sounds machine-generated to a native reader. Write your caption yourself in the language your audience actually uses, and let AI adjust length and tone, not invent the message. For Hindi posts, check every AI-generated Hindi word, because a spelling error in a caption is more visible than a spelling error in a long article, and your audience will comment on it before they comment on your product.

Timing matters, but generic best-time-to-post advice is not your audience's best time. Buffer's paid best-time recommendations are based on your unique audience, while free advice is generic, and generic advice for a US audience does not apply to an Indian audience in a different timezone with different working hours. Measure your own audience for a month using Buffer's thirty days of free analytics or Meta Business Suite's native analytics, and post when your own audience is actually online, not when a blog post says you should. For Indian audiences, test morning, lunch, and evening slots for two weeks each, and keep the slot that gives you the most real engagement, not just the most views.

What not to automate is as important as what to automate. - **Automate:** scheduling, caption length adjustment, and visual sizing, because those are mechanical.
- **Do not automate:** replies to customers, comments on your posts, or crisis responses, because an AI reply to a customer complaint sounds cold and can damage your brand faster than a slow human reply.
- **Do not automate:** posting without reviewing, because an AI-scheduled post can go live at the wrong moment, like during a tragedy or a controversy, and damage your reputation. Review every scheduled post before it goes live, even if you scheduled it a week ago, because context changes faster than your queue.

## What to avoid

- Do not connect and disconnect channels in Buffer carelessly, because its lifetime limit of eight unique channel connections counts permanently, even for disconnected channels.
- Do not rely on Canva free's fifty AI credits as a monthly allowance, because they are lifetime, not monthly, and do not rely on Canva free for Background Remover, Magic Resize, or transparent PNG export, because those are Pro features.
- Do not use Buffer free analytics for Reels decisions, because they do not include Reels-specific engagement metrics, and check Reels in Meta Business Suite instead.
- Do not post AI captions without editing them for your audience, your language, and your brand, because generic captions are ignored.
- Do not pay for Hootsuite, Later, or SocialBee before you have measured a month of free posting, and do not pay for Buffer Essentials until its ten-post queue repeatedly blocks your schedule, not just once.

A final trap is posting more and engaging less. Beginners often schedule thirty posts, feel productive, and then ignore comments, messages, and replies, which kills engagement faster than posting less. Social media rewards engagement, not just posting, and an account that posts daily but never replies will grow slower than an account that posts three times a week and replies to every comment. Use AI to save production time, then use that saved time to reply, to comment, and to talk to your audience, because that conversation is the part AI cannot do for you.
