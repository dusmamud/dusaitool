## The short answer

Start with three apps: **SwingVision** for match analysis and line calls, **OnCourt AI** for stroke feedback, and **ATP WTA Live** for free scores and rankings. **PlaySight** is a club system with installed cameras. All four work in India without a VPN, but free tiers are limited and prices change — re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## SwingVision: the best all-rounder

SwingVision turns one iPhone or iPad camera into a match analyst. Mount the phone behind the baseline and it tracks shot speed, depth, accuracy and rally length, keeps score, calls lines and cuts highlight clips with the score overlaid. It is the official player and ball-tracking app of Tennis Australia and the LTA.

The catch is the platform: SwingVision is iOS-only, with no Android app yet, and live line calling needs an Apple Watch. Free plan: 8 hours a month, last seven days only, exports capped at five minutes. Paid plans: Plus $14.99/month or $95.99/year (16 hours); Pro $24.99/month or $179.99/year (30 hours, full-court line calling); Max $39.99/month or $299.99/year (60 hours, 4K).

## OnCourt AI: stroke feedback from a short video

OnCourt AI is the cheapest way to get a second opinion on your technique. Upload a photo or video of a forehand, backhand, serve or volley and it scores the stroke, flags what to fix and suggests drills. Its site says it tracks more than 20 body points and answers in under 30 seconds.

Free plan: 10 lifetime analyses and 30 chat messages every 30 days. Pro costs $19.99 a month, Elite $49.99. Warning: sources disagree — a UK version of the site lists 3 free analyses a month and Pro at £8.99 a month, so confirm the plan at sign-up. The web version is the reliable way in; the iPhone app was still coming soon in late 2026.

## PlaySight SmartCourt: the club option

PlaySight is not a personal purchase. It is a multi-camera system installed at a club — the SmartCourt — that live-streams matches, records video, gives instant replay and offers AI analytics, highlights and frame-by-frame coaching tools. The app is free on iOS and Android, but only useful if your club has the hardware, which reportedly costs around $12,500 per court plus $500 a month. If your academy has one, use it; if not, SwingVision covers most of what one player needs.


## ATP WTA Live: scores, draws and rankings, free

For following the pro game, the official ATP WTA Live app is the straightforward pick. Free, with no in-app purchases, it covers live scores, schedules, draws, rankings, stats, news and Challenger live streaming in India on iOS and Android. Tennis TV is the ATP's separate paid service for full matches.

## Using these tools in India

Most Indian players need only a simple combination: SwingVision on iPhone, OnCourt AI on Android, and ATP WTA Live for scores. Court time in Indian cities is expensive, so record practice matches and review the numbers at home. Check the analysis before buying gear — it shows whether your second serve or backhand is the real weakness. Carry a power bank and tripod: summer heat drains batteries, and a phone in direct sun can overheat and stop recording.

## What to avoid

Do not pay for a yearly plan before a full month of real use — monthly billing first. Do not trust one AI line call in a serious match; even the best systems quote around 97% accuracy on close calls. Do not upload videos of other players without consent. Do not buy apps promising guaranteed ranking jumps — no app can do that. Do not assume a tool works on Android because its website shows a phone — SwingVision has no Android app.

## FAQ

**Which AI tennis app is best for beginners in India?**
OnCourt AI: free to start, on web and Android, with drills per stroke. SwingVision suits regular match players with an iPhone.

**Is SwingVision available on Android in India?**
No. As of late 2026 it has no Android app; it runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and live line calling needs an Apple Watch.

**Are these tools really free?**
ATP WTA Live is fully free. The others have free tiers with real limits — recording hours or analysis counts.

**Can AI line calling replace a line judge in a tournament?**
No, not at club level. Electronic line calling is approved for specific professional setups; a phone app's calls are a training aid.

**Do I need expensive hardware to use AI tennis tools?**
No. SwingVision and OnCourt AI need only a phone (plus a mount for SwingVision). PlaySight needs installed court hardware.
