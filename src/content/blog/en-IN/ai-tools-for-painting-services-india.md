A painting contractor in India does four jobs at once: help the customer pick a colour, measure the work, quote a fair price, and keep the customer updated until the last coat dries. The tools below cover all four, and the useful ones are free.

Prices and free limits change often — re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## 1. Colour with Asian Paints — show the colour before you paint

Colour with Asian Paints is a free app for Android and iOS. Photograph the customer's wall, then try shades, textures and wallpapers on that photo. Its colour picker matches any photo colour to the nearest Asian Paints shade.

At the first site visit, try two or three shades with the customer beside you and save the chosen look, so nobody argues later about the shade. The website's paint calculator estimates the paint a room needs.

It has over 9.6 million Google Play installs, but recent reviews say updates made it harder to use, so test it first. Confirm the final shade with a shade card or a test patch.

## 2. Dulux Visualizer IN — live preview through the camera

Dulux's free app, made by AkzoNobel, works differently: point your phone camera at a wall and the colour changes live on screen as you walk around. Without the motion sensors camera mode needs, a photo mode works with a still image.

Its colour picker matches a colour from an object — a sofa, a curtain, a painting — against more than 1,200 Dulux colours. It suits the quick "yes or no" test: a customer unsure about a dark blue accent wall sees it live in ten seconds.

It has over 2.3 million installs in India, but shows only Dulux shades.

## 3. Zoho Invoice — quotes and GST invoices without paying

A verbal quote is where most painting disputes start. Zoho Invoice is free forever for Indian businesses, and it turns a quote into a proper document: itemised work, paint and labour split out, payment terms, and GST. Once the customer approves, the quote converts into an invoice in one step.

The free plan covers up to 500 invoices a year, two users and three projects, with "Powered by Zoho Invoice" branding — enough for most small painting contractors. Setup takes 30 to 60 minutes the first time. Do it once, on a quiet day, and every later quote takes minutes.

## 4. WhatsApp Business — your customer desk

Most Indian customers will message you on WhatsApp before they ever call. The WhatsApp Business app is free and separate from ordinary WhatsApp.

It adds a business profile; a catalogue of your painting packages with photos and prices; quick replies for questions you answer fifty times a day; greeting and away messages; and labels to sort chats into new enquiry, quote sent, work in progress and paid.

Set up labels on day one — a chat labelled "quote sent" that nobody follows up is a lost job. Send before-and-after photos in the same chat thread as the quote. Never broadcast to strangers — spam gets numbers blocked.

## How to use these tools in India

Follow a simple routine. At the site visit, photograph every room in daylight and preview two shades with the customer. Measure the walls and use the paint calculator so your material estimate is honest. Send an itemised Zoho Invoice quote the same evening, with the saved colour preview attached.

Update the customer on WhatsApp with a photo after each day's work, and ask for a review when done. ## What to avoid

Do not promise an exact colour from a phone screen — screens differ, lighting differs, and the real wall always surprises. Do not quote from memory; measure and write it down. Do not take a full cash advance without a receipt, and do not start without a written scope stating coats, paint, and exclusions. Never share a customer's home photos publicly without their clear permission.

## FAQ

**Can I show a customer the paint colour before starting work?**

Yes. Preview shades on the actual wall in daylight with the Asian Paints or Dulux app, then confirm the final shade with a shade card or a test patch.

**Which free tool makes a proper painting quote?**

Zoho Invoice is free for Indian businesses and makes itemised quotes with GST that convert into invoices once approved. Its free plan covers up to 500 invoices a year.

**How do I keep track of many customers and follow-ups?**

Use WhatsApp Business labels such as new enquiry, quote sent, work in progress and paid. Check the "quote sent" label every evening and follow up once.

**Do these colour apps work without a VPN in India?**

Yes. The Asian Paints and Dulux apps are made for India, are free on Google Play and the App Store, and need no VPN.

**What should a painting quote always include?**

The area, coats, paint brand and product, surface preparation, labour, GST, payment schedule, and exclusions such as putty work or furniture shifting.

## Final verdict

Start with the two free brand visualizers, Zoho Invoice for quotes, and WhatsApp Business for customers — that combo covers most small painting jobs. Measure honestly, quote in writing, and confirm every colour on the real wall.
