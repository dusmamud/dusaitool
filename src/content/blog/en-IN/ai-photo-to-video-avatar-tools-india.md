## Using avatar AI in India: consent, language, and the free test ladder

Consent is the first rule of avatar AI in India, and it is not optional: never animate a real person's photo without their explicit permission, and never clone a real person's voice without their explicit permission, because a talking photo and a cloned voice together can impersonate someone, spread misinformation, or harm their reputation, and Indian law, like laws elsewhere, is increasingly treating non-consensual deepfakes seriously. Animate only your own photo, a stock avatar from the tool, or a photo you have explicit permission to use, and for a business video, use a stock avatar or a willing presenter, not a celebrity photo or a customer's photo without permission. For a birthday wish or a family video, use your own photo or a family member's photo with their permission, and tell them it is AI, because a surprise talking photo of someone who did not agree to it is not a gift, it is a violation.

Label every AI avatar video as AI, in the video itself and in its description, because labelling protects your audience, protects the person in the photo, and protects you, and a labelled AI video is a creative video, while an unlabelled AI video is a deception.
