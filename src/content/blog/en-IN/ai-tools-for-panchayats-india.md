Most panchayat offices do not need expensive software. Three free tools cover the daily work: eGramSwaraj for official planning, accounts and payments; Google Forms for grievances and service requests; and ChatGPT Free for drafting notices in Hindi or the local language. All three work in India without a VPN, even on a basic Android phone. Remember: official records belong on government systems, never inside a chatbot.

| Job | Best tool | Free limit |
|---|---|---|
| Plans and accounts | eGramSwaraj | Free |
| Grievances | Google Forms | Free; 500,000 per form |
| Notice drafts | ChatGPT Free | Free chats; uploads capped |

Prices and limits change — re-check the official page before paying or promising a deadline.

## 1. eGramSwaraj — official records and payments

eGramSwaraj is the Ministry of Panchayati Raj portal at egramswaraj.gov.in, launched on 24 April 2020. It covers Gram Panchayat Development Plans, work-based accounting, asset records and PFMS-linked payments to vendors. Over 2.7 lakh panchayats use it, with 2.5 lakh-plus plans uploaded in 2025–26. It is free, but access needs authorised login credentials from the block or district office.

## 2. Google Forms — grievances and service requests

A Google Form linked by QR code at the office or shared on WhatsApp can collect complaints, birth-certificate requests and meeting feedback without paper registers. Responses land in a Google Sheet automatically. It is free with a Google account; Google's help pages now note a limit of 500,000 responses per form, far above what a village office sees. File uploads require respondents to sign in to Google.

## 3. ChatGPT Free — drafting notices in Hindi

ChatGPT drafts meeting notices, circulars and reply letters in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil or other Indian languages in seconds, and can simplify government circulars into plain language. Everyday text chats are free and unlimited on the free plan, subject to abuse safeguards, though file uploads are capped and limits change often. Always read the draft before printing — it can invent dates, amounts and rule numbers.

## Using these tools in a panchayat office

Start with one shared office Google account, not personal accounts, so records survive staff changes. Draft notices in the local language and pin them at the office and on the village notice board. Keep a paper register as backup where internet is patchy. Never type Aadhaar numbers, bank details or personal grievance details into a chatbot. See also our guide to [AI tools for accountants](/blog/ai-tools-for-accountants-india/) for billing work.

## What to avoid

Do not pay for a "panchayat software" package before checking the free government portal. Do not share eGramSwaraj login credentials on WhatsApp. Do not paste personal data into AI chatbots, and never trust an AI draft's dates or figures without checking the original circular.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is eGramSwaraj free for gram panchayats?** Yes. It is a government portal run by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj, and panchayats use it without paying a fee.

**Can villagers file complaints without a smartphone?** Yes — through a written complaint at the office that staff enter into the form, or through a family member's phone.

**Can ChatGPT write notices in Hindi?** Yes, it drafts in Hindi and other Indian languages, but staff should check names, dates and amounts before printing.

**Do these tools need a VPN in India?** No. eGramSwaraj, Google Forms and ChatGPT all work in India on ordinary mobile data or broadband.

## Final verdict

eGramSwaraj for records, Google Forms for grievances, ChatGPT for drafts — free, and enough for most panchayat offices.
