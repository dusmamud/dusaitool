Free checkers handle drafts well, but none matches Turnitin's institutional database. Compare two tools; treat scores as hints, not proof.

1. Turnitin — the university standard for plagiarism and AI writing, only via institutions.
2. Copyleaks — checks plagiarism and AI text together; free scans cover 25,000 characters.
3. GPTZero — AI-text detector with sentence highlights; free plan allows 10,000 words monthly.
4. QuillBot — AI detector plus paraphraser; free checks cover 1,200 words, six scans daily.
5. Scribbr — free AI detector, unlimited 1,200-word checks, no account needed.
6. ZeroGPT — paste-and-scan AI detector; free checks allow 15,000 characters.
7. Quetext — plagiarism reports; free tier allows 500 words, three checks.
8. Grammarly — free plan flags copied text, but full reports need Premium.
9. Originality.ai — for publishers; free checks allow 2,000 words, three scans daily.
10. DupliChecker — no-signup checker with side-by-side comparison; free limit 1,000 words.

**Can a free checker replace Turnitin?** No — tools miss paraphrased text and your university's private database.

## Verdict

Start with Scribbr or Copyleaks, cross-check with GPTZero, and use Turnitin through your college for finals.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
