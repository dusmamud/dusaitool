These ten tools animate images and text in India; limits change.

1. Runway animates images; free: 125 credits.
2. Kling animates people; free: 66 daily credits.
3. Pika makes fast clips; free: credit packs.
4. Animaker builds cartoon explainers; free: 3 downloads.
5. PixVerse makes stylised clips; free: daily credits.
6. Hailuo makes cinematic clips; free: trial credits.
7. Genmo animates text prompts; free: sign-up credits.
8. Powtoon builds animated presentations; free: 720p exports.
9. Steve AI scripts become cartoons; free: few downloads.
10. Vyond makes business animations; free: trial only.

## FAQ

**Which free tool suits beginners best?** Pika for speed; Kling for practice.

## Final verdict

Start with Kling for daily practice and Animaker for cartoons.
