## Hevy — strength training log

Hevy is a free workout tracker for iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and Wear OS, and the simplest way to log strength sessions: build routines, record sets, reps and weights, and track progress week by week. The free plan is usable, with unlimited workout logging, routine building and supersets. The caps: up to 4 saved routines, up to 7 custom exercises, and only 3 months of statistics history. Pro removes all three caps and adds a warm-up calculator and the Hevy Trainer feature. Pro costs $2.99 a month, $23.99 a year, or $74.99 once for lifetime access (checked September 2026).

## Cronometer — diet and micronutrient tracking

Making weight is half of wrestling, and Cronometer is the most detailed free nutrition tracker. Its free Basic plan logs food, exercise and biometrics, tracks up to 84 nutrients, and includes a barcode scanner at no cost. The free limits are fair but real: you see ads, and reports and charts are limited to a 7-day window. Gold, the paid tier, costs $59.99 a year in the US, or $10.99 a month, and adds photo and voice logging, a fasting timer and unlimited history. Check the in-app rupee price before subscribing. The weakness for Indian wrestlers is food coverage: Indian dishes often need manual entry.

## HealthifyMe — Indian food and diet coaching

HealthifyMe is India's most downloaded nutrition app. Its real advantage is the Indian food database, built over a decade, covering regional dishes and home cooking far better than any international app. The honest catch is the price. The free tier in 2026 is very limited: basic manual calorie logging, limited database access, no photo meal logging, no AI nutritionist access and constant premium prompts. Meaningful use needs Pro at ₹999 a month, or ₹4,999 a year on the annual plan, which unlocks the full database, photo logging, AI nutritionist Ria and macro tracking. Pro Plus, with a human coach, costs about ₹1,499 a month. If you can pay, it is the best Indian diet option here.

## Coach's Eye — video analysis for technique

Coach's Eye turns a phone into a video analysis station: record training footage, review it in slow motion, scrub frame by frame, and draw on it to mark positions. Pricing needs a warning, because sources disagree. The Android version is listed as free, with AppBrain reporting a free plan of 7 videos with all annotation tools and a Pro tier for unlimited videos. Older listings from the original maker, TechSmith, describe a VIP membership around $120 a year. The app has changed hands, so trust your app store's price. Test the free version on real akhara footage first before paying anything.

## Using these tools in India

All four tools work in India without a VPN and accept Indian payment through the app stores. Prices change without notice, so re-check the official pricing page before paying or promising a deadline. Tournament information should come from official sources, not apps. The Wrestling Federation of India publishes circulars and schedules on its own website, and United World Wrestling publishes the international calendar. In 2026 the WFI ran events in Gonda and Salem, and the Pro Wrestling League season ran in January. Verify dates and eligibility against the current WFI circular before travelling.

## What to avoid

Do not trust any app that promises a guaranteed weight cut. Rapid weight cutting is dangerous, and no AI tool can make it safe. Never share Aadhaar details, passport numbers or bank details inside a fitness app; tournament forms ask for those, but only through official federation channels. Finally, do not let tracking replace coaching: an app can count calories and log squats, but only a coach can correct a bad habit before it becomes an injury.

## FAQ

**Which AI tool is best for wrestlers in India?**

No single tool covers everything: Hevy for strength, Cronometer for diet, HealthifyMe for Indian food, Coach's Eye for video. Start free.

**Can wrestlers use these tools for free?**

Yes, to a point. Hevy and Cronometer have usable free plans; HealthifyMe's is very limited.

**How do wrestlers track Indian food accurately?**

HealthifyMe has the deepest Indian food database; Cronometer is stronger on nutrients but weaker on Indian dishes. Weigh food for two weeks first.

**Do these apps help with making weight for a tournament?**

They help plan calories and track weight trends, but do not make rapid weight cutting safe. Plan your weight class weeks ahead with your coach.

**Where can I find wrestling tournament dates in India?**

The Wrestling Federation of India's website for national events, and United World Wrestling for international ones. Confirm against the current circular.

## Final verdict

Start free: Hevy for lifts, Cronometer for diet, Coach's Eye for video. Pay for HealthifyMe Pro only if you need deep Indian food data.
