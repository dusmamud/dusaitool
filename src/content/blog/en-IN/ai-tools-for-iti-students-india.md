## The short answer

Yes. Free AI tools can help ITI students understand trade theory in simple language, prepare for practicals, practise interviews, and draft a resume. They cannot replace workshop practice, your instructor, or the syllabus. Keep it simple: one chatbot for doubts, one for Hindi, and the official portal for openings.

| Tool | Best for | Free tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | Doubts, mock tests, resumes | Free; uploads limited |
| Gemini | Hindi help, Android | Free; Google account |
| Copilot | Windows, summaries | Free; Microsoft account |

## 1. ChatGPT

ChatGPT is the best starting point. Ask it to explain a trade concept simply in English or Hindi, list viva questions, or make a mock test. The free plan offers unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse guardrails, but uploads, images, voice, and deep research are limited. Plus costs about ₹1,999 a month.

## 2. Gemini

Gemini is Google's AI assistant and a strong choice on Android phones. It answers in Hindi and several other Indian languages. Photograph a page or diagram and ask what it shows, or summarise a chapter. The basic app is free with a Google account, though daily limits apply.

## 3. Microsoft Copilot

Copilot suits students on a Windows laptop. The free version runs at copilot.microsoft.com and can summarise web pages and PDFs, explain concepts, draft emails, and create images. Note that free Copilot does not work inside Word, Excel, and PowerPoint; that needs a Microsoft 365 subscription.

## Using AI tools in India

Before a practical, ask the chatbot to list the tools, safety steps, and common mistakes for that job, then verify each step with your instructor. For apprenticeships, register on the official NAPS portal at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in. Sources disagree on current stipend figures, so check the portal itself. Never pay anyone promising a job.

## What to avoid

Do not copy AI answers word for word into assignments or practical records; instructors can spot it. Do not trust AI for exact exam dates, cut-offs, safety voltages, or wiring values without checking the official syllabus. Never share Aadhaar, bank details, or passwords with a chatbot.

## FAQ

**Can AI tools replace ITI practical training?**

No. Fitting, wiring, welding, and machining are learned by doing them in the workshop. AI can explain a procedure and help you revise, but use it around practicals, not instead of them.

**Which free AI tool is best for Hindi-medium ITI students?**

Gemini, for its good Hindi support on Android phones. ChatGPT also handles Hindi well, so try both with the same question and keep the one you understand better.

**Can AI help me get an apprenticeship after ITI?**

Partly. It can help with a resume, interview practice, and finding openings, but you must apply through the official NAPS portal or the employer's own process. No AI tool can guarantee a seat.

**Are these tools really free in India?**

Yes. All three work in India without a VPN, but each limits some features, such as uploads or image creation. Prices and limits change, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## Final verdict

Start free with ChatGPT and Gemini for study help, and use Copilot on Windows; check the NAPS portal for real apprenticeships.
