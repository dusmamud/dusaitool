Both cost nothing to start and work in India without a VPN. Pay for AI video analysis only when you film matches regularly. This article is general information only, not professional coaching or medical advice. Prices change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## Balltime — AI video stats for volleyball

Balltime, now a Hudl product, is the closest thing to a true AI volleyball analyst here. Upload a match video and it tags every ball contact and player, cuts dead time between rallies, and turns footage into stats and highlights. The Player plan is about $20 a month for 15 hours of video, with a 14-day free trial; team pricing is on request. It runs on iPhone and iPad only — no Android app was found. Use it if you already film full matches.

## Koach — line-ups, live scoring and drills

Koach is a volleyball coaching app built by a volleyball coach, and it is honest about what it is not: it has no AI and never looks at your footage. You score each point live in two taps — who scored, and how: attack, block, ace or opponent error. By the first time-out you see your momentum and weakest rotation. It works offline in the gym and syncs later. It also plans practice: line-ups in minutes and 200+ animated drills. Players and parents use it free; coaches get 14 days free, then about €2.99 a month (Coach) or €9.99 (Club).

## Coach's Eye — frame-by-frame technique review

Coach's Eye turns your phone into a video analysis station for one skill at a time. Film a serve or spike, step through it frame by frame, and draw lines and angles to show where the arm, jump or landing went wrong. The free tier covers a small number of videos with all drawing tools included, so test it on a real practice before paying. Paid plans remove the cap; check the price inside your own store first, as it varies.

## TeamSnap — schedules, rosters and messages

TeamSnap is the biggest team-management app here, used by more than 24 million users across hundreds of sports including volleyball. It handles schedules, rosters, availability replies, messaging and fee collection. Its free plan covers scheduling, messaging, assignments, payments and rosters under 15 players, plus a 21-day trial of premium features. Paid plans are priced in dollars, so use the free plan while your roster stays small.

## Spond — free team organisation

Spond covers scheduling, attendance, line-ups, fees and messaging — and its core app is free. It works on iOS and Android, and its listing says it saves a coach two hours a week on admin. There is no roster cap in the free tier, which makes it the better default for Indian college and club teams with large squads and no budget. Pair it with Koach for scoring or Coach's Eye for technique.

## Using these tools in India

Start with the free tier and one match, not a subscription. Film one full match on a phone on a tripod, score it live in Koach, and share clips in Spond. That evening tells you whether your team will keep filming. Courts often have patchy Wi-Fi, so favour offline tools: Koach scores offline and syncs later. For tournaments, check the official prospectus: fees, dates and eligibility come from the organiser, not an app. Skip automatic camera systems such as Veo for now; they cost far more than any app here. ## What to avoid

Do not buy a yearly plan before a one-month test with your real team. Do not trust auto-generated stats without watching the rallies — AI tagging misses touches and struggles with poor indoor light. Do not film other players, especially minors, without consent, and do not upload footage to the cloud without checking who can see it. Do not build a training plan from an app alone with pain or a past injury; that needs a qualified coach or clinician.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool is best for volleyball players in India?**

Koach plus Spond — scoring, rotations and drills in Koach, free team chat in Spond. Add Balltime only if you film matches weekly.

**Is there a free AI volleyball analysis app?**

Balltime offers a 14-day free trial, and Coach's Eye analyses a few videos free. No fully free, unlimited AI video analysis app for volleyball could be verified.

**Can I analyse my volleyball serve with my phone?**

Yes. Film the serve, open it in Coach's Eye, step through it frame by frame and draw the arm and landing angles. A coach watching the clip with you is worth more than any app score.

**Which app is best for managing a volleyball team?**

Spond, for most Indian teams: free, on Android and iPhone, handling schedules, attendance, line-ups and payments. TeamSnap is the alternative if your team is under 15 players.
