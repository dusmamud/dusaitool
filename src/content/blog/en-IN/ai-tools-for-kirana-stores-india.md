A kirana store needs three jobs done well: fast billing, stock that never surprises you, and an udhaar ledger customers trust. For most single-counter shops, the best start is free: Vyapar for billing and stock, Khatabook for credit, and WhatsApp Business for orders. Pay only when free limits pinch; re-check official prices first.

| Job | Best free start | Later cost |
|---|---|---|
| Billing and stock | Vyapar (Android) | Paid desktop plans |
| Udhaar ledger | Khatabook | Core free; paid add-ons |
| Full accounting, GST | TallyPrime Silver | ₹22,500 + GST, or ₹750/month |
| Orders and delivery | WhatsApp Business | Free app; paid API for big sellers |

## Vyapar: billing and stock in one app

Vyapar is the strongest all-rounder for a kirana counter. It makes GST and non-GST bills in seconds, scans barcodes, and cuts stock automatically with every sale, so shelf counts stay honest without a nightly register. It works offline when the connection drops and shares bills on WhatsApp. The Android app is free for life for basic billing and inventory, per Vyapar's own pages and independent reviews. Desktop and premium features are paid; confirm the price on vyaparapp.in before paying. Android and Windows only — no iOS app.

## Khatabook: the digital udhaar bahi khata

Udhaar is where kirana trust lives, and Khatabook replaces the paper bahi khata with a ledger customers can follow. Add a customer by name and phone number, record credit given and payments received, and the app keeps the running balance. Automatic WhatsApp or SMS reminders carry the outstanding amount, and customers can pay by UPI QR code. It works in 12 regional languages, and offline entries sync later. The core khata is free, and Khatabook says it is used by more than 10 million shopkeepers. Enter every udhaar sale the same day.

## TallyPrime: the accountant's choice, at a price

Almost every Indian accountant already knows TallyPrime. It covers ledgers, GST returns, e-invoicing and inventory in one desktop package, works offline, and produces the reports a CA wants. Silver, for one user on one PC, costs ₹22,500 + 18% GST one-time, or ₹750 a month on rent; Gold costs ₹67,500 + GST, or ₹2,250 a month. One year of support is included; renewal then costs ₹4,500 a year for Silver and ₹13,500 for Gold, plus GST. For a counter that only needs billing, that is heavy — buy it when GST filing and your CA matter most.

## WhatsApp Business: orders and delivery without a website

The free WhatsApp Business app turns your customers' messaging habit into an ordering system. You get a business profile, a catalogue of up to 500 items with photos and prices, quick replies, greeting and away messages, and labels such as "New order", "Paid" and "Delivered" to track each order. Customers send their list in chat; you confirm stock, collect UPI or cash on delivery, and send it out with your own delivery boy or a local courier. Know its limits: the free app does not process payments or sync stock with your billing app, and broadcasts are capped at 256 contacts. The paid Platform is for big sellers, not small shops.

## How to use these tools in an Indian kirana store

Start with one phone and one routine. Put your 100 fastest-selling items into Vyapar with MRP, barcode and a low-stock level, and bill every sale through it for a week, even cash sales. Move the udhaar book into Khatabook the same week, and tell regulars the ledger has gone digital before the first WhatsApp reminder goes out. Put your top 20 items in the WhatsApp catalogue with honest prices and a delivery note. At closing, check sales, udhaar collected and pending deliveries against the cash in the drawer. Back up weekly — a lost phone must never mean a lost ledger. One more option: myBillBook covers iPhone and web too, but sources disagree on whether its free tier still exists, so check before depending on it. ## What to avoid

Do not buy a paid plan in the first month — free tiers exist so you can test the app on your counter. Do not run billing in one app and stock in another; the two will disagree within a week. Do not promise city-wide delivery before you have a delivery boy or courier rate; start with your neighbourhood and a minimum order. And never trust a price in a blog, including this one — check the official pricing page on the day you pay.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which single app should a small kirana store start with?**
Vyapar — billing and stock are the daily jobs, and its Android app is free for the basics.

**Is Khatabook really free?**
Yes, the core khata is free; only optional add-on services cost money.

**Do I need GST billing software if my turnover is small?**
No — non-GST bills are enough below the threshold; switch to GSTR-ready software once registered.

**Can WhatsApp Business replace a delivery app?**
Yes for a neighbourhood shop, but pair it with a billing app — it cannot take payments or sync stock.

**Is TallyPrime worth ₹22,500 for a kirana store?**
Only if GST filing and your CA matter more than counter speed; otherwise free Vyapar is enough.

Start free with Vyapar for billing and Khatabook for udhaar, plus WhatsApp Business for orders. Pay only when the free limits pinch.
