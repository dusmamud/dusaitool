## The direct answer

A modeling agency in India runs on trust and timing: brands and photographers need the right face at the right time, models need castings that are real and paid on time, and the agency needs to remember who fits which brief, who is available this week, and who was paid for the last shoot. Most of this work happens on WhatsApp and Instagram, with portfolios shared as links and casting calls sent as messages, which works until a casting is missed or a model's photos are lost in a chat. The direct answer is simple: use Instagram for portfolios and discovery, Canva for comp cards and casting posts, Google Sheets to track models, castings and payments, ChatGPT for casting briefs and client messages, and WhatsApp Business for bookings and confirmations.

These tools work because they match how Indian fashion work actually happens. A brand manager scrolls Instagram to find a look, a photographer asks for a comp card on WhatsApp, and a model checks a casting call on their phone the same evening. A clean portfolio with real photos wins more work than a flashy profile with edited pictures that do not match the model in person. But there is a hard rule that matters more here than anywhere else: never ask a model, especially a young one, for inappropriate photos, never share a model's personal details publicly, and never promise work in exchange for money.

## 1. Instagram: the portfolio every brand checks first

Instagram is the portfolio for Indian modeling, because it is where brands, photographers and casting directors already look. A model's profile with real photos, a clear bio, and recent work tells a client more in thirty seconds than a long PDF. For an agency, Instagram is also discovery: a good post can bring a new model to your attention, and a casting post can reach hundreds of models in a day. The app is free, and only optional ads cost money, so check the official page before spending anything.

How to start: make sure every model's profile has real, recent photos, height and city in the bio, and a link to their full portfolio. Watch out for: never post a model's photo without their written permission, never share a model's phone number publicly, and never use heavily edited photos that do not match the model in person.

## 2. Canva: comp cards and casting posts that look professional

Canva helps an agency make comp cards, casting calls and rate cards that look professional without hiring a designer. A comp card is a one-page card with a model's best photos, height, measurements and contact, and a clean one gets a model shortlisted faster than a messy chat message with ten photos. Canva's free plan has templates for cards and social posts, and you can add your agency name and the casting details, then share the card on WhatsApp or Instagram. The free plan covers most templates and basic editing, and Pro is paid, so check current pricing on the official page before upgrading.

How to start: make one comp card template for your agency, and use it for every model so clients recognise your format. Watch out for: use only real photos of the model, with their permission, and never add measurements or details the model did not agree to share.

## 3. Google Sheets: models, castings and payments in one sheet

Google Sheets is the booking book for a modeling agency. One sheet lists each model with their city, height, portfolio link and availability, and a second sheet lists each casting with the client, date, models sent, models selected and payment status. That record stops the two most common problems in an agency: sending the same model to two castings on the same day, and forgetting to pay a model for a shoot. The sheet works on any phone, and it is free with a Google account, so check the official page for paid Workspace features you probably do not need yet.

How to start: make one sheet with this month's castings and the models sent to each, and check it before sending anyone to a new casting. Watch out for: never store a model's full address, ID or bank details in a shared sheet; keep only what the casting actually needs, and protect the sheet properly.

## 4. ChatGPT: casting briefs, client replies and model messages

ChatGPT handles the writing a modeling agency does every day: a casting brief for a brand, a polite reply to a client asking for more options, a message to models about a casting on Saturday, or a thank-you note after a shoot. Give it the facts, such as the brand, the look needed, the date and the pay, and it drafts a short message in Hindi or English that sounds professional, not pushy. It can also help write a model's bio or a portfolio introduction. The free plan is enough for daily writing, and paid plans add more features, so check current pricing on the official page.

How to start: ask it to draft a casting brief template with the brand, look, date, location and pay, then use that same template for every casting. Watch out for: AI can invent a date, a rate or a requirement, so check every detail before sending a brief to models or a client.

## 5. WhatsApp Business: bookings, confirmations and call times

WhatsApp Business is the booking desk for an Indian modeling agency, because castings and shoot confirmations already happen on WhatsApp. A client messages a brief, the agency sends shortlisted comp cards, and the model gets a confirmation with the date, time, location and what to bring. The Business app adds quick replies for common questions, labels for clients, photographers and models, and a catalogue for your services and rates, so no booking gets lost. The app is free, and only mobile data or Wi-Fi costs apply, so check the official page.

How to start: confirm every booking in writing with the date, time, location, pay and what the model should bring, and send a reminder the day before. Watch out for: never share a model's phone number or personal details with a client who does not need them, and never confirm a booking verbally without a written message.

## Using AI in India: modeling agencies

Modeling in India has its own shape, and the tools should fit it. First, trust: models and parents worry about safety and fake castings, so an agency that communicates clearly, pays on time and never asks for money for work earns a good name fast. Second, diversity: Indian brands need models of many looks, ages and regions, so keep portfolios that reflect that range instead of one narrow look. Third, cities: most work is in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru and other metros, so track which models are in which city and available this week.

## What to avoid

Avoid asking any model for inappropriate photos, and avoid any casting that asks a model to pay money for work, because that is a scam. Avoid sharing a model's phone number, address or personal details publicly or with clients who do not need them, and avoid posting a model's photos without written permission. Avoid promising a model work you cannot deliver, and avoid using heavily edited photos that do not match the model in person. Avoid trusting AI for casting dates, rates or requirements without checking them, and avoid working with clients who refuse to confirm a booking in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best free tool for a modeling agency in India?**
Instagram is the most important free tool, because brands and photographers already discover models there. Pair it with Canva for comp cards, Google Sheets for castings and payments, and WhatsApp Business for bookings.

**What is a comp card and how do I make one for free?**
A comp card is a one-page card with a model's best photos, height, measurements and contact. Canva's free plan has card templates, and you can add your agency name and the model's real details, then share it on WhatsApp or Instagram.

**How do I keep track of castings and model payments?**
Use Google Sheets: one sheet for models with city, height and availability, and one sheet for castings with client, date, models sent and payment status. Check it before sending anyone to a new casting.

**Can ChatGPT write casting briefs for my agency?**
Yes, it can draft briefs, client replies and model messages in Hindi or English. Give it the real brand, look, date, location and pay, and check every detail before sending, because AI can invent a date or a rate.

**Is it safe for models to share photos on Instagram?**
Yes, if photos are professional, shared with the model's written permission, and no phone number or address is public. Models should never send inappropriate photos to anyone, and an agency should never ask for them.

**How do I spot a fake casting call?**
Be suspicious of any casting that asks a model to pay money, meet in a private place, or send inappropriate photos. A real casting confirms the brand, date, location and pay in writing, and a real agency never charges a model for work.
