Commerce students in India juggle three kinds of work: accounting practice, long theory notes in business law and economics, and exam prep for B.Com or CA Foundation. AI tools help with all three, but only when each tool does the job it is good at. A chatbot explains a journal entry well but gives poor GST rates, and copying its answers unchecked is risky.

The short answer: study from your own notes with NotebookLM, now called Gemini Notebook, because it answers only from sources you upload. Use Gemini or ChatGPT free to explain concepts and work through accounting problems. Use Quizlet to drill definitions, formulas and provisions. Written for India: B.Com, BBA, M.Com, CA Foundation; English and Hindi medium; a normal phone; UPI; no VPN. Prices change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying. ## 1. NotebookLM: study from your own material

NotebookLM is Google's source-grounded study tool, now branded Gemini Notebook. You upload class PDFs, scanned notes, a textbook chapter or a YouTube lecture link, and it answers only from those sources, citing the exact passage. The free tier gives up to 100 notebooks and 50 sources in each. Sources disagree on exact daily chat counts, because Google replaced fixed daily caps with a compute-based budget in 2026.

## 2. Gemini: the everyday doubt-clearing tutor

Gemini is Google's AI assistant on the web, Android and iOS, already on most Android phones and working in Hindi and eight other Indian languages. Ask it anything from your syllabus. The free plan costs ₹0 with a Google account. Since October 2026 the free tier runs the lighter Flash-Lite model, with limits that refresh every five hours. Paid steps are AI Plus at ₹399 a month and AI Pro at ₹1,950 a month. Students aged 18 and over can claim AI Plus free for one year before 31 December 2026. Check every number against your textbook. See also [AI tools for exam prep](/blog/ai-tools-for-exam-prep-india/).

## 3. ChatGPT: best for accounting problems

ChatGPT is the strongest general chatbot for step-by-step accounting work. Paste a problem, ask it to show every journal entry with narration, then ask it to rework the same problem with different numbers so you practise rather than memorise. It also turns long chapters into one-page summaries. The free plan costs ₹0. OpenAI's own pages describe free as unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse guardrails, with separate limits on file uploads, images, voice and deep research. Older guides quote ten messages every five hours, but OpenAI's current pages no longer say that. Go costs ₹399 a month, Plus ₹1,999. Then solve it yourself on paper.

## 4. Quizlet: drill what exams actually test

Accounting and law exams reward memory as much as understanding: definitions, formats, section numbers, formulas, dates. Quizlet turns those into flashcards, and its Learn and Test modes drill you with spaced repetition until answers come without thinking. Build your own sets. The free tier covers creating and studying flashcards and the Match game, but Learn mode is capped at about five rounds per set and Test mode at one practice test per set, with ads. Quizlet Plus costs about $7.99 a month, or $35.99 a year, and adds AI tools, offline study and more rounds. Quizlet retired its Q-Chat AI tutor in June 2025, so do not buy Plus expecting a chatbot tutor. Twenty cards a day beats two hundred the night before the exam.

## Using these tools in India

All four tools work in India without a VPN, on a normal phone, and accept UPI where Indian pricing exists. Study groups work well too — one friend makes the Quizlet set, another uploads shared notes to NotebookLM.

## What to avoid

Do not upload question papers, answer sheets or personal documents to any AI tool, and never paste your roll number, phone number or bank details into a chatbot. Do not trust any AI tool for current tax rates, GST slabs or exam dates; those change by notification. Do not copy AI-written assignments word for word — universities and ICAI treat that as academic misconduct.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool is best for commerce students in India?**

NotebookLM is the best starting point because it studies only from your own notes, so its answers stay inside your syllabus. Add Gemini or ChatGPT free, and Quizlet.

**Can AI tools help with CA Foundation preparation?**

Yes, for practice and revision, not as a replacement for ICAI study material. Use a chatbot for problems, NotebookLM for official material, and Quizlet for provisions and formulas. Verify answers against ICAI material.

**Are these tools really free for students in India?**

The free tiers are real: NotebookLM gives 100 notebooks, Gemini costs ₹0, ChatGPT covers everyday chats, and Quizlet covers flashcards.

**Can I use AI tools in Hindi for commerce studies?**

Yes. Gemini works in Hindi and other Indian languages, ChatGPT handles Hinglish well, and NotebookLM accepts Hindi sources too. For English-medium exams, practise the English terms as well.

**Should I pay for ChatGPT Plus or Gemini Pro as a student?**

Almost certainly not at first. Try the free tiers for a month and note which limit blocks you. If eligible, claim the free year of Google AI Plus first.

## Final verdict

Start free with NotebookLM for your own notes, Gemini for doubts, and Quizlet for revision. Pay nothing until a free limit actually blocks you.
