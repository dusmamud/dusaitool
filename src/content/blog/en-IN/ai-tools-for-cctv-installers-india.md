A CCTV installer in India does four jobs at once: surveying sites, quoting prices, installing on schedule, and remembering every AMC visit. A free chatbot drafts quotes, a phone app maps the survey, a free GST tool handles bills, a board tracks each job, and WhatsApp Business manages AMC renewals. Prices and limits change, so re-check the official pricing page before paying or promising a deadline.

## ChatGPT — quotations, checklists and customer messages

ChatGPT turns rough site notes into a clean quotation, survey checklist, AMC letter, or message in Hindi, Hinglish or English. Give it the cameras, cables, DVR model and rates, ask for an itemised quote with GST separate — then check every line, because it can miscalculate totals. Free text chat is unlimited on GPT-5.6 Luna since August 2026, though some tests still cite older caps, so sources disagree. Never paste customer addresses or DVR logins into it.

## magicplan — floor plans and camera positions on site

magicplan turns your phone into a survey tool: walk the rooms and it builds a 2D or 3D floor plan for marking camera positions, cable routes and the DVR location, with photos and notes attached. The free tier gives 2 complete projects with full features. Paid prices disagree across sources — Sketch about $10 to $13 a month in some listings, team plans from $250 a month in others — check the official page first. LiDAR needs an iPhone Pro; on Android, verify measurements with a tape.

## Zoho Invoice — GST quotations, bills and AMC billing

Zoho Invoice is free for Indian businesses and handles the money side: GST-compliant quotations and invoices, UPI, card and net-banking payment links, automated reminders, and recurring invoices, which are exactly what an annual maintenance contract needs. Free limits are 500 invoices a year, 2 users and 3 projects, invoices carry "Powered by Zoho Invoice" branding, and Zoho may delete data after 180 days of inactivity, so keep your own backups.

## Trello — tracking every installation and AMC visit

Trello is a simple board that stops jobs slipping through the cracks. Make one board with lists such as Enquiry, Survey Done, Quoted, Installation and AMC, and one card per site, holding the checklist, due date, technician and site photos. The free plan allows 10 boards per workspace, 10 collaborators, unlimited cards, 250 automation runs a month and 10 MB per file. Standard costs about $5 per user a month on annual billing.

## WhatsApp Business — customers, catalogues and AMC reminders

Most CCTV customers in India live on WhatsApp, so the free WhatsApp Business app is where enquiries, updates and AMC reminders belong. Set up a business profile, a catalogue of camera packages with prices, labels such as Quoted and AMC Due, and quick replies. Broadcast lists reach up to 256 contacts each, but only people who have saved your number receive them, so ask customers to save it at handover. Send one polite reminder before expiry and one after; spam reports can get a number banned.

## Using these tools in India

All five tools run on Android, and only magicplan's LiDAR mode needs an expensive iPhone. Quote cable length, DVR hard-disk size and a UPS separately so the customer sees what they are paying for. ## What to avoid

Do not promise "AI" features a camera does not actually have: person detection, number-plate reading and face recognition are features of specific models, not of every CCTV camera. Do not upload site photos showing addresses, or any DVR password, to a free chatbot. Do not quote cable lengths from memory; a wrong estimate eats your margin.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which free tool writes a CCTV quotation fastest?** ChatGPT for the draft, Zoho Invoice for the final document. Dictate the cameras, cables and rates, ask for an itemised quote with GST separate, then check every total.

**Can I do a site survey with just my phone?** Yes, for most homes, shops and small offices. magicplan builds a floor plan from your phone camera and lets you mark camera spots and cable routes, with 2 free projects.

**How do I remember every AMC visit?** Put each AMC on a Trello card with a due date a week before expiry, label the customer AMC Due in WhatsApp Business, and send one reminder before expiry and one after.

**Is WhatsApp Business really free?** The app is free, including the catalogue, labels, quick replies and broadcast lists of up to 256 contacts. The WhatsApp Business API is a different, paid service with per-message charges.

**Do I need to tell customers about cameras and data rules?** Discuss camera placement, recording, viewing access and signage with the customer first, and check the current official rules, including India's data protection law. This article is general information only, not legal advice.

Start with ChatGPT for quotes, magicplan for surveys, Zoho Invoice for GST billing, Trello for jobs and WhatsApp Business for AMC reminders. It costs nothing to begin and covers survey to AMC renewal.
