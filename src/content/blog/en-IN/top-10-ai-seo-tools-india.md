For Indian sites, start free: these ten AI SEO tools cover keywords, content scoring, audits and tracking, without a VPN.

1. Semrush: AI keyword and competitor research; free account allows 10 reports daily.
2. Ahrefs: backlinks and competitor analysis; free for verified sites, 1,000 rows.
3. Google Search Console: free clicks, indexing and error reports; no usage cap.
4. Surfer: scores drafts against ranking pages; no free plan, sources disagree on trial.
5. Frase: builds briefs and scores drafts; 7-day free trial, no card needed.
6. NeuronWriter: budget content scoring against ranking pages; 7-day free trial.
7. Scalenut: creates and optimises AI-search articles and keyword clusters; 7-day trial.
8. Clearscope: simple content grading for editorial teams; 14-day free trial only.
9. MarketMuse: plans topics across a whole site; free plan, 10 queries monthly.
10. ChatGPT: brainstorms keywords, outlines and meta descriptions; free unlimited everyday chats.

## FAQ

**Which AI SEO tool should Indian beginners try first?** Google Search Console, then Semrush free, because both cost nothing.

## Final verdict

Start with Search Console and Semrush free, add Frase only when paid scoring earns its cost.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
