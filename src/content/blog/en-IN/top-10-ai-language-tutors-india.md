These ten AI tutors teach speaking, pronunciation and grammar.

1. Duolingo: 40+ languages; free with ads.
2. Talkpal: 57+ languages; 10 free minutes daily.
3. ELSA Speak: English pronunciation; 2 free lessons daily.
4. Speak: real conversations; 7-day trial only.
5. Babbel: structured grammar; first lesson free.
6. Busuu: community feedback; free with ads.
7. Memrise: vocabulary via native videos; free.
8. Mondly: 41 languages; free daily lesson.
9. Praktika: avatar tutors; 3 avatars free.
10. Gliglish: speaking; free 10 minutes daily.

## FAQ

**Which free tutor suits beginners best?** Duolingo for daily lessons; Talkpal for speaking practice.

## Final verdict

Start with Duolingo, then add Talkpal for speaking — both free in India.
