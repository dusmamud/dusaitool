A PhD in India means dozens of papers, messy notes and a thesis deadline. Three free tools cover most of it: Zotero organises papers and citations, Elicit searches the literature, and NotebookLM turns your papers into searchable notes. All three work in India without a VPN. Start free, and re-check official pricing before paying.

| Tool | Best for | Free plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zotero | Citations and paper library | Free app, 300 MB cloud sync |
| Elicit | Literature search and summaries | Free search, 2 reports a month |
| NotebookLM | Notes and Q&A on your papers | Free, 50 sources per notebook |

## 1. Zotero — citations and your paper library

Zotero is a free, open-source reference manager. Its browser connector saves papers in one click, and it inserts citations into Word and Google Docs in thousands of styles. The app is free forever; only cloud storage beyond 300 MB costs extra, from about $20 a year for 2 GB. Local storage is unlimited. See also our [AI PDF tools](/blog/ai-pdf-tools-summarize-india/) guide.

## 2. Elicit — finding and summarising papers

Elicit searches a database of more than 125 million papers from a plain-language research question, then summarises findings and extracts methods into comparison tables. The free Basic plan gives unlimited search and summaries, but only two automated reports a month. Coverage is strongest in empirical fields, and its help centre warns outputs must be checked.

## 3. NotebookLM — notes and Q&A on your own papers

NotebookLM, now renamed Gemini Notebook by Google, answers using only your uploaded sources, citing the exact passage. The free plan allows 100 notebooks with up to 50 sources each, 50 chats a day and three audio overviews a day. Upload thesis chapters and key papers, then ask it to compare methods. Treat its summaries as study aids, never as citable references.

## Using these tools in India

All three run in India with no VPN. Zotero works fully offline, which helps on patchy campus Wi-Fi. NotebookLM needs a Google account; Elicit accepts international cards. Check for university institutional access before paying. Prices are usually in dollars, so the rupee cost moves with exchange rates.

## What to avoid

Never paste an AI-generated citation into your thesis without opening the original paper. Do not upload unpublished data or a supervisor's draft to any cloud tool without checking university rules. Avoid yearly plans in your first month; use the free tier until you hit a real limit.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool is best for a PhD literature review?**
Elicit, because it searches millions of papers and builds comparison tables. Pair it with Zotero to store and cite what you find.

**Can I use these tools for free as a PhD student in India?**
Yes. Zotero is free, Elicit's search is free, and NotebookLM's free plan covers most thesis work. Paid plans only add volume or storage.

**Is it safe to cite AI-generated summaries in a thesis?**
No. Summaries can misread tables, so verify against the original paper and cite the paper, never the AI tool.

**Do these tools work without a VPN in India?**
Yes, all three work on a normal Indian connection, and Zotero works fully offline once your library is downloaded.

## Final verdict

Zotero for citations, Elicit for literature search, NotebookLM for notes — start free, verify every AI claim.
