Zoho Books and HelloBooks lead on free GST filing in India; Wave suits freelancers, Xero suits growing firms.

## Top 10

1. Zoho Books: GST invoicing, AI receipt scans, free below ₹25 lakh.
2. HelloBooks: AI categorisation, free GST filing for one GSTIN.
3. Wave: free unlimited invoices, bookkeeping; bank import needs Pro.
4. QuickBooks: Intuit Assist AI answers questions; 30-day trial, limited India GST.
5. Xero: JAX AI assistant, unlimited users; 30-day trial only.
6. FreshBooks: AI expense categorisation for freelancers; 30-day trial only.
7. Vyapar: GST billing, stock for shops; basic Android app free for life.
8. TallyPrime: India's standard GST ledger; 7-day trial, no free plan.
9. myBillBook: mobile GST billing, 15 free invoices monthly; listings differ, check first.
10. Dext: AI reads receipts into Xero, QuickBooks; 14-day trial, no card.

## FAQ

**Which free AI accounting tool is best for GST in India?** Zoho Books or HelloBooks — both file GST returns free, within their limits.

## Final verdict

Start with Zoho Books or HelloBooks for free GST filing; add Dext when receipts pile up.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
