For an Indian blood bank, the best starting stack is e-RaktKosh for official stock and donor records, RAKT for day-to-day donor, camp and inventory work, and WhatsApp Business for donor reminders. Together they cover donors, camps, stock and hospital alerts — without a VPN or a large budget.

| Tool | Best for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| e-RaktKosh | Official stock, camps and donor records | Free |
| RAKT | Donor-to-issue workflow, camps, expiry alerts | Paid — check site |
| WhatsApp Business | Donor reminders and camp alerts | Free app |

## e-RaktKosh — the official backbone

e-RaktKosh is the Government of India's centralised blood bank system, built by C-DAC under the National Health Mission, with over 3,800 blood banks registered. Banks update stock by blood group, list camps and keep donor records, while hospitals and patients search live availability nearby. It is free and needs no VPN. Licensed centres are expected to register, but figures are only as fresh as each bank's last update.

## RAKT — donor-to-issue workflow

RAKT is an Indian cloud blood bank system used by 200+ blood centres. It covers donor registration, grouping, screening, component preparation, inventory, crossmatch and issue, with registers generated from live records instead of re-keyed at month-end. Its RAKT Pass app registers camp donors on a tablet, syncs stock with e-RaktKosh and links ABHA donor identity. Pricing is not published — ask for a quote and re-check the official pricing page before budgeting.

## WhatsApp Business — donor reminders that get read

WhatsApp Business is the free app most Indian donors already use, so reminders and camp alerts actually get seen. A bank can send camp invites, thank donors, and remind repeat donors when eligible again — donors must wait about three months between whole-blood donations. The free app caps a broadcast list at 256 contacts, and messages need the donor's opt-in. Bulk messaging needs the paid WhatsApp Business API.

## Using these tools in India

Start with e-RaktKosh registration, then add WhatsApp reminders for repeat donors — that pair costs nothing. Add RAKT when camp volume or paperwork grows. Keep donor data private under India's DPDP Act, 2023, take consent before messaging, and never share a donor's name or phone number publicly. For hospital coordination, see our guide on [AI tools for hospitals](/blog/ai-tools-for-hospitals-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not promise a unit based on a portal listing without calling the bank first — stock changes fast. Do not buy donor phone lists, spam donors, or store health data in shared spreadsheets. Never let an AI tool decide donor eligibility; only trained staff and medical screening can do that.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which is the best free tool for an Indian blood bank?**
e-RaktKosh — the official, free government system for stock, camps and donor records, which licensed centres are expected to use.

**Can AI predict blood demand?**
Some systems flag near-expiry stock and shortage patterns, but forecasts are only as good as the data entered. Treat them as prompts for staff, not guarantees.

**How do I remind donors without spamming them?**
Use WhatsApp Business with opt-in only, message repeat donors near their next eligible date, and keep messages short and useful.

**Do these tools work without a VPN in India?**
Yes — all three work on a normal Indian connection, no VPN needed.

## Verdict

Start with free e-RaktKosh and WhatsApp Business, add RAKT when daily volume justifies a paid system.
