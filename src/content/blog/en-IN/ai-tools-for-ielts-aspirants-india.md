The best free starting point for IELTS preparation in India is the British Council's IELTS Ready: short mock tests with AI feedback and two full mock tests at no cost. For guided courses with teacher-marked Writing and Speaking, E2Language is the strongest paid option, and Cambridge Write & Improve is the cheapest writing checker. No tool can guarantee a band score.

## British Council IELTS Ready

IELTS Ready is the British Council's own preparation package, and the safest start because the British Council co-owns IELTS. The free tier includes short mock tests with estimated scores and AI feedback, plus two full mocks with Reading and Listening scores and model answers. Book your test with the British Council and Premium, with 40 full mocks, is free.

## E2Language

E2Language (E2 IELTS) is a coach-based course where human teachers mark your Writing and Speaking, its main difference from AI-only apps. It offers video lessons, live classes, and full and mini mock tests designed with input from former examiners, with tutor feedback usually within 48 hours. You get a 14-day free trial with no credit card, then paid packages from about US$69 for Express.

## Cambridge Write & Improve

Write & Improve, from Cambridge University Press & Assessment — another IELTS co-owner — is the best dedicated IELTS writing checker. Pick a task, write under timed conditions, and get a band score in seconds, with feedback to revise. The Academic workbook has 60 tasks and General Training has 40; each costs £4.50 a month, with 10 free Academic tasks to try first.

## Using these tools in India

All three tools work in India without a VPN. The IELTS test fee in India is around ₹19,000, so prepare well before booking. E2Language charges in US dollars and Write & Improve in pounds, so an international card helps. Prices and free limits change often — re-check the official pricing page before paying. ## What to avoid

Do not trust ads promising a guaranteed Band 8 — nobody can promise that. AI band scores are estimates, not examiner marks. Do not memorise model answers; examiners spot memorised language. Never pay for a full course before trying its free tier.

## FAQ

**Can AI predict my real IELTS band?**

No. They estimate your band from official criteria, and some score stricter than real examiners. Treat it as a guide to weak areas, not a promise.

**Which tool is best for Speaking?**

IELTS Ready gives free AI feedback on short speaking mocks; E2Language adds human teacher feedback. Combining both usually works best.

**Is IELTS Ready really free?**

Yes, the basic tier is free, with short mocks and two full mocks. Premium, with 40 full mocks, is free only if you book with the British Council.

**How many mock tests before the exam?**

Four to six full mocks under exam conditions, plus shorter practice in your weakest skill. Review every mistake — quality beats quantity.

## Final verdict

Start free with IELTS Ready, add Write & Improve for writing, and pay for E2 only if you need teacher feedback.
