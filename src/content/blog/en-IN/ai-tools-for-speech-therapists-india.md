This article is general information only, not professional advice. It does not replace your clinical judgement, a client's treating doctor, or your professional body's guidance. An AI tool can suggest an exercise, but it cannot examine a client, diagnose a disorder, or decide a treatment plan.

## 1. Constant Therapy — best for adult speech and language exercises

Constant Therapy is an exercise app for people recovering from stroke or brain injury, or living with aphasia, apraxia or dementia. It offers over a million exercises across 90-plus areas and adjusts difficulty as the client improves. For India, it supports English (India) alongside US, UK and Australian English. The Clinician app is free for one-to-one sessions; the patient app has a 14-day trial, then about $29.99 a month.

## 2. Tactus Therapy — best one-time purchase for aphasia exercises

Tactus Therapy sells individual apps, each drilling one skill: Naming, Comprehension, Reading, Writing and more. Most cost about $29.99 as a one-time purchase, with single apps from about $9.99 to $89.99, and no subscription. Almost every app ships a free Lite version, and the apps work offline with no login. The trade-off: no programme adapts on its own.

## 3. Speech Blubs — best for young children and late talkers

Speech Blubs is built for children aged 1 to 8, using video modelling: short clips of real children saying words and sounds. The child copies the clips, and a voice-activated progress bar listens for the target word. It says plainly that it supplements, not replaces, professional therapy. Pricing is about $14.99 a month or $59.99 a year after a 7-day free trial; the App Store shows $14.49, so check before subscribing.

## 4. Articulation Station Hive — best structured articulation drills with data

Articulation Station Hive, from Little Bee Speech, is a subscription app for iPad, iPhone and Mac. It follows a clinical hierarchy, from single sounds to words, sentences and conversation. There is no speech recognition: the child records an attempt, you play it back, and you tap correct, approximate or incorrect. Those taps become score graphs and session notes you can share with parents. Pro costs about $14.99 a month, or $9.99 billed yearly, with a 14-day free trial. Apple devices only.

## 5. Stamurai — best stuttering support made in India

Stamurai is the one Indian-made tool here, built by Demosthenes Technologies of New Delhi, focused entirely on stuttering and stammering. The app offers customised daily practice and personalised plans for all ages, plus one-to-one online sessions. Pricing is in rupees: ₹6,000 for 8 sessions and ₹15,000 for 24 sessions, with a free trial session to start. Be careful with stuttering marketing: several providers promise a "100% cure", and no honest tool can guarantee that.

## Using these tools in India

Most tools here are priced in dollars, so the rupee price depends on the store's conversion on the day. Before recommending one, check on the client's own phone that it installs without a VPN and works on their data connection. Tactus apps work offline, which matters for patchy connectivity; Constant Therapy's Indian English beats US-only listening material. Get written consent before recording a child's voice.

## What to avoid

Do not buy a tool before the free trial or Lite version has been used with a real client for at least a week. Do not let an app's score replace your own listening; both apps here depend on an adult judging each attempt. Do not promise a family a result the app itself will not promise, and do not hand a client an app and call it therapy. Prices change without notice — re-check the official page before paying.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can an AI app replace a speech therapist?**
No. These tools support practice between sessions; assessment and diagnosis still need a qualified speech-language pathologist.

**Which tool is best for a child who is a late talker?**
Speech Blubs, for toddlers and late talkers aged 1 to 8. Use its 7-day free trial first.

**Which tool is best for an adult after a stroke?**
Constant Therapy, for its adaptive exercises and Indian English support. Tactus apps are cheaper for a single skill such as naming.

**Do any of these tools work offline in India?**
Yes — Tactus Therapy apps work without Wi-Fi and need no login. The others need an internet connection for most features.

**Are there good Indian-made options for stuttering?**
Yes. Stamurai, made in New Delhi, offers practice plans and one-to-one sessions from ₹6,000 for 8 sessions.

**How much should a clinic budget for these tools?**
Start free: the Clinician app, Tactus Lite versions and trials cost nothing. After that, roughly $10 to $30 a month per subscription.

## Final verdict

Start with Constant Therapy's free clinician app and Stamurai for stuttering; pay only after a trial proves it fits your caseload.
