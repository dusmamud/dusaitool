2. Screener.in: Indian fundamentals, free, 50-company watchlist.
3. Tickertape: 200-plus filters, free screener.
4. Trendlyne: DVM scores, free access.

8. Koyfin: global data, free, 2 screens.
9. Kavout: ML rankings, free tier.
10. StockEdge: Indian scans, free app.
