Adobe Express and PhotoRoom cover most free cutouts in India; remove.bg has the cleanest edges, but its site closes 1 December 2026. Adobe's free terms are disputed.

## Top 10

1. **remove.bg** — one-click portraits; free low-res previews, one high-res download.
2. **Adobe Express** — Firefly remover; free Firefly cutouts; terms vary.
3. **PhotoRoom** — product photos, AI backgrounds; about 250 watermarked exports monthly.
4. **Canva** — remover inside designs; free plan allows few premium AI uses.
5. **Pixlr** — editor, no account; free with ads, HD needs Plus.
6. **Clipdrop** — sharp edges, tricky photos; 20 free removals daily, 1024px.
7. **Fotor** — built-in remover editor; free basic removal, HD needs Pro.
8. **Slazzer** — simple one-click cutouts; free low-res downloads, HD needs credits.
9. **Erase.bg** — clean, no-account tool; free downloads up to 4K resolution.
10. **Removal.AI** — cutouts plus erase and restore; free low-res download; bulk paid.

## FAQ

**Which free background remover is best in India?** Adobe Express or PhotoRoom daily; remove.bg for one portrait.

## Verdict

Start free with Adobe Express or PhotoRoom; keep remove.bg for one-off portraits.
