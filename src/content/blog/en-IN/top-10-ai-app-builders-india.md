Start with Lovable or Bolt: both turn a plain-English prompt into a working web app, hosted free, no coding or VPN needed.

1. Lovable — full-stack web apps from chat; free: 5 credits daily, 30 monthly.
2. Bolt — browser app builder, hosting included; free: 1M tokens monthly, 300K daily.
3. Replit — prompt-to-app workspace, database and hosting; free: daily Agent credits.
4. v0 — Vercel builder for React interfaces; free: 7 messages daily.
5. Base44 — all-in-one apps, auth, database, payments; free: 25 credits monthly.
6. Google AI Studio — free prompt-to-app builder; first two apps deploy free.
7. Emergent — one-prompt full-stack apps; free: 10 credits (renewal differs by source).
8. Bubble — visual no-code builder; free: 500 AI credits monthly.
9. Cursor — AI editor; free: limited agent and chat requests.
10. Appy Pie — no-code native app builder; free build and preview, publishing paid.

## FAQ

**Best builder for beginners?** Lovable: free to start, works with Hindi prompts, hosts your app free.

**Final verdict:** Bolt for the biggest free allowance, Lovable for the easiest start.

Prices and free limits change — check official pricing before paying.
