A vegetable vendor needs three things every morning: the mandi rate before buying, a way to tell regular customers today's prices, and a record of who paid and who took udhar. Three free tools cover all three — Agmarknet for mandi prices, WhatsApp Business for customers, and Khatabook for payments — and all work on a basic Android phone.

| Job | Best free tool | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mandi price check | Agmarknet / e-NAM | Wholesale rates by market |
| Customer orders | WhatsApp Business | Catalogue, quick replies, broadcasts |
| Udhar and payments | Khatabook | Digital ledger, reminders, UPI QR |

## Agmarknet and e-NAM — check the mandi rate first

Before leaving for the mandi, check yesterday's modal price on Agmarknet or the e-NAM price dashboard. Both are free government services showing minimum, maximum and modal prices by market, so you can spot overnight moves in tomato or onion. Treat the figure as guidance, not a guaranteed offer: rates vary by grade and variety. Farmers use the same data — see our guide for [farmers](/blog/ai-tools-for-farmers-india/).

## WhatsApp Business — your shop inside the chat

The free WhatsApp Business app turns your phone into a shop. Add each vegetable to the catalogue with a photo and today's price, set quick replies for rate and delivery questions, and send the morning price list to a broadcast list of up to 256 saved contacts. Customers order in chat. Skip the paid Business Platform — it charges per message and suits larger sellers.

## Khatabook — the digital udhar khata

Khatabook replaces the paper khata with a free digital ledger. Record what each customer owes, send payment reminders over WhatsApp or SMS, and collect payment through a UPI QR code or payment link. Ledger and reminders are free; check the app before paying for optional extras. It works in 11 Indian languages, and customers need no app for reminders.

## Using them together in India

Each day: check Agmarknet before buying, update the WhatsApp catalogue with the day's prices, and enter every udhar sale in Khatabook before closing. All three are free at the basic level and need no VPN. Prices and limits change — re-check the official page before paying. Fixed-shop vendors can also see our guide for [kirana stores](/blog/ai-tools-for-kirana-stores-india/).

## What to avoid

Skip paid "mandi rate" groups and price-alert apps — Agmarknet data is free. Never give udhar without a ledger entry, however small the amount. Never share your UPI PIN or OTP; no genuine support team asks for them.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there a free way to check mandi prices?** Yes — Agmarknet and the e-NAM dashboard publish reported wholesale prices by market, free. Check the report date; some markets report a day late.

**Can vendors take orders on WhatsApp for free?** Yes. The app is free, with a catalogue, quick replies and broadcasts to 256 contacts. Only the separate Business Platform (API) charges per message.

**Which app tracks udhar best?** Khatabook is the most widely used free digital ledger for Indian shopkeepers. It records credit, sends reminders and collects payment by UPI.

**Do I need GST to use these tools?** No, none requires GST registration. If your turnover crosses the GST threshold, check the official GST portal or a tax professional — this is general information only, not professional advice.

## Final verdict

Start free: check mandi rates on Agmarknet, sell on WhatsApp Business, track udhar on Khatabook.
