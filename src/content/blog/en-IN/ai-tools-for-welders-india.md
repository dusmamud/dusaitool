## Short answer

Three free tools cover most daily work for welders in India. ChatGPT drafts quotes, messages and safety checklists. Zoho Invoice turns an accepted quote into a GST invoice and payment reminder. Canva makes a rate card or job poster for WhatsApp. None welds for you, and none replaces a trained safety check. Start free; pay only when free limits slow you down.

| Tool | Best for | Free plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | Quotes and checklists | ₹0; uploads and images limited |
| Zoho Invoice | Quotes and GST invoices | ₹0; yearly invoice limit applies |
| Canva | Rate cards and posters | ₹0; about 5 GB, small AI allowance |

## 1. ChatGPT — quotes, messages and safety checklists

Describe the job with size and material, and ask for a clear quote in Hindi or English. It can also draft a short safety checklist: helmet, gloves, ventilation, fire watch and gas-cylinder checks. The free plan costs ₹0 and covers everyday text chats; uploads, images and voice are limited. Paid plans start around ₹399 a month; re-check official pricing first.

## 2. Zoho Invoice — professional quotes and GST billing

Zoho Invoice is free for Indian businesses. Create a quote with rates and an expiry date, let the customer accept it, then convert it to an invoice in one click. It supports GST invoices, UPI payments and reminders for unpaid bills. A yearly invoice limit applies; sources disagree on the number, so check the official pricing page first.

## 3. Canva — rate cards and posters that win customers

Canva turns your services into something a customer can forward. Make a one-page rate card or poster with your phone number and a photo of finished work. The free plan costs ₹0, with about 5 GB of storage and a small monthly AI allowance; check the exact allowance inside the app. Pro is reported around ₹4,000 a year. Share it on WhatsApp or print it for your shop.

## Using these tools in India

In India, welding work still comes mostly from word of mouth and repeat customers — use these tools to look professional, not to replace that trust. Send the quote the same day you measure the job. Builders face the same customer problem — see our guide for [builders](/blog/ai-tools-for-builders-india/). This is general information only, not professional safety advice; follow official guidance and trained supervision on site.

## What to avoid

Never copy an AI price blindly; check steel and transport rates in your market first. Do not promise a date without checking your workload. Never skip safety gear because a checklist looked short, and never share a customer's address or photos without permission. Prices and limits change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## FAQ

**Can AI tools find welding jobs for me?**

Not directly. They improve your quotes, messages and posters, but jobs still come from customers, contractors and referrals. Reply fast and ask happy customers to recommend you.

**Which free tool should a welder try first?**

ChatGPT — it covers quotes, replies and safety checklists for ₹0. Add Zoho Invoice when billing gets messy, and Canva for a rate card.

**Is Zoho Invoice really free in India?**

Zoho says it is free for Indian businesses, with GST invoicing and UPI payments included. A yearly invoice limit applies, so confirm it first.

## Final verdict

Start free with ChatGPT for quotes and safety, Zoho Invoice for billing, and Canva for customer posters.
