These ten tools give Indian job seekers free AI mock interviews and feedback.

1. Yoodli: spoken roleplays, 5 free sessions.
2. FreeMockInterview: graded mocks, free, no login.
3. ChatGPT: voice interviews, limited free voice.
4. Gemini Live: voice practice, free plan.
5. MockIF: CV-based mocks, 5 free credits.
6. Revarta: resume-based mocks, 2 free interviews.
7. Aced: peer tech mocks, 5 free monthly.
8. interviewing.io: free AI interviewer, peer mocks.
9. Huru: video rehearsal, free; cap disputed.
10. Interviews by AI: likely questions, 3 free monthly.
