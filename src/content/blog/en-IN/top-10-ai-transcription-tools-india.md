Start with Fathom or TurboScribe: both are free for everyday use.

1. Fathom: records and transcribes meetings free, unlimited recordings, five summaries monthly.
2. TurboScribe: transcribes audio and video free, three files daily, thirty minutes each.
3. Otter.ai: live meeting transcription free, 300 minutes monthly, 30 per call.
4. Fireflies: meeting notes free, unlimited transcription, 400 minutes team storage.
5. Notta: meetings transcribed free, 120 minutes monthly, three per recording.
6. Rev: accurate transcription free, 45 minutes monthly, English only.
7. Descript: edits audio by editing text free, one hour monthly, watermarked exports.
8. Sonix: transcribes audio and video, fifty-four languages, thirty-minute one-time trial only.
9. Happy Scribe: subtitles in 150+ languages; uploads get ten free minutes once.
10. Google Docs voice typing: free live Hindi-English dictation, microphone only.

## FAQ

**Which free tool is best for long meetings?** Fathom, because its free plan has no recording cap — sources differ on Happy Scribe's free meetings.

## Final verdict

Fathom for meetings, TurboScribe for files, Google Docs for free dictation — start free and check limits before paying.
