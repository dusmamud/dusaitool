For most Indian sellers, HubSpot or Zoho runs the CRM, Apollo finds leads, ChatGPT writes outreach. Start free; pay when limits bite.

1. HubSpot — free CRM, 2 users, 1,000 contacts, deal tracking.
2. Apollo.io — finds B2B leads; free: 75 credits monthly.
3. Zoho CRM — free for 3 users, 5,000 records.
4. Hunter — finds verified emails by domain; free: 25 searches monthly.
5. Freshsales — CRM with built-in phone; free for 3 users.
6. Lusha — reveals emails and phones; free: 40 credits monthly.
7. Snov.io — finds emails, runs drip campaigns; free: 50 credits monthly.
8. Calendly — sales-call booking; free: one event type.
9. ChatGPT — drafts cold emails and scripts; free plan, no card.
10. Pipedrive — deal pipeline with AI insights; 14-day trial, no free plan.

## FAQ

**Which free tool should a small Indian team start with?** HubSpot or Zoho, plus Apollo for leads.

## Final verdict

Start with HubSpot or Zoho free, add Apollo for leads, and pay only when limits bite.
