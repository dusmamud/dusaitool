If you run a mobile repair shop in India, your day is a queue of cracked screens, dead batteries, water-damaged phones and customers asking the same question: how much will it cost, and when will it be ready? The repair itself is skilled work, but the business around it is memory work. You need to remember which phone belongs to whom, which spare part was ordered, what price was quoted, and which customer is still waiting for a call. When that memory fails, a customer waits three days for a phone that was ready yesterday, or two customers are quoted two different prices for the same screen.

The direct answer is simple: start with WhatsApp Business for estimates and ready-for-collection messages, Google Business Profile so nearby phone owners find you when their screen cracks, Google Sheets to track every phone, part and price, ChatGPT to write polite estimates and replies, and Canva for offer posters and before-after photos. All five are free to begin with, and together they cover the whole repair journey, from the first worried message to the customer collecting a working phone and leaving a good review.

## 1. WhatsApp Business: bookings and reminders that bring customers back

WhatsApp Business is the free business version of the WhatsApp your customers already use every day. It adds a business profile with your hours, services and location, quick replies for the questions you answer again and again, labels to sort customers, and a catalogue that lists your services with prices.

For a mobile repair shop, this means sending a written estimate with the part price and repair time before opening the phone, then one message when the phone is ready to collect. The customer can show the estimate to family before agreeing, and the shop has proof of the quoted price if there is any argument later.

How to start: Set up your catalogue with three to five services and prices, write quick replies for price and timing, and send reminders only to customers who agreed to messages.

Watch out for: Never spam customers with daily offers, and never share a customer's phone number or personal details with anyone else.

## 2. Google Business Profile: be found when nearby customers search

Google Business Profile is the free listing that decides whether a nearby customer finds you or your competitor. When someone searches for your service near them, Google shows your profile with photos, hours, phone number, directions and reviews.

For a mobile repair shop, this means appearing when someone nearby searches for a screen repair or a battery replacement, with photos of the shop, correct hours and honest reviews. A customer with a broken phone does not browse for long; they call the first trustworthy-looking shop near them.

How to start: Claim your profile, add ten real photos of your work, correct hours and phone number, and reply to your last five reviews.

Watch out for: Fake reviews or wrong hours damage trust fast, so keep the details honest and reply politely even to unhappy customers.

## 3. Google Sheets: customers, jobs and cash in one sheet

Google Sheets is a free spreadsheet that works on any phone and becomes the record book of a small business. One sheet can list every customer, the service they took, the date, the amount paid and the next due date, so you can greet a regular by name and suggest the right service.

For a mobile repair shop, this means one row per phone: customer name, phone model, problem, part ordered, quoted price, and collection date. When the customer calls to ask where their phone is, the answer takes ten seconds instead of a hunt through paper slips and memory.

How to start: Make one sheet with this month's customers, services and dates, and use it to send reminders next week.

Watch out for: Do not store a customer's full address, ID or payment details in a shared sheet; keep only what the business actually needs.

## 4. ChatGPT: messages, replies and offers in minutes

ChatGPT is an AI assistant that handles the writing a small business owner does every day. Give it the facts — service, price and date — and it writes a short, polite message in Hindi or English, replies to a customer asking about price, drafts a festival offer, or answers a complaint calmly.

For a mobile repair shop, this means a polite message explaining why a screen costs what it does, a calm reply when a customer says the price is high, and a short festival offer for screen guards and covers. The owner gives the facts, and the tool writes the message in Hindi or English.

How to start: Ask it to write a short Hindi reminder for customers whose last visit was three weeks ago, then read it and send it yourself.

Watch out for: AI can invent a price or a service you do not offer, so check every number and every promise before sending a message.

## 5. Canva: offer posters and photos that sell the next visit

Canva helps a small business look professional without hiring a designer. Its free plan has templates for offer posters, price cards and festival discounts, and its photo tools can brighten a real before-and-after photo so the difference is obvious on a phone screen.

For a mobile repair shop, this means a clean poster for a screen-guard offer, a price card for common repairs like screens and batteries, and a before-after photo of a cracked phone made new again. Real photos of real repairs build more trust than any stock image.

How to start: Make one festival offer poster with your real prices and phone number, and share it on WhatsApp Status this week.

Watch out for: Do not use stock photos of work you did not do, or a heavily edited photo that hides what you did not fix.

## Using AI in India: mobile repair shops

The pattern that works for Indian repair shops is fast, honest and local. Most customers come from within a few kilometres, find you on Google or through a neighbour, and pay by UPI or cash, so your Google profile, your WhatsApp replies and your honest estimates matter more than any paid ad. Customers are anxious about their data, so say clearly whether photos and contacts will be safe, and never open a phone without telling the customer what it will cost. Spare parts matter too: a genuine part and an honest warranty earn more repeat business than the cheapest quote in the market.

## What to avoid

Avoid quoting a price you cannot honour, using duplicate parts while charging for genuine ones, and promising a same-day repair when the part is not in stock. Avoid keeping a customer's phone for days without an update, and never look through a customer's photos or messages while the phone is with you.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best AI tool for mobile repair shops in India?**
There is no single best tool, because a mobile repair shop needs bookings, records, reminders and customer trust. Start with WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile, Google Sheets, ChatGPT and Canva, and add paid software only when the free tools start losing jobs.


**Can AI replace the skilled work in this business?**
No. AI can organise bookings, write messages and keep records, but it cannot do the skilled work itself or judge quality. Every AI message must be checked by a human before it reaches a customer.


**How much do AI tools for mobile repair shops cost in India?**
The starting set is free: the tools in this article all have free plans that cover daily work for a small business. Check current pricing on the official page before paying for anything.


**How do I remind customers without spamming them?**
Send one polite reminder at the right time, only to customers who agreed to messages, and stop if they ask you to. One useful reminder beats ten daily offers that get your number blocked.


**How do I handle a customer who says my repair price is too high?**
Show the written estimate, explain the part cost and your labour separately, and offer a cheaper part only if it is genuinely available and safe. Never cut the price by quietly using a duplicate part. A customer who understands the price usually accepts it, and one who does not is better lost than served with a bad part.


**Should I keep a customer's phone overnight?**
Only if the part is not available the same day, and only after telling the customer clearly when it will be ready. Give a written receipt with the phone model, problem and quoted price, and store the phone safely with the customer's name on it. Never keep a phone overnight without telling the customer.
