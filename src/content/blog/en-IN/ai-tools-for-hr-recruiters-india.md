A small team in India usually does not need an enterprise hiring suite. It needs three things: one place to collect applications, a cheap way to reach candidates, and a simple way to schedule interviews without endless phone calls. The tools below cover exactly those three jobs, and every free limit in this article was checked against the vendor's own pricing or help page.

The short answer is this: start with [Zoho Recruit](https://www.zoho.com/recruit/) free for one live job, post the same job free on Indeed and LinkedIn, and let candidates book interview slots themselves with Calendly free. Add Cutshort if you hire tech roles in India and want a shortlist instead of a pile of applications. Pay for Manatal only when your candidate volume grows past what the free tools handle comfortably.

Prices and limits change, so before you pay for anything or promise a candidate a deadline, re-check the official pricing or help page linked in each section. What follows reflects the vendor pages as they stood in late 2026.

## Zoho Recruit: the free applicant tracker for one live job

Zoho Recruit is the most practical free starting point for a small Indian team because its free edition is a real applicant tracking system, not just a job board. You get a career site, candidate management, and interview organisation for one active job at a time. That fits a team that hires one role at a time, which is how most startups and small businesses actually hire.

The honest limit matters. The free edition allows only 1 active job, and resume parsing is not included in the free edition — on the Standard plan, parsing is capped at 250 per day. So if you receive 200 resumes for one opening, you will be reading and entering many of them by hand on the free plan. The Standard plan, at ₹1,250 (see [Zoho's INR plan comparison](https://www.zoho.com/sites/default/files/recruit/zoho-recruit-corporate-inr.pdf)) per recruiter per month billed annually (₹1,500 billed monthly), raises the limit to 10 active jobs per recruiter and adds parsing, tasks, calls, events, and premium job boards. The Enterprise plan is ₹2,500 per recruiter per month billed annually.

Use Zoho Recruit free when you hire one role at a time and your team is comfortable doing some manual resume entry. Upgrade to Standard when you run several jobs at once or when parsing volume starts eating your day. Zoho is an Indian company, its pricing is published in rupees, and payment in INR is straightforward for Indian businesses. It works in India without a VPN, like every tool in this article.

## Indeed: free job posts that reach jobseekers across India

Indeed is the widest free reach in this list for most roles in India. According to [Indeed's own free job post policy](https://in.indeed.com/help/employers/articles/free-job-post-limit?co=IN&hl=en) for India, free jobs show for up to 30 days, and the number of free job posts is not limited at this time in countries outside its listed group (the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Ireland, Belgium, the Netherlands, Spain, Sweden and Switzerland, where the limit is 3 free jobs per calendar month). India falls in the unlimited group, but note the careful wording: "not limited at this time" is not a promise forever, and quality standards and usage limits still apply.

Practically, this means a small team can post several roles free on Indeed and keep them visible for a month each. When a free post stops showing, or when you need more visibility, you can sponsor it for a fee. Sponsored pricing varies by role and location, so check it inside your employer account before committing.

One warning: free reach is not the same as good applications. A free Indeed post for a popular role can bring hundreds of applications, many of them unfiltered. Pair Indeed with Zoho Recruit free so applications land in one tracker instead of scattered across email inboxes, and write the job description tightly — the next section on ChatGPT and Claude explains how — so the wrong candidates filter themselves out.

## LinkedIn: one free job, best for professional roles

[LinkedIn's free job post](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a519737/differences-between-free-and-promoted-jobs?lang=en) is narrower than Indeed's but better targeted for professional, managerial, and tech roles. From a personal LinkedIn account, you can have a maximum of 1 free job active at a time, with a limited number of free job posts in a 30-day window. The free job is paused after being active for 14 days, and it is hidden from search results after reaching the free applicant limit, which typically ranges from 10 to 30 applicants depending on the role. You also get 25 recommended candidates based on your criteria and one evaluation of a top-fit applicant, plus a preview of Interview AI.

That applicant cap is the key limit to plan around. If your role attracts more than 10 to 30 applicants quickly, the post stops appearing in search, though it stays accessible through a direct link or your company page. To unpause it, you must promote it, which costs money. So use the LinkedIn free post for a role where you expect a modest, qualified response — a specialist or senior role, for example — and use Indeed free for high-volume roles. Post the LinkedIn job early, because its 14-day active window is shorter than Indeed's 30 days, and share the direct link in your own network and on WhatsApp groups while it is still searchable.

## Cutshort: a shortlist for tech hiring in India

[Cutshort](https://cutshort.io/hiring) is built for hiring tech talent in India, and it works differently from a job board. Job boards give you a large pool to filter; Cutshort gives you a shortlist of candidates who match your requirements on skills, salary, location, and competency, so you spend less time screening. The company says companies can find 10 top candidates in about 11 minutes and start making offers in about 3 days, though your own results will depend on the role and the market.

Cutshort offers a free plan for hiring on your own, with low pricing for that self-serve route, and an assisted option where its team handles hiring for you starting at 8.33% of CTC. For a small team hiring developers, designers, or product people, the free self-serve plan is worth trying before paying an agency, because agency fees in India are typically a much larger share of CTC. Cutshort is India-focused, so its candidate pool and salary data fit Indian tech hiring better than a generic global board.

Use Cutshort when the problem is screening time, not reach. If you already have too many applications, Cutshort will not help; if you have too few good ones for a tech role, it is the most targeted free option in this list.

## Manatal: the paid step when volume grows

[Manatal](https://www.manatal.com/pricing) is an AI recruitment platform with a Professional plan at $15 per user per month for individuals and small teams, covering up to 15 jobs per account and up to 10,000 candidates, with unlimited hiring managers. The Enterprise plan is $35 per user per month and adds workflow automation. No free plan appears on Manatal's pricing page, so treat it as a paid tool from the start.

Why pay when Zoho Recruit free exists? Manatal's case is volume and AI assistance: candidate enrichment, AI recommendations, and automation across up to 15 jobs suit a team that hires continuously rather than one role at a time. At $15 per user per month, it is cheaper than most enterprise systems, but it is priced in dollars, so check the INR cost after conversion and any taxes before committing. Start on Zoho Recruit free, and move to Manatal when you regularly run more than one or two jobs and the manual work in the free tracker costs more than $15 a month of someone's time.

## ChatGPT and Claude: writing job descriptions and screening questions free

You do not need a hiring-specific AI tool to write a good job description. ChatGPT's free tier and Claude's free tier both draft job descriptions, screening questions, and interview scorecards in minutes. [OpenAI's own help pages](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001354-gpt-6-and-other-models-in-chatgpt) confirm that free users get a default model — currently GPT-5.6 Luna for free users — while paying users get the higher-capability models. Model names and limits change often, and different sources name different models, so treat any specific model name in an older article as possibly out of date and check the current free tier before relying on it.

The right way to use these tools is as a drafting assistant, not a decision-maker. Ask for a job description with the role, salary range, location, and must-have skills, then edit it yourself: remove inflated requirements, add the real salary band, and state whether the role is remote, hybrid, or on-site. Ask for five screening questions and a simple scorecard, then use the same scorecard for every candidate so comparisons stay fair. Never paste a candidate's resume, phone number, or personal details into a public chatbot, and never let the chatbot auto-reject candidates — that is where bias and legal trouble creep in, and it is covered in the "What to avoid" section below.

For teams that already use the tools in our [AI writing assistants](/blog/ai-writing-assistants-free-vs-paid-india/) guide, the same free-versus-paid logic applies here: free drafts are good enough for job descriptions, while paid plans matter mainly when you need higher limits or longer documents.

## Calendly: interview scheduling without the phone tag

[Calendly's free plan](https://calendly.com/pricing) solves the most annoying part of small-team hiring: finding an interview time. The free plan includes one event type, one calendar connection, one-on-one scheduling, a customisable booking page, and a browser extension. You create one interview event type, share the link with shortlisted candidates, and they pick a slot that already fits your calendar. No group emails, no "are you free at 4?" chains.

One event type is enough for a single interview round. If you run two rounds with different lengths or different interviewers, you will hit the free limit and need a paid plan or a second free workaround, such as separate Calendly accounts per interviewer, which gets messy fast. For most small teams, one 30-minute screening call event type covers the first round, and later rounds can be scheduled directly once the candidate pool is small. Calendly works in India without a VPN, and candidates book in their own time zone, which matters if you interview candidates in other Indian cities.

## TurboHire: the India-built option, demo first

TurboHire is an India-built applicant tracking system — its India office is in Hyderabad, Telangana — used by thousands of recruiters, and it offers a [free demo](https://turbohire.co/demo/) where a representative walks you through the platform. No public free plan could be confirmed from its site, so treat TurboHire as a demo-and-quote tool rather than a free tool. Ask for the demo, state your team size and hiring volume clearly, and get the price in writing before you move candidate data into it.

TurboHire fits a team that wants an Indian vendor with local support and is ready to pay for an ATS, rather than a team that needs something free today. If your budget is zero, start with Zoho Recruit free instead and keep TurboHire on the list for later.

## Using hiring AI in India: language, payment, and data

Language first. Most candidates in India read English job posts, but a Hindi or regional-language note in the post — even one line about the work location and shift timing — widens your pool for delivery, retail, and support roles. Write the main post in English, add key details in Hindi where the role needs it, and keep screening questions simple so a candidate on a phone can answer them. Our [AI translation tools](/blog/ai-translation-tools-hindi-english-india/) guide covers free tools for that translation step.

Payment is simpler than it looks. Zoho Recruit and Cutshort price for India, Indeed and LinkedIn accept Indian payment methods for sponsored or promoted posts, and Manatal bills in dollars, so confirm the final INR amount. All of these tools work in India without a VPN, and none of them requires a foreign phone number to sign up.

Data needs care. Candidate resumes contain phone numbers, addresses, and employment history. Keep them inside your ATS, not in shared spreadsheets or public chatbots, and delete data for rejected candidates on a schedule your team actually follows. If you hire for roles that involve handling other people's money or data, say so in the post and check references properly — an AI screening score is not a background check.

## What to avoid

**Do not build on Freshteam.** Freshworks has sunset Freshteam: according to [its own support page](https://support.freshteam.com/support/solutions/articles/19000162935-freshteam-sunset-support-faqs), free accounts expired on 7 March 2026, all renewals stopped on 7 June 2026, and accounts remain accessible only for 90 days after the subscription end date for data export. If an old guide still recommends Freshteam's free tier, that advice is out of date. Do not start a new hiring process on it, and if your data is still there, export it before the window closes.

**Do not let AI auto-reject candidates.** Screening tools can rank and shortlist, but an automatic rejection based on a keyword or a score can quietly filter out good candidates — career gaps, non-English colleges, and unusual job titles are all common in India and all badly handled by rigid filters. Let AI suggest a shortlist, then let a human read it.

**Do not paste candidate data into public chatbots.** Resumes contain personal data. Use your ATS for storage, and if you use ChatGPT or Claude for drafting, paste only the role description, never the candidate's details.

**Do not buy enterprise tools for a small team.** HireVue, Workday, and similar enterprise systems are built for large companies with dedicated HR teams. For a team hiring a few people a quarter, they cost more than the problem they solve. Start free, pay only when a specific limit — one active job, 14 days, or 30 applicants — blocks you repeatedly.

**Do not promise what the free tier cannot do.** If your free LinkedIn post pauses after 14 days or your Zoho Recruit free plan holds only one job, tell the hiring manager that upfront. A missed hiring deadline costs more than a small paid upgrade.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can a small Indian team hire completely free with AI tools?** Yes, for low volume. Zoho Recruit free handles one live job, Indeed gives free posts in India, LinkedIn gives one free job at a time, and Calendly free handles scheduling. You pay only when volume or speed forces an upgrade.

**Is Zoho Recruit really free?** Yes, with limits. The free edition covers 1 active job, but resume parsing is not included and several features — tasks, calls, events, premium boards — need the Standard plan at ₹1,250 per recruiter per month billed annually.

**How many free jobs can I post on Indeed in India?** According to Indeed's own policy, free posts in India are not number-limited at this time and each shows for up to 30 days, though quality standards and usage limits apply and the policy can change.

**Why did my LinkedIn free job stop showing?** Two common reasons: it was active for 14 days and got paused, or it reached the free applicant limit, typically 10 to 30 applicants. Promoting the job unpauses it, or you can share the direct link while you decide.

**Is Cutshort free?** Cutshort offers a free plan for hiring on your own. Its assisted service, where its team hires for you, starts at 8.33% of CTC, which is a different product from the free plan.

**Should I use AI to screen resumes automatically?** Use AI to organise and suggest, not to decide alone. Read the shortlist yourself, because keyword filters miss good Indian candidates with career gaps, regional colleges, or unusual titles.

**What happened to Freshteam?** Freshworks sunset Freshteam in 2026. Free accounts expired on 7 March 2026 and all renewals stopped on 7 June 2026, with 90 days of access after subscription end for data export. Do not start new hiring on it.

## The final verdict, and a checklist before you pay

Start with Zoho Recruit free, Indeed free posts and Calendly free; pay for Manatal only when candidate volume grows. Before you pay for anything, run this checklist: does the free limit actually block you, or are you paying for convenience? Is the price in rupees or dollars, and what does it cost after tax? Can you export your candidate data if you leave? And have you re-checked the official pricing page today, not last month's blog post? If the answer to the last question is no, check it now — in hiring tools, the free tier you read about last year is often the one that changed.
