These ten planners work in India without a VPN.

1. Walnut auto-tracks SMS expenses; free.
2. Jupiter gives savings pots; free.
3. Fi Money AI answers questions; free.
4. ET Money tracks budgets, SIPs; free.
5. Money Manager logs expenses manually; free.
6. YNAB gives every rupee a job; 34-day trial, manual entry.
7. PocketSmith forecasts cash flow; 2 accounts free.
8. Monarch Money tracks budgets, investments; 7-day trial.
9. Cleo chats about spending; free.
10. Goodbudget plans envelopes; free, ten envelopes.
