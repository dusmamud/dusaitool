For an electronics shop in India, the best setup is simple: Vyapar for GST billing and stock, Zoho Inventory when online orders grow, and WhatsApp Business to show products and answer customers. Together they cover billing, inventory, and customer contact for little or no cost, and all three work on a basic Android phone.

## Short answer

| Tool | Best for | Free limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vyapar | GST billing and stock | Free basic Android app |
| Zoho Inventory | Online orders and stock | 50 orders a month |
| WhatsApp Business | Product catalog and chats | Free app, 500 items |

## 1. Vyapar — GST billing and stock

Vyapar is built for Indian small shops. It creates GST invoices with CGST and SGST calculated automatically, tracks stock with low-stock alerts, and supports barcode scanning while billing. It works offline on Android and Windows, and you can share bills on WhatsApp. The basic Android app is free; desktop and premium features are paid, so check Vyapar's official pricing page before paying.

## 2. Zoho Inventory — online orders and stock

Zoho Inventory suits shops that also sell online. Its free plan covers 50 orders a month for one user at one location, with stock tracking, purchase orders, and links to Zoho Books, Shopify, and Amazon. Paid plans add more users, warehouses, and barcode and batch tracking. Free-plan limits vary slightly across sources, so confirm them on Zoho's pricing page before promising a delivery date.

## 3. WhatsApp Business — catalog and customers

WhatsApp Business turns your phone into a shop window. Its free catalog lists up to 500 products with photos, prices, and descriptions, and customers can send an order as a chat message. Quick replies and greeting messages handle common questions, and broadcast lists reach up to 256 contacts. It does not process payments, so collect money through UPI or cash on delivery.

## Using these tools in India

Start with Vyapar on the counter phone for daily billing, keep Zoho Inventory for online orders, and update the WhatsApp catalog every week with new arrivals and festival offers. Keep GST rates and HSN codes correct, and back up data weekly. This is general information only, not professional tax advice, so check official GST guidance or your CA for tax decisions. ## What to avoid

Do not buy a paid plan before the free version feels limiting. Do not trust AI-written prices or warranty terms without checking them. Never share customer numbers with random tools, and never promise delivery dates from stock figures you have not counted.

## FAQ

**Can an electronics shop use only free tools?** Yes, for a small shop. Vyapar's free Android app handles billing, WhatsApp Business handles customers, and Zoho Inventory's free plan covers up to 50 orders a month.

**Do these tools work without the internet?** Vyapar works offline on Android and Windows. Zoho Inventory and WhatsApp Business need the internet for syncing and messages.

**Which tool should I try first?** Vyapar, because billing is daily work. Add WhatsApp Business the same week, and Zoho Inventory only when online orders grow.

**Are prices and limits fixed?** No. Prices and free limits change, so re-check each tool's official pricing page before paying or promising a deadline.

## Final verdict

Start with Vyapar for billing, WhatsApp Business for customers, and Zoho Inventory when online orders grow. Free plans cover a small shop well.
