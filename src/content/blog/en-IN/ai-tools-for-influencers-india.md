## The short answer

Four free tools cover almost the whole job for an influencer in India. ChatGPT handles ideas, scripts and captions. Canva handles thumbnails and carousels. Metricool handles scheduling and analytics. Beacons AI handles a link-in-bio page, media kit and digital store. All four have a real free plan and work in India without a VPN. Prices change often.

## Which tool does what

| Tool | Best for | Free plan |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Canva | Thumbnails, carousels | 5 GB storage; limited AI uses |
| Metricool | Scheduling, analytics | 1 brand; 20 posts a month |
| Beacons AI | Link in bio, media kit | Unlimited links; 9% sales fee |

## 1. ChatGPT — planning, scripts and captions

ChatGPT is the fastest starting point. Give it your niche, your audience and three of your best posts. Ask for thirty ideas, ranked by how easy each one is to shoot. It writes hooks, outlines and captions in English, Hindi and Hinglish. The free plan costs nothing and includes unlimited everyday text chats, per OpenAI's own pages. Uploads, images, voice and deep research are limited. In India, Go costs about Rs 399 a month and Plus about Rs 1,999. Rewrite everything in your own voice, and never let it invent facts about a product you promote.

## 2. Canva — thumbnails, carousels and a consistent look

Canva is where most Indian creators design thumbnails, carousel posts, story templates and YouTube covers. The free plan includes many free templates and assets, 5 GB of storage and limited AI uses each month, reported as around twenty standard uses. Background remover, Magic Resize and full brand kits are paid. Canva Pro in India is listed at about Rs 500 a month or Rs 4,000 a year. For design basics, see our guide to [AI tools for graphic design beginners in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-graphic-design-beginners-india/).

## 3. Metricool — scheduling, analytics and knowing what worked

Metricool gives you a visual calendar and scheduling across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Pinterest. Its analytics show which posts actually grew your account. The free plan is genuinely usable: one brand, up to twenty scheduled posts a month, thirty days of analytics, five competitor profiles and five AI text credits a month. That twenty-post cap is the number to plan around. Daily posters outgrow free within three weeks and need Starter, listed from about $20 a month. Four or five posts a week fit inside free.

## 4. Beacons AI — brand deals, media kit and selling

Beacons AI turns your bio link into a small business. The free plan gives you unlimited links, a media kit page to send to brands, a digital store and fifty email sends a month. The catch is the sales fee. Free and the $10-a-month Creator plan take about 9% of each sale, on top of payment processing of roughly 2.9% plus $0.30. The $30-a-month tier drops the Beacons fee to 0%. Put follower counts, audience split, past brand work and rates in one link, and send that link instead of screenshots in a chat.

## Using these tools in India

First, payment: ChatGPT and Canva accept Indian payment methods, while Metricool and Beacons bill in dollars. Second, language: test every tool with Hindi and Hinglish prompts first, because AI writing varies across Indian languages. Third, disclosure is not optional. Under ASCI guidelines and the Consumer Affairs endorsement rules, any paid post, gifted product, free trip or affiliate link needs a clear disclosure such as #Ad at the start of the caption, plus the platform's paid partnership tag.

## What to avoid

Do not buy followers or fake engagement. Brands check audience quality, and one exposed screenshot ends more deals than a slow month. Do not trust "lifetime deal" sellers on Telegram or WhatsApp offering shared logins; they get banned and you lose your library. No tool here needs a VPN. Do not sign a brand contract without reading the usage rights: a brand that can reuse your face in ads forever should pay far more than one posting your Reel once.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool should an Indian influencer start with first?**

Start with ChatGPT on the free plan. It helps with ideas, scripts and captions on day one, costs nothing, and works in Hindi, English and Hinglish.

**Are these tools really free, or is there a catch?**

All four have a real free plan with no expiry date. The catch is always the limits: ChatGPT limits uploads and images, Canva limits AI uses and locks the background remover, Metricool caps you at twenty posts a month, and Beacons takes about 9% of your sales.

**How much should a small Indian influencer charge for a brand deal?**

There is no standard rate card, and anyone quoting one fixed number is guessing. Rates depend on niche, engagement rate, deliverables and usage rights.

**Do I need to disclose paid posts in India?**

Yes. Under ASCI guidelines and the Consumer Affairs endorsement rules, any material connection — payment, free product, discount, trip or affiliate commission — must be disclosed clearly, such as #Ad at the start of the caption plus the platform's paid partnership tag.

**Can AI tools grow my followers on their own?**

No. They save time on planning, design, scheduling and reporting, leaving you more time to make better content.

## Final verdict

Start free with ChatGPT, Canva and Metricool, and add Beacons when brand deals begin. Each paid upgrade earns its place only when a free limit blocks your work.
