Google Translate covers the most languages, DeepL sounds most natural, and ChatGPT handles tone best. Pick by language pair, not brand.

1. Google Translate: free, 240+ languages, camera and offline.
2. DeepL: natural-sounding text, Hindi included; free cap varies by source, roughly 1,500 characters.
3. Microsoft Translator: free app, live conversation mode, API free tier 2 million characters.
4. ChatGPT: free unlimited everyday text chats, best for tone and idiom.
5. Gemini: free with a Google account, limits refresh every five hours, Hindi support.
6. Claude: free plan, limits reset every five hours, good for formal documents.
7. Reverso: free, 2,000 characters per request, context examples and verb conjugation.
8. Papago: free, for Korean, Japanese and Chinese, 3,000 characters without signing in.
9. Yandex Translate: free, 10,000 characters per request, for Russian and Asian languages.
10. Lingvanex: free tier, 100+ languages, offline mode and document translation.

**Which free translation tool is best for Hindi?** Google Translate or Gemini for Hindi; DeepL for polished English from Hindi.

## Final verdict

Google Translate for breadth, DeepL for polish, ChatGPT for tone — all free to start in India.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
