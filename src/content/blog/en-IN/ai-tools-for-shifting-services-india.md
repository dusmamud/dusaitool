Porter gives customers instant quotes and live tracking, so phone-and-paper movers now look slow. Start free; re-check prices before paying.

## 1. Bill Bilty — the movers' all-in-one app

Bill Bilty calls itself the first app built only for packers and movers. It creates survey lists, quotations from over 20 templates, GST invoices, packing lists and the LR (bilty). Live tracking shows customers where their shipment is, and MoverMaker offers 50+ free website templates. The catch is pricing: no simple app price list is published, so confirm the cost on billbilty.com first.

## 2. MoveMitra — a CRM made for Indian movers

MoveMitra is a CRM designed specifically for Indian packers and movers, claiming over 2,000 moving companies use it. It covers enquiries, quotations, follow-ups, scheduling, crew assignment, vehicle availability, tracking and GST-ready billing. Its pitch: automatic quotations save over ten hours a week and follow-ups stop leads going cold. No price is published, so compare it with Zoho CRM's prices first.

## 3. WhatsApp Business — where your customers already are

Most Indian customers would rather message on WhatsApp than fill in a form. The free app gives a business profile, service catalogue, greeting and away messages, quick replies, labels and broadcasts of up to 256 contacts. The paid API adds chatbots and bulk messages, but costs a platform fee plus Meta's per-message charges — about ₹1.09 for marketing and ₹0.15 for utility messages in India, with replies within 24 hours free. Check Meta's pricing page first — rates vary.

## 4. Zoho CRM — the free pipeline for small teams

Zoho CRM is free for up to 3 users with 5,000 records, and it is one of the few free plans that still includes workflow rules, so you can automate simple follow-ups without paying. That tracks every enquiry from first call to delivered move. Paid plans start at ₹800 per user per month (Standard, annual billing), plus 18% GST. Limits are real — one pipeline, no custom reports — but it replaces the diary for a team of two or three.

## 5. Porter — instant quotes customers already trust

Porter began as an intra-city logistics app and now offers packers and movers services across 38 Indian cities. Customers enter pickup and drop locations, see an upfront quotation, book in the app and track the vehicle live. Shifting is listed from around ₹1,200; the real quote depends on distance, items and packing. Movers should note: customers compare your quote against its instant price in seconds.

## Using these tools in India

Collect shifting details — origin, destination, floor and lift, item list, date — with a Google Form linked from your website and WhatsApp catalogue, sending responses to a Sheet or Zoho CRM. Draft quote messages with ChatGPT or Gemini, but check numbers yourself — AI cannot know your diesel cost. Send the formal quotation from Bill Bilty or MoveMitra so it carries GST details, confirm on WhatsApp and share the tracking link on moving day.

## What to avoid

Never promise a fixed price before a survey — the top complaint is a low phone quote followed by a higher bill on moving day. Do not buy a paid CRM or API plan under ten enquiries a week — the free tools are enough. Do not share live tracking links publicly; a tracking link is for one customer only. Do not let an AI chatbot answer pricing or delivery-date questions alone — a wrong promise at midnight becomes your problem in the morning.

## FAQ

**Which is the best software for packers and movers in India?**
Bill Bilty and MoveMitra are the India-specific options for quotes, invoices, LR and tracking; a small team can run on Zoho CRM free plus WhatsApp Business.

**Can I get a free quote for shifting in India?**
Yes — Porter and NoBroker give instant app quotes, and most movers quote free after a phone or video survey. Get it in writing, itemised.

**How can customers track their shifting in real time?**
Porter tracks vehicles live in its app; Bill Bilty and MoveMitra share live tracking links. If tracking stalls, ask the driver for a WhatsApp location update.

**Is WhatsApp Business really free for a shifting business?**
Yes — the app is free, including catalogue, greeting and away messages, quick replies and broadcasts. You pay only on the WhatsApp Business API.

**Do I need a CRM if I only get a few shifting enquiries a week?**
Not a paid one. Zoho CRM is free for up to 3 users and reminds you to follow up — follow-ups win back most lost bookings.

**Is Porter available in my city?**
Porter lists 38 Indian cities, including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune. Check the app for your exact locations.

## Final verdict

Bill Bilty and MoveMitra run the move; WhatsApp Business and Zoho CRM win the customer. Start free, pay only when bookings grow.
