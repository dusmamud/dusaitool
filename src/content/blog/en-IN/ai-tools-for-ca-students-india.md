CA exams reward solving problems under time pressure and revising weak topics before you forget. AI helps — but it also misleads: a chatbot can explain a tax provision well and still get the section number wrong. Short answer: ChatGPT or Gemini free for explanations, Gemini Notebook for your own ICAI material, Quizlet or Anki for revision, and ICAI for mock tests.

Tax law changes yearly — check AI answers against ICAI material. Prices and limits below come from public sources and change often; re-check the official page before paying.

## 1. ChatGPT — best all-round tutor

[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) is the most useful general tutor for a CA student, and the free plan covers most daily needs. Ask it to explain a concept, work a journal entry, or turn a chapter into practice questions. Its Study Mode quizzes you instead of handing over answers. In India, Go costs ₹399 a month with about ten times the free tier's limits; Plus costs ₹1,999. Verify every rate in your study material.

## 2. Gemini — best if you live in Google apps

[Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) is free with a Google account on web, Android, and iOS. It can summarise Docs, help with spreadsheets, and check facts with web search. Eligible Indian students 18+ could claim Google AI Plus free for a year, redeemed by 31 December 2026, auto-renewing at ₹399 a month. When they disagree, ICAI material decides.

## 3. Gemini Notebook — best for revising your own material

Gemini Notebook, earlier called NotebookLM, is the tool most CA students underuse. Upload your own sources — ICAI PDFs, notes, lecture links — and it answers only from them, with citations. No invented provisions. The free Standard plan gives up to 100 notebooks and 50 sources per notebook, plus study guides, flashcards, quizzes, and Audio Overviews. Verify cited numbers before the exam.

## 4. Quizlet — best for quick flashcard revision

[Quizlet](https://quizlet.com/) is the easiest place to build flashcards fast, with ready-made sets in its public library. Make one set per chapter and revise on your phone. The free plan covers flashcards and the Match game, with ads. The catch: Learn and Test modes are rationed on free accounts — roughly a few Learn rounds and one test per set. Plus costs $7.99 a month or $35.99 a year.

## 5. Anki — best for daily revision that sticks

[Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) is free, open-source spaced-repetition software, and the strongest revision tool here. Grade each card Again, Hard, Good, or Easy, and weak topics return often. It is free on Windows, Mac, Linux, the web via AnkiWeb sync, and Android via AnkiDroid; only the iOS app costs money, a one-time $24.99. Building decks takes effort, but each card forces you to decide what matters.

## Mock tests: start with ICAI, not with a paid app

No AI tool replaces an official mock test. ICAI's Board of Studies publishes Mock Test Papers, Revision Test Papers, and previous-year papers, free from the BoS Knowledge Portal (boslive.icai.org) and the ICAI BoS app. For January 2027, Series I and II ran at branches and online. Attempt two full MTPs per paper under real timing, then use AI to find weak chapters. See the [exam prep tools](/blog/ai-tools-for-exam-prep-india/) guide.

## Using these tools in India

All five work in India without a VPN, on a normal phone, with UPI for paid plans. Most students need only a free ChatGPT or Gemini account, one Notebook per subject, and Anki. Both chatbots accept Hindi questions; Notebook can summarise English sources in Hindi. Never pay for "AI notes" copied from ICAI. See the [accounting tools](/blog/ai-tools-for-accountants-india/) and [Excel tools](/blog/ai-tools-for-data-excel-india/) guides.

## What to avoid

Never treat a chatbot as a source of law: sections, rates, and due dates come from ICAI material or the bare Act. Do not buy three test series at once — one mock series for your level is enough. Do not memorise AI-written answers. Never upload your admit card to an AI tool. Do not confuse watching AI solve a sum with solving it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool is best for CA students in India?**
ChatGPT free for explanations, Gemini Notebook free for your own ICAI material, and Anki free for daily revision — all free.

**Are ICAI mock test papers free?**
Yes — MTPs, RTPs, and previous-year papers with suggested answers, free on the BoS Knowledge Portal and ICAI BoS app.

**Can I use ChatGPT for CA prep?**
Yes — for concepts, practice questions, and weak-topic analysis. But verify sections, rates, and amendments against ICAI material; AI answers can be outdated or wrong.

**Is Gemini Notebook really free?**
Yes — up to 100 notebooks and 50 sources per notebook on the free Standard plan, with study guides, flashcards, quizzes, and Audio Overviews.

**Should I pay for Quizlet Plus or ChatGPT Plus for CA prep?**
Not at first. Try free plans for two weeks; pay only if you hit a limit daily, and only for one tool.

## Final verdict

Start free with ChatGPT, Gemini Notebook and ICAI mock tests, then revise daily with Anki. Pay only for one mock series for your exact CA level.
