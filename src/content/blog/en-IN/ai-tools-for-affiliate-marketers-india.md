Affiliate marketing in India comes down to three things: the link, the tracking, and the payout. EarnKaro turns store links into commission-earning Profit Links, Amazon Associates pays for Amazon.in sales, and Bitly shortens links for sharing. Start free, promote products you use, and check every rate on the official page before counting on an income.

*General information only, not financial advice. Rates and payout rules change — re-check official pages before paying or promising a deadline.*

## 1. EarnKaro — easiest start for Indian creators

EarnKaro is an Indian platform. Pick a product from partners such as Flipkart and Myntra, turn its link into a Profit Link, and share it on WhatsApp or YouTube. You earn commission on confirmed sales. Confirmed profit reaches your bank from ₹10, but confirmation takes 30 to 90 days after the return window closes. Joining is free; no website is needed.

## 2. Amazon Associates India — the big catalogue

Amazon Associates India is the official programme for Amazon.in. You get a tracked link for any product and earn on qualifying sales within 24 hours of the click. Current rates pay 8% on beauty, 7.5% on apparel, 1% on most phones, and 0% on iPhones. Payment is by NEFT from ₹1,000, about 60 days after month-end. PAN and an Indian bank account are needed.

## 3. Bitly — short links, weak free tracking

Bitly shortens long affiliate links for sharing, plus QR codes. The free plan allows 5 short links, 2 QR codes, and 3 custom back-halves a month, with unlimited clicks. But free links now show an ad page first, and Bitly's own guide says the free plan includes no click analytics. Sources disagree on a 50-click tracking cap, so check the pricing page first.

## Using these tools in India

Indian creators earn fastest from WhatsApp groups, YouTube, and blogs. Disclose every affiliate link clearly — Amazon's rules require it. A [blog with real content](/blog/ai-tools-for-bloggers-india/) gets approved faster than an empty profile. Affiliate income is taxable; keep PAN and bank details ready, and never pay a fee to "approve" your account — joining is free.

## What to avoid

Do not promote unchecked products, hide links, or buy fake clicks. Do not promise fixed earnings: returned orders pay nothing, rates change, and payouts take weeks. Avoid tools needing a VPN in India or payment before any earning.

## FAQ

**Do I need a website to start affiliate marketing in India?**

No. EarnKaro needs no website, and Amazon accepts social profiles — though a real blog gets approved faster.

**Which affiliate programme pays fastest in India?**

EarnKaro's minimum is only ₹10, but confirmation takes 30–90 days. Amazon pays about 60 days after month-end. Neither pays instantly.

**Is the Bitly free plan enough for affiliate links?**

For a few links, yes. But 5 links a month, ad pages, and no analytics mean serious campaigns outgrow it fast.

**Do I have to pay tax on affiliate income?**

Yes, affiliate earnings are taxable income in India. Keep payout records and check current rules with a tax professional.

## Final verdict

Start free with EarnKaro and Amazon Associates; use Bitly only for short links, not analytics.
