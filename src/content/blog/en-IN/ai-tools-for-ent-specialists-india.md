## Practo Ray — appointments and patient records

Practo Ray fits ENT OPDs well. Its calendar handles online booking through Practo.com, sends SMS confirmations and reminders, and stores patient history, prescriptions and reports in one digital record. The CM Atom plan costs ₹999 a month on a one-year term, with 3,000 free SMS and 5 GB storage. Watch the extras: per-appointment fees can push the real cost above the headline price.

## KiraDoc — AI notes from the consultation

KiraDoc listens to the consultation and drafts structured SOAP notes in seconds, saving typing time. It supports 13 Indian languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Marathi, and is ABDM and ABHA ready. The free Starter tier covers 50 consultations a month; the Clinic plan costs ₹2,999 a month for up to 1,000 consultations and five practitioners. Always get the patient's consent before recording.

## Hearing Test app — hearing screening

The free Hearing Test app (by e-audiologia) turns a phone into a basic hearing screener: the patient listens to tones through headphones and gets a simple audiogram. Indian studies back it for screening: a 2024 study of 250 adults found fair agreement with pure tone audiometry, and a 2025 study reported about 85% sensitivity. It cannot test bone conduction or middle-ear disease, so flagged patients still need proper audiometry.

## Using these tools in India

These tools work in India without a VPN, with rupee pricing, Indian languages and SMS reminders patients read. Start with the free tiers — KiraDoc's 50 free consultations and the free Hearing Test app — and add Practo Ray when daily patient numbers make manual booking painful. ## What to avoid

Never use a phone hearing test as a diagnosis, and never let an AI note enter the record unchecked — scribes can mishear drug names and dosages. Avoid tools that store patient data abroad without a clear privacy policy.

## FAQ

**Can a phone app replace pure tone audiometry?**

No. Phone apps only screen for hearing loss. Pure tone audiometry in a sound-treated room remains the gold standard — only it measures bone conduction and confirms the type and degree of loss.

**Is it legal to record consultations with an AI scribe in India?**

Recording needs the patient's consent, and India's data-protection law makes consent a clear duty. Tell patients before you start and stop if they refuse.

**Do these tools work for a solo ENT clinic?**

Yes. A solo clinic can run on KiraDoc's free tier and the free Hearing Test app, then add Practo Ray when bookings grow.

## Final verdict

Practo Ray for bookings and records, KiraDoc for notes, and a free hearing-test app for screening — a solid, low-cost ENT setup.
