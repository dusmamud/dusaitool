For sugarcane growers, the best free starting kit is Plantix for disease diagnosis, IFFCO Kisan for weather and mandi prices, and Kisan Suvidha for official advisories. None pays your cane dues or replaces your mill slip — but they cover spotting red rot early, timing irrigation and harvest, and tracking dues.

## Plantix — photo diagnosis for red rot and pests

Plantix turns an Android phone into a crop doctor. Photograph a sick leaf or stalk and it suggests a probable disease or pest within seconds. It covers around 30 crops, works in Hindi, and has over 10 million downloads. Treat it as a hint, not a lab test — confirm with your agriculture officer first.

## IFFCO Kisan — weather, prices and voice advice

IFFCO Kisan gives weather forecasts, mandi prices and crop advisory in 11 Indian languages, in text and audio. Check rain before irrigating, compare mandi prices before selling intercrops or jaggery, and follow seasonal cane advice. It is free and needs no VPN.

## Kisan Suvidha — the official government app

Kisan Suvidha is built by the Department of Agriculture, Cooperation and Farmers Welfare with CDAC Mumbai. It gives weather forecasts, market prices, plant protection information and dealer details. It is the one to trust when an app and a WhatsApp forward disagree. Check it before a harvest or a spray.

## Using these tools in India

Sugarcane takes 10 to 18 months from planting to harvest, so records matter. Keep a notebook per plot: planting date, variety, costs, irrigation, cane supplied, slips and payments. Under the Sugarcane (Control) Order, 1966, mills must pay the Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) within 14 days. For 2025–26 the FRP is widely reported as ₹355 per quintal, though some pages list ₹340 — check the official notification first. Photograph every mill slip the day you get it.

## What to avoid

No app can force a mill to pay, so never pay for one promising guaranteed cane payment. Never spray on one photo diagnosis alone, and never share bank details, OTP or Aadhaar with anyone claiming to release cane dues. Prices change, so re-check the official page before paying.

## FAQ

**Can an app tell me when my cane will be paid?**

No. Payment depends on your mill and the state cane department, not on any app. Track each delivery and payment, and follow up with the mill if payment crosses 14 days.

**Which app is best for sugarcane disease?**

Plantix is the strongest choice for photo-based diagnosis, because it is built for exactly that job. Confirm its suggestion with a local agriculture officer, especially for red rot, which can destroy a whole crop if misdiagnosed.

**Are these apps really free?**

All three are free to download and use for their core features. Free limits can change, so check the app's official listing before assuming a feature is still free.

## Final verdict

Plantix, IFFCO Kisan and Kisan Suvidha form the best free starter kit for Indian sugarcane growers. Start with Plantix for disease checks, add the other two for weather and prices, and keep your own mill records.
