## The short answer

For most small ambulance operators in India, the best setup is LifeSupport EMS for dispatch, MedCab Partner for fleet and drivers, and Zoho Invoice for GST billing. Together they cover bookings, the nearest available vehicle, hospital handover, and clean invoices. In a real emergency, call 108 first.

## Quick comparison

| Need | Best pick | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Dispatch | LifeSupport EMS | Built in India for ambulances |
| Bookings and fleet | MedCab Partner | Made for Indian ambulance owners |
| GST billing | Zoho Invoice | Free plan, GST invoices |

## 1. LifeSupport EMS — dispatch and control room

LifeSupport EMS is a dispatch system from Interaxe Technologies in Mysuru, Karnataka. It handles patient intake, assigns the nearest ambulance, tracks vehicles on live GPS maps, and keeps shift and trip records. It powered Karnataka's COVID warroom dispatch, coordinating 1,000+ ambulances in Bengaluru, Hubli and Dharwad. Pricing is not published, so ask for a quote first.

## 2. MedCab Partner — bookings and fleet

MedCab Partner is an Android app for Indian ambulance owners to manage drivers and vehicles. Owners can add drivers and ambulances, see each driver's live GPS location and on-duty status, and dispatch the nearest available driver. It suits small fleets that take bookings by phone and need one screen for who is free. Free to download from Google Play — check the listing for in-app charges first.

## 3. Zoho Invoice — GST billing

Zoho Invoice turns each trip into a proper GST invoice with payment tracking, so hospitals, companies and families get clear bills instead of handwritten slips. Most sources say the free plan covers 500 invoices a year for 2 users, though one source calls it unlimited — re-check Zoho's pricing page before promising a client anything. Payment links via gateways such as Razorpay help small operators get paid faster.

## Using these tools in India

Start with the free pieces: MedCab Partner for daily fleet work and Zoho Invoice for billing, then add LifeSupport EMS when call volume grows. Keep patient details private, share live location links only with consent, and confirm hospital beds by phone — no tool can guarantee a bed. For hospital workflows, see our guide on [AI tools for hospitals](/blog/ai-tools-for-hospitals-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not promise arrival times an app cannot guarantee, and never delay calling 108 while fiddling with software. Avoid tools that store patient data abroad without clear consent, and never share patient records on public WhatsApp groups.

## FAQ

**Is there a free ambulance booking app in India?**

MedCab Partner is free to download and is built for ambulance owners rather than patients. In an emergency, patients should call 108, the free government ambulance number.

**Which tool handles hospital handover best?**

LifeSupport EMS, because dispatchers track the ambulance live and alert the receiving hospital before arrival. Even so, a phone call to the hospital remains the reliable backup.

**Can Zoho Invoice handle ambulance trip billing?**

Yes. Each trip becomes a GST invoice listing distance, waiting time and oxygen charges separately, with payment links for online payment.

**Do these tools work without a VPN in India?**

Yes. All three are made for or widely used in India. Prices and free limits change often, so re-check the official pricing or help page before paying.
