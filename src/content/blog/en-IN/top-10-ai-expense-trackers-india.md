These ten AI expense trackers work in India; free limits vary.

1. Wallet auto-categorises; free: 3 accounts.
2. Spendee scans receipts; free: 1 wallet.
3. Money Lover tracks wallets; free: 1 wallet.
4. Money Manager logs manually; free: 15 accounts.
5. Zoho Expense scans receipts; free: 20 scans.
6. Expensify scans receipts; free: 25 SmartScans.
7. Splitwise splits bills; free: about 4 expenses daily.
8. Goodbudget budgets envelopes; free: 20 envelopes.
9. Spendex reads bank SMS; free: 2 accounts.
10. jUMPP predicts spending; free: core features.

## FAQ

**Which suits beginners best?** Spendee for receipts; Wallet for budgets.

## Final verdict

Start with Wallet personally and Zoho Expense for business.
