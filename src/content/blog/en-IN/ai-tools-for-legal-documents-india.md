If you need a rental agreement, an affidavit, or a simple contract in India, you no longer have to start from a blank page. Several free tools can draft the document from your details in minutes. But read this first: these tools produce templates and first drafts, not legal advice. A wrong clause, a wrong stamp value, or an outdated section number can cost far more than the draft saved. Use the free tools for the first draft, then get an advocate to check anything that involves real money, property, employment, or a dispute.

The short answer is simple. For a free, no-signup first draft, start with [OneFiling's AI draft generator](https://onefiling.in/draft-generator/) or [GetNyay's free templates](https://getnyay.in/templates). For a rental agreement that is checked clause by clause, look at [DocScore AI's rent agreement tool](https://www.docscoreai.com/drafting/rent-agreement). For a basic ready-made template you can fill and download, [Vakilsearch's documents section](https://vakilsearch.com/documents) has a free tier. A general chatbot such as ChatGPT or Claude can help you word a letter, but never paste sensitive personal details into one, and never trust its section numbers without checking them.

## OneFiling: the free AI draft generator for contracts and notices

OneFiling's AI draft generator covers rental agreements, legal notices, non-disclosure agreements, employment contracts, affidavits, board resolutions, and consent letters, and it also offers reply drafts for tax, GST, and legal notices. You describe what you need in plain language and it produces a clean draft in seconds, which you can download or copy immediately.

The free limit is unusually clear: its own FAQ says the generator is completely free with no signup. That is the strongest free offer among the tools in this article. There is no stated document cap on the page I checked, but free offers like this can change, so re-check the page before you rely on it for a deadline.

What makes OneFiling worth starting with is its own honesty about the limits of AI drafting. Its page warns that the quality of a draft depends almost entirely on the specifics you give it: who the parties are, what was agreed, the amounts, the dates, and the outcome you want. A vague prompt such as "write a legal notice" produces boilerplate; a prompt with exact facts produces something close to usable. It also warns you to check the parts a generator cannot know: exact legal names and addresses, the jurisdiction clause, the governing law, the notice period, and the section numbers cited. That last point matters a lot in India right now, because the criminal codes changed to the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) in 2023. A citation that was correct under the old code and is repeated out of habit is exactly the kind of error that survives an inattentive read.

Use OneFiling when you need a first draft fast and you are willing to read it line by line. Do not use it as the final word on a live dispute. Its own guidance says the same: treat the output as a competent first draft, and have an advocate review anything with real money or liability attached. If you are a freelancer who needs client contracts, the same discipline applies — see also [AI tools for freelancers in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-freelancers-india/).

## GetNyay: free bilingual templates in Hindi and English

GetNyay takes a different approach: instead of an open-ended chatbot, it gives you ready-made templates for specific Indian documents, in both Hindi and English. Its template list includes a general affidavit, a non-disclosure agreement, a cheque bounce notice under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, a legal notice for breach of contract, a consumer complaint for the District Consumer Commission, an employment termination letter, a money recovery legal notice, and a legal notice for non-payment of rent.

The free limit is stated plainly on its templates page: every template is free, and you can open the generator, fill it in, edit any line, and download a print-ready Word (.docx) or PDF document with no watermark, no payment, and no signup. That makes it the easiest option if you already know which document you need and just want the correct format.

The bilingual option is genuinely useful in India. Many people need a notice or affidavit in Hindi for a local matter, and a machine-translated legal document is a bad idea. Here the Hindi versions are provided as templates in their own right, so you are not relying on a translation pass. Still, check the Hindi wording as carefully as the English: a template cannot know whether your facts fit its standard clauses.

GetNyay is best for standard, routine documents: a rent non-payment notice, a cheque bounce notice, a simple affidavit. It is not a substitute for advice when the facts are unusual, when the other side has a lawyer, or when the amount at stake is large. If the document will be filed somewhere, read it against the filing requirements first — a consumer complaint, for example, has its own format and fee rules at the commission.

## ChatGPT and Claude: useful for wording, dangerous for facts

General chatbots such as ChatGPT and Claude are not legal tools, but many people in India use them to draft letters and simple agreements, so they belong in this article with a warning attached. Both have free tiers: ChatGPT's free tier includes unlimited text chats per OpenAI's pricing page, and Claude's free tier has usage limits measured in hours and weeks. Sources disagree on the exact current model names and limits — one comparison I checked mentioned GPT-6 Luna for ChatGPT free and Claude Sonnet 5.5 for Claude free, while another mentioned different model names — which is itself the lesson: free chatbot limits change often, so check the official pricing page on the day you use them, and do not build a deadline around a free tier.

What chatbots are good at is wording: turning a rough instruction into a polite, structured letter, or turning a list of agreed points into a readable draft agreement. What they are bad at is exactly what matters in legal documents: correct section numbers, current law, jurisdiction-specific rules, and whether a clause is enforceable in your state. They can also invent plausible-sounding citations. Never send a chatbot draft without checking every number, date, name, and section reference against the real statute or a lawyer.

There is also a privacy rule that matters more here than in any other article in this series. Do not paste Aadhaar numbers, bank details, addresses, or the full facts of a dispute into a general chatbot. Free chatbot chats may be used for training unless you change the settings, and a legal dispute is the worst possible thing to leak into a training set. For the same reason, be careful with any drafting tool: read its privacy policy before uploading an existing agreement for analysis. ## Making the draft usable in India: stamp paper, e-stamping, and registration

A draft is not a finished legal document. In India, a rental agreement must be executed on non-judicial stamp paper, and the stamp duty varies by state. DocScore AI's own guidance gives Maharashtra as an example, where it says the duty is 0.25% of annual rent — but that is an example for one state, not a rate you can apply everywhere. Check your state's rate before you buy stamp paper or pay online.

E-stamping is the modern route and is available in many states. It is a computerised, tamper-proof method of paying stamp duty: instead of buying physical stamp paper from a licensed vendor, you pay through an authorised portal and receive a Unique Identification Number (UIN)-based e-stamp certificate. The certificate is generated by the Stock Holding Corporation of India (SHCIL) or state-authorised agencies and is verifiable online. The route varies by state: Karnataka uses Kaveri 2.0 for digital e-stamps, while Maharashtra uses its GRAS and IGR leave and licence services. Always use the applicable government portal and treat its issued amount as final. You can start at [SHCIL's e-stamping page](https://www.stockholding.com/estamp-index.php) and then follow your state's portal from there.

Two more practical points. First, a rent agreement is legally valid when signed by both parties in the presence of witnesses, and getting it notarised or registered with the local sub-registrar gives stronger enforceability, especially for agreements exceeding 11 months — which is why so many Indian rental agreements are written for 11 months. Second, keep the final version you actually signed, not just the generated text, together with the date, the mode of delivery, and any acknowledgement. If a dispute arises months later, that bundle is what your advocate will ask for first.

## What to avoid

Avoid drafting a document from memory when the facts are disputed. If the other side disagrees about dates, amounts, or what was agreed, a generated draft will happily produce a confident document based on your version alone, and that document can hurt you if it is wrong. Get the facts and the proof first, then draft.

Avoid copying a template from a random blog or a forwarded WhatsApp document. Templates from unknown sources may carry old law, wrong state rules, or clauses that were never valid in India. Use the named tools above, whose pages you can check, rather than a file of unknown origin.

Avoid signing anything you have not read in full. AI drafts look polished, and that polish is the risk: a lock-in clause, a penalty clause, or a jurisdiction clause you did not notice is still binding once you sign. Read every clause, especially lock-in period, deposit refund, termination notice, and jurisdiction.

Avoid sending legal notices in anger. A legal notice is a formal step that can start a dispute process. A chatbot can draft one in seconds, but it cannot tell you whether sending it helps or harms your position. If the matter involves a dispute, talk to an advocate before you send anything, even if the draft itself was free.

Avoid uploading sensitive documents to tools you have not checked. An existing agreement contains names, addresses, and financial terms. Before uploading one for analysis, read the tool's privacy policy and remove anything the analysis does not need. If the tool is a general chatbot, do not upload it at all.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is an AI-drafted rent agreement legally valid in India?** Yes, when it is signed by both parties in the presence of witnesses and executed on the correct stamp paper or e-stamp for your state. Notarisation or registration with the local sub-registrar gives stronger enforceability, especially for agreements exceeding 11 months. The AI draft itself is only the text; the signing and stamping are what make it a legal document.

**Are these tools a substitute for a lawyer?** No. They are templates and first drafts. They are fine for routine, standard documents that you read carefully. For anything involving significant money, property, employment termination, or an active dispute, get an advocate to review the document. That review is the part you should pay for, not the first draft.

**Which free tool should I start with?** If you know the document type, start with GetNyay for a ready-made bilingual template. If you need a custom draft from your facts, start with OneFiling's free generator. If you already have a rental agreement and want to check it for risky clauses, look at DocScore AI's analysis. If you want a basic template from a large platform, Vakilsearch's free tier is the fallback.

**Do I need stamp paper for an AI-drafted agreement?** Yes. A rent agreement must be executed on non-judicial stamp paper or through e-stamping, and the duty varies by state. Use your state's government portal or SHCIL's e-stamping route, and treat the issued amount as final. Do not guess the stamp value from another state's example.

**Can I get these documents in Hindi?** GetNyay provides ready-made Hindi templates alongside English ones. The other tools draft mainly in English. Do not rely on a machine translation of a legal clause you are about to sign; use a Hindi template or get the Hindi wording checked.

**What should I check before sending a legal notice generated by AI?** Check the exact legal names and addresses of the parties, the jurisdiction clause, the governing law, the notice period, the amounts, the dates, and every section number cited — especially since the criminal codes changed to BNS, BNSS, and BSA in 2023. Then ask whether sending the notice helps your position, and talk to an advocate first if there is a live dispute.

**Is it safe to upload my existing agreement to an AI analysis tool?** Only after reading the tool's privacy policy. Remove details the analysis does not need, and never upload a sensitive agreement to a general chatbot. Keep the final signed version, the date, and the delivery acknowledgement together in one file.

Start with GetNyay or OneFiling for free templates, then get a lawyer to check anything with real money at stake. That is the whole method: free tools for the first draft, a human advocate for the final check, and your state's e-stamping portal for the finished document.
