If you are a track and field athlete in India, start with Strava free for your training log and Intervals.icu free for performance analysis. That pair costs nothing, works in India without a VPN, and covers recording every session and spotting load climbing too fast. Add a paid tool only when a specific limit blocks you.

Track and field is not one sport: sprinters need video, distance runners need pace and load trends, and throwers need session notes. No single app does all of that well.

## Strava: the training log most athletes already use

Strava's free tier records GPS activities with distance, pace, splits and elevation, keeps your full activity history, and syncs from almost every watch brand. The full Training Log, Fitness and Freshness charts, route building and segment leaderboards need the subscription. Prices disagree — sources quote $11.99 a month or $79.99 a year in the US, others $59.99 a year — so check Strava's pricing page first.

## TrainingPeaks: the coach's workspace

TrainingPeaks is where coaches write plans and athletes report back. Its free Basic account lets you log workouts, sync devices, follow a training plan and work with a coach — enough if your coach does the analysis. Premium adds the Performance Management Chart of fitness, fatigue and form, plus drag-and-drop scheduling. Sources quote $19.99 a month or $119.99 to $134.99 a year, and disagree, so re-check the official pricing page.

## Garmin Connect: free, but only with Garmin hardware

Garmin Connect is the free companion app for Garmin watches, and the deepest free analysis if you own the watch. It turns runs, sleep, heart-rate variability and load into Training Status, Training Readiness, Body Battery and VO2 max estimates, and Garmin Coach offers free adaptive running plans. The catch is hardware: even cheaper Garmin models cost several thousand rupees. See also our [AI fitness tools in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-fitness-india/) guide.

## Athletica.ai: the true AI coach in this list

Athletica.ai builds adaptive plans for running, triathlon, cycling, rowing and HYROX, adjusting daily from your training data, heart-rate variability, perceived effort and notes. It syncs with Garmin, Strava, Coros and Wahoo. There is no permanent free tier — only a 14-day free trial with no card required, then about $19.90 a month. It suits endurance athletes more than sprinters or throwers, so trial it in a normal fortnight and cancel if the plan never changes sensibly.

## Onform: video analysis for technique events

For sprints, hurdles, jumps and throws, numbers are not enough — you need to see the movement. Onform offers slow-motion and frame-by-frame playback, drawing tools, voice-over feedback and side-by-side video comparison for frame-by-frame coaching. The Basic plan is free when connected to a coach; paid individual plans start at about $9.99 a month for up to 1,000 videos. Some features work better on iOS, so check the app listing for your phone first.

## Using these tools in India

All five tools work in India without a VPN, with Android and iOS apps. Most sources quote prices in dollars and they change often; the rupee price in the Play Store or App Store is what you actually pay, so confirm it on the official pricing page before paying. Verify a personal best on a measured track, not phone GPS alone. Video uploads need Wi-Fi, so record at the ground and upload at home. ## What to avoid

Do not buy three subscriptions at once — one log, one analysis layer, and your coach's system is the ceiling. Do not trust an AI readiness score over pain: green never means a sore tendon is fine. Keep clubs and posts private, and never share children's training videos publicly. Never pay from a screenshot price; re-check the official pricing page first.

## FAQ

**Which AI tool is best for track and field athletes in India?** Strava free for logging plus Intervals.icu free for analysis is the best start, because both cost nothing and work in India without a VPN. Add TrainingPeaks if your coach uses it, and Onform for video work.

**Is there a completely free AI training app for athletes?** Yes. Intervals.icu offers fitness, fatigue and form charts free, Strava's free tier logs unlimited activities, and Garmin Connect is free if you own a Garmin watch.

**Can AI replace my coach?** No. AI adjusts plans from data, but it cannot see your technique, manage an injury, or make race-day calls. Treat any training or health suggestion as general information, not professional advice.

**Do these tools work without a smartwatch?** Yes. Strava, TrainingPeaks, Athletica.ai and Onform all work with a phone alone, using GPS or manual entries. A watch adds heart rate and sleep data, but it is not required.

**How much should an Indian athlete budget for these apps?** Nothing to start — the free pair covers logging and analysis. If you later pay, expect one subscription at a time, and check the rupee price in the app store first.

Start free with Strava plus Intervals.icu; add TrainingPeaks only when a coach asks for it, and never let an app overrule pain.
