## The direct answer

If you run a travel agency in India — a one-person shop in Jaipur, a family agency in Kochi, or a small team selling Kashmir, Kerala, Rajasthan and international group tours — the best AI setup is not one magic platform that does everything. It is a small stack that matches how Indian customers actually buy travel: they discover you on Instagram or Google, ask questions on WhatsApp, compare two or three package options, pay in parts, and then need documents, reminders and help while they travel.

Start with three things. First, a general AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Gemini for drafting package descriptions, day-wise itineraries, customer replies and Hindi-to-English translations. Second, a proper itinerary builder such as Travefy, so the quote a customer receives looks professional, branded and easy to open on a phone, instead of a long forwarded text message. Third, WhatsApp handled properly — the free WhatsApp Business app for a very small agency, or a WhatsApp platform such as Wati or Interakt with an AI agent once enquiry volume grows beyond what one phone can handle.

Only after those three work should you add a customer system and a payment system. A CRM such as Bigin by Zoho keeps every lead, follow-up, passport detail and payment stage in one place, so a hot lead from last week does not get lost in a chat thread. Payment links from Razorpay let customers pay by UPI, card or net banking without you needing a website, and they create a cleaner record than collecting large sums into a personal UPI account. Design work for posters, package cards and social posts can be done in Canva, and India-specific trip planning can be drafted with Tripzio, which understands Indian trains, budgets and Hindi or Hinglish requests.

Do not buy an expensive all-in-one tour-operator platform first. Tools such as Tourwriter and Lemax are built for destination management companies and operators designing complex, multi-day, tailor-made trips with live supplier rates and costing. They are powerful, but their pricing is typically quoted rather than listed, and they are overkill for an agency whose main problem is slow replies, messy quotes and lost follow-ups. Fix replies, quotes and follow-ups first. The fancy platform can wait until your monthly package volume proves you need it.

A warning that applies to every tool in this article: prices, free limits and features change often, sometimes without notice. Before you pay for anything, or before you promise a customer a price or a deadline that depends on a tool, re-check the official pricing and help page for that tool. Where this article reports a limit or a price, it says where that figure came from and that it must be re-checked, because a number that was true in October 2026 may not be true when you read this.

This article is general information only, not professional tax, legal or financial advice. Travel money, GST invoicing and business registration rules depend on your exact business structure, so check official sources and your chartered accountant before changing how you bill or collect money.

## ChatGPT: the everyday drafting assistant

ChatGPT is the most useful general starting point for a travel agent because almost every daily writing job in an agency is a drafting job: a three-day Udaipur package description, a polite reply to a customer who wants the price lowered, a day-wise plan for a family of four, a cancellation message, or a follow-up that does not sound pushy. You describe the customer, the budget, the dates and the style of trip, and ChatGPT produces a draft in seconds. You then correct it, because the draft is only a starting point, never the final quote.

Its free tier in India is genuinely usable for this kind of work. According to help and pricing pages checked in October 2026, ChatGPT Free offers unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse-prevention safeguards, with web search, file uploads and image creation included. The limits that do exist apply separately to things such as file uploads, image generation, voice and data analysis, rather than to ordinary chat. That is good news for an agent, but it is not a promise: OpenAI changes limits, and heavy image or research use can still hit caps. If a feature suddenly stops, check the in-app usage screen before assuming the tool is broken.

The practical rule for a travel agent is simple. Use ChatGPT to write, summarise and rephrase, and never let it invent the facts that cost money. It can draft a beautiful Kashmir itinerary, but it can also confidently name a hotel that is closed, a train that does not run on that day, or a price that was true last season. Every hotel name, train number, flight time, inclusion and price in an AI draft must be checked against the supplier, the airline or the hotel before it goes to the customer. Keep a template for this: destination, dates, number of travellers, hotel category, transport, inclusions, exclusions, payment schedule and cancellation terms. When the AI fills the template, your job is to verify each line, not to forward the draft untouched.

ChatGPT also helps with the part of agency work nobody counts: tone. A customer who writes in Hinglish should get a reply that feels natural, not a stiff English paragraph. Ask the assistant to draft the same reply in simple English and in Hindi, read both, and send the one that sounds like your agency. Save the replies that work as templates, so the next agent on your team does not start from zero every time.

## Gemini: the Google-connected alternative

Gemini is the best alternative — and for many Indian agents, the better first choice — if your agency already lives inside Google: Gmail for supplier emails, Google Sheets for package costing, Google Maps for distances, and Google Flights and Hotels for checking fares. Gemini is presented by Google as an AI trip planner that can find flights and hotels through Google Flights and Hotels, build a custom route grouped by neighbourhood, and adjust plans when something changes, such as a closed attraction or sudden rain. For a customer who says "four days in Goa under this budget," Gemini can produce a structured starting plan faster than searching ten tabs one by one.

Its free tier works differently from ChatGPT, and the difference matters. Google describes standard limits for users without a paid plan as a compute budget that refreshes every five hours, up to a weekly cap, rather than a fixed number of messages. Heavier features — the Pro model, extended thinking, Deep Research, and image or video generation — use up that budget faster, and Deep Research may pause at busy times for free users. The free context window is reported as 32,000 tokens, which is enough for a long article but not for a very large PDF of supplier rates. You can check what remains under Settings and then Usage Limits on the Gemini website.

For an agency, the honest comparison is this: ChatGPT Free is simpler for unlimited everyday drafting, while Gemini Free is stronger when you want flight and hotel checking inside the same conversation and you already use Google tools. Some Indian users may also already have a paid Google AI plan at no extra cost — for example, eligible Jio users on certain unlimited 5G plans have been offered Google AI Pro free for a period, and eligible students have been offered Google AI Plus free for a year. Check whether such an offer already applies to you before paying for either assistant, and check its current terms, because these offers change.

Whichever assistant you pick, the verification rule is identical. Gemini, like ChatGPT, links out to booking sites rather than booking for you, and its flight and hotel results must be re-checked on the day you quote, because fares and availability move hourly in peak season.

## Travefy: itineraries that win the booking

Travefy is the tool in this list most directly built for travel advisors and small tour teams. Its job is to turn supplier confirmations, hotel details and destination notes into a branded, mobile-friendly itinerary that a customer can open anywhere, with your logo on it rather than Travefy's. That matters commercially: a customer comparing three agencies will remember the one whose itinerary looked professional and was easy to share with family on WhatsApp.

Its standout feature is Smart Import. You drop in a supplier confirmation or upload a PDF, and the AI turns it into a day-by-day trip with dates, times and booking details already in place, instead of you retyping every check-in time by hand. It also offers destination guides, hotel details and templates from other advisors, which help a small agency produce a polished multi-city or group itinerary without hiring a designer. A comparison of itinerary builders published in October 2026 listed Travefy as a subscription product for advisors and small tour teams, but noted that no single list price was published on the pages reviewed — so treat Travefy as a "check the pricing page and ask for a trial" purchase, not as a fixed-price buy.

Travefy is an itinerary and client-document tool, not a reservation system. It does not replace your supplier bookings, your accounting or your payment collection. Its value is speed and presentation: quotes go out faster, look better, and are easier for the customer to approve. If your agency still sends quotes as long text messages or unformatted PDFs, Travefy alone can lift your conversion rate, because the customer can see the whole trip clearly and say yes sooner. Before committing, run one real package through it end to end — import a confirmation, brand it, send it to your own phone on WhatsApp, and check how it looks on a small Android phone, because that is how most Indian customers will open it.

## Tripzio: India-first trip drafts with trains and budgets

Tripzio is different from the global tools because it is built only for travel within India. You describe a trip in one line — in English, Hindi, Bengali or Hinglish, for example "Kolkata se 5 din Darjeeling, 2 log, ₹30,000 budget" — and it returns a day-by-day itinerary with named places, timings, entry fees, real Indian Railways train names and numbers, hotel suggestions that fit the budget, and a full cost breakdown across transport, stay, food and activities. It also warns about festival and peak-date clashes, such as Durga Puja or Diwali, when hotels book out and prices rise, and it gives season and weather advice for the month of travel.

For an Indian agency, that India-specific knowledge is the point. A global assistant can suggest a generic Rajasthan circuit; Tripzio starts from how Indians actually travel — by train, on a family budget, across multi-city circuits such as Ooty to Munnar to Alleppey — and it produces plans in Hindi or Bengali as well as English, so the itinerary can be shared with the family member who actually approves the trip. Its site states that planning is free to try with no signup and no credit card for the first trip, that a free account lets you save, share and download plans, and that there is no subscription. It also states clearly that Tripzio does not take bookings itself; it links to booking sites so you book directly.

Use Tripzio as a drafting and research tool, not as your booking record. Let it build the first draft of a domestic package, then replace its hotel suggestions with your contracted hotels, verify every train number and timing on the day of quoting, and re-price the package with your own margins. For agents, it also offers planning for multiple clients and branded plans. If most of your business is domestic India travel, Tripzio can save hours of first-draft work every week. If most of your business is international, ChatGPT or Gemini plus Travefy will serve you better, and Tripzio becomes a secondary tool for the domestic side.

## WhatsApp: where Indian travel is actually sold

Almost every Indian travel booking conversation happens on WhatsApp, so this is the tool category to get right before any other. The free WhatsApp Business app already covers a one-person or two-person agency well: a business profile with your address and hours, a product catalogue for your popular packages, quick replies for common questions such as "price?", "inclusions?" and "EMI available?", greeting and away messages, and labels to mark leads as new, quoted, paid or travelling. Used consistently, labels alone prevent the most common agency failure — a good lead forgotten in a long chat list.

When enquiry volume grows beyond what one phone and one person can answer — during holiday season, after an Instagram reel goes viral, or when you run click-to-WhatsApp ads — a WhatsApp platform with an AI agent becomes worth considering. Wati and Interakt are two India-relevant options. Wati's AI agent, called Astra, answers questions, qualifies leads and hands over to your team, learning from your PDFs, Word documents and website. Interakt offers AI agents for travel, including itinerary sharing, document collection and e-ticket messages, with a lead qualification agent that asks a few questions and saves the answers to the contact. A comparison of WhatsApp AI agents noted an important market difference: pricing and packaging for these AI add-ons differ between India and other countries — for example, Wati's India Growth plan has been reported to include Astra, while its US pricing lists Astra as a separate add-on on higher plans. Sources disagree by market, and plans change, so check the India pricing page and confirm exactly what is included before paying.

Whichever route you take, set three rules for AI on WhatsApp. First, the AI answers only from your own approved material: your package list, your price list, your terms. It must not invent a hotel, a discount or a departure date. Second, it must hand over to a human the moment money, a date change, a cancellation or a complaint is involved — never let an AI confirm a booking or a refund on its own. Third, every AI conversation must be reviewable, so you can read what was promised and correct it the next day. Meta's own business agent is also an option to try first where it is offered, described as free to start, with more use through paid Meta plans where available. Try the free route first, measure how many enquiries the AI genuinely resolves, and only then pay for a platform.

## Bigin by Zoho: a CRM small agencies can actually afford

A travel agency without a CRM runs on memory and chat search, and that breaks as soon as you have more than a handful of live enquiries. Bigin by Zoho is a small-business CRM that turns each enquiry into a trackable pipeline: new lead, quote sent, follow-up, advance paid, balance pending, documents collected, travelling, completed. You can see at a glance who needs a follow-up today, which packages are selling, and which customer paid what and when. It also offers contacts, products, activities, automations, dashboards, a business card scanner, and integrations with email, WhatsApp Business, Zoho Books and Zoho Invoice.

Its pricing is unusually transparent for India, which is why it earns a place here. Bigin's own India pricing clarification states a forever-free plan, with the Express Edition at ₹400 per user per month billed annually, Premier at ₹720, and Bigin 360 at ₹1,080, with local taxes extra. Those figures are from Bigin's own clarification page; the pricing page itself may show dollar figures depending on region, so make sure you are looking at the India price, and re-check before buying. For a two-person agency, the free plan is enough to test whether a pipeline habit sticks. If it does, the Express plan is cheaper than one lost booking a month.

Set Bigin up around your real sales stages, not around the default template. A travel pipeline might be: enquiry, itinerary shared, quote revised, advance received, balance due, documents pending, trip completed, review requested. Add custom fields for the things agents actually need: destination, travel month, group size, hotel category, passport status and preferred contact language. The CRM only helps if every enquiry goes into it on the same day it arrives; a CRM updated once a week is just an expensive address book. Connect it to WhatsApp and email so enquiries land in the pipeline automatically where possible, and review the "follow-up today" list every morning before you open Instagram.

## Canva: package posters customers forward

A travel package sells first on its image. Canva lets a small agency produce package posters, itinerary covers, social media posts, WhatsApp status images and price cards without hiring a designer for every festival offer. Its free plan includes a large template library and basic design tools, and its AI features — background removal, text-to-image and design suggestions — help a non-designer produce a clean poster quickly. A third-party check of free AI limits reported up to 20 uses per month of standard or premium AI tools on Canva's free plan, resetting monthly, with ultra tools excluded. Treat that figure as reported and re-checkable, not as a guarantee, because Canva changes AI allowances.

The practical value for an agency is speed and consistency. Build one branded template for each package type — Kashmir, Kerala, Dubai, group tours, honeymoon — with your agency name, phone number, price validity line and a clear "price per person" note, then reuse it for every new offer instead of designing from scratch. Use real photos of the destination or your own trip photos where you have permission, and never use a hotel's or another agency's photos as if they were yours. Every poster must carry the same honesty rule as every itinerary: the price, the dates and the inclusions on the poster must match the quote you will actually honour. A beautiful poster with a wrong price creates the exact complaint that costs you reviews.

Canva is not a booking tool and not a CRM. Its job ends when the customer messages you. Keep that boundary clean: Canva makes the offer attractive, WhatsApp answers the questions, Travefy presents the itinerary, Bigin tracks the lead, and Razorpay collects the payment. When each tool does one job well, the whole stack is cheaper and easier to fix than one platform that does five jobs badly.

## Razorpay Payment Links: getting paid without a website

Many small Indian agencies still collect package money into a personal UPI account, and that creates real problems. Personal accounts make accounting messy, make GST invoicing hard to link to the payment, and can attract scrutiny when large, frequent credits arrive in a savings account. Guides for Indian travel agents recommend the cleaner setup: a business current account in the agency's name, a business UPI VPA, a payment gateway for payment links, and a proper GST invoice for every booking. For large group bookings, a bank transfer by NEFT or RTGS against the invoice is often cleaner than splitting UPI payments.

Razorpay Payment Links fit this setup because they need no website or app. You create a link from the Razorpay dashboard, share it by WhatsApp, SMS or email, and the customer pays by UPI, card, net banking or EMI. Links can carry an expiry date, allow partial payments for advance and balance instalments, and send automated reminders for outstanding amounts. Razorpay's own documentation lists Payment Links as available in India, among other countries. Transaction fees vary by payment method and volume, and settlement timelines vary by provider, so check the current MDR and terms on Razorpay's official site — and compare Cashfree and PayU, the other two gateways commonly used by Indian travel businesses — before you commit.

The agency rule is simple: invoice first, then collect against that invoice, and never promise a booking as confirmed until the advance has actually arrived. A payment link also protects you in disputes, because there is a formal merchant record of what was paid, for which package, and on what terms, instead of a chat message saying "payment sent." Keep the link, the invoice and the itinerary together in your CRM record for that customer, so that six months later you can answer any question about that booking without searching five apps.

## Google Translate: serving customers in their language

India's travel market is multilingual, and language is often the difference between a quote that gets approved and one that gets ignored. Google Translate, used carefully, lets an agency share the same itinerary in English and Hindi, answer a customer who writes in Hindi or Hinglish, and draft simple messages in Bengali, Tamil or Marathi for customers who read those languages more comfortably. It is free, works in India without a VPN, and is already on every customer's phone, which makes it more practical than a niche translation app.

The limit is accuracy on the details that matter. Machine translation handles "five days, four nights, breakfast included" well, but it can garble hotel names, misplace a date, or turn a cancellation condition into something softer than you intended. The rule is: translate, then read the translation as if you were the customer, and check every name, date, price and condition against the English original before sending. For the final terms and cancellation policy, send the English version alongside the translation, and make it clear which version governs if there is a difference. ChatGPT and Gemini can also produce Hindi drafts that read more naturally than raw machine translation, but they need the same human check. Tripzio's Hindi and Bengali itinerary output is useful here too, because it is generated for travel from the start rather than translated afterwards.

## How to use these tools in India, day by day

The stack works best as a daily routine, not as a set of apps you open occasionally. In the morning, check Bigin for follow-ups due today and WhatsApp for overnight enquiries. Answer new enquiries with a quick reply and a question that qualifies the lead: destination, month, group size and budget. For a serious lead, draft the itinerary in ChatGPT or Gemini, or start from Tripzio for a domestic trip, then build the polished version in Travefy and send it on WhatsApp with one clear next step: "Reply YES and I will hold this price till tomorrow evening."

When the customer approves, move the lead in Bigin to the next stage, create the GST invoice with your accountant's structure, and send a Razorpay payment link for the advance, with the balance date written in the same message. Collect documents — ID proofs, passport details where needed — through WhatsApp or your CRM, not through random forwarded chats, and store them securely with access limited to your team. Before departure, send the final Travefy itinerary, hotel vouchers, emergency contact and a short Hindi or English note on what to carry. After the trip, ask for a review while the experience is fresh, and log what sold well so next season's packages start from evidence, not guesswork.

India-specific details deserve extra care. Train-heavy domestic packages must be re-checked against live railway timings, because a plan built weeks earlier can be wrong by the time the customer travels. Festival dates change hotel prices dramatically, so every quote needs a price-validity line. Group tours need a clear per-person price, a single supplement note, and a written rule for what happens if the group size falls below the minimum. And for international packages, visa timelines and passport validity are the customer's real deadline — an AI tool can remind you, but only you can check the embassy requirement for that nationality and that destination. Build these checks into your template, so no package goes out without them.

## What to avoid

Do not let an AI tool confirm availability, prices or bookings it cannot see. A chatbot that does not connect to your hotel inventory, your supplier rates or live flight data should never say "available" or "confirmed." It should say "let me check and confirm," and then a human should check. This single rule prevents most AI-related complaints in travel.

Do not publish AI-generated hotel photos, destination images or reviews as if they were real. A customer who arrives at a hotel that looks nothing like the AI image will blame you, not the tool. Use real photos with permission, label illustrative images honestly, and never fabricate a review or a testimonial.

Do not collect customer money into personal accounts, and do not promise a price, a visa outcome or a refund timeline that you cannot guarantee. AI drafts make promises sound confident; your business is responsible for keeping them. Keep customer identity documents, passport copies and payment details secure, share them only with the supplier who needs them, and delete what you no longer need. If a tool asks for broad access to your WhatsApp, your CRM or your customer list, check what it stores, where it stores it, and how to revoke access before connecting it.

Do not buy every tool at once. Start free, prove the workflow with ten real enquiries, and only pay for the tool that removes a bottleneck you can name. If follow-ups are the bottleneck, pay for the CRM. If replies are the bottleneck, pay for the WhatsApp platform. If quotes look weak, pay for the itinerary builder. A tool bought before its bottleneck is just a subscription.

## FAQ

**Which AI tool is best for a small travel agency in India?**
For most small agencies, the best starting combination is ChatGPT or Gemini for drafting, Travefy for professional itineraries, and the WhatsApp Business app for customer chats, with Bigin added once lead volume grows. There is no single best tool, because drafting, itinerary design, messaging and payments are different jobs, and each has its own best starting tool.

**Can I run my travel agency using only free AI tools?**
Yes, at a small scale. ChatGPT Free and Gemini's free tier cover drafting, Canva's free plan covers posters, the WhatsApp Business app covers messaging, Bigin's free plan covers basic lead tracking, and Tripzio is free to try for domestic planning. You will still pay transaction fees when you collect money, and free AI limits may slow you down in peak season, but a one-person agency can start without paid AI subscriptions.

**Does Travefy work for Indian travel agents?**
Yes, as an itinerary builder it works for any advisor who needs branded, mobile-friendly itineraries, and its Smart Import feature turns supplier confirmations into day-wise trips. It is not a booking or payment system, and its subscription price is not always listed publicly, so check the current pricing page and trial it with a real package before subscribing.

**Is Tripzio free, and does it book hotels or trains?**
Tripzio is free to try for planning, with no signup needed for the first trip and a free account for saving and sharing plans, according to its own site. It does not take bookings itself; it links to booking sites so you book directly, which means you still verify live prices and availability before quoting a customer.

**Should I use a WhatsApp AI agent or reply myself?**
Reply yourself while volume is low, because personal replies build trust and you learn what customers actually ask. Add a WhatsApp AI agent from Wati, Interakt or Meta's own agent when enquiries regularly go unanswered, and set it to answer only from your approved packages and prices, handing over to a human for payments, changes and complaints.

**What does Bigin by Zoho cost in India?**
Bigin offers a forever-free plan, and its own India clarification lists Express at ₹400 per user per month billed annually, Premier at ₹720 and Bigin 360 at ₹1,080, with taxes extra. Check the India pricing page before buying, because the price shown can differ by region and plans change.

**How should a travel agent collect payments safely in India?**
Use a business current account and a payment gateway such as Razorpay, Cashfree or PayU, invoice first, and collect against that invoice through a payment link or bank transfer. Avoid collecting large package sums into a personal UPI account, check current MDR and settlement terms on the provider's official site, and confirm your GST invoicing structure with your chartered accountant.

**Do AI itineraries need to be checked before sending to customers?**
Yes, always. AI tools can invent or outdated hotel names, train timings, flight times and prices, so every name, date, inclusion, exclusion and price must be verified against the supplier or live booking site before the itinerary goes to the customer. The AI draft saves writing time; it does not remove the verification step.

**Can AI tools help with Hindi-speaking customers?**
Yes. Tripzio produces itineraries in Hindi and Bengali as well as English, Google Translate handles quick message translation, and ChatGPT or Gemini can draft natural Hindi replies. Always have a human check names, dates, prices and cancellation terms in the translated version before sending it.

**What should a travel agent avoid when using AI?**
Avoid letting AI confirm availability or bookings it cannot see, publishing AI images or reviews as if they were real, promising visa outcomes or refund timelines you cannot guarantee, collecting money into personal accounts, and buying several paid tools before proving the workflow with free tiers on real enquiries.

## Final verdict

Start with ChatGPT or Gemini, Travefy and WhatsApp; add a CRM and payment links before any expensive all-in-one platform.

That order matters because it fixes the jobs that lose bookings first: slow replies, weak quotes and forgotten follow-ups. Once those three are clean and your payment record is proper, you will know from your own numbers — not from a sales page — whether a bigger tour-operator platform is worth paying for.
