For Indian businesses, free plans cover the basics: ChatGPT for copy, Canva for creatives, Buffer for scheduling.

1. ChatGPT — drafts ads, captions, emails; free plan covers text chats.
2. Canva — designs posts, ads; free plan gives about 20 AI uses monthly (sources differ).
3. Copy.ai — writes ad copy; free plan allows 2,000 words monthly.
4. Jasper — writes on-brand campaign copy; no free plan, 7-day trial only.
5. HubSpot — runs CRM, email, landing pages; free tools include 2,000 sends monthly.
6. Mailchimp — sends newsletters; free plan covers 500 contacts, 1,000 emails monthly.
7. Brevo — emails lists with automation; free plan allows unlimited contacts, 300 emails daily.
8. Buffer — schedules social posts; free plan covers 3 channels, 10 posts.
9. Semrush — finds keywords, tracks competitors; free account allows 10 searches daily.
10. Grammarly — polishes ad copy, emails; free plan includes 100 AI prompts monthly.

**Which free marketing tool should beginners start with?** ChatGPT and Canva — copy and creatives cover most daily work.

## Final verdict

Start with ChatGPT, Canva, Buffer free; add HubSpot or Brevo as your list grows.

Prices and free limits change — check official pricing before paying.
