Nursing in India means long shifts and heavy documentation. A 2025 study found nurses spend about 23% of a 12-hour shift on records. The short answer: use the free plans of ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for study help, blank handover templates and patient-education drafts; use Notion to plan shifts; and use UWorld for NCLEX-RN preparation.

One rule governs everything below. Never paste a patient's name, phone number, diagnosis or report into a general AI chatbot — these tools are not your hospital's approved record system. Check your employer's AI policy first. This article is general information only, not medical or professional advice.

## 1. ChatGPT — the everyday assistant

Ask ChatGPT to make quiz questions, explain a procedure simply, draft a shift-swap request, or build a blank handover template. Study Mode adds practice questions. Free plan: unlimited everyday text chats; uploads, images, voice and deep research are limited. Go costs ₹399 a month in India.

## 2. Claude — the careful writer

Claude suits long, careful writing: study summaries and plain-language handout drafts. Its free plan has a usage limit that resets over a rolling five-hour window. Pro gives about five times the free usage. You must be at least 18 to hold a Claude account. Verify every fact — it can sound confident about a dose and be wrong.

## 3. Google Gemini — the Google-apps helper

Gemini is the natural pick if your study life lives in Gmail, Google Docs and Google Drive. It is strong on Indian languages, including Hindi, which helps with patient-education drafts. The free plan comes with a Google account and has daily limits. Eligible students aged 18 or older can claim a year of AI Plus free, by 31 December 2026.

## 4. Notion — shift planning and study organisation

Notion is where the planning lives. Build one page per month with your roster, leave dates, exam dates and study targets. The free plan costs $0 for one person, with unlimited pages, 5 MB uploads and 7-day history. It includes only a trial of Notion AI, so pair it with a free chatbot for writing. Plus costs $10 per member a month billed yearly. Keep patient information out of it entirely.

## 5. UWorld NCLEX-RN — the exam question bank

For the NCLEX-RN, UWorld is the top question bank. It has more than 3,100 questions, with rationales by practising nurses and a study planner. It runs on web, iOS and Android. No free plan: $139 for 30 days to $449 for 730 days, $229 for 90 days, and self-assessments $25 each or $100 for all six. Buy the shortest plan covering your exam date.

## Using these tools in India

Most Indian nurses work rotating shifts, so the phone is the workplace. All five work in India without a VPN, on phone or laptop. Set the week's roster in Notion on Sunday, revise 20 minutes each evening, and open a blank handover template before each shift. For job hunting, a free chatbot can polish your CV — see our [AI tools for resume and jobs in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-resume-jobs-india/). If your hospital runs its own AI, use that for patient work and keep personal tools for personal work. See also our [AI tools for hospitals in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-hospitals-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not paste patient details into any chatbot or notes app — not even a first name or bed number. Do not let AI write a clinical note, dose or care plan without checking every line against the prescription and chart. Do not trust an AI answer on a protocol or drug interaction without checking an official source. Do not share your login. Hospital policy decides what is safe at work, not the marketing page.

## FAQ

**Can nurses use ChatGPT for patient notes?**
Only for blank templates and general drafts with no patient in them. Real patient notes belong in your hospital's approved record system.

**Which free AI tool is best for nursing students?**
Start with Gemini Free, and claim the free year of Google AI Plus if you are an eligible student, before 31 December 2026.

**Is there a free AI tool for nurse shift scheduling?**
None for individual nurses — hospital tools like ShiftWizard are bought by hospitals. For personal use, a Notion roster page or Google Calendar is the free option.

**Can AI help me pass the NCLEX-RN?**
It helps with revision and quizzes, but the question bank matters most. UWorld starts at $139 for 30 days with no free plan.

**Is it safe to use AI tools in Hindi for patient education?**
Yes, for general drafts — both handle Hindi well. Always have a colleague check the draft, because a translation error can mislead a patient.

**Do these tools work on a phone during a shift?**
Yes — all five work in India without a VPN. Keep personal tools off hospital devices unless your employer allows it.

## Final verdict

Use free ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for study and drafts, Notion for shifts, UWorld for NCLEX — never paste patient details into any of them.
