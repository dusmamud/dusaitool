Google Flow is the best free start for Indians; HeyGen suits presenters, Runway suits cinematic clips, and sources disagree on Pika.

## Top 10 tools

1. **Google Flow:** 50 free credits daily, about five Veo clips.
2. **Kling:** free login credits, watermarked clips, amount varies by account.
3. **Runway:** 125 one-time credits, enough for a few short clips.
4. **Pika:** free credits vary; watermarked short clips at 480p only.
5. **PixVerse:** signup bonus plus daily credits, about one clip daily.
6. **Hailuo:** daily free credits, about two or three watermarked clips.
7. **HeyGen:** three free avatar videos monthly, one minute each, watermarked.
8. **Synthesia:** 500 free credits monthly, about ten minutes, no downloads.
9. **InVideo AI:** free weekly minutes for quick short prompt-to-video drafts.
10. **Pictory:** no free plan, only a 14-day trial, ten minutes.

## FAQ

**Which free tool should beginners try first?** Google Flow, because its daily credits give about five short clips without paying.

## Final verdict

Start with Google Flow for free clips, HeyGen for presenters, and check limits before paying.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
