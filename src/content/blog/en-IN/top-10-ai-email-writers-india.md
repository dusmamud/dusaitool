ChatGPT and Claude cover most emails free; Gmail and Outlook add-ons handle replies in your inbox.

## Top 10

1. **ChatGPT:** drafts any email from a prompt; free, unlimited everyday chats.
2. **Claude:** careful, natural long drafts; free, daily message limits.
3. **Gemini in Gmail:** writes drafts in Gmail; in India, free access is limited, sources disagree.
4. **Grammarly:** fixes grammar, tone and rewrites; free, 100 AI prompts a month.
5. **QuillBot:** free email writer with formality levels; paraphraser caps at 100 words.
6. **Rytr:** template emails in 30+ languages; free, 10,000 characters a month.
7. **Mailmeteor:** AI writer in Gmail for bulk mail; free, 50 emails a day.
8. **MailMaestro:** replies in Outlook and Gmail; free, 3 requests a week.
9. **Lavender:** scores sales emails, suggests fixes; free, 5 emails a month.
10. **Shortwave:** AI Gmail client with smart replies; free plan, capped AI usage.

## FAQ

**Which free tool is best for daily emails?** ChatGPT for most, Grammarly to polish tone.

## Final verdict

Start with ChatGPT and Grammarly free; add Mailmeteor for bulk Gmail mail and Lavender for sales outreach.

Prices and free limits change — check the pricing page before paying.
