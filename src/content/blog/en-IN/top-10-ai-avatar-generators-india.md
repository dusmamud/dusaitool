Need a talking video avatar or a stylised profile picture? These ten cover both, and all work in India without a VPN.

## Top 10

1. HeyGen: talking presenter videos, free: 3 one-minute videos monthly.
2. Synthesia: polished training videos, free: 10 minutes monthly, no download.
3. Vidnoz: talking photo videos, free: about 60 seconds daily at 720p.
4. D-ID: makes still photos talk, free: 14-day trial with 3 minutes.
5. Colossyan: training videos with quizzes, free: trial only, limits vary by source.
6. Hedra: any photo becomes an expressive talking character, free: about 100 credits.
7. Fotor: professional headshots from selfies, free tier with limited generations.
8. Lensa: stylised Magic Avatars from selfies, paid packs, free trial only.
9. Canva: cartoon avatars inside its editor, free builder, limited AI credits.
10. VRoid Studio: free 3D anime avatars for games and VTubing, fully free.

Start with Vidnoz for free volume, HeyGen for your own face, and Fotor for profile pictures.
