Ten AI builders turn a short description into a live website, most free to start. All work in India without a VPN; free plans carry branding, subdomains, or page caps.

1. **Wix**: full site from a prompt; free plan, Wix subdomain, ads.
2. **Framer**: polished pages from prompts; free plan, capped AI credits.
3. **Hostinger**: cheap AI site with hosting; trial only (length varies), no free plan.
4. **Squarespace**: polished sites with AI text; 14-day trial, no free plan.
5. **Webflow**: visual builder with AI; free Starter, two static pages.
6. **GoDaddy Airo**: site plus logo from prompts; free plan, 50 AI credits monthly.
7. **Durable**: business site in seconds; free plan, three pages.
8. **10Web**: AI-built WordPress sites; 7-day trial, no free plan.
9. **Canva**: design-led one-page sites; free plan, Canva subdomain.
10. **Jimdo**: simple sites from short answers; free plan, five pages, ads.

**Which AI website builder is best for small businesses in India?** Wix, for its free plan and toolkit; Framer if design matters most.

## Verdict

Start free with Wix or Framer; pay only for a custom domain.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
