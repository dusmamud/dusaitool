A bookshop rarely fails for lack of good books. It fails slowly: stock is counted by memory, the same title is ordered twice, a customer is never told their book has arrived, and billing takes so long that a buyer walks out. These tools handle the mechanical work.

The short answer: Vyapar's free mobile app for GST billing and stock, ChatGPT's free tier for customer messages, and WhatsApp Business, which is free, for orders. Later, add Zoho Inventory for online orders, or Zoho Books for accounting while turnover stays under ₹25 lakh. If your CA already works in Tally, TallyPrime is the safe choice, though it costs more. Prices change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## Vyapar: billing and stock at the counter

Vyapar is the most popular free GST billing app for small Indian shops. The Android app is free, works offline, and covers GST invoices, stock tracking with low-stock alerts, and bills shared on WhatsApp. The free version is mobile-only; desktop and multi-user access are paid. Desktop Silver is listed at about ₹3,419–₹3,599 a year; check Vyapar's pricing page before paying.

## Zoho Inventory: orders from more than one place

Zoho Inventory is the tool to add when the shop sells beyond the counter: its own website, a marketplace, or phone orders from schools. Its free plan covers 50 orders a month for one user at one location. Standard is ₹999 a month billed annually, for 500 orders a month, excluding 18% GST. It connects with Zoho Books and supports barcode scanning. Its weakness is that it is built for general retail: there is no built-in book catalogue by ISBN.

## Zoho Books: accounting that stays free at small scale

Zoho Books is full cloud accounting with GST built in. Its free plan is genuinely usable: free while annual revenue stays under ₹25 lakh, for one user plus an accountant, with up to 1,000 invoices a year. It handles GST invoices, bank reconciliation and GSTR reports, and is a registered GST Suvidha Provider. Standard is ₹749 a month billed annually, for three users.

## TallyPrime: the CA standard

TallyPrime is the default choice when the shop's chartered accountant already works in Tally, which most Indian CAs do. It covers accounting, GST invoicing, inventory and banking in one desktop product. There is no free plan. Silver is ₹22,500 plus GST once, or ₹750 plus GST a month on rental. Annual TSS renewal is ₹4,500 plus GST for Silver.

## ChatGPT and WhatsApp Business: customers and orders

The cheapest AI in a bookshop is a chatbot used well, plus the free WhatsApp Business app. ChatGPT's free tier offers unlimited everyday text chats, with limits on uploads and images, and it works in India without a VPN. A bookseller can draft arrival messages, short recommendations and Instagram captions. Never let it invent a price, date or ISBN. WhatsApp Business is free and gives the shop a catalogue and customer labels.

## Using these tools in India

Spend one week entering the real stock: title, author, publisher, ISBN, purchase price, selling price and quantity. Use the ISBN as the item code, because two books can share a title. Keep one price list and one discount rule, and let the software apply it. For school and library orders, keep a separate customer record with the institution's GSTIN. Back up data every week. No billing app files GST returns by itself; the CA still reviews and files them.

## What to avoid

Do not buy a yearly plan before a free plan or trial has survived a full busy week at your own counter. Do not enter stock in a hurry with wrong prices. Do not trust an AI chatbot with book facts, prices or availability; it guesses. Do not keep customer phone numbers only inside one app with no export. Do not promise a delivery date on a supplier's verbal assurance; confirm stock in writing first.

## FAQ

**Which tool should a small bookshop start with?**
Vyapar free for billing and stock, WhatsApp Business for orders, ChatGPT free for messages.

**Is there a free accounting tool for a bookshop?**
Yes. Zoho Books is free while annual revenue stays under ₹25 lakh, with up to 1,000 invoices a year.

**How do I keep track of thousands of book titles?**
Use the ISBN as the item code and barcode scanning where supported.

**Can AI help a bookshop sell more books?**
Yes: personal recommendations, a message when a reserved book arrives, and short posts about new arrivals.

**Should a bookshop use TallyPrime or Zoho Books?**
TallyPrime if your CA already works in Tally; Zoho Books if the shop is small and turnover is under ₹25 lakh.

**Do these tools work without a VPN in India?**
Yes, all six work in India, with rupee pricing and UPI payment.
