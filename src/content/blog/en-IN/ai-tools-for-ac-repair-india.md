An AC repair business in India lives or dies on answering fast in May, dispatching the right technician, and getting paid without follow-up calls — and the tools below cover booking, dispatch, parts, invoicing and reminders. None fixes a compressor; they organise the work so fewer jobs slip through the cracks.

## Short answer

| Tool | Best for | Free option | Rough starting cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zoho FSM | Small Indian teams, INR billing | Free: 30 appointments/month | From ~₹1,800/month per user |
| ServiceM8 | Solo technicians, low cost | Free: 30 jobs/month, 1 user | Starter about $29 a month |
| Jobber | Growing teams, quotes and payments | 14-day free trial | About $49/month |
| Housecall Pro | Teams wanting a price book | 14-day free trial | About $59/month, annual billing |
| WhatsApp Business | Customer chats, reminders, catalogues | Free app | Free; paid extras optional |

Prices change; re-check official pages before paying.

## Zoho FSM

Zoho FSM is the most India-friendly option here. It bills in rupees, and its pricing page lists a free plan of up to 30 appointments a month, plus a 15-day trial of paid plans. The free tier covers work orders, dispatch, reports, invoicing and payments. Strengths: dispatch, parts tracking, AMC plans. Weakness: the free cap — one busy summer month forces an upgrade.

## ServiceM8

ServiceM8 is job management software for trades, and the cheapest professional option here. Its free plan covers one user and 30 jobs a month, with scheduling, quoting, invoicing and job photos. Paid plans start at about $29 a month for 50 jobs, charging per job, not per user, so adding technicians costs nothing extra. Catch: the full field app is built for iPhone and iPad; Android technicians get a limited companion app. Test the Android app first.

## Jobber

Jobber is a field service platform for home service trades including HVAC, covering quoting, scheduling, invoicing and payments. It offers a 14-day free trial, no card required, and its app lets technicians update jobs, collect payments and log time. Pricing is messy: reviews put the entry plan at $49 a month, about $29 annually, while other sources differ. Jobber bills in dollars, so GST and currency conversion add to the real cost. It fits teams of two to twelve technicians.

## Housecall Pro

Housecall Pro covers online booking, scheduling and dispatch, estimates, invoicing, card payments and a price book, plus a technician app. Its Basic plan is listed at about $59 a month billed annually, or $79 month to month, for one user. Higher plans add routes, GPS tracking, flat-rate pricing and QuickBooks sync, but sources disagree on team pricing: Essentials is quoted at $149 or $299 a month for five users. No free plan; AI answering and payroll are paid add-ons.

## WhatsApp Business

WhatsApp Business is Meta's free app for small businesses, and in India it may be the most important tool here. It adds a business profile, service catalogue, quick replies and automated greeting and away messages. List services, gas refills and AMC plans with prices. Use it as the customer-facing layer on a job tool: a customer messages you, you book the job, and you send the invoice and reminder back on WhatsApp. The free app suits one operator; two people on one number need the paid Platform.

## Using these tools in India

Most Indian AC customers find you on Google or by recommendation, message on WhatsApp, and pay by UPI. Minimum setup: a Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business with a catalogue, and one job tool. Add UPI links to invoices; card payments carry fees around 2.6 to 2.9 percent. Record part, price and warranty on the job card, and photograph the old part — that prevents most billing disputes. For AMC customers, set recurring jobs before summer and send reminders in March and April, not June.

## What to avoid

Do not buy the most expensive plan in April and use a third of it by July. Start free or on trial and run twenty real jobs first. Do not promise a same-day slot your board cannot deliver, and do not auto-send messages to customers who never agreed. Avoid per-user pricing for seasonal teams, and any "AI" add-on claiming to diagnose AC faults from a photo; none here does that reliably. Do not store customer data in a personal WhatsApp account.

## FAQ

**Which tool is best for a one-person AC repair business in India?**
ServiceM8's or Zoho FSM's free plan. Both cover 30 jobs or appointments a month for one user, and WhatsApp Business handles chats for free. Upgrade when you cross the free limit regularly.

**Can these tools handle annual maintenance contracts?**
Yes, mostly. Zoho FSM has maintenance plans on higher tiers, Housecall Pro has recurring service plans on Max, and ServiceM8 supports recurring jobs on all plans. 

**Do these tools work without a website?**
Yes. WhatsApp Business gives you a catalogue and short link, and Jobber and Housecall Pro offer booking links to share on WhatsApp or Google Business Profile. A website helps but is not required.

**How do I collect payments from AC repair customers?**
Most support card payments, but in India UPI is faster and cheaper. Send the invoice with a UPI link on WhatsApp.

**What should I check before paying for any of these tools?**
Re-check the official pricing and help pages on the day you pay: prices, free limits and features change, and reviews disagree. Trial it with real jobs on your team's phones.

## Final verdict

Start free with Zoho FSM or ServiceM8, add WhatsApp Business for customers, and pay for Jobber or Housecall Pro only when job volume grows. That covers bookings, technicians, parts and payments without locking a small business into a costly plan too early.
