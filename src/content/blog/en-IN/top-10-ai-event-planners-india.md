These ten tools plan, promote and run events across India.

1. ChatGPT drafts agendas; free, limited access.
2. Canva designs invites; free, some assets locked.
3. Notion tracks tasks; free, limited AI trial.
4. Luma builds event pages; free, 5% fee.
5. Eventbrite sells tickets; free events cost nothing.
6. RSVPify manages RSVPs; free, 100 monthly.
7. Otter.ai transcribes meetings; free, minutes capped.
8. Cvent runs conferences; quote-only, no free plan.
9. Whova powers apps; quote-only, no free plan.
10. Bizzabo runs conferences; no free plan.

## FAQ

**Which free tool suits beginners best?** Luma for events; ChatGPT for planning.

## Final verdict

Start with Luma and ChatGPT, both free in India.
