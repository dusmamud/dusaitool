## The short answer

For most kabaddi players in India, three free apps cover the basics: Strava for conditioning and team clubs, Coach's Eye for raid and tackle technique on video, and MyFitnessPal for food and weight. None is built for kabaddi, and none replaces a coach — but together they give a small-town player a professional training log.

## Strava — conditioning and team clubs

Strava records runs, rides and gym workouts with GPS. Its free tier covers activity tracking, the social feed and clubs, so a kabaddi team can run a free club with weekly leaderboards and the coach sees who actually trained. Premium adds deeper analytics and offline routes; Indian pricing has been reported around ₹2,499 a year, but sources disagree, so check the official price first.

## Coach's Eye — video analysis for technique

Film a raid or a tackle, then scrub it frame by frame, draw angles and add voice notes. That is exactly what a kabaddi coach needs to fix a mistimed touch or a weak ankle hold. The official site says the free tier covers three videos with every drawing tool; Pro costs about $64.99 a year. Video stays on your device.

## MyFitnessPal — food and weight

Kabaddi players cut and gain weight around tournaments, and guessing meals is how weight misses happen. MyFitnessPal's free tier logs food by searching a huge database, tracks calories and macros, and records weight. The barcode scanner moved to Premium in 2022, so free users type food names instead, and Indian packaged foods are patchy in the database. US Premium is listed around $19.99 a month; check the official page first.

## Using these tools in India

All three apps work in India without a VPN. Strava clubs are free. Coach's Eye stores video locally, so it works on weak data. MyFitnessPal's database is thinner for Indian home food, so save your own recipes and reuse them. Prices and free limits change often — re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## What to avoid

Do not pay for a premium plan before the free tier limits you. Do not trust any app's calorie score as a medical fact. Do not film other players without consent, and do not share match video publicly. Avoid apps promising kabaddi-specific AI coaching with no real kabaddi footage or coach behind them. This is general information only, not professional medical, nutrition or coaching advice.

## FAQ

**Can these apps replace a kabaddi coach?**

No. They record, measure and replay what happened, but only a coach can read a raid and plan a season.

**Which app should a beginner start with?**

Strava, because it is free and builds the habit of logging every session. Add Coach's Eye for technique, and MyFitnessPal when weight becomes a concern.

**Do these apps work offline?**

Mostly yes. Strava records GPS without a signal, Coach's Eye stores video on the device, and MyFitnessPal logs meals offline and syncs later.

**Is there an app made only for kabaddi?**

Not one we could verify with real kabaddi features and an active team behind it. For wider training ideas, see our guide to [AI tools for athletes in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-athletes-india/).

## Final verdict

Start free: Strava for fitness, Coach's Eye for technique video, MyFitnessPal for diet — pay only when the free limits bite.
