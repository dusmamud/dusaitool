Short answer: free WHO Anthro on the clinic computer for growth, free AnthroCal on your phone for quick z-scores and IAP charts, and free TinyDose for parent vaccination reminders. Use free WhatsApp Business for parent messages; consider Practo Ray only when the clinic needs appointments, records, and billing in one paid system. ## 1. WHO Anthro: the reference standard for growth

WHO Anthro is the World Health Organization's free software for child growth, for Windows, with an online version too. Its individual-assessment module calculates z-scores and percentiles for height-for-age, weight-for-age, weight-for-length, and body mass index-for-age in children under five. Because it follows WHO Child Growth Standards, its numbers match any colleague's. Keep it on the clinic computer for careful, documented growth readings.

## 2. AnthroCal: growth charts in your pocket

AnthroCal is a free Android app that computes z-scores from the WHO 2007 charts and the revised Indian Academy of Pediatrics charts for ages 0 to 18. It plots measurements on graphs, saves visits, and shares the chart with parents. Caution: some users report the newer version feels slow, and it is a calculator, not a record system.

## 3. TinyDose: vaccination reminders parents actually follow

TinyDose is a free-to-download baby vaccination tracker for iPhone and Android. Parents enter the birth date, pick the UIP or IAP schedule, and the app builds the timeline, sends reminders before each dose, and records every dose given. Schedule viewing and dose recording are free; some reminders are paid. The card and the doctor's advice come first. India has two schedules: the government schedule and the IAP schedule, which adds influenza, typhoid, hepatitis A, and chickenpox.

## 4. WhatsApp Business: the parent communication channel

WhatsApp Business is a free app from Meta, and the tool most Indian parents already check daily. The free app gives a clinic a business profile, greeting and away messages, quick replies, and labels such as Vaccine due. Broadcasts reach only saved contacts, about 256 per list. The paid API, needed for automation or several staff sharing one number, charges per message; sources disagree on the India rate, about Rs 0.12 to Rs 0.50 per message in 2026, so check Meta's official pricing page first. Never send a child's identifiable health details to a group.

## 5. Practo Ray: the paid all-in-one clinic system

Practo Ray is India's best-known clinic management software, covering appointments, records, prescriptions, billing, and SMS reminders, and it states ABDM compliance. The honest problem is price: Practo does not publish one clear price list, and different sources report different figures, from about Rs 999 to about Rs 1,499 a month and higher, with marketplace booking fees possibly added on top. A 7-day free trial with all features is the right way to test it. In the trial week, check whether vaccination due lists are easy to build and reminders reach parents.

## Using these tools in an Indian child clinic

Keep WHO Anthro on the clinic computer and AnthroCal on your phone. Ask every parent to confirm their WhatsApp number and preferred language at registration. Google Calendar's free appointment schedule can give the clinic a shareable booking page with email confirmations. The government's U-WIN portal is the official immunisation record system. ## What to avoid

Do not upload a child's name, phone number, photograph, or identifiable growth record to a general AI chatbot; use anonymised drafts only. Do not trust any single app's vaccine dates without checking the current National Immunisation Schedule or IAP schedule. Do not buy a paid clinic system on a demo alone. Do not use a personal WhatsApp number for the clinic. Finally, no tool replaces the growth chart in the child's file.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which free tool is best for tracking a child's growth in India?**

WHO Anthro is the reference standard for children under five. AnthroCal is the faster daily option, adding IAP charts for older children.

**Which app reminds parents about vaccinations in India?**

TinyDose supports both the government UIP and IAP schedules, builds dates from the birth date, and sends reminders before each dose. Schedule viewing and dose recording are free.

**Can a pediatrician use WhatsApp Business for vaccine reminders?**

Yes, and most Indian clinics already do. The free app handles reminders, quick replies, and labels for a solo clinic. For automation or several staff sharing one number, the paid API is needed; check its price on Meta's pricing page first.

**Is Practo Ray free for pediatricians?**

No. It is a paid subscription with a 7-day free trial, and its exact price is not published in one place, with sources reporting roughly Rs 999 to Rs 1,499 a month and higher. Confirm the price on Practo's plans page first.

## Final verdict

Start free with WHO Anthro, AnthroCal, TinyDose and WhatsApp Business; pay for Practo Ray only when the clinic outgrows them.
