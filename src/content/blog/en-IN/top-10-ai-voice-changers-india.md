These ten tools change voices across India without a VPN.

1. Voicemod changes live voices; free rotates.
2. Voice.ai changes live voices; free 5,000 monthly credits.
3. ElevenLabs revoices recordings; free 10 monthly minutes.
4. Murf converts voiceovers; free 10 minutes once.
5. Kits.AI converts singing voices; free 15 monthly minutes.
6. Clownfish changes system-wide voices; free forever.
7. FineVoice changes recorded voices; free tier limits.
8. Media.io changes browser audio; free filters unclear quota.
9. VoiceChanger.io changes uploaded audio; free online.
10. Voxal changes live voices; free home use.

## FAQ

**Best for beginners?** Clownfish is free forever; Voicemod suits live gaming.

## Final verdict

Start with Clownfish for free calls and ElevenLabs for polished recordings.
