These ten tools cover writing, notes, research, design, meetings and automation in India, no VPN needed. Start free first.

1. ChatGPT drafts emails, plans; free chat unlimited, uploads 3 daily.
2. Gemini answers questions, summarises documents in Hindi; free with usage limits.
3. Notion AI organises notes, tasks; free AI trial, about 20 responses.
4. Copilot drafts documents, emails; free chat, about 15 image boosts daily.
5. Grammarly fixes grammar, tone; free, 100 AI prompts a month.
6. Otter transcribes meetings, writes summaries; free, 300 minutes monthly.
7. Perplexity answers research questions with citations; free, 3 Pro searches daily.
8. Canva Magic Studio designs posts, slides; free AI credits monthly, limited.
9. Zapier connects apps, automates repetitive tasks; free, 100 tasks monthly.
10. Todoist organises tasks, reminders; free, 5 projects, 10 voice sessions monthly.

## FAQ

**Which tool should I try first?** ChatGPT or Gemini — both free, work in Hindi and cover most daily tasks.

## Final verdict

Start with ChatGPT and Gemini free; add Otter and Zapier when meetings and automation pile up.
