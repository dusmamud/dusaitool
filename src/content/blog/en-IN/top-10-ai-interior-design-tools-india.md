## Top 10

1. **Homestyler** — 3D floor plans; free Basic, unlimited 1K renders.
2. **Interior AI** — restyles room photos; free, 5 renders a day (limits vary by source).
3. **REimagineHome** — photo redesign and staging; 5 free designs, no card.
4. **Planner 5D** — 2D and 3D floor plans; free, unlimited projects.
5. **RoomGPT** — quick photo restyle; 1 free redesign, no card needed.
6. **RoomsGPT** — restyles photos in 60+ styles; free daily credits.
7. **Spacely AI** — photo redesign and staging; 40 free trial credits.
8. **IKEA Kreativ** — places real IKEA furniture in photos; completely free.
9. **ChatGPT** — edits room photos from prompts; free, daily image caps.
10. **Gemini** — edits room photos from prompts; free with Google account.
