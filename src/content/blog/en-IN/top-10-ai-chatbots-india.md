Most Indians need two or three chatbots: ChatGPT for writing, Gemini for Hindi, Perplexity for research. All ten work in India without a VPN.

1. ChatGPT: writing and coding; free text unlimited, uploads about 3 daily.
2. Gemini: Hindi chat in Gmail and Docs; free, limits refresh every 5 hours.
3. Claude: careful long writing; free, no official count, 15–40 per 5 hours.
4. Copilot: free with Microsoft account; no published count, image limits.
5. Perplexity: cited answers and research; free, 3 Pro searches daily.
6. Meta AI: chat in WhatsApp, Instagram; free, Hindi, no published cap.
7. DeepSeek: free reasoning and coding; no published cap, slow at peak.
8. Grok: real-time news and X; free allowance about 10 per 2 hours.
9. Mistral Vibe: European chat; free messages limited, about 25 daily.
10. Poe: many models in one app; free, about 300 points daily, 15–30 messages (older guides said 3,000).

**Which chatbot is best for students in India?** Gemini or ChatGPT: both free, Hindi-friendly, strong for study.

## Final verdict

Start with ChatGPT and Gemini, add Perplexity for research.
