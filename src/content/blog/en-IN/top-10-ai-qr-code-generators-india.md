These ten tools make branded QR codes for Indian shops.

1. QRCodeChimp: 10 dynamic codes, 1,000 scans monthly.
2. QRCode Monkey: unlimited static codes, no expiry.
3. Flowcode: 2 dynamic codes, 500 scans free.
4. QR Tiger: 3 dynamic codes, 500 scans total.
5. ME-QR: 10,000 codes, unlimited scans, ads included.
6. Canva: static and dynamic codes free.
7. Adobe Express: unlimited static codes, no signup.
8. goQR.me: free static codes, vector downloads included.
9. Hovercode: 3 dynamic codes, unlimited scans free.
10. TQRCG: unlimited static codes, 2 dynamic codes.
