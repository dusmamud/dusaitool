2. ClearTax: guided AI filing; basic free.
3. Tax2Win: AI error checks; filing free.

5. EZTax: regime optimiser; preparation free.

7. myITreturn: ITR-1 free under ₹2.5 lakh.

10. Aaykar Setu: official app; free calculators.
