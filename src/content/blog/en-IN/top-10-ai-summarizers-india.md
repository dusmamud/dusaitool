ChatGPT or Gemini covers most everyday summaries free; QuillBot suits longer texts, NotebookLM research papers.

## Top 10

1. ChatGPT — summarises articles, PDFs and notes; free, uploads capped daily.
2. Gemini — summarises Docs, Gmail, PDFs; free, daily limits.
3. Claude — handles long PDFs clearly; free usage resets every five hours.
4. NotebookLM — summarises 50 sources per notebook; free.
5. QuillBot — shortens articles with a slider; free limit 1,200–2,500 words.
6. TLDR This — one-click web article summaries; free, 10 AI credits.
7. ChatPDF — chats with PDFs and summarises them; free, 2 files daily.
8. SciSummary — summarises research papers simply; free, 5 summaries monthly.
9. Perplexity — answers questions with cited summaries; free basic searches daily.
10. Wordtune Read — PDF summaries with margin notes; free, 3–5 monthly.

## FAQ

**Which free summarizer is best for long PDFs?** NotebookLM for research, Claude for single documents — both free.

## Final verdict

Start with ChatGPT or Gemini daily, NotebookLM for research — all free, no VPN needed in India.
