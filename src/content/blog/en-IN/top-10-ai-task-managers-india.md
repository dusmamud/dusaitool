These ten organise tasks in India; re-check official pricing before paying.

1. Todoist: fast capture, 5 free projects.
2. ClickUp: team tasks, unlimited tasks, 60 MB storage.
3. Asana: team projects, unlimited tasks, 2 users.
4. Notion: docs, tasks, free, 10 guests.
5. Trello: visual boards, 10 boards, 10 collaborators.
6. TickTick: calendar tasks, 9 lists, 99 per list.
7. Microsoft To Do: simple lists, fully free.
8. Google Tasks: Gmail tasks, fully free.
9. Taskade: AI drafting, free for 2 members.
10. Reclaim: auto-scheduling, free Lite, 1 user.

## FAQ

**Which tool includes AI free?** Taskade drafts free; most AI here is paid.

## Final verdict

Start with Todoist for personal tasks and ClickUp for teams.
