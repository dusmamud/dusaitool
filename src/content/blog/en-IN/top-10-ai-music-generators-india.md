These ten tools cover full songs, background music and film scoring, and all work in India without a VPN.

1. Suno makes vocal songs; free gives 50 credits daily, downloads vary by account age.
2. Udio makes vocal songs; free gives 10 credits daily, 100 monthly.
3. Mubert makes ambient music; free gives 25 tracks, 5 downloads monthly.
4. Soundraw makes royalty-free instrumentals; free plan allows previews only, no downloads.
5. Beatoven.ai makes mood-based background music; free plan gives a few generations.
6. AIVA composes orchestral and cinematic music; free gives 3 downloads.
7. Boomy makes songs in seconds; free allows unlimited creation, 25 saves, no downloads.
8. Loudly makes EDM and hip-hop loops; free gives 1 short download daily.
9. Stable Audio makes music and sound effects; free 12 generations monthly.
10. ElevenLabs Music makes short licensed tracks; free gives 11 minutes, no downloads.

## FAQ

**Which free tool suits beginners best?** Boomy builds songs fastest; Suno gives the best vocal songs daily.

## Final verdict

Start with Suno for songs and Soundraw for background music — both free in India.
