NEET UG draws over two million aspirants for roughly one lakh MBBS seats, and the paper rewards accuracy under pressure as much as knowledge. You no longer need an expensive coaching package for exam-quality practice: free government platforms offer full mock tests, and AI tools can analyse mistakes, plan revision, and clear doubts in seconds.

The short answer: start with SATHEE and NTA Abhyas, both completely free, for weekly full-length mocks. Add Embibe or Physics Wallah only for deeper analytics. Use ChatGPT for doubt-solving, but verify its answers against NCERT — AI can sound confident while being wrong. No tool replaces previous years' papers and NCERT revision.

## 1. SATHEE — the free starting point

SATHEE (Self Assessment Test and Help for Entrance Exams) is built by IIT Kanpur and backed by India's Ministry of Education, and it is completely free: no premium tier, no locked features, no ads. It offers NCERT-mapped lectures, chapter-wise tests, previous years' questions, full-length mocks in a real exam interface, and an AI mock generator that adapts difficulty and targets weak areas.

## 2. NTA Abhyas — the official pattern

NTA Abhyas is the official practice app from the National Testing Agency, which conducts NEET. Its tests match the real paper most closely — 180 questions, +4 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong one, in a 3-hour-20-minute sitting. It offers 15 or more full-length tests, chapter-wise practice, and previous years' papers, all free. Take one full mock here every week in the last three months, under strict exam conditions,

## 3. Embibe — the analytics engine

Embibe tells you *why* you lost marks: which chapters leak marks, whether errors are conceptual or careless, how your time compares with toppers, and even a predicted rank. The catch is cost, and sources disagree: one comparison lists Pro around ₹299 a month, while the app's own Play Store listing shows plans from around ₹709 a month billed annually. Use the free tier first; only pay if the paid analysis genuinely changes how you study.

## 4. Physics Wallah — the affordable all-rounder

Physics Wallah (PW) is the most popular budget option: most mock tests are free, lectures cover the full syllabus in Hindi and English, and its AI tool Alakh AI answers doubts in real time. Full AI analytics reportedly cost around ₹99–199 a month — confirm it in the app before budgeting around it. Use PW for chapter-wise tests after each NCERT chapter and for quick doubt-clearing. ## 5. ChatGPT — the doubt-solver and revision coach

ChatGPT is not a NEET platform, but it is the most flexible study helper here. Ask it to explain a tough NCERT concept in simple Hindi, turn a chapter into flashcards, build a 30-day revision plan around weak chapters, or quiz you. Its free tier offers unlimited everyday text chats Two warnings: it can invent facts, including wrong formulas, so cross-check against NCERT; and paid plans change often — Go ₹399 and Plus ₹1,999 a month in late 2026 — so check the pricing page before deciding.

## How to use these tools in India

Follow a simple weekly rhythm. Monday to Friday, study NCERT and solve chapter-wise questions on SATHEE or PW. Saturday, take a full mock on NTA Abhyas under exam conditions. Sunday, spend two hours on analysis: list your five weakest chapters and use ChatGPT to build flashcards and a revision plan for them.

## What to avoid

Avoid any tool promising a guaranteed rank, a leaked paper, or "sure-shot" questions — those are scams. Avoid paying for three test series at once; one good series, finished and analysed properly, beats three half-used ones. Avoid using AI to skip NCERT: NEET is famously NCERT-centric, especially Biology, and an AI summary is a revision aid, not a replacement.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool is best for NEET mock tests in India?**

SATHEE and NTA Abhyas are the best free options: SATHEE for NCERT-mapped tests with AI analysis, NTA Abhyas for the most realistic official pattern. Embibe adds deeper analytics if you pay.

**Are there completely free AI tools for NEET preparation?**

Yes. SATHEE is fully free with no paid tier, and NTA Abhyas is free as the official NTA app. Both cover mocks, previous years' papers, and practice questions without payment.

**Can ChatGPT help me crack NEET?**

It helps with doubts, explanations, flashcards, and revision planning, but cannot replace NCERT, previous years' papers, and timed mocks. Verify its answers — it sometimes states wrong facts confidently.

**How many mock tests should a NEET aspirant take?**
