Need a first draft of a contract or NDA? These ten AI tools draft, review and explain legal documents — but none replaces a lawyer.

1. **ChatGPT** — drafts contracts, explains clauses plainly; free, uploads capped.
2. **Claude** — reads long agreements, summarises risks; free, five-hour resets.
3. **Gemini** — drafts in Docs and Gmail; free with Google account.
4. **Microsoft Copilot** — drafts and summarises in Word; free with limits.
5. **Genie AI** — drafts contracts from 500+ templates; free, capped tokens.
6. **Spellbook** — AI drafting and redlining in Word; seven-day free trial.
7. **LawDepot** — questionnaires build NDAs, wills and leases; one-week free trial.
8. **Rocket Lawyer** — templates, e-signatures and lawyer Q&A; seven-day free trial.
9. **FormsPal** — prompts become signable documents; free, five daily.
10. **AI Lawyer** — plain-English answers and document review; free to start.

**Can AI replace a lawyer in India?** No — it drafts and explains; a lawyer must check before signing.

## Verdict

ChatGPT and Claude for drafting, Genie AI for contracts — start free, lawyer-check before signing.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
