The short answer: a small repair shop in India needs no expensive software on day one. Start free with WhatsApp Business and Zoho Bookings, use ChatGPT free to read error messages, and pay for RepairShopr or RepairDesk only when jobs and spare parts outgrow a notebook. The paid tools cost roughly 5,000 to 8,000 rupees a month, which a one-person shop will not recover from organisation alone.

Prices below were checked through public web search; where sources disagree, this article says so. Prices change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## WhatsApp Business: the shop's front desk

WhatsApp Business is the free app from Meta for small businesses, and for most Indian repair shops it is the most useful tool here. It adds a business profile, a catalogue of up to 500 items, labels, and quick replies for daily questions: "How much for a screen replacement?" and "Is my laptop ready?" The app is free; only the separate Business Platform is paid. Never share a customer's password or personal files over chat.

## Zoho Bookings: appointments without phone tag

Zoho Bookings lets customers book a repair slot from a link. Its free plan is genuinely free for one user, with a booking page, calendar sync and reminders, and customers need no account to book. An Indian partner lists Basic at about Rs 360 and Premium at about Rs 540 per user per month, billed annually before GST, but some sources describe a lower free-plan cap. Zoho is an Indian company with INR pricing and GST invoices.

## ChatGPT: a diagnostic assistant, not a technician

ChatGPT's free tier helps in a repair shop, but it cannot diagnose hardware by itself. Paste an error message or blue-screen stop code and it usually explains the likely cause and suggests checks, cheapest first. It can draft estimates and polite replies, and translate them into Hindi. In India the free tier costs nothing, Go costs Rs 399 a month and Plus Rs 1,999, payable by UPI. Never paste customer files, passwords or Aadhaar details into any AI chatbot.

## RepairShopr: the full repair system

RepairShopr is cloud software for repair shops, covering tickets, inventory, estimates, invoicing and point of sale. A ticket follows a device from check-in to pickup, so nothing gets lost. Listings put the starting price at about $60 a month, with no free plan. Sources disagree on a free trial: Capterra lists one, G2 says there is none, so ask the vendor directly. At roughly Rs 5,000 a month it makes sense only when missed parts and forgotten follow-ups cost more.

## RepairDesk: tickets, POS and parts in one place

RepairDesk is similar to RepairShopr, with repair tickets, barcode scanning, inventory, billing, POS and messaging. It serves computer, phone and electronics repair shops, including multi-store ones. Essential is $99 a month for five users and Growth $149, with no free plan. Sources disagree on a free trial here too: Capterra lists one and G2 says there is none. Choose on a demo: run your ten most common jobs through each and check GST invoicing.

## How an Indian repair shop should use these tools

For a one- or two-person shop: WhatsApp Business handles enquiries and updates; Zoho Bookings takes appointments; ChatGPT helps read error codes; a spreadsheet tracks parts until volume justifies RepairShopr or RepairDesk. For multi-branch or authorised centres, Fixably is worth a look, but it publishes no price, offers no free version or trial, and needs a custom quote. Back up records weekly, and write the device condition and agreed price on the ticket before starting.

## What to avoid

Do not buy a paid system before you have a steady flow of jobs; a subscription will not create customers. Do not trust an AI answer without testing it on the actual machine; a wrong diagnosis wastes parts and trust. Do not promise a deadline based on a tool's estimate, and do not quote a price from an old article — check the official pricing page first. Avoid tools that need a VPN in India, tools that block data export, and any "free" tool that asks for payment details upfront.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the cheapest way to run a computer repair shop in India?**
Start with free WhatsApp Business and Zoho Bookings, plus ChatGPT's free tier. That covers messages, appointments and diagnostic help at zero cost.

**Which tool tracks repair tickets and spare parts together?**
RepairShopr and RepairDesk both do: tickets, inventory, estimates, invoicing and POS in one system. RepairShopr starts at about $60 a month and RepairDesk at $99, with no free plan on either.

**Can ChatGPT diagnose a broken laptop?**
No, not by itself. It can explain error messages and suggest checks, but it cannot test hardware, so verify its suggestions on the actual machine.

**Do I need a paid tool for appointments?**
Not at first. Zoho Bookings has a free plan for one user with a booking page, calendar sync and reminders. Upgrade only when you need payments or several staff members.

**Is WhatsApp Business really free?**
Yes, the app is free, including the catalogue, quick replies, labels and greeting messages. The separate Business Platform for bulk messaging is paid, but a small shop does not need it.

## Final verdict

Start free with WhatsApp Business and Zoho Bookings; pay for RepairShopr or RepairDesk only when tickets and parts outgrow a notebook.
