For a small Indian pet shop, start with WhatsApp Business for customer chats and orders, Vyapar for GST billing and stock, and Canva for posters and offers. All three have free plans, work on an Android phone without a VPN, and together cover pets, food, accessories, repeat customers and daily billing.

## Short answer

| Tool | Best for | Free plan |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Business | Customer chats, catalogue, repeat orders | Yes, free app |
| Vyapar | GST bills, stock, low-stock alerts | Yes, free mobile tier |
| Canva | Posters, price lists, festival offers | Yes, free plan |

## WhatsApp Business — chats, catalogue and repeat orders

Most pet-shop customers already message on WhatsApp. The free Business app adds a shop profile, a product catalogue with photos and prices for food, toys and accessories, quick replies for common questions, and labels to track orders and payments. List your best 15–20 items first and always show prices.

## Vyapar — GST billing and stock

Vyapar is an Indian billing app made for small shops. Its free Android tier covers GST invoices, basic stock tracking, low-stock alerts and customer ledgers, which suits pet food and accessories. Sources disagree on paid prices — some quote around ₹699 a year for mobile plans, others around ₹3,399 for desktop — so check the official Vyapar pricing page before paying.

## Canva — posters, price lists and offers

Canva's free plan gives a pet shop ready poster and social-media templates, so a new-arrival or discount offer takes minutes instead of a designer's fee. Use your own pet photos, keep the shop name, phone number and one offer per poster. Premium templates and background removal are paid Pro features, so check for the crown mark first.

## Using these tools in India

All three work in India without a VPN, on Android phones, and with UPI payments. Vyapar handles Indian GST billing, WhatsApp is where customers already ask questions, and Canva supports Hindi text for local posters. Start on the free tiers and re-check prices and free limits on each official pricing page before paying or promising a delivery date.

## What to avoid

Do not buy a costly pet-shop software suite before the free tools prove too small, and never share a pet's medical history or a customer's phone number publicly. Avoid AI feeding or medicine advice — ask a vet instead — and never trust a price quoted here without re-checking the official page.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which free tool is best for a small pet shop in India?**

WhatsApp Business — customers use it, the catalogue is free, and it handles orders and repeat sales from one phone.

**Can Vyapar handle pet food expiry dates?**

Its stock tracking includes batch and expiry tracking on paid tiers; the free mobile tier covers basic stock and low-stock alerts. Check the tier on Vyapar's site.

**Do I need a website for my pet shop?**

No. A WhatsApp catalogue plus a Google Business Profile listing is enough to start; read our [AI tools for Amazon sellers](/blog/ai-tools-for-amazon-sellers-india/) guide before selling accessories online.

**Is AI feeding advice safe for pets?**

No. Treat AI answers as general information only, not veterinary advice, and ask a qualified vet before changing a pet's diet or medicine.

Start free with WhatsApp Business and Vyapar, add Canva for posters — that covers billing, stock and customers for most small pet shops.
