Ten tools turn text or photos into 3D models.

1. Meshy: textured models; 100 free credits monthly.
2. Tripo: game-ready models; 200 free credits monthly.
3. Rodin: detailed models; previews free.
4. Spline: web 3D; AI needs paid plan.
5. Sloyd: game models; one free generation daily.
6. Scenario: game assets; 50 free credits daily.
7. Polycam: scanning tool; three free AI captures.
8. Hunyuan3D: models; open-source, free, needs GPU.
9. TRELLIS: meshes; open-source, free, needs GPU.
10. PrintPal: printable models; ten free monthly.
