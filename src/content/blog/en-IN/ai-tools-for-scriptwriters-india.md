## Final Draft: the industry standard

Final Draft is the software most producers, agents, and contests expect. It formats scene headings, action, dialogue, and transitions automatically, with pagination matching the industry standard exactly. It also offers a Beat Board and revision tools for coloured shooting-draft pages. The catch is price: $249.99 one-time, often $199.99, with no free plan. Trial length is disputed — some sources say 30 days, others 5 days for the Suite. Buy it for festivals or production work.

## WriterDuet: the best free start for co-writers

WriterDuet is built around real-time collaboration. Two or more writers can type in the same script at once, see each other's cursors, and use full revision history. It imports and exports .fdx, Fountain, and PDF files. The free plan covers three projects with no time limit. Paid plans start at about $9.99 a month and add unlimited projects and offline mode.

## Arc Studio Pro: the modern all-rounder

Arc Studio Pro is a browser-first app with desktop and mobile apps, cloud sync, comments, and real-time collaboration. Its outlining tools stand out: plan beats, acts, and arcs on a board, then write against that structure. It also offers AI extras such as table-read voices, some sold as add-ons. The free tier covers two scripts, but PDF exports carry a watermark and collaboration is paid. Pro costs $99 a year for the first year and $79 on renewal.

## Celtx: script plus production planning

One project can hold a breakdown, schedule, budget, storyboard, and call sheets — useful for filmmakers who write and shoot their own work. The free tier covers one active project with the basic editor and PDF export. Paid plans start at about $15 a month; higher tiers add the production tools. The free tier is the most restricted here — one project, and no Final Draft export on some plans — so treat it as a trial.

## Sudowrite: AI help for scenes and rewrites

Sudowrite is an AI assistant for fiction and screenwriting. It suggests continuations in your voice, rewrites passages, and helps brainstorm when a scene stalls. It is good for breaking writer's block and testing different versions of a line. It is not a screenplay formatter: draft and format elsewhere and use Sudowrite as a sidekick. There is no permanent free plan, only a free trial with no credit card required. Paid plans start at $10 a month on annual billing ($19 monthly), and all usage is metered in credits.

## How to use these tools in India

Most of these tools work in India without a VPN. Pay in dollars with an international card, and check your bank's forex markup. For Hindi or Hinglish scripts, test first: Roman-script Hindi works well, but Devanagari can behave oddly in some formatting engines, so export a test PDF and check the font. For pitching, export a clean PDF with a title page, a one-page synopsis, and a logline — most Indian producers still read PDFs, not app links.

## What to avoid

Do not pay for a tool before finishing one script on a free plan. Do not let AI write your entire script: it produces generic dialogue and clichés. Do not upload an unpublished script to a free AI service without reading its terms. Do not send a watermarked free-tier PDF to a producer or contest — it looks unfinished. Do not promise a deadline based on a price in a review; check the official pricing page before you pay.

## FAQ

**Which AI tool is best for scriptwriters in India?**

WriterDuet: three scripts free, with collaboration and Final Draft export. Arc Studio Pro suits heavy outliners. Final Draft wins only when a producer or contest demands its format.

**Is there a completely free screenwriting tool?**

Yes. WriterDuet gives three projects free, Arc Studio Pro gives two scripts free, and Fade In's free demo only watermarks PDF output. Open-source Trelby is fully free on Windows and Linux.

**Can AI write a full screenplay for me?**

Technically yes, but do not let it. AI writes flat dialogue and predictable plots. Use it for brainstorming, outlines, and rewriting weak scenes, and keep the story in your own hands.

**Do I need Final Draft to sell a script in India?**

No. A clean, correctly formatted PDF from WriterDuet, Arc Studio Pro, or Fade In is enough for a read. You need Final Draft only when a production house, writers' room, or contest asks for a .fdx file.

**How do I pitch a script to producers or OTT platforms?**

Prepare a one-line logline, a one-page synopsis, and a polished PDF. Research each producer's submission rules first — many accept scripts only through agents or writers' associations. Never send a script to an unverified email address.

**Which tool is best for writing with a partner?**

WriterDuet: real-time co-writing is its core feature, and the free plan includes it. Arc Studio Pro is next best, but collaboration needs its paid Pro plan.
