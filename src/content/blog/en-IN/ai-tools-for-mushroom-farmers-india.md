A mushroom farmer needs four answers at the right time: which variety suits my climate, is my room too hot or damp, what is wrong with this bag, and who will buy today's harvest? Mushrooms are perishable, so a slow answer is a lost sale. The tools below work on an ordinary phone in India. Prices change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

The short answer: use [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) or [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) free to plan your crop and write buyer messages in Hindi. Use [Plantix](https://plantix.net/en/) free to photograph a sick crop, but treat the result as a first check. Use [WhatsApp Business](https://business.whatsapp.com/) free to take orders, and [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) free for posters. No free app replaces daily checks with a thermometer and hygrometer.

## 1. ChatGPT — planning and paperwork

ChatGPT handles the boring work: a week-by-week oyster plan, a cost sheet for straw, spawn, bags and labour, and a buyer message in Hindi. Ask in Hindi or Hinglish; its free tier covers text chats. Go is listed at ₹399 a month, so check OpenAI's pricing page before paying, and never act on its numbers without checking your KVK.

## 2. Gemini — Hindi voice help in the growing room

Gemini is Google's chatbot, and its strength is voice. Gemini Live is included in the free plan on the mobile app, so you can ask hands-free in Hindi. Like every chatbot, it can sound confident about a wrong answer, so cross-check its advice and never spray on its word alone.

## 3. Plantix — photograph a problem before it spreads

Plantix is a free Android app built for small farmers in India. Photograph a sick crop and it gives a probable diagnosis within seconds, covering about 30 crops in 19 languages including Hindi. Plantix was built for field crops, and its mushroom-specific coverage is not confirmed. A photo result for mushrooms is a lead, not a diagnosis, so confirm it with your KVK before acting.

## 4. WhatsApp Business — where the harvest sells

Mushrooms spoil fast, so use the app your buyers already open every morning. The WhatsApp Business app is free for small businesses, and adds a business profile, a product catalogue, quick replies and labels. Put today's harvest, price per kilo and delivery time in the catalogue, and send the morning list to regular buyers. The free app is for answering by hand; the paid Platform charges per message, so check Meta's pricing page first.

## 5. Canva — posters and labels that look professional

A clean poster outside your gate or a neat label on a 200-gram pack makes a small farm look like a serious supplier. Canva's free plan covers templates for social posts, flyers, price lists and labels. Reuse one template weekly, changing only price and date. Premium templates, the background remover and brand kits need Canva Pro, listed recently near ₹499 a month in India, so check the live price before upgrading. Never put health claims on labels.

## How to use these tools in India

Match the variety to your climate before spending anything. Oyster suits most of India at roughly 20–30 °C and 80–85% humidity, button needs roughly 15–20 °C, and milky tolerates 30–35 °C. Follow your KVK's advice, since sources differ slightly. Buy spawn only from ICAR centres, KVKs or licensed suppliers, and take a short course first at ICAR-DMR Solan or ICAR-IIHR Bengaluru. Indian sensor systems such as Tekari Agro and Silir exist, but they are paid systems with unpublished prices. For wider farm reading, see [AI tools for farmers in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-farmers-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not trust one chatbot answer for a spray, a dose or a variety choice, and never buy spawn or sensors from a social media ad without checking the seller with your KVK. Do not trust an app diagnosis for mushrooms without a human expert confirming it, because a wrong treatment can wipe out a whole room. Do not pay for a premium AI plan until the free tier actually blocks your work. This article is general information only, not professional advice; check official sources before spending money or treating a crop problem.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which AI tool should a mushroom farmer try first?**
ChatGPT or Gemini free for planning and buyer messages, and Plantix free for sick-crop photos.

**Can AI control my mushroom room's temperature and humidity?**
No free phone app can do that alone. You need sensors and a controller, or daily manual checks.

**Is Plantix reliable for mushroom disease?**
Only partly. It is proven for field crops, but its mushroom-specific coverage is not confirmed. Confirm results with your KVK.

**Which mushroom variety is easiest for a beginner in India?**
Oyster is the usual beginner choice: it grows at roughly 20–30 °C on pasteurised straw and fruits in a short cycle.

**How do I sell mushrooms daily without a middleman?**
List the morning harvest on WhatsApp Business with a price and delivery time, and deliver the same day to regular buyers.

**Do I need to pay for any of these tools?**
No. All five tools have free tiers covering the work here. Pay only when a free limit blocks you.

## Final verdict

Start free with ChatGPT or Gemini, Plantix for sick-crop photos and WhatsApp Business for sales; confirm disease calls with your KVK.
