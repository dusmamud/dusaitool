These ten tools make thumbnails in India without a VPN; limits differ.

1. Canva: templates plus AI; limited free use.
2. Adobe Express: Firefly images; free daily generations.
3. Fotor: AI thumbnails; free daily credits.
4. Snappa: quick template thumbnails; three downloads monthly.
5. Thumbnail.AI: dedicated AI thumbnails; free editor, paid.
6. Simplified: AI thumbnails; free plan includes AI.
7. vidIQ: YouTube AI thumbnails; 150 credits monthly.
8. 1of10: YouTube-trained AI thumbnails; 60 credits free.
9. Leonardo.Ai: illustrated thumbnail scenes; 150 tokens daily.
10. Ideogram: text-accurate thumbnail images; weekly slow credits.
