These ten tools cover scheduling, captions, design and analytics for Indian creators, and all work in India without a VPN.

## Top 10

1. **Buffer:** schedules posts; free: 3 channels, 10 queued posts each.
2. **Metricool:** schedules and analyses posts; free: 1 brand, 20 posts monthly.
3. **Publer:** queues posts; free: 3 accounts, 10 scheduled posts each.
4. **Later:** visual Instagram planner; free: 12 posts per profile monthly.
5. **Canva:** AI designs posts; free: about 20 AI uses monthly, sources differ.
6. **ChatGPT:** writes captions; free: unlimited text chats, image limits apply.
7. **Meta Business Suite:** free scheduler for Facebook, Instagram; 25 posts a day.
8. **Hootsuite:** schedules many accounts; no free plan, 14-day trial only.
9. **Predis.ai:** AI makes posts; no free plan, 3-day trial needs card.
10. **Flick:** AI captions, hashtag research; 7-day trial only, no free plan.

## FAQ

**Which free tool should an Indian creator try first?** Buffer or Meta Business Suite, both genuinely free for daily posting.

## Final verdict

Start free with Buffer or Meta Business Suite, add Canva for design — pay only when limits bite.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
