A rice mill runs on four daily jobs: buying paddy right, milling it well, knowing godown stock, and selling with correct GST invoices. The out-turn — head rice, broken, bran, and husk from each quintal — decides the season's margin. On paper alone, a wrong moisture reading becomes a bad purchase and a forgotten lot a buyer dispute.

No single AI tool runs a rice mill end to end — use a combination: Vyapar for GST billing, stock, and ledgers; TallyPrime when volumes outgrow a phone app; Zoho Inventory for multi-channel sales; ChatGPT or Gemini only for drafting, never to decide a paddy price; and Google Sheets for the daily milling register.

## 1. Vyapar — billing, stock, and ledgers from a phone

Vyapar is an Indian billing and inventory app, and it fits a small or medium rice mill because GST is native to it. It makes GST invoices, tracks stock by item and batch, keeps supplier ledgers, and works offline — vital where signal is weak. The mobile app is free on one device; desktop starts from about ₹3,399 a year, plus 18% GST. Check the official pricing page before paying, as figures change.

## 2. TallyPrime — when the mill outgrows a phone app

TallyPrime is the accounting software most Indian accountants already know, and it is the right step when billing, inventory, and GST work become too big for a phone app. It covers ledgers, banking, inventory, GST reports, e-invoicing, and e-way bills. Silver (single-user) costs ₹22,500 plus 18% GST as a lifetime licence, or about ₹750 a month on rent, with a yearly TSS renewal of ₹4,500 plus GST from year two. See also our guide to [AI tools for accountants in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-accountants-india/).

## 3. Zoho Inventory — orders and stock across godowns

Zoho Inventory is a cloud inventory tool from Zoho, a Chennai company, and it suits a mill selling through several channels or storing stock in more than one godown. Its free plan covers 50 orders a month for one user, with composite items — one quintal of paddy becomes head rice, broken, bran, and husk. Paid plans start at about ₹999 a month, billed annually. Treat the free plan as a trial — a busy mill crosses 50 orders fast — and check Zoho's pricing page, as sources disagree on free-plan locations.

## 4. ChatGPT — drafting, lists, and second opinions

ChatGPT's free plan costs ₹0 in India and, per OpenAI's own pages, offers unlimited everyday text chats, while uploads, images, voice, and deep research are limited. Paid plans are Go at ₹399 a month and Plus at ₹1,999 a month. For a mill it drafts rate lists, payment reminders, and quotation letters — it decides nothing. Re-check its arithmetic — chatbots make confident mistakes. Never paste Aadhaar numbers or bank details into a chatbot, and never let it set a buying price. Gemini (free, ₹0) does the same inside Gmail and Sheets, on the Flash-Lite model; paid plans start at ₹399 a month. See our [data and Excel guide](/blog/ai-tools-for-data-excel-india/).

## How to use these tools in an Indian rice mill

Start with one register and one billing tool, not five apps at once. Keep a daily milling sheet in Google Sheets with one row per lot, bill every sale from Vyapar so stock falls automatically, and reconcile the godown physically once a week. Take the mandi rate from the mandi each morning, never a chatbot. For buying directly from farmers, see our [farmers guide](/blog/ai-tools-for-farmers-india/).

## What to avoid

Avoid any tool promising to grade paddy quality or predict out-turn from a phone photo alone; no verified tool does this reliably. Avoid keeping stock only in a chatbot conversation or WhatsApp chat — neither is a ledger and neither produces a GST report. Try the free tier through one full procurement cycle before paying yearly. Never share personal or bank details with a chatbot.

## FAQ

**Which single tool should a small rice mill start with?**

Start with Vyapar's free mobile plan: billing, stock, and ledgers are daily jobs, and it works offline.

**Can AI tell me the right price to pay for paddy?**

No. The price depends on variety, moisture, the mandi rate that day, and buyer demand, and none of these tools knows your mandi's rate today.

**Is there a free tool for rice mill inventory?**

Yes, with limits: Vyapar mobile is free for basic inventory, and Zoho Inventory's free plan covers 50 orders a month.

**Do I need TallyPrime for GST filing?**

Not necessarily. Vyapar prepares GST reports for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, and many small mills file with their accountant's help. Confirm e-invoicing thresholds officially.

**Can these tools work without good internet at the mill?**

Partly. Vyapar's mobile app works offline and syncs later, while Zoho Inventory, ChatGPT, and Gemini all need internet. Keep a notebook backup.

**Is my mill's data safe in these apps?**

Safer than a paper register, but not risk-free. Use a strong password and two-step verification, and never paste Aadhaar numbers, bank details, or passwords into a chatbot. This is general information only, not professional advice; check GST rules officially or with your accountant.

## Verdict

Start with Vyapar for GST billing and stock, add ChatGPT or Gemini for drafting, and move to TallyPrime as volumes grow. Keep one Sheets register, reconcile weekly, and never let a chatbot set a price or file a return. Prices and limits change — re-check official pricing before paying.
