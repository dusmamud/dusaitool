Three free or low-cost apps cover most of what a karate player in India needs: slow-motion video review for kata and kumite, remote coach feedback, and strength and mobility work between classes. None replaces a qualified sensei, and none can award a belt — grading stays with your federation and dojo.

## 1. Hudl Technique — slow-motion kata review

Record a kata or kumite drill, then play it back frame by frame at up to 240 fps. Drawing tools mark hip rotation, stance width or a late guard, and side-by-side comparison puts your rep next to a senior's. The app is free to download on iOS and Android, with paid in-app packs.

## 2. OnForm — feedback from your coach

OnForm is built for coach-athlete video exchange. You record a drill, your coach replies with voice-over notes and drawings on the clip, with side-by-side video comparison. Athletes linked to a subscribing coach use the Basic plan free; alone, you get a 14-day free trial, then Individual Standard is listed at $9.99 a month.

## 3. Nike Training Club — strength and mobility

Karate needs explosive legs, a strong core and flexible hips, and this app covers all three with free trainer-led workouts. Filter by duration, equipment and level, from bodyweight sessions to full gym programmes, plus yoga and mobility flows for recovery. It is free on Google Play and the App Store with no subscription, though some on-demand classes are region-limited in India.

## Using these apps in India

All three apps work in India without a VPN and download from the app stores. Dollar prices shift at checkout, so re-check the official pricing page before paying. Dojo Wi-Fi is often weak, so download workouts at home, and check tournament rules before filming bouts — some ban phones at the mat.

## What to avoid

Do not copy advanced kata or heavy sparring drills from random videos without your sensei's okay — bad habits set fast and injuries follow. Avoid apps promising a black belt online or charging for 'grading' without a recognised federation. Never train through sharp joint pain.

## FAQ

**Can an app help me pass a belt grading?** Only indirectly. Apps sharpen technique and fitness, but grading is judged in person by your federation or dojo.

**Which app is best for kata?** Hudl Technique, because frame-by-frame playback and side-by-side comparison expose small stance and timing errors that are hard to feel in the moment.

**Are these apps free in India?** Nike Training Club and Hudl Technique are free to download; OnForm is free when your coach subscribes, otherwise a 14-day trial, then a paid plan.

Hudl Technique, OnForm and Nike Training Club cover kata review, coach feedback and fitness — free or cheap for Indian karate players.
