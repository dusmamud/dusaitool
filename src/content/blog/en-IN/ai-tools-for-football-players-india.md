For most Indian football players, the best start is simple: film your matches, measure your numbers, and review one clip at a time. Veo suits clubs that film weekly, Trace suits players who want personal highlights, and Playermaker suits players who want hard data on touches, speed, and distance. No tool replaces a coach.

## 1. Veo — AI camera for clubs

Veo Cam 3 sits on a tall tripod, films the whole pitch, and uses AI to follow the ball and tag goals and key moments. Footage uploads to the Veo Editor for clipping and sharing. The catch is cost: you buy the camera and pay a subscription, and Veo says the camera cannot be used without an active plan.

## 2. Trace — personal highlights for players

Trace is built for players and parents rather than clubs. Its AI camera films the game and creates a highlight reel for every player. The camera is leased free with a yearly plan, and PlayerFocus plans run from about $15 to $25 a month when billed yearly, or about $180 to $300 a year. Highlights can take up to 12 hours to appear.

## 3. Playermaker — a sensor on your boots

Playermaker 2.0 straps two motion sensors to your boots and tracks more than 25 metrics, including touches, passes, speed, distance, and kicking power. It needs no GPS and syncs to its app over Bluetooth. The sensors carry FIFA Quality certification under FIFA's EPTS programme. The kit costs about $199 to $229, with 12 months of app access included.

## Using these tools in India

All three work in India without a VPN, but import duties, shipping, and currency conversion push the real cost above the headline price, and big match uploads need steady broadband. Many academies start cheaper — a phone on a tripod — adding one paid tool only once weekly filming is a habit. Also see our [AI tools for athletes](/blog/ai-tools-for-athletes-india/) guide.

## What to avoid

Do not buy hardware before a trial match, and do not trust AI stats blindly — mistagged goals happen. Never post children's match footage publicly without parental consent, and never pay for a yearly plan on the promise of a trial or selection.

## FAQ

**Which AI tool is best for a school football team in India?**

Veo, if the team plays weekly and shares the cost. For one player, Trace or Playermaker gives more value for less money.

**Do I need a Veo camera to analyse my matches?**

No. A phone on a tripod films well enough, and free tools can analyse the footage. Veo only removes the need for a camera operator.

**Can Playermaker help me get selected for a club?**

It can support your case with real numbers on touches, speed, and work rate, but no sensor guarantees selection. Coaches still judge decision-making and positioning in live play.

## Final verdict

Film with Veo if your club plays weekly; track yourself with Trace or Playermaker if you train alone. Start cheap, and let the footage — not the marketing — decide the upgrade.
