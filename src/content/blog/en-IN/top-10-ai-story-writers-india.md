Ten story tools, all working in India.

1. ChatGPT drafts stories free; limits vary.
2. Claude writes natural fiction; free resets five-hourly.
3. Gemini drafts stories free; limits refresh regularly.
4. Sudowrite drafts novels; trial, about 10,000 credits.
5. NovelAI continues stories; trial gives 50 generations.
6. Novelcrafter plans novels; 21-day trial, own key.
7. Squibler builds book drafts; free 1,000 monthly credits.
8. Rytr writes short stories; free 10,000 monthly characters.
9. Jasper drafts story copy; 7-day trial only.
10. Perchance makes unlimited stories free; no sign-up.

## FAQ

**Which tool suits beginners best?** ChatGPT, free and simplest for short stories.

## Final verdict

Start with ChatGPT for free stories and Sudowrite for serious novels.
