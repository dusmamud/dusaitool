For most NRIs, three tools cover the daily work: Wise for sending money home at the real mid-market rate, ChatGPT for drafting documents and explaining paperwork plainly, and Gemini for family coordination and translation. None replaces a bank, accountant or lawyer, but together they cut the back-and-forth that makes NRI life slow and expensive.

## Short answer

| Need | Best pick | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remittances | Wise | Mid-market rate, fee upfront |
| Documents | ChatGPT | Free plan drafts and translates |
| Family and Hindi | Gemini | Free, Hindi, in Google apps |

## 1. Wise — remittances home

Wise converts at the real mid-market rate — the rate on Google — and shows its fee before you send, instead of hiding profit in the exchange rate. Its bank-funded fee runs near 0.45% to 1.5%, and most transfers arrive within a day. It pays into an ordinary Indian bank account. Fees change, so check the live quote first.

## 2. ChatGPT — documents, letters and explanations

ChatGPT's free plan costs ₹0: unlimited everyday text chats, uploads capped at about three a day, and image creation, voice and deep research capped too. It drafts rent agreements and letters, explains OCI, PAN and tax notices plainly, and translates across Indian languages. Go is about ₹399, Plus about ₹1,999 a month; start free. Never paste passport numbers, OTPs or bank passwords into a chatbot.

## 3. Gemini — family coordination and Hindi help

Gemini is free in India with a Google account and works in Hindi and other Indian languages, inside Gmail, Docs and Drive. It summarises long property papers and school circulars, drafts family messages, and explains investment options simply. Eligible Indian students get Google AI Plus free for a year, normally ₹399 a month — redeem by 31 December 2026, cancel before renewal.

## Using them from India

All three work in India without a VPN, on phone and web. Pay Wise from your overseas bank account, not a credit card. Keep one folder for FIRC certificates, receipts and tax papers; get each document summarised as it arrives. Learn about investments with them; open accounts only via SEBI-registered platforms. Compare chatbots: [AI chatbots beyond ChatGPT and Gemini](/blog/ai-chatbots-beyond-chatgpt-gemini-india/).

## What to avoid

Never trust a rate, fee or deadline without checking the official page that day. Do not send money through strangers or social-media agents promising better rates. AI can draft a tax return or legal document, but an accountant or lawyer must check it before you file or sign.

## FAQ

**Which is the cheapest way for an NRI to send money to India?** Usually Wise or a similar transparent service, with the fee shown upfront and the mid-market rate. Compare the live quote that day.

**Can ChatGPT help with NRI tax and OCI paperwork?** It explains forms and drafts letters plainly, but rules change. Confirm with the Income Tax Department or a chartered accountant.

**Is Gemini free in India?** Yes, with a Google account, and it works in Hindi. AI Plus is about ₹399 a month; student offers have deadlines — check Google's page.

**Can AI tools manage my family's investments in India?** They explain options and track a simple plan, but cannot open accounts or advise personally. Use SEBI-registered platforms and a licensed adviser.

## Final verdict

Wise for money, ChatGPT for documents, Gemini for family — start free and verify every fee before you pay.
