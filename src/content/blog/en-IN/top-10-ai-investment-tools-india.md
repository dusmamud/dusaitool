These ten tools cover research, screening and advisory.

1. Screener.in screens stocks; free: 50 companies.
2. Tickertape screens stocks; free basic filters.
3. Trendlyne gives DVM scores; free basic scorecards.
4. TradingView charts NSE/BSE; free two indicators.
5. StockEdge scans sectors; free basic scans.
6. Sensibull builds options strategies; free Lite chains.
7. Streak builds no-code strategies; free for Zerodha.
8. Jarvis Invest manages AI portfolios; SEBI-registered, starts ₹30,000.
9. Smallcase offers theme baskets; free browsing.
10. Perplexity Finance answers stock questions; free.
