A dental lab in India needs three things from software: CAD design, a case tracker that never loses an order, and a simple way to receive digital scans. exocad covers design, 3Shape LMS covers case management, and Medit Link covers scan intake. Together they handle crowns, bridges, dentures and implant cases without paper job cards.

## 1. exocad DentalCAD — CAD design

exocad DentalCAD designs crowns, bridges, dentures, implant bars and splints from digital scans. It sells Core, Advanced, Implant and Ultimate bundles for different lab sizes. It is paid software — Flex subscription or perpetual licence — with resellers listing Core from about $125 a month. There is no free version, so budget for the licence, a capable Windows PC and training before promising faster turnarounds.

## 2. 3Shape LMS — case management

3Shape LMS, formerly LabStar, is a cloud system tracking every case from order entry to design, production, billing and delivery. It syncs case details with 3Shape Dental System. 3Shape lists Premium from €79 a month for 100 cases, with a free trial, and prices rise with volume. It suits labs already using 3Shape scanners or CAD; confirm Indian pricing with a local reseller.

## 3. Medit Link — scan intake

Medit Link is the free, open platform with Medit intraoral scanners, letting clinics send scans and case notes to the lab. It exports open STL, OBJ and PLY files, so the lab is not locked to one design software. Medit Design, its design app, is free for scanner owners and $99.99 a month without one, with the first month free. It is the cheapest way to receive digital impressions from many clinics.

## Using these tools in India

Most Indian labs still take orders on WhatsApp, so the biggest win is moving job cards into 3Shape LMS and asking clinics to send scans through Medit Link instead of couriering impressions. Ask resellers for rupee quotes, including GST and import duty, before budgeting. ## What to avoid

Do not buy CAD software before checking your PC meets its requirements. Avoid cracked or grey-market licences, which can stop working after an update. Never promise a delivery date based on a marketing claim; prices and limits change, so re-check official pricing pages before paying.

## FAQ

**Which tool should a small dental lab buy first?**
Start with Medit Link if clinics already send digital scans, because it costs nothing with a Medit scanner. Add 3Shape LMS when lost cases become regular, and buy exocad only when design volume justifies the licence.

**Is exocad free for dental labs?**
No. exocad DentalCAD is paid software — Flex subscription or perpetual licence — with Core listed from about $125 a month. Trial availability varies by reseller, so ask before you buy.

**Do these tools work in India without a VPN?**
Yes. All three are sold and used in India through local resellers, and none needs a VPN. Confirm rupee pricing with your reseller.
