These ten tools turn flat designs into realistic product photos.

1. Mockey: apparel mockups, 3 daily downloads.
2. Placeit: lifestyle mockups, templates watermarked.
3. Canva: device mockups, free basic templates.
4. Mediamodifier: print mockups, limited downloads.
5. Dynamic Mockups: bulk mockups, 50 credits.
6. Printful: product mockups, unlimited with account.
7. Printify: store mockups, unlimited with account.
8. Previewed: app mockups, 720p free, attribution.
9. Mockuuups: device mockups, 5 exports.
10. Artboard Studio: branding mockups, limited templates.
