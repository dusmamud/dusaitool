The direct answer is this: for JEE Main and Advanced in India, start with the free NTA Abhyas AI app for official-style mock tests, add Embibe for AI-analysed practice on weak topics, use Doubtnut when a textbook question needs a quick video solution, and use ChatGPT or Gemini in study mode when a concept needs explaining step by step. The routine that works is simple: one timed mock every week, daily practice on your weakest chapters, and revision of every mistake within two days.

## NTA Abhyas AI: the mock-test starting point

NTA Abhyas AI is the National Testing Agency's own practice app, and that is why it comes first. It offers full-length, subject-wise and chapter-wise mock tests for JEE Main Paper 1 and Paper 2 in English and Hindi. Tests can be downloaded online and attempted offline. The official listing describes it as completely free, with no subscription, paywall or advertisements.

## Embibe: AI practice that finds your weak topics

Embibe is an Indian AI platform built for JEE, NEET and school exams. After a test or practice set, it analyses your attempts and sorts errors into types, such as conceptual mistakes, careless errors and time-management problems, then suggests practice on the topics where you lose the most marks. A good amount of practice content and mock tests is free, while detailed analytics and some premium test series need a paid subscription.

## Doubtnut: photo a question, get a video solution

Doubtnut is built for one job: instant doubt solving. Photograph a question from a textbook, worksheet or previous-year paper, and the app finds a step-by-step video solution. It covers Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics for JEE, plus NCERT material for Classes 6 to 12, in Hindi and English. Basic features are free, with premium content behind a subscription, and the free version may show upgrade prompts. Use it to unblock yourself, never as a replacement for solving problems on paper.

## ChatGPT: a study partner for concepts

ChatGPT's free plan offers unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse-prevention safeguards, while file uploads, image creation, voice chats and deep research have separate limits. Its Study Mode asks guiding questions instead of handing over answers, and can turn a topic into quizzes and flashcards. Two limits matter: it can make mistakes in multi-step numerical problems, so verify every final answer against a textbook.

## Gemini: guided learning and quick revision

Gemini is Google's AI assistant, free with a Google account, with daily limits and a lighter model on the free plan. Its Guided Learning mode works like a tutor: it walks through a topic step by step, asks questions, and can make quizzes and flashcards from your notes. Eligible college students aged 18 and over have been offered one year of Google AI Plus free, redeemable by 31 December 2026. Like ChatGPT, Gemini can get a calculation wrong, so cross-check numerical answers.

## Using these tools in India

Take one full mock on NTA Abhyas AI in exam conditions: phone on aeroplane mode, three hours, no breaks. Spend the next day reviewing every wrong and guessed answer — Doubtnut for textbook questions, ChatGPT Study Mode or Gemini Guided Learning for concepts, Embibe practice sets for the chapters where you lost marks. Khan Academy is worth adding for concept revision, since its full library is free, but it has no dedicated JEE test-preparation course, so use it for foundations, not JEE-pattern mocks.

## What to avoid

Do not copy an AI answer into your notes without checking it, especially for numerical problems and Organic Chemistry mechanisms, where a confident wrong step is worse than no answer. Do not buy a paid plan in the first week: use the free tier for a fortnight, and pay only for the feature you actually hit a limit on. Finally, do not let AI replace NCERT and previous-year papers, still the highest-value material for JEE Main.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which is the best free AI tool for JEE mock tests in India?**

NTA Abhyas AI: the exam body's own free app, following the JEE Main pattern.

**Can AI tools replace coaching for JEE?**

No. They explain concepts and analyse mistakes, but cannot replace a teacher or timed practice on paper.

**Is Embibe free for JEE preparation?**

Partly: much practice content is free, but detailed analytics and some test series need a paid subscription.

**Can ChatGPT solve JEE Advanced numerical problems correctly?**

Sometimes, but not reliably: it explains the method well, yet can slip in a calculation, so verify the answer.

**How many mock tests should a JEE aspirant take per week?**

One full-length timed mock per week, with chapter-wise tests on weak topics in between.

**Should I pay for Gemini AI Plus or ChatGPT Go for JEE?**

Probably not at first: the free plans cover most revision, and the Gemini student offer is only for eligible 18+ students.

## Final verdict

Start free with NTA Abhyas for mocks, add Embibe for weak-topic practice and a chatbot for doubts, and verify every AI answer. That routine, repeated weekly, is worth more than any paid plan bought in a hurry.
