ChatGPT drafts best, Claude handles long documents, and Grammarly polishes the final text. All ten work in India without a VPN.

1. ChatGPT — all-round drafting; free chats unlimited, uploads capped.
2. Claude — long documents and edits; free, resets every five hours.
3. Gemini — research, summaries and drafting; free with a Google account.
4. Grammarly — grammar, spelling, tone checks; free, 100 AI prompts monthly.
5. QuillBot — paraphrases short passages; free, 125 words per pass.
6. Rytr — short marketing copy; free, 10,000 characters a month.
7. Wordtune — rewrites sentences clearly; free, 10 rewrites a day.
8. LanguageTool — grammar checks, 20-plus languages; free, 10,000 characters per check.
9. ProWritingAid — edits long fiction, reports; free, 500 words per check.
10. Microsoft Copilot — drafts in Word, Outlook; free plan, limits unpublished.

## FAQ

**Which free AI writing tool is best for students in India?** ChatGPT for drafting plus Grammarly for checking — both free, no card needed.

## Final verdict

Start with ChatGPT and Grammarly — both free in India; pay only when a limit blocks your daily work.
