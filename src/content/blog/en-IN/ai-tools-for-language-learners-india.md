Learning a language is three jobs at once: remembering words, speaking out loud, and getting grammar right. No single app does all three well, so the stack that works for most Indian learners is a combination: one app for lessons, one for vocabulary, one for pronunciation, one for memory, and a free AI voice chat for speaking. Everything below works in India without a VPN and has a usable free tier.

## The short answer, in one table

| Tool | Best for | Free tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duolingo | Daily habit | Every course free, ads, limited hearts |
| Memrise | Vocabulary | Core lessons, some videos, limited AI chat |
| ELSA Speak | English pronunciation | Limited daily lessons, 7-day trial |
| Anki | Long-term memory | Free on desktop, Android, web |
| ChatGPT, Gemini Live | Free speaking | Free voice chat, with limits |

## Duolingo: the daily habit

Duolingo is the easiest place to start, and the hardest to quit. Its short gamified lessons cover vocabulary, grammar and listening in over 40 languages, including English, Hindi, Spanish, French and German. Every course is free with ads and a hearts system that stops lessons after too many mistakes. Super removes ads and adds unlimited hearts and offline lessons; Max adds AI conversation in the app only. Grammar is taught only lightly, so treat it as a habit, not your only teacher.

## Memrise: vocabulary that sounds real

Memrise is built around vocabulary, and every word comes with a video of a native speaker saying it in a real situation. The free plan covers core lessons, spaced-repetition review and some native-speaker videos, with ads and limits on AI chat. Pro unlocks all lessons, the full video library, offline downloads and unlimited AI conversation, at around $62 a year in the US, though sources disagree on the monthly price, reporting $20 to $25. Memrise does not teach grammar systematically, so pair it with a grammar source.

## ELSA Speak: pronunciation, sound by sound

ELSA Speak is built for one job: English pronunciation. Its AI scores your speech sound by sound, showing which vowel or consonant went wrong, and it offers over 1,600 lessons on pronunciation, stress and intonation. It teaches English only. The free plan is limited — about two lessons a day, plus a 7-day Premium trial — and prices disagree: one review found Pro at $89.99 and Premium at $99.99 a year in the US, while another lists Premium at $159.99. Check elsaspeak.com before paying.

## Anki: the memory engine

Anki is not a course at all. It is a free, open-source flashcard app built on spaced repetition: it shows each card just before you would forget it, so fifteen minutes a day keeps thousands of words in memory. It is free on desktop, Android and the web, with AnkiWeb sync; the only cost is the iPhone app, a one-time $24.99 purchase. Cards can hold audio and example sentences, and thousands of free shared decks exist, though quality varies. It teaches nothing by itself — you supply the words, and it makes sure you keep them.

## ChatGPT and Gemini Live: the free speaking partner

A general AI chatbot is the cheapest speaking partner available, and both big ones are free in India. ChatGPT's free plan gives unlimited text and limited voice chats, with no published free-minute figure, and you can ask it to correct your grammar or role-play a job interview. Gemini Live comes free with a Google account in the Gemini mobile app, and replies in dozens of languages. Neither has a curriculum or tracking, and neither corrects you unless asked — start each session by saying "correct my mistakes and explain them briefly."

## Using these tools in India

Do 15 minutes of Duolingo and 10 of Anki daily, plus one speaking session. For live partners, practise between 8 and 11 pm IST. HelloTalk connects you with native speakers who want to learn your language, free for chat and calls, with daily caps on translation. In September 2026 Airtel began offering Duolingo Super free for a year via the Airtel Thanks app — check there before paying. Prices change often — re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## What to avoid

Do not pay for a lifetime plan on your first day. Memrise has changed features before, so a lifetime deal only pays off if the app stays the same. Do not collect five apps you never open; one lesson app, one memory app and one speaking routine beats five half-finished subscriptions. Do not trust AI corrections blindly — check anything odd against a dictionary. Do not skip speaking because it feels embarrassing; ten minutes of imperfect talking beats an hour of silent lessons. Never share personal details with strangers on language-exchange apps.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I learn a language completely free?**
Yes, for the basics — the free tiers of these four tools cover lessons, vocabulary, memory and speaking, enough to reach intermediate level.

**Which tool is best for speaking practice?**
Gemini Live or ChatGPT voice for free AI speaking, ELSA Speak for pronunciation, and HelloTalk for real humans.

**Is Duolingo enough on its own?**
No — grammar is light and the free plan has no real conversation. Pair it with Anki and an AI voice chat.

**Do these tools work for Indian languages?**
Partly. Duolingo offers Hindi, but for Tamil, Telugu, Bengali or Marathi the choice is much thinner — try Anki decks and YouTube.

**How long before I can hold a basic conversation?**
With 30 minutes a day, most learners manage conversations in three to six months.

## Final verdict

Duolingo for daily habit, Memrise for words, ELSA for pronunciation, Anki for memory, and Gemini Live for free speaking practice. Start free and speak every day.
