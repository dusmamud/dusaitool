Upscayl is the best free pick for most Indians; Topaz Gigapixel suits paid pro work.

1. **Upscayl** — free open-source app, unlimited, no watermark, needs Vulkan GPU.
2. **Topaz Gigapixel** — sharpest photo detail, paid only, $149 a year.
3. **Let's Enhance** — batch upscaling, 10 free credits, watermarked, up to 8MP.
4. **Magnific** — creative AI-art upscaling to 16x, no free plan, paid only.
5. **Clipdrop** — browser upscaler, free 20 2x upscales daily.
6. **Upscale.media** — browser upscaling to 8x, free account gives 3 credits monthly.
7. **Bigjpg** — anime upscaling, free 20 images monthly, up to 4x.
8. **waifu2x** — free anime upscaler, no signup, up to 4x, 5MB limit.
9. **Pixelcut** — product-photo upscaling, three free downloads daily.
10. **Remini** — old blurry photos fixed, free download, ad-gated and watermarked.

## FAQ

**Which upscaler is best for free use in India?** Upscayl, if your PC has a Vulkan GPU; otherwise try Clipdrop's 2x upscales.

## Final verdict

Start with Upscayl free; pay for Topaz only for pro photo work.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
