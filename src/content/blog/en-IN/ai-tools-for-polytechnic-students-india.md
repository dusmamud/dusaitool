Polytechnic students need three things from AI: organised notes, help with practicals, and a placement-ready resume. The best free trio is ChatGPT for doubts and practical write-ups, Notion for notes and timetables, and Canva for resumes. All three work in India without a VPN, with a genuinely usable free plan.

| Need | Best free pick | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notes and revision | Notion | Unlimited pages, timetables, search |
| Practicals and lab work | ChatGPT | Explains procedures, drafts write-ups |
| Resume and placements | Canva | Free templates, PDF export |

## ChatGPT — doubts, practicals and lab write-ups

ChatGPT is the most useful all-rounder for diploma students. Ask it to explain a workshop procedure, check a circuit calculation, or turn lab readings into a clean observation table. The free plan costs ₹0; OpenAI lists unlimited everyday text chats, though uploads, images and voice are limited. Older guides still quote a "10 messages per 5 hours" cap, so treat exact limits as changeable. Never copy a practical write-up word for word — wrong values can fail a viva.

## Notion — notes that stay organised

Notion keeps all your subjects' notes, practical records and revision timetable in one workspace. The free plan gives one user unlimited pages and blocks, with a 5 MB cap per file upload. Students with a school email can claim the Plus plan free, which lifts the upload cap. But Notion AI itself is only a short trial on free and student plans; full AI needs a paid Business plan. Treat Notion as a notes system, not an AI.

## Canva — placement resumes that look professional

Canva turns a plain diploma resume into a clean, professional PDF with free templates and drag-and-drop editing. The free plan costs ₹0 and includes PDF export; Pro costs about ₹500 a month in India. Pick a plain, single-column template: heavily designed two-column resumes often fail the automated screening (ATS) used by recruiters.

## Using these tools in India

All three tools accept Indian payment methods — ChatGPT Go costs ₹399 a month and Plus ₹1,999, while Canva Pro bills in rupees — and none needs a VPN. Prices and free limits change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying. Campus Wi-Fi and low-end phones handle all three fine, since they run in the browser. ## What to avoid

Do not submit AI-written practicals or assignments without checking every value, formula and unit yourself. Do not pay for a premium plan before the free plan actually limits you, and never share institute logins or personal documents with a random AI website.

## FAQ

**Which AI tool is best for polytechnic students?** ChatGPT, because it covers doubts, practicals and interview prep in one place, and its free plan is enough for most diploma work.

**Can I use these tools for free?** Yes. ChatGPT, Notion and Canva all have free plans that cover the core needs; pay only if you hit a real limit.

**Will AI write my practical file for me?** It can draft it, but you must verify every reading and calculation — examiners ask viva questions on exactly those details.

**Is a Canva resume accepted by recruiters?** Yes, if you choose a simple single-column template and export a clean PDF; fancy graphics can confuse screening software.

## Final verdict

ChatGPT, Notion and Canva cover notes, practicals and placements — all free to start in India.
