Homeopathy runs on long, detailed case-taking: symptoms in the patient's own words become repertory rubrics, then remedies are compared in the materia medica. Software helps exactly there — finding rubrics faster, keeping case files searchable, and making sure follow-ups are not forgotten. It does not prescribe for you, and it cannot diagnose conditions that need modern medical care. Prices change often; re-check official pricing pages before paying.

## The short answer

Start free and pay only when the caseload justifies it: Similia's free tier is the best first step for repertory work, Practo Ray's free tier covers records and appointments for a solo doctor, and the free WhatsApp Business app handles reminders. Complete Dynamics has a free offline edition; Zomeo is the paid heavyweight.

## 1. Similia — free AI repertory search

Similia is a browser-based homeopathy platform combining a classical repertorisation workspace with AI features. Its semantic search finds matching rubrics from Kent, Boericke and Boenninghausen for symptoms typed in the patient's own words. It returns text already in the books, so it cannot invent a rubric. The free tier includes eight classical repertories, 27 materia medica books and three new cases a month. Paid Pro adds AI case analysis, transcription and photo analysis; prices disagree (about €22.48 or $29.99 a month), so check similia.io first.

## 2. Hompath Zomeo — the big paid library

Hompath Zomeo is the long-established Indian homeopathy software, used by practitioners, teachers and students. It advertises 1,300+ materia medica and repertory books, including Kent, Boenninghausen, Boger and the Complete Repertory, plus a full mobile app. The catch is price: TechJockey lists plans from about ₹45,000 per licence excluding GST, while other listings quote ₹36,000 or ₹90,000. A 30-day trial is offered. It suits a busy practice; a new solo clinic will rarely recover that cost in a year.

## 3. Complete Dynamics — the offline workhorse

Complete Dynamics is a cross-platform repertory program built around the Complete Repertory. Its free Amateur edition includes Kent's Repertory and basic materia medica — enough for a student or practitioner wanting a reliable offline reference. The paid Practitioner edition adds the Complete Repertory, more books and case management, at about ₹600.79 a month per TechJockey, or about €8 elsewhere. Its strength is offline use: once installed, it works without internet. Its library is smaller — about 40 books against Zomeo's 1,300.

## 4. Practo Ray and WhatsApp Business — records and reminders

Repertory software does not run a clinic. Practo Ray handles the administrative half: scheduling, patient records, digital prescriptions, billing and SMS reminders. Its free tier covers one doctor, though one review caps it at 50 appointments a month; billing, clinical notes and ABDM need a paid plan. Paid plans start at about ₹999 a month per TechJockey. For reminders, the free WhatsApp Business app reaches patients where they are: India has roughly 535 million WhatsApp users, with open rates near 98%. Message only patients who agreed to WhatsApp contact; never share a diagnosis or prescription detail in a chat. ## Using these tools in India

Start with the free stack — Similia for repertory, Practo Ray for records, WhatsApp Business for reminders — which costs nothing. Use a clinic-owned number, not your personal number, and take patient consent before storing records or sending messages: under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, consent is central, and health data is highly sensitive. Keep a paper or local backup of every case file: a cloud account can be locked and a subscription can lapse.

## What to avoid

No AI tool can diagnose or pick a remedy — repertory software is a reference aid, and prescribing remains the practitioner's responsibility. Do not paste identifiable patient details into a general chatbot such as ChatGPT or Gemini; those chats are not a medical record system. Avoid cracked copies of paid repertory software: they may carry malware that steals patient data. Do not pay a large licence fee before a trial. Homeopathy is a traditional and alternative system; where modern medical care is needed — an emergency, a serious infection, a worsening child — refer promptly.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can AI choose a homeopathic remedy for my patient?**
No. AI can suggest a shortlist of rubrics and remedies, but the final remedy, potency and repetition are the practitioner's decision.

**Which repertory software is free for homeopaths in India?**
Similia's free tier and Complete Dynamics' free Amateur edition: eight repertories and three cases a month on Similia, Kent's Repertory offline on Complete Dynamics.

**Is Hompath Zomeo worth ₹45,000 for a small clinic?**
Only if you use its 1,300-book library and mobile app daily. A new solo clinic can manage with Similia free plus Complete Dynamics Amateur.

**How do I send appointment reminders to homeopathy patients?**
Use the free WhatsApp Business app: a confirmation at booking, a reminder the evening before, and a nudge two hours before the visit.

Similia's free tier is the best starting point; add Practo Ray and WhatsApp Business for records and reminders. Start free, test every tool with real cases for a month, and pay only for what has proved its worth.
