## The direct answer

Kabaddi is one of India's oldest sports, and the Pro Kabaddi League turned it into prime-time television, with packed stadiums, star raiders and defenders, and fans who argue about every raid. A kabaddi fan today wants simple things: to know when their team plays, to watch the match live without missing the best raids, to check the score while travelling, and to settle an argument about which player scored the most points. The direct answer is simple: use JioHotstar to watch matches live, FanCode for scores and schedules, the Pro Kabaddi app for official stats and news, YouTube for highlights and player interviews, and ChatGPT to explain rules, stats and match stories in plain language.

These tools work because they match how Indian sports fans actually follow a game. Most fans watch on a phone, check the score during a commute, and discuss the match on WhatsApp the same evening. A good app gives the score in seconds, shows the points table, and lets you catch the highlights if you missed the live match. But there is an honest limit: scores and stats should come from official sources, because AI and fan pages can get a score, a player's record or a match time wrong. All five tools have free plans, though live streaming usually needs a subscription, so check the official page before paying.

## 2. FanCode: fast scores, schedules and points table

FanCode is the app many Indian sports fans open for a quick score, because it shows live scores, match schedules, the points table and player stats in a clean, fast layout. For kabaddi, it is useful when you cannot watch the match live: a quick check during a commute tells you the score, who is leading, and which raider is scoring. How to start: follow your favourite team in the app and turn on match alerts for their games. Watch out for: live scores can lag by a few seconds, so for a very close finish, the official broadcast is the most accurate source.

## 3. Pro Kabaddi app: official stats, news and fixtures

The official Pro Kabaddi app is the best source for facts about the league, because it comes from the league itself. It has fixtures, results, the points table, player statistics such as raid points and tackle points, team news and videos, all in one place. For a fan who argues about which raider is the best this season, the app settles it with real numbers. It is free, and it is the safest place to check a match time or a player's record before repeating it to friends. Check the official page for the latest version.

How to start: check the points table and the top raiders list before your team's next match, so you know the story of the game. Watch out for: stats pages update after each match, so a number from yesterday may already be old; check the date on the page.

YouTube is where kabaddi fans go after the match, for highlights, the best raids of the night, player interviews and analysis videos. The official Pro Kabaddi channel posts highlights and short clips, which is perfect when you missed the live game and want the best moments in ten minutes. ## 5. ChatGPT: explain the rules and the match story

ChatGPT is useful for fans who are new to kabaddi or who want to understand a match better. Ask it to explain the rules in simple words, such as what a super raid is, how the points table works, or why a defender gets a point for a tackle. It can also summarise a match story, compare two players, or explain a rule in Hindi for a friend who is watching for the first time. The free plan is enough for fan questions, and paid plans add more features, so check current pricing on the official page.

How to start: ask it to explain one rule you never understood, then watch the next match and spot that rule in action. Watch out for: AI can invent a score, a record or a match result, so never trust it for live scores or player stats; check those on FanCode or the official app.

## Using AI in India: kabaddi fans

Kabaddi fandom in India has its own shape, and the tools should fit it. First, language: commentary and discussion happen in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi and other languages, so pick apps and channels that cover your language and your team. Second, timing: most matches are in the evening, so set alerts and check the schedule in the morning instead of missing the first half. Third, community: kabaddi is a family and friends sport, so watching together and discussing on WhatsApp is half the fun, but keep the discussion friendly and never share unofficial streaming links. Finally, money: live streaming usually needs a subscription, so check the plan and share it honestly within the family instead of paying for three separate subscriptions.

## What to avoid

Avoid unofficial streaming links on social media, because they are often illegal, full of malware, and can steal your login details. Avoid trusting AI or fan pages for live scores, player records or match times, and check those on FanCode or the official app. Avoid paying for three separate subscriptions when one family plan covers everyone, and avoid arguing about a player or a team in a way that turns abusive. Avoid sharing your streaming login with strangers, and avoid betting on matches, because betting is illegal in most of India and loses money fast.

A super raid is when a raider scores three or more points in a single raid, usually by touching multiple defenders or getting them out. Ask ChatGPT to explain it with an example, then watch for it in the next match to see it in action.
