HubSpot and Zoho CRM are the safest free starts for Indian teams, working without a VPN. Sources disagree on Freshsales and Bitrix24 limits.

1. HubSpot: Breeze AI drafts emails, tracks deals; free: 2 users, 1,000 contacts.
2. Zoho CRM: Zia AI scores leads and follow-ups; free: 3 users, 5,000 records.
3. Freshsales: Freddy AI scores leads, prioritises deals; free: 3 users.
4. Bigin: Zoho's simple pipeline CRM for solos; free: 1 user, 500 records.
5. Capsule: simple pipeline and contact history; free: 2 users, 250 contacts.
6. EngageBay: CRM plus email marketing; free: 15 users, 250 contacts.
7. Odoo CRM: one full app free, unlimited users; extra apps cost more.
8. ClickUp: lists and boards shaped into a CRM; free forever, unlimited members.
9. Bitrix24: CRM, chat and tasks together; free plan, user cap disputed.
10. Attio: AI-enriched contacts, flexible pipelines; free: 50,000 records.

**Which free AI CRM is best for a small Indian team?** HubSpot for two users, Zoho CRM for three; both work without a VPN.

**Verdict:** Start with HubSpot or Zoho CRM free; upgrade only when you outgrow the cap.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
