Most Indians need only Grammarly for emails, LanguageTool for other languages, and QuillBot for rewriting. All ten work in India without a VPN.

1. Grammarly: fixes spelling, grammar and tone; free, 100 AI prompts monthly.
2. LanguageTool: grammar in 30+ languages; free, 2,000 characters per check (sources differ).
3. QuillBot: paraphrases and fixes grammar; free, 125 words per paraphrase.
4. ProWritingAid: deep grammar and style reports; free checks 500 words at once.
5. Hemingway Editor: flags long sentences and passive voice; free editor, readability only.
6. Trinka: academic grammar for research papers; free Basic plan, 5,000 words monthly.
7. Ginger: corrects grammar from sentence context; free version has weekly limits.
8. Wordtune: rewrites sentences and fixes grammar; free, 10 rewrites daily.
9. DeepL Write: polishes short text in several languages; free, 1,500 characters.
10. Sapling: grammar suggestions in Gmail and Docs; free plan covers basic corrections.

**Which free grammar checker suits Indian students best?** Grammarly for daily writing, QuillBot for paraphrasing, Trinka for research papers.

**Final verdict:** Start with Grammarly free, add LanguageTool for other languages, and pay only when free limits block your work.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
