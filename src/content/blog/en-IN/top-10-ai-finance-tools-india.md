These ten tools cover budgeting, investing and credit in India without a VPN. Start free; never share OTPs or passwords.

1. ChatGPT — explains budgets and SIPs plainly; free, uploads capped.
2. Gemini — summarises statements in Hindi; free with Google account.
3. Perplexity — checks fund facts with citations; free, 3 Pro searches, limits vary.
4. INDmoney — tracks net worth across banks; free, ₹0 AMC demat.
5. ET Money — tracks spends, runs direct SIPs; free, premium ₹99/month.
6. Groww — buys stocks and direct funds; free opening, ₹0 AMC.
7. Zerodha Kite — ₹0 delivery brokerage; opening free, AMC later.
8. Moneyview — auto-sorts bank SMS into spending categories; free expense tracking.
9. CRED — pays card bills, tracks score; free, needs 750+ score.
10. Kuvera — invests in zero-commission direct funds; free, no demat needed.

**Which tool should a beginner start with?** ET Money or Groww: free, simple, and built for Indian accounts.

## Final verdict

Start with ET Money for spending, Groww for investing, and ChatGPT for plain explanations — all free.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
