Running a housekeeping business in India means juggling bookings, staff, checklists, payments and complaints — often on WhatsApp. Start free, and pay only when volume forces you to. WhatsApp Business plus Connecteam's free plan covers bookings, checklists and staff chat at zero cost. Past about 40 recurring bookings a month, ZenMaid is the cheapest purpose-built option at roughly $19 a month, while Jobber fits larger crews that also need quotes and invoicing.

## Quick comparison

| Tool | Best for | Free option | Starting paid price |
|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Business | Bookings and chat | Free app | Free |
| Connecteam | Staff, checklists, clock-in | Free up to 10 users | $29/month per hub, annual |
| ZenMaid | Recurring bookings | 14-day trial | $19/month, 40 appointments |
| Jobber | Quotes, invoicing, payments | 14-day trial | $29/month, annual |

Prices change often — re-check before paying.

## WhatsApp Business

WhatsApp Business is the free app from Meta for small businesses, and in India it is where most housekeeping bookings start. It adds a business profile, a catalogue of cleaning packages with prices, quick replies and automatic greeting and away messages. The limits matter: it suits one operator on one number, broadcasts cap at 256 recipients, and there is no booking calendar or payment collection — pair it with a scheduling tool once bookings grow.

## Connecteam

Connecteam is a mobile-first app for deskless teams: scheduling, a GPS time clock, team chat and checklists. For housekeeping, its strongest feature is the checklist. Build a room-by-room checklist once — kitchen, bathrooms, floors, windows — and every cleaner ticks it off on their phone, with photos as proof. The free Small Business plan covers up to 10 users across all hubs. Paid plans start at about $29 a month per hub billed annually, but each hub is billed separately, and it handles no payments or payroll.

## ZenMaid

ZenMaid is scheduling software made for residential maid services. Its calendar is built around recurring appointments — same client, same day, same cleaner — with online booking forms, reminders, and invoicing via Stripe and Square. Pricing is a base fee plus a per-cleaner charge: Starter costs about $19 a month plus $4 per cleaner, capped at 40 appointments a month; Pro is about $39 plus $14 per cleaner, unlimited. No free plan, only a 14-day trial; text reminders cost extra. It works in India without a VPN but bills in US dollars.

## Jobber

Jobber is a broader field-service platform: quotes, scheduling, invoicing, payments and reporting in one app. For firms adding deep cleaning, pest control or handyman work, that breadth is the point — one system instead of four. The cost is the catch: Core starts at about $49 a month month-to-month, or $29 a month billed annually, for one user, with extra users at about $29 each. Confirm the current price on Jobber's own page. There is no free plan, only a 14-day trial.

## Urban Company

Urban Company is not management software — it is India's largest home-services marketplace. Customers book deep cleaning, bathroom, kitchen and sofa cleaning through the app, with city-wise prices. Recent Delhi-NCR listings put deep cleaning from about ₹3,199, with bathroom cleaning from about ₹499. As a provider you get demand without marketing, but the platform owns the customer relationship and takes its cut. Many small firms use it for discovery and WhatsApp for repeat customers.

## Using these tools in India

First, customers expect WhatsApp and UPI, so keep WhatsApp Business as the front door even after buying scheduling software. Second, most of these tools bill in US dollars — a $19 plan is roughly ₹1,700 a month before conversion charges. Third, staff phones are usually budget Android devices on patchy data, so test on your cleaners' actual phones first. Start with the free stack and track missed or double-booked jobs each month.

## What to avoid

Do not buy an annual plan on day one. Run the trial with real bookings and staff, and pay annually only after a full busy week. Do not promise an AI chatbot until you have tested it — some advertised AI features are paid add-ons. Do not store customer addresses, keys and entry codes in plain chat; use the tool's customer records instead. Never hand over Aadhaar or bank details during signup — legitimate scheduling software does not need them.

## FAQ

**Can I run a housekeeping business with free tools only?**
Yes, up to about ten staff. WhatsApp Business handles bookings and chat for free, and Connecteam's free plan covers scheduling, checklists and clock-ins for up to ten users. You pay only when you outgrow those limits.

**Which tool is best for recurring weekly cleaning?**
ZenMaid — its calendar is built around repeat appointments with the same cleaner and client. Connecteam schedules recurring shifts too, but customer self-booking is weaker.

**Do these tools work in India without a VPN?**
Yes, all five work in India without a VPN. ZenMaid, Jobber and Connecteam bill in US dollars, so check conversion charges and confirm prices on the official pricing page before paying.

**How do I handle payments from customers?**
Use UPI for direct customers, and the payment feature inside Jobber or ZenMaid if you already use one. Urban Company handles payment itself for marketplace bookings. Confirm the UPI ID belongs to your business, and send a WhatsApp receipt after every job.

## Final verdict

Start free with WhatsApp Business and Connecteam; add ZenMaid when recurring bookings grow past 40 a month. That stack covers bookings, staff, checklists and customers without paying for features you do not yet need.
