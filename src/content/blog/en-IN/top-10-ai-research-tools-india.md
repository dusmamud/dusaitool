Start free with Elicit, Consensus and Semantic Scholar for papers, plus Perplexity for cited research. All ten work in India without a VPN.

1. Elicit: searches 138 million papers; free: unlimited search, 2 reports monthly.
2. Consensus: answers from 250 million papers; free: 3 Deep Searches monthly.
3. Semantic Scholar: free search across 200 million papers; no account needed.
4. Perplexity: cited answers from the live web; free: 3 Pro Searches a day.
5. Gemini Deep Research: reports in the free Gemini app; daily limits.
6. ChatGPT: explains methods and runs deep research; free daily limits.
7. NotebookLM: answers from your papers; free: 100 notebooks, 50 sources each.
8. ResearchRabbit: maps citation networks for related papers; free: 50 seed articles.
9. Connected Papers: visual graphs of similar papers; free: 5 graphs monthly.
10. Google Scholar: search across literature; citation tracking, no account needed.

**Which free AI research tool suits Indian students best?** Start with Semantic Scholar and Elicit, then add Perplexity.

## Verdict

Start free with Elicit, Semantic Scholar and Perplexity, verify every citation, and pay only when a limit blocks real work.
