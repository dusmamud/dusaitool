For most Indian electronics manufacturers, the best start is Landing AI for visual quality inspection and Zoho Inventory for orders and stock. Instrumental is the specialist choice for PCB assembly lines that must prove defect rates to large buyers.

## Short answer

| Need | Best tool | Free option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visual quality inspection | Landing AI | Free tier, 1,000 credits a month |
| PCB assembly defect tracking | Instrumental | No free tier; sales-led pricing |
| Orders, stock and buyers | Zoho Inventory | Free plan, 50 orders a month |

## Landing AI

Landing AI (LandingLens) trains a visual inspection model from photos of good and bad parts, flagging scratches, missing components, wrong placement and solder faults. It works with only a few defect examples, which suits small units that cannot collect thousands of faulty boards. The free tier offers 1,000 credits a month; paid plans start around $39 a month, though sources disagree, so re-check official pricing before paying.

## Instrumental

Instrumental is made for electronics assembly. Its cameras record every unit, link each defect to the station and step that caused it, and build a record buyers can audit. That traceability matters when an OEM or export customer asks why a batch failed. Pricing is not public and is quoted per factory, so it fits once volumes or buyer demands justify a sales call.

## Zoho Inventory

Zoho Inventory, from Chennai-based Zoho Corporation, handles the commercial side: sales and purchase orders, stock, GST invoices and buyer records in one place. The free plan covers 50 orders a month for one user; paid Indian plans start at ₹999 a month billed annually, excluding GST. It connects to Amazon and Shopify, so online and offline orders stay together.

## Using these tools in India

Start on one line, not the whole factory. Photograph good boards and every defect you find for two weeks, then train Landing AI on that set. Run Zoho Inventory beside your register for a month before switching. All three work in India without a VPN, but prices and free limits change, so re-check official pages before paying or promising a buyer a deadline.

## What to avoid

Do not buy an inspection system before collecting real defect photos from your own line — a model trained on generic images will miss your actual faults. Do not promise zero defects based on a demo, and do not upload customer designs to a free AI tool without checking its data policy.

## FAQ

**Which AI tool is best for PCB quality inspection in India?**

Landing AI is the best start for small units: its free tier lets you test on your own boards. Instrumental is stronger for high-volume lines needing defect traceability for buyer audits.

**Can small electronics manufacturers use these tools for free?**

Partly. Landing AI offers 1,000 free credits a month and Zoho Inventory 50 free orders a month. Instrumental has no verified free tier, so budget for a quote.

**Do these tools work without a VPN in India?**

Yes — all three run on normal internet. Zoho is Indian, with INR billing and GST invoicing, which simplifies payment and tax records.

**Will AI inspection replace manual quality checks?**

No. AI catches visual defects consistently and fast, but cannot judge every fault, such as functional test failures. Use AI to screen units and keep humans for final checks.

## Final verdict

Start with Landing AI's free tier for inspection and Zoho Inventory for orders; add Instrumental only when buyer audits demand it.
