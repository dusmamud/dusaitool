Namelix is the best free start; Shopify suits online stores. All ten tools work in India without a VPN, though sources differ on limits.

1. **Namelix** — short brandable names; free, logos paid.
2. **Shopify** — about 100 store names; free, no signup.
3. **Looka** — names plus logos; names free, logos paid.
4. **NameSnack** — catchy names from keywords; free, .com checks.
5. **Wix** — industry names, domain checks; free, unlimited searches.
6. **Squadhelp (Atom)** — AI names, human contests; generator free.
7. **BrandBucket** — premium names with domains; from about $1,000.
8. **Hostinger** — quick AI names; free, domain checks included.
9. **DomainWheel** — domain-first names, .com/.net/.org checks; free.
10. **Namify** — AI names, domain/social/trademark checks; free.

**Which free tool should Indian founders try first?** Namelix for brandable names, or Shopify for a store name.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.

## Final verdict

Start with Namelix, then Shopify — both free; verify domain and trademark first.
