Yes — three free tools cover almost everything an SSC aspirant needs. Testbook gives you timed mock tests and previous-year questions, ChatGPT explains reasoning questions step by step, and Gemini turns your notes into Hindi quizzes for revision. Start with all three free tiers, and pay only when a paid plan clearly adds something you use.

### 1. ChatGPT — best for reasoning doubts

ChatGPT's free plan costs ₹0 and offers unlimited everyday text chats, subject to guardrails; uploads, images, and voice are limited. Ask it to explain a reasoning question step by step, generate twenty mixed questions, or drill a weak topic daily. Cross-check every answer against official SSC material — AI can state wrong facts with full confidence. Plus is ₹1,999 a month in India; re-check pricing before paying.

### 2. Gemini — best for Hindi quizzes and revision

Gemini is free with a Google account and works well in Hindi and Hinglish. Paste your notes and ask for a twenty-question quiz, or a one-page revision summary. Google has added free full-length mock tests inside Gemini for exams such as JEE, but no SSC-specific official mock has been confirmed, so use it for self-made quizzes rather than real SSC papers. Check Gemini's official page for current student offers first.

### 3. Testbook — best for timed mocks and previous-year questions

Testbook's SSC CGL series lists 2,537 tests, including 55 free ones, in English and Hindi. You get sectional tests, chapter-wise quizzes, previous-year questions, and detailed analysis after each attempt. The app is free on Google Play, but unlimited access needs a paid Pass, so check the current price on Testbook's site before buying. Timed mocks with negative marking mirror the real exam better than any chatbot.

## Using these tools in India

All three work in India without a VPN, on a basic Android phone and patchy mobile data. ChatGPT and Gemini both accept Hindi prompts, and Testbook's tests are bilingual. Paid plans bill in rupees via UPI or cards, but prices and free limits change often — re-check the official pricing page before paying. If you are weighing other exam paths too, see our guide to [AI tools for CA students](/blog/ai-tools-for-ca-students-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not trust AI-generated GK facts, dates, or current-affairs answers without an official source. Do not buy a yearly pass before finishing the free tests. Do not skip previous-year papers, and do not study only from AI summaries — the exam rewards the official syllabus, not chatbot notes.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can AI tools replace coaching for SSC?** No. They are best for practice, doubt-solving, and revision. Coaching or a good book still matters for syllabus, strategy, and discipline.

**Are these tools really free?** The core features are free: ChatGPT text chats, Gemini quizzes, and dozens of Testbook tests. Full mock access and advanced features are paid.

**Which tool is best for GK and current affairs?** None of these is a GK source. Use them to quiz yourself, but learn current affairs from newspapers and official sources.

**Can I prepare fully in Hindi?** Yes. Gemini and ChatGPT accept Hindi, and Testbook offers bilingual tests — though SSC's English section still needs English practice.

## Final verdict

Start free: Testbook for timed mocks, ChatGPT for reasoning doubts, Gemini for Hindi revision — verify every GK fact before trusting it.
