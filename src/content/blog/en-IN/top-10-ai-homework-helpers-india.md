Ten AI helpers explain school homework.

1. ChatGPT tutors any subject; free, daily limits.
2. Gemini explains with diagrams; free, daily limits.
3. Photomath solves maths from photos; steps free.
4. Gauth solves STEM from photos; free limit varies.
5. Brainly answers all subjects; free with ads.
6. Khan Academy lessons free; AI tutor costs extra.
7. Google Lens explains homework photos; completely free.
8. NotebookLM studies your notes; free, 50 sources.
9. Quizlet makes flashcards, quizzes; free, limited AI.
10. Wolfram Alpha computes maths; steps need Pro.

## FAQ

**Which helper is best for maths?** Photomath for steps; Gauth for science too.

## Final verdict

Start with ChatGPT for subjects and Photomath for maths, both free.
