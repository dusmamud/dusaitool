A sports goods shop stocks hundreds of items — bats, rackets, boots, gloves, jerseys in every size. The right tools cut billing time, stop stock-outs, and keep academy customers coming back. Here are five tools that work in India without a VPN, with honest free limits. Prices change often, so re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## Vyapar — billing and stock for a single shop

Vyapar is a billing and inventory app built for Indian small businesses. The Android app has a permanently free tier covering GST invoicing, basic inventory, ledgers, and payment reminders, and it works fully offline. Low-stock alerts and barcode scanning flag a size-9 boot that sells out on Saturday before a customer asks. Paid desktop plans start from about ₹3,399 a year plus GST.

## Zoho Inventory — when orders come from more than one place

Zoho Inventory is the step up when a shop sells through its counter, its website, and a marketplace at the same time. It manages stock and orders across channels, with composite items and backordering even on the free plan. The free plan covers 50 orders a month for one user at one location. Paid plans start at about ₹999 a month billed annually. But the 51st order in a month forces an upgrade.

## myBillBook — cheap billing with a free-plan question mark

myBillBook is a mobile-first Indian GST billing app used by more than one crore small businesses. It handles GST bills, barcode billing, multi-location inventory, e-invoicing, and automatic GSTR reports. Its barcode alerts matter most: scan a bat, and stock drops automatically. But sources disagree about its free plan. Its website describes a free basic plan, while its App Store listing describes a 14-day trial, with continued access needing a paid subscription. Paid plans reportedly start at about ₹399 a year for Android-only Silver; Plus is about ₹3,490 a year. Treat it as a low-cost paid tool; confirm the offer on mybillbook.in first.

## WhatsApp Business — your shop's order counter on the phone

Most Indian customers message a shop on WhatsApp before visiting, so the free WhatsApp Business app is a sports shop's cheapest sales channel. Its catalogue lets customers browse bats, rackets, shoes, and kits with photos and prices inside WhatsApp. Quick replies answer the questions every shop hears — "size 8 available?", "what is the price?" — with one tap. Broadcasts reach up to 256 contacts who saved your number. Keep broadcasts to two to four a month.

## Canva — posters and offer cards without a designer

Canva's free plan covers a sports shop's marketing: templates for Instagram posts, WhatsApp catalogue cards, offer posters, and price lists, plus about 5 GB of storage and one Brand Kit. A season-sale poster takes minutes, not a trip to a print shop. The free plan has a limited monthly allowance of AI tools, and background removal and resizing sit on paid plans. Reported India pricing puts Pro at about ₹4,000 a year, with a cheaper Pro Lite tier. Eligible Jio users have been offered Canva Pro or Pro Lite free for 12 months, but that is a promotion, not a permanent price.

## How to use these tools in an Indian sports shop

Put every item in Vyapar or myBillBook with its size, colour, and MRP before the season starts, and stick barcodes on the fast movers. Add your 20 to 30 best sellers to the WhatsApp catalogue with real photos from your shop. Use Zoho Inventory only if you genuinely sell online as well as offline; otherwise it adds work without adding sales. Each Monday, check low-stock alerts, order what is short, and send one WhatsApp update about new stock. Record every credit sale the same day.

## What to avoid

Do not buy a paid plan before the free tier proves itself for one full month of real billing. Do not put your whole catalogue on WhatsApp in one day — wrong prices are worse than a small accurate list. Do not send daily broadcasts; customers block spamming numbers, and a blocked number loses orders too. Do not trust any tool's AI price suggestion blindly — check it against your supplier's bill. Do not run the shop on one phone with no backup: export billing data weekly.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which is the best free tool for a small sports shop in India?** Vyapar's free mobile app: GST billing, basic inventory, and offline working in one app built for Indian shops. Pair it with WhatsApp Business for orders.

**Can these tools handle sizes and colours for shoes and jerseys?** Yes. Vyapar and myBillBook track variants and barcodes; Zoho Inventory handles composite items and multi-location stock. Enter each size as its own item, or sizes will run out silently.

**Is WhatsApp Business really free?** The app is free, including the catalogue, quick replies, and 256-contact broadcasts. The separate API charges per conversation and suits larger volumes only.

**Do I need Zoho Inventory if I only sell from my shop?** No. One counter needs only Vyapar or myBillBook. Zoho Inventory earns its place only when you also sell through a website or marketplace.

**Are the prices in this article guaranteed?** No. Prices and free limits change, and sources disagreed about myBillBook's free plan. Always re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## Final verdict

Start free with Vyapar for billing and WhatsApp Business for orders; add Zoho Inventory only when online orders grow. Each paid upgrade after that should be earned by real sales, not bought on hope.
