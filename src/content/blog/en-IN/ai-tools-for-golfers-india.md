## 1. 18Birdies — the all-rounder

18Birdies is the best first download for most Indian golfers. Its free tier covers GPS to greens, hazards and layups on 45,000+ courses, a scorecard, basic stats and side games. Premium (about $99.99 a year or $19.99 a month) adds plays-like distances, club recommendations, 3D green maps, strokes gained and an AI swing analyzer. Its handicap is unofficial.

## 2. Golfshot — best with an Apple Watch

Golfshot's free tier is genuinely usable: GPS on 45,000+ courses, a scorecard for up to four players and basic 3D flyovers. Pro (about $79.99 a year) adds full yardages, Smart Caddy club recommendations, strokes gained and automatic shot tracking (needs Apple Watch Series 8 or newer). Golfshot links with the USGA, so scores can post to a GHIN Handicap Index.

## 3. V1 Golf — swing video, not a caddie

V1 Golf is a video tool, not a GPS app. Record your swing, watch it in slow motion, draw lines and angles, and compare it with tour-pro model swings. The free download covers recording, slow-motion playback and basic drawing; V1 Golf Plus (about $9.99 a month; yearly prices vary by listing) unlocks the full toolkit, model swing library and drills. It shows your swing; it will not diagnose your fault.

## Using these tools in India

All three work in India without a VPN, on the Indian App Store and Google Play. Delhi Golf Club is listed on 18Birdies, so major courses are covered — but confirm your own course is mapped before a paid round, since smaller clubs may be missing. Most Indian clubs are private, so an app never buys you course access.

## What to avoid

Do not pay for all three Premium plans at once — start free for a month and see which one you open. Never trust an app's handicap in a competition; only an official index counts. Switch off plays-like distances and club advice in a counting round, since the Rules of Golf restrict them.

## FAQ

**Which golf app is best for beginners in India?**
18Birdies — its free GPS, scorecard and stats cover most Indian courses.

**Do these apps work without a paid subscription?**
Yes. GPS, scoring and slow-motion video are free; paid plans add club advice and deeper analysis.

**Can I get an official handicap from these apps?**
Not from 18Birdies or V1 Golf. Golfshot links to a GHIN Handicap Index; check what your club accepts.

**Is the AI swing analysis accurate?**
Useful, not definitive. Cameras can misread angles, so confirm feedback with a coach.

## Final verdict

Start free with 18Birdies for GPS and scoring; add V1 Golf for swing video and Golfshot Pro only if you wear an Apple Watch.
