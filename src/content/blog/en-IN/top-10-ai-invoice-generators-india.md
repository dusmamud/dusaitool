For Indian freelancers and small firms, Zoho Invoice leads with free GST invoices, reminders and a client portal. Free caps vary by source.

1. Zoho Invoice: free GST invoices, reminders, client portal; 500 invoices yearly.
2. Refrens: GST invoicing for Indian freelancers; 15 free documents, international payments.
3. Vyapar: offline-first GST billing for shops; free Android app, stock tracking.
4. myBillBook: mobile GST billing for shops; free basic tier, WhatsApp sharing.
5. Invoice Ninja: open-source invoicing, custom templates; free hosted plan, time tracking.
6. ProfitBooks: GST accounting for founders; free Startup plan, 10 invoices monthly.
7. GoGSTBill: lifetime-free GST billing; unlimited invoices, e-way bills, WhatsApp sharing.
8. FreshBooks: polished invoicing, client portal and reminders; 30-day free trial only.
9. Stripe Invoicing: online invoices with payment links; no monthly fee, per-invoice charge.
10. PayPal Invoicing: send invoices free; payment fee applies only when clients pay.

**Which free invoice tool is best for GST in India?** Zoho Invoice, for free GST invoices and GSTR-1 filing.

## Final verdict

Start with Zoho Invoice for free GST billing; try Refrens or Vyapar as you grow.

Prices and free limits change, so check the official pricing page before paying.
