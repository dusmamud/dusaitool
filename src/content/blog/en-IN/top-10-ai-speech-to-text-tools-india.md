Most Indians need two kinds: meeting notetakers and file tools. Start free; check Hindi support first.

1. Otter.ai — live meeting transcription; free: 300 minutes monthly, 30 per call.
2. Fireflies.ai — meeting notes; free transcription unlimited, storage 400 minutes.
3. Fathom — records Zoom, Meet, Teams calls; free unlimited recordings for individuals.
4. Notta — transcribes meetings and files; free: 120 minutes monthly, 3 per recording.
5. Whisper — OpenAI's free open-source model; unlimited if you run it yourself.
6. Descript — edit audio by editing text; free: 60 media minutes a month.
7. Rev — AI transcripts plus human service; free: 45 AI minutes monthly, English only.
8. TurboScribe — Whisper file transcription; free: 3 files daily, 30 minutes each.
9. Google Docs voice typing — free dictation in Docs; no cap, Hindi supported.
10. Windows voice typing — Win+H anywhere; free, no monthly cap listed.

**Which free tool is best for long meetings?** Fathom — its free plan has no minute cap.

## Verdict

Start with Fathom for meetings and TurboScribe for files — both free; check limits before paying.

Prices and free limits change — check the official page before paying.
