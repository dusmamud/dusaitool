Gemini is the best free start for most Indians; Bing gives the clearest daily limit. All ten work in India without a VPN, though limits vary by source.

1. **Gemini** — creates and edits photos in chat; free limit unpublished.
2. **ChatGPT Images** — makes images from text or photos; free limit unpublished.
3. **Bing Image Creator** — text-to-image tool; 15 fast creations daily.
4. **Leonardo** — styles, presets and upscaling; 150 tokens daily.
5. **Ideogram** — readable text in posters; 30 credits weekly.
6. **Adobe Firefly** — safe images for commercial use; free daily generations.
7. **Canva** — images inside posters and slides; 20 AI uses monthly.
8. **Playground** — templates and editing; 10 images every three hours.
9. **Recraft** — vectors, icons and illustrations; 50 credits daily.
10. **Midjourney** — artistic images; no free plan, from $10 monthly.

## FAQ

**Which free AI image generator is best in India?** Gemini for everyday use, Bing for daily limits.

Start free with Gemini or Bing, try Leonardo for styles, and check limits first.
