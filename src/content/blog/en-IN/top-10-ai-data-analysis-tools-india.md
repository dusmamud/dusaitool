Start free with ChatGPT or Gemini in Sheets.

1. ChatGPT — analyses CSVs and Excel files; free, three uploads daily.
2. Gemini in Sheets — writes formulas, pivots, charts; free with Google account.
3. Copilot in Excel — edits workbooks by prompts; needs paid Microsoft 365.
4. Julius AI — chats with spreadsheets, runs charts; free limit disputed, 5–15 messages monthly.
5. Claude for Excel — audits formulas, builds models; free plan is limited.
6. Quadratic — runs Python, SQL, formulas in one grid; free tier, limited sharing.
7. Ajelix — fixes formulas, writes VBA scripts; free, ten requests monthly.
8. Coefficient — pulls live data into Sheets; free, 5,000 rows, three sources.
9. Power BI Desktop — builds dashboards; free download, sharing needs Pro.
10. Tableau Public — creates dashboards; free, but everything published is public.

**Which free tool suits a beginner?** ChatGPT or Gemini in Sheets — both free, no VPN needed.

**Final verdict:** Start with ChatGPT or Gemini free, add Julius or Quadratic for bigger data, and Power BI for dashboards.

Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
