Running a cold storage in India means the same daily work everywhere: trucks arrive, lots go into chambers, and rent is charged per bag per day. The best tools are systems built for Indian cold storage — lot tracking, temperature alerts, gate passes, and billing with GST and hamali. Prices and limits change often — re-check the official pricing page before paying.

## 1. ColdStorageOS by Agrobricks

ColdStorageOS is an ERP built for Indian cold storages handling potato, fruits and multi-commodity stock; its maker, Agrobricks, says more than 100 cold storages across India use it. It shows which lot sits where. IoT sensors watch temperature around the clock and alert instantly when a chamber drifts. Billing covers farmers, traders and the operator, and integrates with NCDEX e-Markets (NeRL) for electronic warehouse receipts for pledged stock. Pricing is not published; ask for a quote.

## 2. StoreCrop

StoreCrop is a newer Indian product that runs agri cold and dry storage with voice and AI. It works out rent daily, bills it, and alerts the owner to anything unusual. It fits Indian conditions: rupees, GST, hamali, bank bonds, and Telugu and Hindi support. Its warehouse map gives every lot an address. Rate cards apply at inward, and bonded stock stays locked until the bank releases it. A free 14-day trial is offered. Pricing is per location per year; sensor monitoring and offline mode are add-ons, so get the full quote in writing.

## 3. Visual Softech CAP

Visual Softech's CAP is a long-running Indian cold-store accounting and inventory package, best when billing and accounts matter more than sensors. It gives real-time stock tracking party-wise and chamber-wise, plus chamber temperature tracking. It covers rental calculations, multi-commodity billing, QR-based stock entry, and daily in-and-out reports. It supports local tax structures and handles most commodities, from vegetables to frozen meat. Trial it with one chamber's real data first.

## 4. Cold Konnect Partner

Cold Konnect helps cold-storage owners fill empty space and watch temperatures from a phone app. Owners list their facility so farmers, traders and companies across India can request space directly, without a broker or commission. A gateway plus temperature and humidity sensors sends instant alerts to the owner's and supervisor's phones if a chamber drifts, and flags silent sensors fast. Promotional material has quoted pricing from ₹25,000 a year — confirm the current price on a free demo first.

## 5. Zoho Inventory

Zoho Inventory is not cold-storage software, but it is the cheapest honest way to digitise lot and batch records when a full ERP is more than you need. Made in India, it bills in rupees with GST invoices. Its free plan covers 50 orders a month, one user and up to two locations, per Zoho's FAQ — though some Indian pages list one location, so check the current plan page first. Batch and serial tracking starts from the Premium plan. Paid plans start at about ₹999 per organisation per month when billed annually, excluding GST; confirm it on Zoho's pricing page. It cannot track chamber temperature, so use it beside a monitoring setup.

## Using these tools in India

Start with the money flow. Map how rent is charged — per bag per day, per quintal, or per month — and check that the tool applies your rate card at inward, with hamali and GST. Then check temperature logging: for food and pharma rooms, sensors with SMS alerts matter more than a pretty dashboard. Nexleaf's ColdTrace, made in India and WHO-prequalified, sets the standard to expect. Many storages sit outside towns with weak networks, so ask every vendor whether the app works offline and syncs later. See also [AI tools for farmers in India](/blog/ai-tools-for-farmers-india/).

## What to avoid

Do not buy on a demo alone. Load one chamber of real lots, run one full billing cycle, and compare the output with your register before paying. Avoid any vendor that will not give a written quote with sensor, installation and renewal charges listed separately — hidden renewal costs are the most common complaint. Once you have sensors, keep digital temperature logs; auditors ask for them, and a gap in the log is treated as a gap in care. Do not promise fixed dispatch dates until bonded-lot tracking is proved.

## FAQ

**Which is the best AI tool for cold storage in India?**

For a full operation, ColdStorageOS and StoreCrop are strongest. For a small unit needing mainly bookings and phone alerts, Cold Konnect is simpler.

**Is there a free cold storage software in India?**

There is no well-known fully free cold-storage ERP. StoreCrop and CAP offer free trials, and Cold Konnect offers a free demo. Zoho Inventory has a genuinely free plan, but it is general inventory software.

**How does temperature monitoring work in cold storage software?**

Sensors send readings to a dashboard every few minutes. When the temperature crosses your set limit, the system alerts the owner and supervisor by SMS or app, and keeps a continuous log for audits and insurance.

**Can cold storage software handle GST billing?**

Yes. StoreCrop builds GST and hamali into its rate cards, and CAP supports local tax structures. Always generate one real bill during the trial and check it with your accountant.

## Final verdict

Start with StoreCrop or ColdStorageOS for full cold-storage operations; Zoho Inventory works as a low-cost inventory add-on.
