Most Indians need only two or three: a free editor, a background remover, and a design app. All ten work in India without a VPN.

1. Canva: designs social posts fast; free plan gives limited AI uses.
2. Adobe Express: templates plus background removal; 25 generative credits monthly free.
3. remove.bg: one-click background removal; free low-resolution downloads only.
4. Pixlr: browser editor with layers; free plan allows few AI edits.
5. Photoroom: clean product-photo backgrounds; 250 free exports monthly, watermarked.
6. Fotor: quick edits and headshots; limited free credits, watermarked exports.
7. Picsart: creative effects and stickers; free, watermark on some AI exports.
8. Remini: sharpens blurry old portraits; about three free enhances daily.
9. Snapseed: Google's phone editor; completely free, no ads, no watermark.
10. Photopea: Photoshop-style layers in browser; free, ad-supported, no install.

**Which AI photo editor is best for beginners in India?** Canva or Adobe Express: both free and simple.

## Final verdict

Start with Snapseed for free editing, add remove.bg for backgrounds, and Canva for social posts.

Sources disagree on some free limits. Prices and free limits change — check the official pricing page before paying.
