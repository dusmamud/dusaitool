Architecture students in India juggle hand sketches, CAD drawings, models, juries and theory exams — often on a tight budget. The right AI tool helps at one stage: turning a sketch into a render, or explaining theory simply. It never replaces drawing skill or your own judgement, and no AI render is a working drawing.

## PromeAI — sketch to render

PromeAI turns a hand sketch, CAD line drawing or SketchUp wireframe into a finished-looking render in seconds. Try exterior, interior or landscape styles before choosing one. It suits jury boards and early concepts, not construction drawings. Sources disagree on its free allowance: one guide reports 10 coins a month. Treat it as a short trial. Paid plans start around $19–$29 a month, per third-party reports — re-check official pricing first.

## LookX — architecture-trained concepts

LookX is built for architecture, and trained on architectural images, so it usually respects walls, openings and proportions better than general tools. Upload a sketch, plan or model view, add a prompt, and generate concept renders. It also offers SketchUp and Rhino plugins. Basic is free with 100 credits and no card; Pro costs about $20 a month or $199 a year. Save credits for jury week — training and upscaling burn them fast.

## ChatGPT — study help and writing

ChatGPT is the most useful of the three for exams and writing. Ask it to explain materials, climatology or history simply, quiz you with viva-style questions, or tighten portfolio text. The free plan costs ₹0 in India, with unlimited everyday text chats; uploads, images and voice are limited — one guide reports 3 file uploads a day. Verify its facts against your textbooks — it can state wrong figures confidently.

## Using these tools in India

All three work in India without a VPN. Start on the free tiers and spend paid credits only near a jury or submission deadline. Lay out AI renders in Canva Free beside your own drawings, and label every AI image clearly so examiners see what is yours. Prices change often — re-check official pricing before paying.

## What to avoid

Do not submit an AI render as a measured drawing — dimensions and structure still need your own work. Do not copy AI text into assignments unchecked, and do not buy a yearly plan before testing the free tier. Never upload confidential drawings to a free tool without permission.

## FAQ

**Can AI tools replace AutoCAD or SketchUp for architecture students?**

No. They make concept visuals from your drawings, but not accurate plans or sections. Keep learning CAD and manual drawing; use AI only to visualise ideas faster.

**Which free AI render tool should I try first?**

Try LookX Basic first — its free plan is clearly stated at 100 credits with no card. Test PromeAI next, but expect a small free allowance and check its site first.

**Is it okay to use AI images in my architecture portfolio?**

Yes, if you label them as AI-assisted concept visuals and show your own sketches beside them. Portfolios are judged on your thinking, so AI images alone will not impress a jury.
